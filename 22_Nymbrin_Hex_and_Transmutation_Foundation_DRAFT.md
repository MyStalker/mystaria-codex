# Nymbrin transmutation and hex — foundation draft
**Author-directed foundations plus PROPOSED implementation; numerical rules NOT YET CANON.**
This replaces the direction of selecting individual rank techniques first. Develop resource/target rules before final rank selection. Reshaping, Recomposition and Vital Transmutation remain active spellcaster disciplines.

## Author's latest directions
- Basic active transmutation resembles innate Hydrokinesis: direct use generates bodily hex rather than directly spending MP.
- Hex is dangerous waste energy stored within the body and naturally dissipates over time.
- Realistic combat use requires expensive blue-mana venting: stand still, become vulnerable, then release a dangerous short-range emission. A strike may interrupt the vent and wound the user.
- During venting the frog-like vocal sac visibly swells as if croaking.
- Add a seventh discipline dedicated to weaponizing hex; successfully landed attacks remove their payload from the attacker.
- Crafting ranks simply unlock corresponding item tiers. Record those rules as **UNAPPROVED / PENDING CANON REVIEW AFTER CENTAUR CRAFTING**.

## Proposed free foundation: Transmutation
All naturally blue-affined Nymbrin receive basic Transmutation without SP, organized into physical reshaping, chemical recomposition and minor biological work. The three active disciplines expand tactical possibilities, permitted complexity and applications. They are not three unrelated lists of fixed spells.

At baseline: reshape simple exposed nonliving matter; separate or recompose understood simple mixtures; close a minor accessible wound or guide an existing biological process. Major reconstruction, permanent mutation, aggressive internal alteration and mana-color conversion require suitable advanced mastery. Highest-level mana conversion remains approved lore, not a baseline action.

A written circle defines the work and its boundary. Portable inscribed tools permit field work; permanent arrays support industry. Ordinary circles focus and specify changes; they do not independently supply hex capacity. Modern philosopher's stones have finite alchemical reserves and cannot erase exchange or pollution.

**Matter persists after completed transmutation** unless its new state requires continued support. A self-supporting stone step remains; a physically impossible suspended shape falls when active support ends. Sustained manipulation and repeated alterations continue to generate hex.

## Proposed numerical starting point
These numbers are playtest proposals, not approved racial statistics.
- Reach: **2 + effective INT meters**, matching Hydrokinesis' base reach.
- Rapid handling: **5 × effective INT kilograms per action**, in one connected affected region and one coherent operation.
- Hex Capacity: **10 + 2 × natural effective END**. Equipment Soul Strain reduces usable MP, not this physical capacity. Equipment-granted END does not increase this proposed capacity.
- Basic mass cost: **ceil(affected kg / 10)**, minimum 1.
- Complexity multiplier: **×1 Reshaping; ×2 Recomposition; ×3 Vital Transmutation** for routine work. Difficult unfamiliar or exceptionally intricate work increases cost or requires mastery; figures do not authorize every conceivable conversion.
- Natural recovery: **1 hex per minute without active alchemy**, too slow to substitute for combat venting. This is bodily neutralization, not rapid tactical dumping into the room.

Example using INT 10 and natural effective END 10: 12-meter reach, 50-kg rapid handling, 30 capacity. Routine 30-kg reshaping generates 3 hex; recomposition 6; biological restructuring 9 if the operation is within current mastery.

Mass counts the actual changed matter. Altering one hinge does not count the whole gate. Altering a narrow load-bearing section can have much larger ordinary physical consequences. Those consequences remain a tactical opportunity, not a free guarantee against reinforced structures.

**Distance is not volume.** Within reach does not grant the whole area as an effect. Air is measured by actual gas mass; a room is not a sealed battlefield-wide breathing attack. Moving, diffusing gas requires continued control, cannot be changed inside protected bodies at baseline and remains limited by reach, time, containment and resistance.

## Target rules
- One rapid action affects one connected region for one coherent change. Independent targets need more actions or a later specialization.
- Feedstock, constituents, mass and required understanding constrain exchange. Hex is a cost, not substitute matter.
- Circles must address a known, accessible region. Surface access does not reveal unknown organs, hidden mechanisms or the inside of sealed armor.
- Living bodies and actively reinforced/enchanting matter resist changes. Targeting an enemy's protected internal tissue is not a free baseline option.
- Minor wound repair can address less mass than a whole body but still requires tissue, anatomical knowledge and appropriate mastery. It does not freely replace blood.
- Whole-body transformations count substantially all transformed tissue; permanent Hexen production requires advanced ability, planning and resources.
- Advanced mastery may enable resisted hostile transformations, with establishment and interruption opportunities. It does not grant unconditional instant defeat.
- Unattended stones, modern circles and allies do not grant free shared capacity.

Large projects proceed through multiple bounded operations and interruptions for recovery/venting. Workshops can use multiple operators and engineered collection, but their time, mana, material and pollution costs remain real. An entire Pazara house is not one instant free cast; its living tissue follows biological complexity when directly changed.

## Body load and overload
| Bodily load | Proposed consequence |
|---|---|
| Below half capacity | Normal operation. |
| Half to below three-quarters | Visible throat/gill flushing, harsh voice and a recognizable warning; no penalty spiral yet. |
| Three-quarters to full capacity | Pain, visible red vapor on strained breaths and increasing vulnerability to a vent mishap. |
| Would exceed capacity | Ordinary action cannot safely complete; the player can stop or deliberately overchannel. |
| Deliberate overchannel | Internal injury and exposure, potentially collapse; no safe extra reserve. Precise injury resolution is unresolved. |

Normal Nymbrin toxin immunity and Haze acclimatization do not protect against internal hex overload. Ordinary users do not automatically become Hexen merely for holding hex; permanent mutation is a separate consequence/event, not a guaranteed meter threshold.

## Proposed innate action: Croakvent
A basic safety mechanism available without buying the seventh discipline.
1. Spend blue MP and begin a stationary channel on your turn. Your vocal sac inflates visibly; a strained croak announces the effort.
2. Remain planted until release on your next turn, giving opponents an intervening opportunity to strike, displace or interrupt you.
3. On completion emit a **3-meter cone**, dangerous to allies and enemies, and remove up to **one-third of Hex Capacity**, rounding down, from bodily load.
4. Proposed blue cost: **1 MP per 2 hex scheduled for removal**, rounding up; paid at the start and lost on interruption. No refund if the target moves away.
5. A damaging strike calls for a stability/resistance check; failure interrupts and wounds the user internally. Forced displacement breaks the stationary channel. Exact check and wound values are unresolved.
6. No hex is removed until successful completion. Canceling early retains it and loses committed MP. A failed vent is not a beneficial discharge.

The example Nymbrin can expel 10 hex for 5 MP, half their unstrained natural 10-MP pool, after an exposed interval. This intentionally makes venting expensive. Equipment Soul Strain can make an ambitious work/combat load unsustainable, encouraging allies, smaller operations, recovery and careful equipment.

Venting discharges dangerous contamination locally and contributes to Haze. It is not purification. Outside combat, communities need places to handle the emissions. A user can choose a smaller affordable vent; low MP must not force an unavoidable overload.

## Seventh discipline: Hexcasting (working title)
The discipline weaponizes stored waste into close-range offensive techniques. **Hexcasting**, **Hexspite**, and **Croakcraft** are naming options, not three additional skills.

The core distinction from venting: a weaponized hex pattern must establish a receiving connection before the body lets go of the committed payload.
- Commit available stored hex to an attack.
- On a successful receiving hit, deduct that payload and contaminate/harm the recipient.
- On a miss, evasion or complete rejection, the payload stays inside the user.
- Reduction is capped by committed payload, ability throughput and what the recipient can actually accept.
- Toxic pain, disruption of magical control and contamination are characteristic effects; hex is not a sixth mana color and cannot refill MP.
- No weapon technique generates free net waste removal or recycles hex into more usable mana.
- A hit on a viable living or mana-active receiver can discharge. Bare ground and inert rocks cannot serve as effortless targets to bypass Croakvent. Abandoned charged devices may be contaminated but have finite acceptance; dumping into allies is harmful.
- Defended/absorbed hits transfer only to the receiver that actually accepted them. A ward is not automatically an unlimited sink.
- Ordinary weapon use can consume the normal attack opportunity. Extra hex damage is not an automatic free attack.
- Basic techniques need bodily hex rather than the large MP purge fee; exotic projections may add explicit MP costs when designed. Exact costs remain unresolved.
- Early techniques favor touch or very short reach. Later ranks should open delivery methods, timing and disruption opportunities rather than just bigger damage.

This creates a choice between an expensive predictable purge and risky offensive discharge. A Hexcaster improves opportunities to manage the resource but cannot escape it by striking air.

## Crafting and environmental accounting
The three manufacturing disciplines retain rank-to-tier access with paid recipes. Do not price their products as free innate transmutation.
- Manufacturing generates hex in the operator or designated finite apparatus; moving it into a reservoir creates a waste-handling problem.
- A completed item does not automatically charge its eventual user the same manufacturing burden again. Item-specific activation emissions must be explicit.
- Permanent Hexen keep generating hex after creation.
- Haze exposure imposes toxic exposure; it need not be identical to voluntary alchemical bodily load. Neither metric grants contamination immunity.
- Natural bodily neutralization handles modest personal exposure; concentrated industrial emissions, leaks and vents still outrun environmental clearance.
- Hex collection relocates contamination. No cleansing recipe or transmutation loop deletes all hex while itself costing nothing.
