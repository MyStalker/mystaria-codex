# Mystaria — Drakyn: Origins, Biology, Sorcery and Wyrmcraft

**Authority:** CANON. **Approved:** 2026-11-03, including the name *Drakyn* and the revised race as a whole.  
**Terminology:** *Drakyn* is both singular and plural; *dragon* is the common descriptive word. **Primary source basis:** the approved Drakyn development conversation and its standalone `Drakyn_Player_Field_Guide.html`. Sorcery prices cross-reference `03_Sorcery.md`.

## I. Essential identity and history

Drakyn are an ancient **mortal** race engineered by the Serpentari Imperium during the **God War** to hunt **immortal Starspawn**. Their physical, elemental, and magical gifts were created through Serpentari experimentation, **not bestowed by a goddess**. The first Drakyn was **Tiamat**, an immense five-headed Chromatic Dragoness, each head possessing a distinct personality. Kobolds especially venerate her; adult dragons more commonly worship other powers.

Early Drakyn were enslaved weapons, usually immobilized between hunts. Later divine interference (associated with Arrest) introduced a strong instinctive aversion to involuntary cages, restraints and captivity. These instincts still create deep distress in imprisoned Drakyn; voluntarily accepted restrictions are distinguished from coercive restraint.

Drakyn created **mortal Sorcery** by adapting Starspawn **Starsong** into structured spoken **Elder Resonance**. Their Wyrmcraft is unrivalled at altering spells' geometry, timing, price, combinations, and delivery. They often dispute authorship or magical primacy with Starspawn.

## II. Shape, growth and perception

| Attribute | Canon rule |
|---|---|
| **Mantleform** | Humanoid form with draconic head/face, scales, claws, **two manipulating arms/hands**, **two legs**, **two separate wings on the back**, and variable horns/tail. |
| **Crownform** | True quadrupedal dragon, four legs, wings separately mounted, and **highly opposable forelegs** capable of manipulation. |
| **Firstshaped Drakyn** | Usually possess only Crownform; approximately **5% of Drakyn**, per the approved race-specific rate. May be extremely small, even pocket-sized. |
| **Hoardgrowth** | Crownform body size increases with the size and personal significance of the dragon's genuinely treasured hoard, **not directly with level or MP**. |
| **Treasure Compass** | Innate directional attraction to desirable treasures, including not-yet-owned ones. Greater treasures generally detectable farther away. Dragons develop conspicuously fixed, narrowed-pupil attention toward a find. |
| **Lifespan** | Ancient, very long-lived mortal beings comparable in longevity to Serpentari. Do not convert this to a precise universal year count without a dated ruling. |

A hoard may hold gold, gemstones, handwritten poetry, rare works, technology, armies, rivals, knowledge, or beloved companions. **Personal treasuring**, not market value alone, drives Hoardgrowth.

## III. Egg-acquired elements

Drakyn inherit their elemental nature from the **environment in which their egg develops**, not their parents' genetic element. A volcano can yield fire, a trench water/sea, a mountain air, a glacier ice, and other environments earth, storm, toxic, etc. **Fire is most common**; ice dragons are exceptionally cold-resistant and generally do **not** hibernate. Many other dragons bulk up and hibernate in the cold unless they heat or migrate.

Elemental Drakyn have biological adaptations and **near-immunity** to their native element, accompanied by contextual vulnerabilities (fire versus cold, ice versus heat, etc.). Fire types may thrive around lava; sea types survive ocean trenches; air types approach the edge of the atmosphere. This does **not** automatically give a mana affinity.

**Innate elemental integration:** A Drakyn may change the expression of **any known Sorcery Incantation** to fit its egg element **with no SP surcharge and no Wyrmcraft prerequisite**. Effects retain their governing magical principle. Examples: toxic Ignis produces a poison blast; ice Ignis becomes freezing; air Asterel can become a lingering deflecting wind barrier rather than a rigid wall. No elemental adaptation bypasses a prerequisite such as Kalreth requiring existing injury or Selvok needing existing mana.

## IV. Mana ecology and Starspawn hunting

- **Natural MP = 0. Rest regeneration = 0.** Drakyn gain mana from consuming mana-bearing creatures, especially Starspawn.
- Eating Starspawn blood, flesh, limbs or equivalent available material permits potentially **100% capture of the mana available in the consumed source**. A willing Starspawn may even agree to a painless whole-reserve transfer by an appropriate lethal device; Celestial Recall restores Starspawn after death, except for exceptional immortality-severing relics such as Celestite.
- Consuming a mortal magical creature can supply at most **half** an equivalent available mana amount; this is considered a severe insult, cruelty, or desperate emergency, and is extremely taboo in most Drakyn cultures.
- Prey with **0 MP contributes 0**. Source mana **color** determines the Drakyn's current available mana and compatible Sorcery Incantations.
- A Drakyn can hold **one active color at a time**. Consuming a different color **replaces** the prior pool; leftover prior-color MP is expelled as ambient mana clouds. Warlocks can harvest these clouds under their ordinary access and handling rules without a special unlock.
- Starspawn meat, including butchered pieces, disappears through **Celestial Recall within hours**. Refrigeration may slow ordinary spoilage but **never stops Recall**. Trade in temporary Starspawn meat creates its own culinary and storage economy.
- Drakyn are Starspawn predators and often hold strong rivalry or contempt toward them. Some honor voluntary painless feeding arrangements; others hunt cruelly. Starspawn can remain personal nemeses through repeated Recalls.

## V. Family, society, religion and social dangers

Drakyn live in small territorial **packs**, often roughly 2–7 kin and chosen family. Roles may include **Crownfang** leadership, **Hoardkeeper**, **Wingmate**, mates and hatchlings. Pack loyalty is intense, while an uninvited dragon entering claimed territory may be treated as a rival sovereign—even if much weaker. Courtship and mating commonly involve exchanged **Consort's dragonmarks**.

Drakyn collect a **dragon tax**, demanding tribute in whatever the local ruler treasures: wealth, poetry, rare inventions, service, prestigious heroes, or learning. Honorable dragons may return real protection and keep exact promises; tyrannical ones may extort. Most adult Drakyn respect **Arrest** and hold oaths sacred without necessarily being kind. Approximately **one-fifth** align with **Baphomet** as the Unbridled, often treacherous, punitive, excessive, or cruel.

### Swarms

A **Swarm** assembles many dragon packs, frequently dozens or hundreds, in chaotic aerial displays and contests of status. It generally requires an acknowledged **Overcrown** to stay coherent. Without one, packs may scatter or turn dangerously competitive. Named patterns include **Crownflight** (courtship), **Red Hunger** (famine), **Warflight** (campaign), **Astral Hunt** (Starspawn), and **Hoardcall** (treasure).

### Relationships

Kobolds worship Tiamat, and **First Devotion** imprinting on local Drakyn can produce genuine physiological adaptations across molts: gills from sea dragons, heat tolerance and cold vulnerability from fire dragons, cold tolerance from ice dragons, etc. It **does not** grant Drakyn Sorcery automatically. Independent Kobold lair workshops often innovate freely in Orbcraft and may shelter fugitives from Naga-controlled lands.

In the **Vren Blood Courts**, Baphomet-aligned Drakyn may enjoy prestige and excess; others are exploited as highly valued mounts or enslaved breath weapons, intensifying their native terror of restraint. Some enter Naga aristocratic society and accumulate immense estates, though slavery presents moral and political tensions.

## VII. Wyrmcraft — all five approved racial Craft disciplines

**Native cost:** 5 SP per rank. Every discipline has ranks I–V; buy earlier ranks in the same tree before later ones. A complete native discipline is **25 SP**; **all 25 Wyrmcraft ranks cost 125 SP**. General gates: I/level 1; II/10; III/25; IV/50; V/80.

**Foreign acquisition:** A compatible outsider needs a **Wyrmscript mark/foreign Awakening Trial**, **5 SP once**; first rank in any foreign discipline **7 SP**, each following rank **6 SP**. First mastered foreign discipline with awakening **36 SP**, each further one **31 SP**; all five fully mastered **160 SP** inclusive of one awakening. A teaching mark is not an unrestricted mana-color exemption.

Wyrmcraft is **metamagic**: it changes the performance of learned Incantations, without supplying unlearned spells or breaking their fundamental magical exclusions. Early draft surcharges of +1/+2/+3/+4/+5 MP by rank are **provisional**, not globally finalized—specific named effects and exceptions override them.

## VIII. Dragonmarks — approved and differentiated

Most dragonmarks are **permanent bite scars**; ordinary healing magic usually cannot erase them. They may signal humiliation, relationship, nemesis, or pledged duty. Types include **ordinary symbolic mark**, **Consort's Mark**, **Rival's Crest**, **Oathmark**, and spell-bearing **Wyrmscript**.

- **Innate Long Reckoning:** Every Drakyn may mark a mortal to **double ordinary lifespan**, or extend it further according to the Drakyn's strength, potentially up to the Drakyn's own lifespan. It is a cantrip-like innate gift/curse with **no MP mark cost**; can preserve favored friends or prolong a captive enemy's life in cruelty.
- **Wyrmscript granting:** Drakyn may **directly grant a known Sorcery Incantation and individual learned alterations** (not just write instructions). Recipient pays **Soul Strain equal to the recipient's ordinary purchase SP price**: standard Incantation 3; each alteration 3; base + five alterations = **18**. They may later **buy off the Strain by paying the normal SP cost** without losing the dragonmark or skill. This does not supply mana affinity, compatible color, or an inappropriate casting ability.
- **Element Mark:** Grants the Drakyn's innate elemental integration technique to a marked caster; does not grant the element's physical resistance, a new mana affinity or all dragon magic. **5 Soul Strain equivalent** appears in approved revision.
- **Silent Thunder mark:** Giftable/usable on self or another, but **only one maintained in existence per Drakyn**. Enables one known Sorcery to be used silently for 0 MP via a miniature ley line that accelerates existing Formless Mana through the cycle into that one spell. The chosen spell still requires its applicable medium. It is conspicuously detectable, vulnerable to ley-line interference, and harms local magical ecology if abused. Its **special mark Soul Strain price was not given a confirmed numeral**.
- **Death Rest Covenant:** Advanced Tooth's Testament (V) magic that binds a mortal's life-force to the granting dragon. Otherwise fatal wounds instead trigger a **deathlike paralysis** with the mind conscious. Endures approximately **one week under extreme dangerous conditions** or **up to one year undisturbed in safety**. Healing before expiry may restore the recipient; exceptionally powerful violence/magic may exceed the protection. The Drakyn is notified of the trigger/location and is **significantly weakened while Death Rest remains active**.
- Marks on Starspawn normally vanish with the body through Celestial Recall; a permanent ordinary bite scar does not routinely defeat immortal restoration. Truly exceptional exceptions are legends, not a universal dragonmark power.

**Itemization example:** A non-Drakyn with a dragonmark granting one base Incantation and two alterations gains the associated knowledge with **9 Soul Strain**; spending **9 SP** later internalizes/buys off that Soul Strain.

## IX. Practical play and continuity

**Best strengths:** massive elemental mobility, profound Sorcery knowledge, transformative hoards, unusual mana flexibility from feeding, precise metamagic, and oath-based diplomacy. **Best vulnerabilities:** natural MP vacuum, color replacement, contested Starspawn feeding ethics, intense hoarding impulses, severe captivity aversion, and social rivalry.

**Celestite cross-reference:** This rare divine metal universally activates every Drakyn's Treasure Compass, regardless of hoarding preference; see `07_Centaurs_and_Smithcraft.md`. Its anti-ley-line field threatens Silent Thunder.

**Unspecified and not to be fabricated:** exact Drakyn universal lifespan in years; exact activation cost for each Wyrmcraft metamagic; full Caught Breath slot/prepayment algorithm; maximum simultaneous Songmarks beyond the overlapping rule; Silent Thunder mark strain price; detailed Tiamat head identities. Preserve these gaps until explicitly ruled.


## Toxic dragon eggs — approved Nymbrin/Haze expansion
**Exposure to the red Haze is the primary cause of dragon eggs developing into toxic dragons.** The Haze is airborne hex pollution from Scarlands alchemical industry; this adds a primary environmental cause without replacing other established dragon biology or Wyrmcraft mechanics. ## November 5, 2026 — Elemental near-immunity clarified

Even a fire-element Drakyn has **near-immunity to heat, NOT absolute immunity**. Heat intense enough, an applicable exception or a relevant weakness can still overcome the creature's natural resistance. This applies to other innate Drakyn elemental protections as well: language such as 'immune' in informal descriptions of elemental defenses denotes **near-immunity** in the ordinary energy/damage framework, never absolute energy-proof invulnerability. **No numerical Elemental Magnitude threshold for overcoming near-immunity is approved.** The principle does not change how the elemental egg type is acquired, existing contrasting vulnerabilities, mana affinity, Sorcery integration, or Drakyn natural MP = 0.

## November 6, 2026 — Crownform battle saddles (LATEST APPROVED EQUIPMENT CONCEPT)

**Approved:** A properly designed **battle saddle** allows a Crownform Drakyn to carry, aim and fire appropriate **side-mounted firearms or mechanically operated crossbows** while remaining on four legs. Firstshaped Drakyn especially benefit because they generally have only Crownform; normal Drakyn may instead use Mantleform when desired. **Crownform forelegs retain their already-approved highly opposable manipulating capability**; saddle technology overcomes stance/mount/control issues, not an inability to manipulate at all. The saddle is an item interface, **not** intrinsic free weapon Power or additional attacks. Engineering must respect Drakyn dimensions, balance, wings, foreleg access, turning arcs, recoil, controls and reloading. Bows and thrown weapons are not automatically saddle compatible. No racial bonus, slot count, rig pricing, manufacturing rank or reload automation yet approved.
