# Moontouched Fashion in the Gilded Coil

**Status:** APPROVED CANON for specific author decisions of November 6, 2026. Optional extensions are labeled **PROPOSAL**.
**Authority:** Latest user decisions; racial and community context in `02_Races.md`, `18_The_Gilded_Coil_Imperial_Gazetteer.md`; approved Moonlace visual direction in `25_Imperial_Collar_Culture_and_Restraints.md`; art direction in `11_Visual_Style_Guide.md`.

## I. Everyday Dress: Comfort and Warmth — CANON

Moontouched are rabbitfolk who value comfortable, welcoming clothes. **Sweaters are an important part of casual wear to keep warm. Socks are likewise especially important and common for warmth.** These garments may coexist with short skirts, fitted outfits or other clothing consistent with Mystaria's mature Imperial fashion tone; softness and ease matter. Avoid treating a servant costume as a universal Moontouched identity.

Warm layers are not proof that every Moontouched is cold at every moment. Clothing varies by weather, location, household, tastes and profession. No new species-wide cold-vulnerability modifier is established.

## II. Formal Clothing — CANON

**Pantyhose are common in Moontouched formal attire**, alongside appropriate dress garments and accessories. This is a cultural fashion tendency, not a legal obligation, caste marker, or mechanic. Formality need not imply maid dress.

## III. Household Service and Maid Dress — CANON

**Moontouched frequently work as maids and wear maid uniforms when performing those duties.** Maid-inspired outfits belong particularly to actual domestic service, relevant workplace presentation and themed hospitality rather than all aspects of Moontouched daily life.

A Moontouched out shopping, enjoying a family gathering, resting at home or attending an unrelated social function is **not** assumed to wear a maid uniform. Their personal wardrobes remain individual.

Imperial domestic slavery and ordinary voluntary employment are not the same: an enslaved person's uniform may be owner-mandated, whereas a free worker can choose an employer and terms. Wearing a maid uniform is not conclusive evidence of ownership or legal status.

The existing **Moonlace** collar visual design remains separately governed by `25_Imperial_Collar_Culture_and_Restraints.md`. Uniform style neither replaces compulsory identification where applicable nor establishes new magical effects.

## IV. Maid Cafés and Themed Hospitality — CANON

**Maid cafés and similar maid-themed hospitality establishments are popular cultural venues for Moontouched.** Maid-inspired uniforms are especially appropriate for staff participating in these roles, without becoming mandatory fashion for Moontouched elsewhere. Establishments vary by management, audience, personal choice and local Imperial conditions. Neither all maid café workers nor all Moontouched are necessarily enslaved.

These venues fit the existing Gilded Coil hospitality economy and the Moontouched affinity for nurturing social environments, while the setting's tensions around ownership and autonomy remain intact. No named chain of cafés, prices, labor law, standard menu, or formal costume specification is established by this approval.

## V. Existing Racial Customs and Open Fashion Proposals

**Already-established racial background:** Moontouched live in Warrens and Hearth Circles and exchange ribbons and small affectionate gifts. Their community traditions, chosen adult naming and Surrender's principle that trust cannot be demanded remain in force.

**PROPOSAL, not yet approved:** Standardizing gifted ribbons into elaborate formal costume traditions; shared Hearth Circle ceremonial wardrobes; a specific pastel palette, lace or floral dress code; obligatory decorative bows; a universal arrangement of layered skirt and cardigan; or a named ceremonial dress tradition. These may be explored later without being treated as established.

## VI. Art and Mechanics Boundaries

Follow the approved Mystaria illustration direction and small rabbitfolk anatomy (normally around 4–5 feet tall). When drawing Moontouched, distinguish cozy sweaters/socks in casual scenes, pantyhose with formal outfits, and maid uniforms in work or maid-café contexts. The race's existing limited innate magic, stats, faith, Moonlace collar, and social institutions are unchanged. No costume confers armor, magical power, social status or immunity to cold by itself.
