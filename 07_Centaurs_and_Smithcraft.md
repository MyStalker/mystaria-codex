# Mystaria — Centaurs, Anvilcant, Firearms, Materials and Celestite

**Authority:** The Centaur race, original Anvilcant foundation, twelve named materials, and Celestite are **CANON**, approved 2026-11-03. The later **modular-equipment and clockwork overhaul is PROPOSED**, not approved. The distinction is explicit throughout.  
**Latest approved attributes (November 5, 2026):** STR 21, DEX 13, END 18, INT 16, SPD 20 (base). Do **not** restore the older +2 DEX/END/INT draft.

## I. Biology and place in the world — CANON

**Approved illustration style — November 4, 2026:** More anime-inspired character design, with controlled linework, simplified facial features, and expressive posing. See `11_Visual_Style_Guide.md`; follow current approved racial anatomy.

Centaurs have the entire humanlike upper torso, arms and hands of an ordinary humanoid joined to the body of a fully formed equine below the waist. Their great size gives enormous lifting and pulling capacity, fast ground movement, and exceptional stamina. Roughly **90% are female (mares)** and **10% male (stallions)**. They are beautiful by local standards and valued both for work and public ornamentation.

**Firstshaped Centaurs are Ponies:** smaller and entirely equine, lacking arms/hands, with extraordinary **oral dexterity** enabling specialized tools. Ponies may be full-grown adults and accomplished artisans, not merely children. Approximately **10% of Centaurs** are Firstshaped Ponies, per the approved race-specific rate. Drakyn have their own approximately 5% rate.

## II. Origins and the holy city — CANON

Early Centaurs were traveling metalworkers and caravan builders who solved immense load-bearing and construction problems as the Canopy Kingdoms developed upward into forests and giant trees. Under the **First Covenant of Iron Boughs**, ancestral smith families pledged labor in exchange for forges, provisions, shelter and access to materials. Reciprocal obligations hardened over generations into hereditary enslavement. The unresolved moral question is whether inherited ownership was a betrayal of the original covenant or its natural continuation; Centaurs and Thornguard both care deeply about honest promises.

The holy city of **Veyravel, the Sevenfold Forge** stands in the Canopy Kingdoms. Its seven ascending ceremonial roads celebrate **Labor, Beauty, Honesty, Endurance, Devotion, Courage and Mastery**. Every able Centaur desires at least one lifetime pilgrimage. At the summit, a pilgrim brings a personally created object to the **First Anvil**, strikes it and makes a defining promise for the years ahead. Many Centaurs fear heights; the journey up the vertical Ascents is a test of courage even where paths are broad and safe. Veyravel's forge architecture is famous; large everlasting **Hearthiron** lights suit its ceremonial surroundings.

**Religion:** Surrender is a principal goddess of freely given love, trust and closeness; Arrest is honored for truth and just obligations. Centaur debates distinguish a **voluntary vow** from inherited or imposed ownership, without resolving all social controversies.

## III. Values, households, romance and work — CANON

Centaurs prize **form and function together** in an object, person, or practice. Complex tail braiding, bells, polished harness fittings, decorated horseshoes and ceremonial jewelry are celebrated. They speak eloquently and have a vigorous poetic tradition with rhythms borrowed from the forge. They love challenge, intense work and even difficult tests; long idleness may make them restless, skittish or anxious. A full day's work by another race may feel unsatisfying to one of them. Wanderlust is common and trusted enslaved Centaurs often travel and return voluntarily after seeking new roads and ideas.

Most Centaurs in the Canopy Kingdoms are enslaved, with many also traded or owned elsewhere. Some proudly embrace service and may demand demanding standards, correction, meaningful labor or stern attention from masters. Some intentionally court difficult tests; pain and pleasure may both be valued. These cultural tendencies are **not universal** and do **not** mean all coercion is welcome or any particular person's consent can be assumed. Some escape; many liberated or escaped Centaurs seek a better-aligned household or patron rather than complete independence. Relationships with owners can involve genuine love and controversy alongside unequal power.

**Yokebound** are Centaurs serving chiefly as beasts of burden, hauling equipment, farmwork or building supplies; their arms and upper body may be restrained during labor. Many value the challenge, while others regard losing the use of their skilled hands as humiliating. A work yoke is **not** a marriage saddle.

Centaurs compete over beauty, work, artistry and the recognition of masters. They often clash socially with **Moontouched (Moonkin in some Centaur speech)**, and professionally with Kobolds, whose Orbcraft competes with Centaur claims to be the greatest makers.

### The Saddle Vow

A properly fitted **riding saddle** offered to and accepted by a Centaur is equivalent to marriage, **including when the chosen mate is the Centaur's legal master**. These saddles are elaborate yet practical objects of personal Smithcraft, emotionally comparable to wedding rings. The couple's **First Ride** solemnizes their shared path. A docile, loyal servant may violently refuse unauthorized proper saddling. Pulling a wagon or allowing a wounded companion to ride **bareback** for a temporary need does **not** imply marriage. Legitimate custom treats a freely accepted vow differently from coerced restraint; owner/enslaved marriages remain controversial.

Centaurs tend to court indirectly through metalwork, poems, braids, gifts, and crafted gestures rather than blunt declarations. The cultural aphorism is **“What is held close is held dear.”**

## IV. Anvilcant — Smithcraft, original approved six-discipline foundation

Anvilcant is a **green-mana, materials-science magical Craft** devoted to forging, alloys, enchantments made permanent in physical construction, and the structural engineering necessary for Centaur architecture. It is **not** the same as Kobold Orbcraft, which uses light through mirrors/orbs to transmit and organize magical data; nor does it supplant Bloomkin's softer fiber and textile crafts.

**Original canon:** six disciplines, each five ranks at **5 SP/rank**, **25 SP/mastered discipline**, **150 SP for all six**. Native gates I/1, II/10, III/25, IV/50, V/80; acquire ranks sequentially. ## V. Equipment affinity — CANON

Centaurs suffer **50% reduced equipment Soul Strain**, and receive **50% reduced equipment-derived stat bonuses**. Both are rounded **up after halving the total item value**. Other activated powers, mundane protection, reach and material traits are unaltered. For example: ordinary Quality III sword **+10 STR / 4 Soul Strain** becomes **+5 STR / 2 Soul Strain** on a Centaur. The reduction does **not** cover unrelated dragonmark strain or Celestite's direct soul damage. Full five-quality benchmarks in `09_Equipment_and_Economy.md`.

## VII. Celestite — the Twelfth Material and the Godslayer (**CANON**)

Celestite is **Mystaria's rarest substance**. It was forged during the God War by elite **Centaurs mastered in multiple Smithcraft disciplines**, with the **personal, simultaneous cooperation of Arrest, Surrender and Baphomet** and a tremendous expenditure of divine power. Existing weapons remain in perfect condition. They were vital to reliably defeating ancient **Serpentari**.

Its power targets the magical **principles** sustaining entities and effects, not just conventional hardness:

- **Starspawn:** a decisive fatal Celestite blow can **sever immortality**, defeating ordinary Celestial Recall. Not every superficial contact is automatically final.
- **Divinity:** it could **conceptually slay even a goddess**; this possibility is terrifying and not proof that such an event has occurred.
- **Ley lines:** can cut ley lines and breach boundaries into the **Celestial Realms**, often creating dangerous rents rather than safe portals.
- **Anti-ley-line field:** constantly absorbs most nearby magic, including friendly protective and healing spells, with only the strongest effects resisting. Particularly effective against magical systems descended from the Serpentari. Resists magic directed at its wielder. Threatens Drakyn **Silent Thunder** marks.
- **Dragons:** **every Drakyn Treasure Compass triggers** in response to Celestite, whatever the dragon normally hoards; this may draw extraordinary Hoardcall swarms. All known relics have been concealed as thoroughly as possible.

### Irrecoverable cost of swinging Celestite

**Every stroke** inflicts direct **soul damage**, not just ordinary Soul Strain: XP is lost, levels may regress, and permanent stats can be lost and must be regained through later growth. Intense use can erode a soul to nothing. Centaur equipment affinity cannot halve this cost.

The bearer needs an **unbreakable heart**: a clear, single purpose held unwaveringly from drawing the weapon onward. Losing conviction risks **False Apotheosis**, a power-fueled delusion of growing godhood even while the bearer spiritually disintegrates. An unwavering goal prevents that delusion but **does not** prevent soul erosion.

Celestite does **not** have a normal Tier VI, a Gem price, or a craftable routine product recipe. All true artifacts require the rare triune goddess-level forging, not just Rank V Smithcraft. Artificial reproduction attempts have failed; the tentative consequences called **Godglass**, **Hollow Saint**, **Riftsilver**, and **Gravesong Iron** are **candidate failed-forgery byproducts**, not additional canon base materials unless separately approved.

Centaur smiths created advanced black-powder firearms. Their main propellant is **Crownsalt**, extremely powerful against modern armor and magical protections but destructive to ordinary gunmetal. Firearms require exotic pressure-bearing components (not necessarily full-heavy-metal construction); **Adamantite** components are common in high-pressure designs. **Trufula** frames can work with compatible **noncombustive propellants**, because ordinary fire would ignite the wood.

## November 5, 2026 — Firearm handling and armor scaling status

**Approved clarification:** The correct material spelling remains **Adamantite**, not Adamantine. **Crownsalt** is also acceptably called **gunpowder**. Guns' offensive force is determined by the gun and ammunition, **not directly by the shooter's Strength**. Each gun is to have a damage-to-required-Strength relationship: insufficient Strength impairs aim/handling and magnifies recoil, with a minimum below which unsupported use is impossible and a sufficient-strength threshold for negligible recoil burden. Exact numeric thresholds are **UNRESOLVED**. Adamantite-reinforced high-end firearms can be extraordinarily heavy; supports or bracing can enable firing weapons too heavy to wield freely but are poor for mobile melee and do not automatically eliminate recoil. Magic including Jester ribbons may mitigate carried weight/movement **without automatically cancelling recoil**. An actual **severe recoil injury advances one wound state**; numerical trigger is unresolved. Trufula frames still require compatible **noncombustive propellants**; use of the colloquial word gunpowder does not change material compatibility.

**Armor scaling — UNFINISHED PROPOSAL:** Reconcile protective ratings, quadratic Endurance +10 defenses, armor penetration and all five equipment-quality tiers with approved Centaur Smithcraft/Anvilcant and equipment-affinity rules. Do **not** treat prior sample armor ratings, penetration percentages or equipment-quality conversion as canon. See `22_Combat_Endurance_and_Shimmer_Guard.md` and `09_Equipment_and_Economy.md`. No Smithcraft ranks, costs or established material properties are altered here.

## November 5, 2026 — Extreme-speed charge principle (cross-reference)

Speed-led attacks may gain modest **Power** from **relative closing velocity** after suitable acceleration and run-up, not from SPD alone and not from extra attacks. Matching the mover's velocity to cancel their closing-speed difference removes that speed-derived charge bonus. **Dash** represents peak burst, sprint one speed-chart tier below, sustainable jog two tiers below; exact dash mph and Power bonuses, including the illustrative 25% at 100 mph and 50% at 500 mph, remain **PROPOSAL**. This does not change Adamantite/crownsalt gun construction, recoil, firearm SPD reaction tiers, established weapon multipliers, or unfinished armor/Speed-penalty integration.
