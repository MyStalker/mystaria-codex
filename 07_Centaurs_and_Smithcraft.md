## II. Origins and the holy city — CANON

Early Centaurs were traveling metalworkers and caravan builders who solved immense load-bearing and construction problems as the Canopy Kingdoms developed upward into forests and giant trees. Under the **First Covenant of Iron Boughs**, ancestral smith families pledged labor in exchange for forges, provisions, shelter and access to materials. Reciprocal obligations hardened over generations into hereditary enslavement. The unresolved moral question is whether inherited ownership was a betrayal of the original covenant or its natural continuation; Centaurs and Thornguard both care deeply about honest promises.

The holy city of **Veyravel, the Sevenfold Forge** stands in the Canopy Kingdoms. Its seven ascending ceremonial roads celebrate **Labor, Beauty, Honesty, Endurance, Devotion, Courage and Mastery**. Every able Centaur desires at least one lifetime pilgrimage. At the summit, a pilgrim brings a personally created object to the **First Anvil**, strikes it and makes a defining promise for the years ahead. Many Centaurs fear heights; the journey up the vertical Ascents is a test of courage even where paths are broad and safe. Veyravel's forge architecture is famous; large everlasting **Hearthiron** lights suit its ceremonial surroundings.

**Religion:** Surrender is a principal goddess of freely given love, trust and closeness; Arrest is honored for truth and just obligations. Centaur debates distinguish a **voluntary vow** from inherited or imposed ownership, without resolving all social controversies.

## III. Values, households, romance and work — CANON

Centaurs prize **form and function together** in an object, person, or practice. Complex tail braiding, bells, polished harness fittings, decorated horseshoes and ceremonial jewelry are celebrated. They speak eloquently and have a vigorous poetic tradition with rhythms borrowed from the forge. They love challenge, intense work and even difficult tests; long idleness may make them restless, skittish or anxious. A full day's work by another race may feel unsatisfying to one of them. Wanderlust is common and trusted enslaved Centaurs often travel and return voluntarily after seeking new roads and ideas.

Most Centaurs in the Canopy Kingdoms are enslaved, with many also traded or owned elsewhere. Some proudly embrace service and may demand demanding standards, correction, meaningful labor or stern attention from masters. Some intentionally court difficult tests; pain and pleasure may both be valued. These cultural tendencies are **not universal** and do **not** mean all coercion is welcome or any particular person's consent can be assumed. Some escape; many liberated or escaped Centaurs seek a better-aligned household or patron rather than complete independence. Relationships with owners can involve genuine love and controversy alongside unequal power.

**Yokebound** are Centaurs serving chiefly as beasts of burden, hauling equipment, farmwork or building supplies; their arms and upper body may be restrained during labor. Many value the challenge, while others regard losing the use of their skilled hands as humiliating. A work yoke is **not** a marriage saddle.

Centaurs compete over beauty, work, artistry and the recognition of masters. They often clash socially with **Moontouched (Moonkin in some Centaur speech)**, and professionally with Kobolds, whose Orbcraft competes with Centaur claims to be the greatest makers.

### The Saddle Vow

A properly fitted **riding saddle** offered to and accepted by a Centaur is equivalent to marriage, **including when the chosen mate is the Centaur's legal master**. These saddles are elaborate yet practical objects of personal Smithcraft, emotionally comparable to wedding rings. The couple's **First Ride** solemnizes their shared path. A docile, loyal servant may violently refuse unauthorized proper saddling. Pulling a wagon or allowing a wounded companion to ride **bareback** for a temporary need does **not** imply marriage. Legitimate custom treats a freely accepted vow differently from coerced restraint; owner/enslaved marriages remain controversial.

Centaurs tend to court indirectly through metalwork, poems, braids, gifts, and crafted gestures rather than blunt declarations. The cultural aphorism is **“What is held close is held dear.”**

## VII. Celestite — the Twelfth Material and the Godslayer (**CANON**)

Celestite is **Mystaria's rarest substance**. **Approved Windling attack identity:** A Windling swarm's characteristic offensive action is **targeted sonic distraction/harassment** emitted by its Cogsong-compatible music-box weapons. Starting a fresh attack, reassigning offensive target or initiating a different attack requires a **new commander action**. **Approved extreme-short-range counterplay:** Windlings must close to **very short range** to threaten with the sonic attack. Escape/separation, physical sound barriers, covering or shielding the ears, and attacking the close-approaching Windlings are valid defenses. Melee combatants can readily strike back when the machines approach. A target escaping the effective zone or blocking the sound stops accumulating **new sonic exposure** immediately; existing damage does not necessarily vanish. Whether Windlings can catch up depends on their actual material-derived SPD, position and environmental interference. No inevitable pressure or homing effects against a superior opponent. **Approved acoustic and electronic interaction:** The sonic music boxes do not interfere with Cogsong command frequencies. A damaged or severed receiver cannot decode **new** orders, but disabling the receiver does not alone destroy the body or erase already accepted simple standing instructions. Exact levels/durations, damage threshold, receiver behavior and protective modifiers remain open.

**Preserve:** All swarm pressure must be earned by stat-relative effectiveness, bounded squad-size multiplier and relevant resistance, with a cutoff against vastly superior opponents. Windlings cost 1 CP per four; mandatory-Loom Cogmarshal I–V CP is 2/4/6/8/12; force size unlocks richer tactics but not unlimited attacks.
