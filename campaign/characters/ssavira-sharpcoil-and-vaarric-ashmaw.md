# Ssavira Sharpcoil & Vaarric Ashmaw

**Status:** Approved campaign NPC bios; mechanics pending specific racial craft abilities.
**Campaign:** Slate's solo Mystaria campaign (Lillia).
**Assignment:** Mercenary agents hired by Ishkarith Saelith Voryss Nharasth (“The Last Architect”, provisional name), overarching Serpentari antagonist seeking six Legendary Treasures. His information-network branch sends agents during a **mid-act beat of Arc One** to **steal the Drowned Meridian**. **Ssavira and Vaarric are the designated agents for this theft.** Their theft or attempted theft, and subsequent recovery of the artifact, can drive Lillia's next adventure. Do not mark the theft as successful before play.

## Ssavira Sharpcoil

- Female **Naga** mercenary, cast out of her coil.
- **Appearance:** Distinctly sharp, angular facial features; narrow eyes, defined brow/cheek ridges, sleek hooked/bladed silhouette, elegant elongated upper body, muted/dark scales, mantle or cowl, defaced old coil insignia or trophy charms. The short-video inspiration is **vague only**, not a visual copy. Layered lamellar/scale torso armor, shoulder guards, reinforced bracers, sash or half-cloak.
- **Backstory:** Exiled after selling coil secrets to outsiders in a succession struggle, hoping to advance herself. The scheme collapsed, and surviving factions blamed her. She resents coil society and refuses to be pitied. She seeks status, revenge, wealth, or a new power base.
- **Personality:** Fast-talking, perceptive, proud, manipulative, theatrical, provocative, taunting, defensive beneath her confidence; treats violence as conversation. Reads fears, shame, resentment and social weaknesses. Resists describing herself as anyone's servant.
- **Combat:** Mobile battlefield controller. **Uses her Naga racial craft specifically for battlefield control**; exact maneuvers/mechanics to be selected from canon. Feints, poison, repositioning, zoning and disrupting enemies. Suggested weapon: hooked spear, war sickle, or barbed glaive; backup fang-dagger and potential alchemical tools. Do not finalize choices before mechanics review.
- **Duo function:** The voice: negotiates, threatens, destabilizes, interrogates and drives exposed enemies toward Vaarric.

## Vaarric Ashmaw

- Male **Kharak** mercenary and seasoned veteran.
- **Appearance:** Worn features, scars, weary eyes, weathering, thick-necked/powerful build, practical and heavy battlefield armor, soot-darkened metal, scorched leather, battered fur or cloak; heavy plated shoulders. Only **vague** visual similarity to the source character.
- **Equipment:** A **long firearm, not a pistol**, preferably a heavily reinforced **hook-rifle** built for punishing armor, cover, and monstrous targets. Other preliminary possibilities include a heavy arquebus/handgonne or repeating trench carbine, subject to setting firearms canon. Possible backup mace, hatchet, or war pick, and bandolier; shield only if appropriate.
- **Backstory:** Once served a banner, brotherhood, faith or code; details not fixed. Survivor of years of ugly work. His remaining anchors are contracts, discipline and endurance. He does not pretend mercenary service is anything else.
- **Personality:** Low-spoken, patient, dry humor, restrained, difficult to rattle, tired rather than emotionless; an eroded conscience remains. Rarely postures. He is decisive once he commits to violence.
- **Combat:** Armored heavy gunner; zone denial, overwatch, ranged anti-armor pressure and physical endurance. **Uses his Kharak racial craft for conditional attacks**, using applicable craft mechanics/triggers rather than inventing a generic damage bonus. Exact abilities and triggers pending review of Kharak canon.
- **Duo function:** The weight: anchors their position, prevents escape and punishes openings created by Ssavira.

## Relationship and Tactics

- Ssavira is needling, loquacious, psychologically invasive and ambitious. Vaarric is laconic, weathered, direct and disciplined.
- She loathes institutions that rejected her; he relies on structure as the last stable thing in his life.
- They share mutual usefulness and grudging respect, not necessarily overt affection. Both have seen the other's worst days.
- She mocks his solemnity, age and caution; he calls her noisy, reckless or exhausting.
- In battle she flushes targets from safety through racial-craft control, while he exploits the resulting **conditional attack opportunities** through his Kharak craft and longarm. Her improvisation complements his deliberate commitment.
- Ishkarith hires them as deniable, brutal, competent troubleshooters, not necessarily fanatically loyal followers.
