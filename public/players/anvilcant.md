Anvilcant is the Centaur smithing tradition: understanding materials, shaping useful things, and maintaining the work. Ordinary personal smithwork can be mana-free; advanced industry still requires tools, skill, materials, and supporting labor.

## What a smith brings

| Field | Practical work |
|---|---|
| **Orewhisper** | Understanding substances and exotic metallurgy. |
| **Formwright** | Shaping physical forms and mechanisms. |
| **Oathsteel** | Permanent enchanted equipment. |
| **Monumentcraft** | Large construction and engineered structures. |
| **Thunderforge** | Firearms and artillery. |
| **Hearthsong** | Maintenance and care of crafted work. |

Several parts of the modular discipline structure remain under revision. Choose a documented rank and prerequisite before spending SP; an overview is not a new completed purchase tree.

## Clockwork

Clockwork is built machinery that uses appropriate mana, operators, and maintenance. Cogsong/Cogmarshal operation and engineering support do different jobs. A device does not become autonomously competent merely by containing fuel.

Physical components have distinct values: precision mechanisms, reservoir material, structure, and armor plating are not all priced as one mass of enchanted metal. Cross-trained alchemists can restore or modernize appropriate machinery while conserving actual material and producing Hex.

## Materials and quality

Choose material for its job and limits. Physical Quality affects the completed construction; an enchantment is a separate enhancement. [The materials sheet](/reference/materials/) compares the important choices, and [enchantments](/learn/enchantments/) explains their power and strain costs.

## Making and maintaining equipment

A real workshop, actual inputs, qualifying skill, and time are required. Repair is not a blanket free reset; missing matter needs replacement. Mundane labor and material costs remain separate from a Hero's divine Gem accounting.

For a player crafter, agree the available workshop, qualifying ranks, intended item profile, materials, and work schedule with the GM before promising the party a finished item.
