## Ranged weapons and equipment Weight

Use the finished weapon's Weight and actual handling requirements. Higher Quality increases Weight and ordinary ranged DEX handling. Bows depend on draw strength; crossbows and firearms derive damage from the mechanism and purchased damage training, rather than the user's strength. Carrying a weapon is not the same as drawing, cocking or controlling it. Material compatibility and exceptional designs matter. Use the GM's working ranged profile for damage and AP; missing values are not zero.

A loadout is a set of tradeoffs. Your weapon determines how you threaten an enemy; armor and shields determine how you withstand them. Weight, handling, reach, and available hands tie those choices together.

## Read a weapon profile

| Entry | What it tells you |
|---|---|
| **Governing attribute or attack mode** | Which attributes contribute to the attack. Some weapons support STR, DEX, Either, or Both identities. |
| **Weapon Rating** | The weapon's independent contribution to damage. It is separate from attributes and purchased damage training. |
| **Damage form and AP** | How the attack interacts with physical armor. Use the AP recorded for that actual constructed weapon and mode. |
| **Reach and coverage** | What you can physically threaten and how much space an attack can affect. |
| **Hands and handling** | Whether you can use it while carrying a shield, implement, or other item, and what requirements you must meet. |
| **Weight** | How much of your shared equipped budget it occupies. |

Ordinary conventional melee weapons, armor and shields use shared Weight rather than unrelated generic stat gates. Specialized Handling and genuine mechanical constraints still apply. Early reference Light/Medium/Heavy armor weighs 6/12/24; shields weigh 4/8/16. A dagger weighs 2, shortsword 5, longsword 8 and greatsword 18 before material and Quality adjustments. Higher Quality concentrates mana into equipment, increasing its Weight and applicable Handling. A refined weapon may require DEX; a heavy weapon may consume much of your loadout capacity. Not every high-tier weapon automatically has the same stat requirements.

::: detail Honed and Hulking weapons
**Honed** weapons trade striking power for accuracy through careful balance. They retain the Weight of equivalent conventional weapons and require applicable DEX Handling.

**Hulking** weapons are heavier and emphasize very large blows. Their working design increases the STR-derived damage contribution and constrains accuracy. They do not have a universal STR entry gate: an END-focused fighter may carry a heavy weapon by forgoing armor.

Honed weapons use the working effective DEX Handling ladder 6/18/32/46/60 at Quality I–V. Junk cannot have these specialties. Hulking Weight is 150% of ordinary Weight. Deflection armor replaces its conventional Weight with an equal DEX Handling requirement; it consumes no armor Weight. Exact Quality Weight progression is being reconciled. Choose the finished item profile supplied by your GM.
:::

## Armor

Armor Rating reduces eligible incoming physical damage after penetration. Better conventional protection costs Weight. Use a single armor ensemble of the chosen class; the old Partial/Standard/Full coverage and armor hit-location rules are suspended for current play.

Light, Medium, and Heavy identify protection choices. They are not automatic character-level restrictions. Your actual Weight budget, handling, availability, and item profile determine what you can equip.

**Deflection armor** trades some equipment Armor Rating for Evasion. It retains half its equipment-derived AR and adds +1/+2/+3 Evasion for Light/Medium/Heavy. Armor training still contributes its full benefit. Its Handling equals the Weight of the corresponding conventional armor; the armor itself costs no Weight.

## Weight and handling

**Shared equipped capacity = max(0, floor(20 + effective STR + effective END)).** Count the complete equipped loadout together. Carrying armor, a weapon, a shield, and a second implement all compete for that capacity.

Weight is not the only requirement. Apply the item's actual handling and anatomical requirements as well. Missing catalogue values require a GM-provided profile; they are not zero by default.

## Shields and prepared defense

A shield is a separate defensive tool with its own profile, coverage, condition, and eligible block or deflection. Facing, occupied hands, attack geometry, and the particular defense matter. It is not another armor bonus that applies to everything from every direction.

Active defensive maneuvers require appropriate preparation. Declare the defense and a narrow trigger before seeing whether the incoming attack succeeds. See [combat at the table](/learn/combat/).

## Quality, material, and enchantment

**Quality** describes supernatural craftsmanship; mana density increases Weight and applicable Handling. **Material** gives properties and limitations. **Enchantment** adds an eligible magical enhancement or effect. They are separate choices.

Compare [the materials](/reference/materials/) to understand why light Trufula, heavy Adamantite, brittle Nullporcelain, or resilient Boughsteel solve different problems. Then read [how enchantments work](/learn/enchantments/) before spending on magical gear.

## Before you buy

Check the finished profile, total equipped Weight, handling, hands, and price. For magical gear also check eligible slots, installed effects, compatible Leywell mana, and any Soul Strain. An incomplete installation specification is a question for your GM, not permission to assume it works.


## Imperial gadgets and augmentations

Arcane Field tools and weapons still need lawful item profiles and resources; heat projection can be precise but beams lose intensity with distance. Imperial firearms favor field-projected energy or field-accelerated ammunition, including micro-codex bullets. Centaur gunpowder firearms remain distinct. Neural cybernetics can restore or exceed natural function but body-interfacing technology causes Hex Rejection: the soul rejects the interface, building bodily Hex. Tougher bodies tolerate more, and medicine can reduce accumulation with strong side effects. Excessive corruption risks delirium, Hexen transformation and potentially demonic corruption. Exact chronic tolerance and treatment profiles require a GM ruling; no extra actions are automatic. The passive cybernetic attribute bonus follows the Quality rule below.

## Using technological equipment

Directed weapons such as laser pistols and Arcblades use ordinary applicable weapon attributes, training and handling. Their modules determine equipment capabilities and operating costs. Powered armor and implants use module-derived protection and assistance; implants incur chronic Hex Rejection. Complex machines have Integration requirements primarily based on INT: meeting them enables full hardware output, while exceeding them does not increase it. Partial Integration of at least 25% permits reduced performance at increased fuel cost; more fuel generates more Hex. At half Integration, performance is 75% and mana cost is four times normal. Individual functions do not have extra universal Integration gates. Gadget Quality averages functional module Qualities equally, but individual components retain their stats. Frame strength, material and handling can limit supported output. Exact machine requirements, prices and module stats remain table questions.

### Integration and material expertise

A powerful living Anima can extract more magical effect from mana than a novice or an automated mechanism. Integration bridges living control and fixed hardware capabilities: a proficient operator realizes the machine's intended output efficiently, without exceeding its physical maximum. Compatible purchased mana does not require personal harmonization.

Orbcraft can fabricate machinery from appropriate prepared exotic stock, including purchased or salvaged alloys and Adamantite. It does not inherently teach ore refining or alloy manufacture; those remain qualified Anvilcant capabilities. Wetcraft can complement both through lawful extraction and material preparation, subject to equivalent exchange and real materials, costs and qualifications.

## Modules, submodules and mounts

A module supplies a capability; a submodule optionally improves it. A gadget performs a complete function; an assembly combines gadgets on a frame (or a suitable host frame). Each integrated gadget uses one Mount, and Fabrication I–V supports 1/2/4/8/16 Mounts. Supporting components do not consume separate Mounts. Recipes allow compatible submodule types, normally one of each per primary module. Shared mana, control and Hex containment are possible, subject to real capacity. Gadgets can use compatible personal mana instead of a Codex, but insufficient filtration can expose the operator immediately. Minimal designs are often weak or inefficient; choose upgrades to suit the job. Magic Mirrors supply visual data. Foundational beams have no color-specific bonus; advanced color effects require matching mana and appropriate research, whose details remain under development.


## Starter casting implements

A Junk casting implement contributes **16 equipment Power** to eligible spells and has a reference price of **50 Gems**. This contribution is separate from the caster's own Power. Check Craft compatibility, actual medium, hands, and the finished implement's tuning. Higher-Quality implement profiles remain table questions.



## Cybernetic augmentation

An installed cybernetic system grants passive attribute bonuses from its total Quality: I **+3**, II **+6**, III **+10**, IV **+14**, V **+18**. The bonus does not need a special power module or spend MP, and does not add ordinary equipment Soul Strain. Cybernetic END can increase applicable MP and the capacity for Soul Strain, but not HP; enchanted END does the opposite. Enhanced Skill never grants additional SP or TP. You can benefit from at most **15 attribute-enhancing items and implants combined**; existing equipment and anatomical limits still apply. Ordinary wearable gadgets can substitute a machine's capabilities for yours but do not raise your attributes directly.

Integrated active functions use actual modules, normal mana expenditure, normal Hex generation and ordinary action and Integration rules. A built-in weapon is not a free attack or automatically twice as mana-efficient. Proper containment handles operating waste as with other technology; it is not automatically poured into your body. The interface still produces chronic bodily Hex independently of operation. Exact chronic rates, displacement, anatomical requirements and treatment remain GM questions. Both divine Gems and mundane Silver can fund implants or enchantments through lawful channels. Implant passive bonuses have equal value per point to Blank enchantments before the working 10% surgery surcharge; active modules add their own costs. Exact prices remain open.



## Masterful integration and machine repair

Your personal Bandwidth is 2 + floor(effective INT / 10), subject to actual machine and connection capabilities. Meeting a machine's Integration requirement grants its full designed output. Exceeding it never raises that output, but actively integrated operation becomes cheaper: at 100%, 200%, 300% and 400% Integration, pay 100%, 75%, 50% and 25% of ordinary MP respectively, interpolated linearly; 25% is the floor. Autonomous operation does not receive this excess-Integration discount. Tool Quality, not a second INT-based proficiency, governs technical tasks alongside applicable knowledge and checks.

Patch a damaged machine with a flux tool, replacement parts and repair time: current structural HP recovers but maximum structural HP decreases. Appropriate green-mana structural healing, or true structural repair by a Nymbrin alchemist capable of making the machine, can restore HP without this penalty. Green structural repair also restores maximum HP lost to previous patches, up to the original intact maximum. Exact repair rates, costs and time require GM adjudication.

## Integrated machines

A piloted or remotely integrated machine uses your actions and hardware statistics rather than gaining its own character levels or training. High-end automatic combat requires one activation action per machine and is designed for about three rounds at full output; direct operation is more efficient. A Hero shares one Shimmer Guard with an integrated machine, based on the greater of their own ordinary maximum HP or the machine's maximum structural HP, preserving the remaining proportion and broken state. Occupied machines retain protection continuously; remotely operated machines retain it until the end of your next turn after a meaningful command. Unlinked autonomous machines have no Guard. Enclosed machines grant complete cover above half structural HP and improved cover at or below half; an enemy can pry open the hatch with a Technique grapple check as difficult as pinning the machine. Status attacks delivered through an occupied shell use its effective status HP and share applicable conditions with the pilot. Direct attacks against exposed pilots use their personal defenses. Exact cover penalties, damage routing and construction costs remain table questions.
