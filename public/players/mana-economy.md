Usable mana powers bodies, spells, and machinery. Spending it leaves depleted mana. Refineries can recover useful energy or change its color, but collecting, moving, and treating the waste remain real work.

## Three things called mana

| Term | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Usable mana / MP** | Energy available for eligible magical work. Its color matters. |
| **Depleted mana** | Particles left after expenditure. They enter environmental circulation or become collected industrial waste. |
| **Managmins** | Biologically available dietary nutrition. Food composition is not the same thing as a tank of usable MP. |

Hex is a distinct, dangerous pollution problem associated with alchemy. It needs its own handling; ordinary depleted-mana recycling does not automatically remove it.

## Where usable mana comes from

| Source or process | How it contributes | Main limits |
|---|---|---|
| **Living creatures** | Souls generate mana; people may supply actual surplus to collection infrastructure. Elementals have exceptional potential. | Reserves, recovery, color, collection losses, and what the person has already spent. |
| **Leshy Beanstocks** | Rare cultivated plants absorb depleted mana and yield bulky mana-rich juice. | Suitable sites, exceptional cultivation, water, color specialization, stress, transport, and waste accounting. |
| **Nymbrin refining circles** | A compatible usable catalyst restores depleted target-color mana. | Correct colors, equivalent exchange, operating resources, and additional Hex. |
| **Industrial Leydrains** | Recover the natural successor color from concentrated depleted feedstock. | Operating energy, inefficiency, residues, collection, and disposal. |
| **Ancient Worldspindles** | Convert prepared matter into one mana color at irreplaceable ley-line installations. | Required feedstock, fixed ancient sites, and dangerous shutdowns. Their downstream fuel still produces waste when spent. |

**Vital Essence** is harvested biological material, not raw mana made into fluid. All five colors exist. It is important to alchemy and living materials such as Pazara and Skyvein, but dedicated extraction can be profoundly harmful and coercive.

## Storage and distribution

Leywells are stable reservoirs. Codexes store compact, large quantities but require appropriate protection because failure can be catastrophic. Beanstock juice is comparatively bulky. Frozen Vital Essence keeps for much longer than ordinary refined fuel.

Scarlands Circuits can relay mana rapidly without storing it at every step. Arriving energy still needs prompt use or actual storage. Canopy distribution relies more on transporting fuel; the Imperium makes extensive use of reinforced Codexes.

::: detail Working cargo and retention references
Gross energy-density benchmarks are **1,000 MP/tonne** for Beanstock juice, **10,000** for ordinary frozen Vital Essence, and **100,000** for reinforced industrial Codex cargo. These are economic reference densities, not personal item capacities.

Ordinary refined stored fuel has a working **30-day half-life**. Frozen Vital Essence has a **1–3 year** spoilage benchmark. Travel time, packaging, reinforcement, refrigeration, and losses affect delivered cost.
:::

## What happens to the waste?

Healthy ecosystems process depleted mana at a finite rate. Too much discharge can damage that capacity, making later accumulation worse. The color and receiving environment matter as well as the quantity.

Canopy infrastructure intercepts waste near the machine, collects what remains at larger facilities, and transports concentrated residues for treatment or suitable dispersion. Capturing or exporting waste moves the burden; it does not make the material disappear.

### Dreamcatchers: catch it before it spreads

Dreamcatchers are engineered Nullporcelain waste-management components. A functioning layer captures about **50% of the depleted mana reaching it**. Machine, factory, and municipal layers catch successive shares of the remainder.

| Functioning layers | Captured from the original waste | Escapes collection |
|---|---:|---:|
| One | 50% | 50% |
| Two | 75% | 25% |
| Three | 87.5% | 12.5% |

Collectors fill and require emptying. Large successive layers need real infrastructure; a handful of small attachments cannot stand in for a municipal system.

::: detail Leshy: the final safety net for settled waste
Leshy can root deep into the earth, selectively extract depleted mana by color, and transform it into Nectar. They are the only established active cleanup route for mana already settled deep in soil.

Healthy ecosystems can handle suitable distributed loads without a Leshy personally treating every particle. Leshy become especially important where pollution has already accumulated beyond ordinary collection. Their Nectar cannot be converted back into usable MP; this is not a perpetual energy loop.

Exact cleanup yields, doses, skill requirements, and costs are not fixed. Beanstocks do **not** remove Hex, and Leshy's ordinary depleted-mana role should not be treated as Hex immunity.
:::

::: detail Leydrains: useful recovery with a disposal bill
The natural successor cycle is **red → blue → green → black → white → red**. Industrial Leydrains accelerate that conversion using collected depleted feedstock. Mobile plants can travel by barge or airship and carry residues onward.

One working example uses **300 depleted MP-equivalents plus 150 usable operating MP**, producing **100 usable successor-color MP and 350 depleted waste**. With two functioning 50% collection layers, 262.5 waste is retained and 87.5 escapes locally; a municipal third layer reduces that local escape to 43.75.

The example consumes net usable energy and creates more total depleted waste. Its value includes recovery of a useful color and exporting a local pollution load to somewhere with suitable spare capacity. Storage leakage, transport fuel, and later expenditure of the recovered mana still count.
:::

::: detail Which discharge colors burden an ecosystem most?
Use the receiving environment's native color and the directed cycle above.

| Comparable concentrated discharge | Relative ecological burden |
|---|---:|
| Native color | 1× |
| Immediate predecessor: feeds the native color | 2× |
| Immediate successor: the native color feeds it | 4× |
| Either nonadjacent color | 6× |
| Unmanaged Hex | 8× |

For a blue environment: blue 1×, red 2×, green 4×, black/white 6×, Hex 8×. These weights compare ecological burdens; they are not safe discharge limits or combat damage multipliers. Ordinary natural coexistence of colors is not industrial contamination.
:::

## The Scarlands' separate Hex problem

All Nymbrin alchemy produces Hex, including helpful healing and construction. Fresh Hex is a coherent, filterable gas; as it loses coherence it becomes harder-to-collect ambient Haze. Its **60-second coherence half-life** describes that change, not destruction of the pollution.

Powered **Hexways** export fresh Hex through linked Circuits. **Purity Circles** protect suitable areas at ongoing cost. **Clearbreaths** provide specialized personal breathing protection. These systems are vulnerable to interruptions, capacity limits, and the receiving region's pollution burden.

::: detail Exactly which depleted colors can Nymbrin combine into Hex?
Deliberate alchemical fusion uses two **nonadjacent** colors on the natural cycle. These five unordered pairs are valid:

| Depleted color | Combine with |
|---|---|
| Red | Green |
| Red | Black |
| Blue | Black |
| Blue | White |
| Green | White |

**One depleted particle of each color produces two Hex particles.** Operating blue mana and its waste, plus operational Hex, are accounted for separately. The process is deliberate alchemy; it does not happen spontaneously whenever these colors coexist in nature.

An unbalanced stock of waste needs compatible feedstock, coordinated industry, or another disposal route. It does not conjure its missing counterpart.
:::

::: detail Hexen reclamation and environmental limits
Hexen can metabolize Hex into usable mana within biological reserves and recovery limits. The working exchange is **2 Hex → 1 usable MP + 1 ordinary depleted particle**; spending the recovered MP produces further ordinary waste. Their continuing emissions must also be counted.

This can support emergency reclamation, but it is not unlimited safe absorption. Overstress and prolonged exposure carry serious risks. Ambient Haze dissipates through ordinary ecological processes at comparable conditions while imposing the separate 8× burden; damaged Scarlands ecosystems have little processing capacity.
:::

## Why this matters to daily life

Energy-rich industry can still be constrained by clean air, collection equipment, healthy receiving land, and skilled maintenance. A cheap source of MP is only the beginning of the bill.
