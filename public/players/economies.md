The Canopy's strength is coordinated cultivation and clockwork production. The Scarlands' strength is resource access and powerful alchemical transformation. Each depends on skilled people, infrastructure, and trade—and each distributes the benefits very unequally.

## Scarlands and Canopy at a glance

| Economic strength | Scarlands | Canopy Kingdoms |
|---|---|---|
| **Land and raw materials** | Most of Mystaria's mountain ranges; important mining opportunities, with dangerous access and subterranean threats. | Productive agricultural territory; local mines are comparatively precious. |
| **Food and plant products** | Sheltered farms, greenhouses, cave enclaves, and imports. Haze and difficult geography constrain open production. | Staple agriculture, medicinal herbs, specialist Leshy cultivation, and increasingly industrial farms. |
| **Manufacturing tradition** | Nymbrin Wetcraft alters, separates, purifies, repairs, and grows substances and living structures. | Centaur engineering and clockwork combine reliable mechanisms with specialist magic and operators. |
| **Mining and refining** | Dirtmole Earthcraft assists excavation and prospecting; alchemical forges are favored for efficient bulk refining, at the cost of Hex runoff. | Engineering, imported materials, and Hearthiron/clockwork infrastructure support durable industrial systems. |
| **Energy supply** | Alchemical refining, biological Vital Essence, exceptional generators, and rapid linked mana transmission. | Collected surplus mana and rare high-output Beanstocks, with stored fuel and transport to users. |
| **Waste management** | Hex collection and export, protected air, and specialized reclamation. Keeping settlements breathable is a major industry. | Dreamcatchers intercept waste; Leydrains service concentrated residues; ecosystems and Leshy handle appropriate remaining depleted mana. |
| **Construction** | Living Pazara buildings, alchemical alteration, and Earthcraft-assisted cave expansion. | Conventional construction strengthened by craft expertise, engineered machinery, and coordinated infrastructure. |
| **Trade advantage** | Mineral resources, alchemical processing, specialized reagents, and transformation services. | Food, botanical knowledge, machinery, engineering, and agricultural output. |
| **Main constraints** | Haze, Hex disposal, dangerous mines, scarcity, coercive extraction, and intensely concentrated wealth. | Capital, trained operators, logistics, fuel compatibility, waste capacity, and dependence on unequal labor arrangements. |

These are regional tendencies, not a claim that one society exclusively owns a technology or produces every example of a commodity.

## Why they trade

Canopy food and herbs support Scarlands consumption and alchemy. Scarlands mineral access matters to regions with fewer mines. Clockwork offers strong long-term infrastructure when investment is available; alchemy can solve problems by changing matter itself.

Beanstocks are especially difficult to cultivate in the Scarlands because of Hex and insufficient suitable water. The Imperium's more varied ecology offers more plausible sites. A color affinity alone does not make a plant immune to pollution or provide the water it needs.

## How prosperity reaches people

The Canopy's organized production can give free households a comfortable life. Hereditary service, local House Scrip, and lordly control still limit the freedom of much of the population. Material comfort and freedom are separate questions.

Scarlands wealth is exceptionally concentrated. Skill, strength, connections, and guile matter to maintaining freedom; Blood Courts, guilds, contracts, and rival coercive powers shape everyday life. Exploitation is integral to the economy, including the severe harm of dedicated Vital Essence extraction.

## Where the Imperium fits

The centralized Imperium combines advanced manufacture, broad infrastructure, and ancient Worldspindles. Free citizens can enjoy substantial abundance while enslaved people are excluded from equivalent rights and benefits. Compact, reinforced Codexes play a major role in energy distribution.

::: detail Compare production stages, not just magical multipliers
Excavation, ore discovery, safe access, ore separation, refining, transport, and waste disposal are separate capacities. Faster digging does not automatically make every stage of a mine faster.

The same applies to farms: crop yield, harvest frequency, acreage, total labor, fuel, capital, and overhead are separate. Industrial productivity measures comparable marketable food per total worker labor across the whole cycle. It is not a free multiplier to every crop's biological yield.
:::
