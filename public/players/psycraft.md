Naga **Psychics** practice Psycraft through three distinct disciplines. Psychic perception can be subtle and powerful, but the target, focus, resistance, and limits of the learned ability still matter.

## Three disciplines

| Discipline | What it explores |
|---|---|
| **Psychokinesis — the Unseen Coil** | Applying psychic force to objects and the physical world. |
| **Telepathy — the Inner Coil** | Mental communication, perception, influence, and deeper contests. |
| **Divination — the Forked Eye** | Information and possible futures, with uncertainty rather than guaranteed prophecy. |

Each has five established ranks. The innate **Sovereign Sense** is distinct from purchasing mastery of those trees.

## Focus and resistance

Naga Psycraft requires an appropriate focus under its actual rules. A focus's functions and stored resources are not free extra reservoirs or automatic implementations of every other tradition.

Mental influence uses its particular delivery and resistance conditions. Emotional control, charm, fear, and direct information are not synonyms for unrestricted domination. Subtle application does not mean undetectable or irresistible application.

## Working with conditions

Hostile status applications count as offensive attacks. Helpers may use suitable counters; a continuing condition, accumulated buildup, and a Finisher's interruption have distinct handling. Follow [the current condition rules](/learn/conditions/) and the GM's specific ability definition.

## In society

Psycraft shapes Imperial institutions, sport, communication, investigation, and power. A person's psychic ability does not settle questions of consent, legal authority, or what an institution permits.


## Explore the learned ranks

Each native Psycraft rank costs **5 SP**; the three complete five-rank trees total **75 SP**.

Use these learned capability trees together with the shared casting, resource, and current condition rules. A capability description does not waive resistance or supply an unspecified damage figure.
