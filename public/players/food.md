Mystarian food supplies **managmins**: dietary magical nutrition made biologically available through digestion. The balance of colors matters as well as the total amount. A filling bowl of grain can still leave a person poorly nourished.

## The staple crops

| Crop | What it is | Dietary role |
|---|---|---|
| **Rosewheat** | Pale, faintly rose-tinted staple grain from white-rich plains. Used for flour, porridge, feed, and brewing. | Rich in white managmins and seriously deficient in red. |
| **Emberbeans** | Hardy cream-colored legumes with reddish markings; suitable for drying and storage. | An affordable complementary red source. |
| **Cindercorn** | Drought-tolerant cereal of drier territories near the Scarlands; dark burgundy seed heads. | Red/black emphasis and useful regional grain trade. The name does not imply Earth maize anatomy. |

## Where food grows

Ordinary grain, beans, roots, and feed chiefly grow in protected ground-level fields. Elevated farms favor valuable or perishable vegetables, herbs, and magical crops. Household gardens provide fruit, spices, and produce; forest margins support honey, mushrooms, and managed wild resources.

Equatorial Canopy conditions can support year-round growing, but a crop still depends on water, soil, rotation, and recovery. Annual yield is not the same thing as yield from each individual harvest.

## Leshy, irrigation, and machines

Leshy provide crop-specific Nectar and cultivation expertise. The right colors matter; the wrong fertilizer can encourage unwanted growth or damage a crop. Nectar is not a fuel that can be recycled back into usable MP.

Tidekin move existing water. Specialists can source underground water or purify seawater, while delivery and distribution remain separate work. An ordinary full irrigation workforce has an approximate half-a-comparable-aqueduct delivery benchmark; finding a better source does not automatically double conveyance.

Clockwork machinery, Centaur engineering, Cogsong operation, skilled specialists, security, and transport combine with ordinary labor. Machinery needs operators and compatible mana. It does not quietly farm by itself.

::: detail What industrial productivity measures
Whole-farm productivity compares marketable food output with **all attributable worker labor across the farming cycle**. Traditional, prosperous smallholding, commercial, industrial, and exceptional operations are useful scenarios.

The working 20–50× industrial ambition is a labor-productivity target, not a multiplier to biological yield, acreage, or each task. Track land preparation, planting, care, irrigation, harvest, handling, and shared support labor separately.
:::

## Meat, milk, and Plenty Day

Starspawn are intelligent beings with enduring memories. Their immortality does not make confinement, slaughter, or death painless. Ranching and meat markets are part of Mystaria's societies and their moral contradictions.

Unassimilated flesh is reclaimed when Celestial Recall begins, normally roughly a day after death. Butchering, cooking, freezing, or shipping does not prevent Recall. Nutrients already biologically assimilated by another creature remain. This makes living transport and local slaughter much more practical than distant carcass shipping.

Harvested milk, eggs, and fur can persist under their own established rules. Mossbacks support communal meat meals called **Plenty Day**; customary subsidy often includes enslaved households in upstanding towns, with local variation.

## Prices you can use as examples

Industrial reference retail quotes are roughly **0.19 silver accounting units/kg for Rosewheat flour**, **0.28 for dried Emberbeans**, and **0.17–0.20 for Cindercorn near suitable sources or accessible regional markets**. Long transport can change that substantially.

These are calibrated economic examples, not one universal shop list. Geography, production method, freight, waste handling, and local arrangements matter. [Work, money, and trade](/economy/trade/) keeps the accounting units and household examples distinct from Hero Gems.
