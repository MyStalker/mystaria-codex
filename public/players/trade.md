Mystaria has ordinary household economies alongside a Hero's divine Gem economy. Keep their currencies, prices, and purposes separate.

## Money and purchasing power

**Gems and Glints** belong to the Hero equipment economy. One Gem equals **ten Glints**. Gems reward qualifying adventures and purchase goods through the Shimmer's established shop system. They do not supply an assumed exchange rate to every local currency.

Economic studies use **silver accounting units** to compare labor and goods. The physical silver coin's name, weight, and a universal gold/silver exchange standard are not yet fixed.

Canopy **House Scrip** is local nonprecious currency controlled by a lord. Spending restrictions vary; unauthorized outside exchange is generally illegal. Guaranteed necessities and discretionary scrip can coexist with hereditary servitude.

## Working wage examples

| Work | Reference compensation |
|---|---:|
| Ordinary free Canopy fieldworker | 9 silver accounting units/day |
| Reference Thornguard town guard | 12.5/day |
| Free Leshy cultivation specialist | 27/day |
| Visiting Nymbrin magical healer | 50–90/professional day, plus travel/materials |

These are economic working references, not universal legal wage rates. A reference guard earning 3,000 units over 240 paid days supports a household model; it is not the income of every guard or the production cost of enslaved labor.

## Slavery still has costs

An enslaved worker's lack of wages does not make production free. Food, housing, dependents, medical care, training, supervision, coercion, risk, and lost alternatives all belong in the model. Owners' profits and enslaved people's wellbeing are different measures.

Comfort, time off, or spending money cannot stand in for consent or the ability to leave. Conditions differ greatly between regions, houses, and occupations.

## Freight changes the price

| Route | Working silver/tonne-km | Working travel km/day |
|---|---:|---:|
| Ordinary Worldcoil | 0.04 | 30 |
| Accelerated Worldcoil | 0.06 | 150 |
| Overland caravan | 0.35 | 30 |
| Skyvein bulk airship | 0.50 | 100 |

These are provisional transport references, not universal tariffs or speed ceilings. Actual route access, vessels, security, loading, and supporting infrastructure still matter. Couriers carry a different kind of cargo from a bulk grain vessel.

For mana fuel, travel time also loses usable energy. A cheap tonne shipped slowly may be expensive per surviving MP on arrival.

## Why there is no single price list for everything

Material supply, trained labor, extraction risk, machine investment, food, fuel color, waste treatment, and transport interact. An alchemical forge may use less mana per comparable output yet owe more for Hex disposal. A highly productive farm still needs water and a route to market.

Use named reference quotes for their stated context. Household subsidy trials, unfinished engineering budgets, and prospective services are optional economic models, not automatic entitlements or finished market laws.
