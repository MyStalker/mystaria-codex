Combat is about making a useful choice with the space, time, equipment, and resources you actually have. An accurate attack, a protected ally, a prepared defense, or a well-timed retreat can matter more than a large number on its own.

## Your turn

You have **two actions per turn**, with **at most one offensive attack action**. Some attacks require both actions. A hostile status attempt is an offensive attack even if it deals no direct damage; a failed attempt still uses the committed action.

Use the remaining opportunity for lawful movement, preparation, equipment handling, assistance, or another supported nonoffensive action. An enchantment or zero-MP cantrip follows the same attack limit.

A **Quick Attack** (one action), whether a weapon strike, offensive spell or martial technique, takes **−2 Attack** and can use at most half the applicable Channel Limit. A **Full Attack** (two actions) takes no commitment accuracy penalty and can use the full applicable limit. Ordinary weapon damage remains unchanged either way. Channeling still shares the turn's available throughput; mana costs and legal technique requirements still apply. Use the ordinary casting rounding and minimum rules. A nonoffensive action does not gain an attack penalty.

## Making a physical attack

1. Choose a target you can actually reach or shoot, with the right hands, weapon readiness, and line of effect.
2. Apply the actual attack bonus and target Evasion, including relevant positioning, Pressure, equipment, and statuses.
3. Roll against **10 + target Evasion**: a total of **d20 + Attack** meeting that defense hits.
4. Resolve the target's lawful defenses and the attack's damage or status effect.

In the selected training rules, a **natural 1 misses** and a **natural 20 hits and threatens a critical**. Confirm with a second roll using the same original attack bonus and actual defense: natural 1 fails confirmation; natural 20 succeeds. A confirmed critical doubles post-armor HP damage and applicable buildup.

An automatic hit still respects mitigation. It can deal zero HP damage against sufficient armor.

## Pressure and positioning

Pressure represents meaningful tactical disadvantages: being pinned into a bad position, threatened by coordinated opponents, or constrained by a condition. Count the applicable advantages and meaningful counter-pressure, then use the net result.

Each effective Pressure Point reduces Evasion and Reflex checks by **2**, up to **three net points**. At three the target is **Trapped** under the current rules. Eligible automatic or passive shield defenses can still function; Trapped is not permission to ignore every defense.

Space matters. Reach, cover, facing, a genuine attack path, and room to swing remain real constraints. A large damage rating is not automatic area coverage.

**Smoke:** Each smoke-filled tile along the attack sightline imposes −2 Attack, including the tiles you and the target occupy. At −10 from smoke alone, you cannot see or directly attack that target. Pyromancer Smoke Sense ignores smoke in both occupied endpoint tiles, but not intervening tiles. Other attack modifiers still apply.

## Prepare a defense before the result

For an active prepared defense, commit the required action(s) on your own turn and declare a narrow trigger in advance. “The next attack by that enemy” can be a legitimate trigger when the defense supports it. Waiting to learn whether the attack hits before deciding to defend is not preparation.

The effect must still have the required equipment, coverage, mana, medium, and position when the trigger occurs.

A falling character or ally may prepare an eligible defensive action to catch or cushion a fall, with a specific trigger and actual means of stopping the impact. See [health and defense](/learn/health/) for falling damage, emergency Shimmer Break and knockout recovery.

A deliberately aimed falling object needs a successful attack and at least 10 ft of fall. The falling object takes falling damage; the struck target takes physical collision damage: raw max(20, twice the object's actual surface AR), minus target AR. At 250/510 ft the raw collision damage doubles/quadruples at −2/−4 Attack. No critical collision damage or AP. See [disciplines](/learn/disciplines/) for Tractor Beams.

Large falling structures use one Reflex AoE avoidance check against 10 + attacker Attack (including the drop penalty), or a GM-set natural hazard DC. On success, move within your speed to actual safety outside the footprint or reach actual cover inside; escape prevents damage and Burial, ordinary cover halves both, sufficiently strong cover may prevent both. Failure receives the full applicable consequences. Natural collapses need not strike at high velocity. See [conditions](/learn/conditions/) for Buried.

## Movement and escape

The movement foundation starts at **30 feet**, or six five-foot tiles, with eligible Movement training applied under its rules. One Dash doubles the ordinary allowance; Double Dash doubles it again and uses both actions.

A Double Dash retreat ignores the first tile for hostile zone-of-control purposes. Later controlled tiles, actual obstacles, bindings, and walls still matter.

## Handling items

Retrieving an accessible item or opening an ordinary door normally takes one action. A weapon action can draw or sheathe up to two eligible weapons. A ready throwing weapon in a bandolier or quiver may be drawn and thrown in one action with a suitable free hand; the throw uses your offensive attack.

A potion normally takes one action to draw and one to drink. Inaccessibility, a grapple, or specialized loading procedures can add genuine constraints.

## After an attack

Use [health and defense](/learn/health/) for damage, Shimmer, and recovery. Use [conditions](/learn/conditions/) for accumulating statuses and helping an affected ally. Your GM resolves the remaining edge cases and any maneuver whose HP conversion is not yet specified.

## Junk equipment in combat

Attacks made with a Junk melee or ranged weapon take **−2 Attack** once. Wearing Junk armor gives **−2 Evasion** once. These cancel when a Junk weapon attacks a Junk-armored target; neither reduces the weapon's damage or armor penetration. See [equipment](/learn/equipment/) for the item profiles and separate shield and implement rules.


## Weapon construction and eligibility

A weapon's finished construction burden determines its ordinary requirement: Weight weapons spend that amount of shared equipment capacity; Handling weapons require the same numerical DEX instead, without paying that amount again as equipped Weight. Materials, Quality, reinforcements and installed hardware alter the same burden. Qualified DEX users may operate and reload arbalests without an additional STR cocking gate; ordinary loading/setup rules still apply. Repulsors need sufficient STR to withstand recoil or powered Full Recoil Control with real anchoring. Existing Magic Handling and Mastercraft exceptions remain.
