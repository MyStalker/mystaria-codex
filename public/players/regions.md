Mystaria is a world of mana-shaped landscapes, intelligent animal-inspired peoples, ancient ruins, and Starspawn. The largest societies offer very different mixtures of comfort, danger, opportunity, and control.

## The Canopy Kingdoms

The Canopy is a **feudal confederation of sovereign kingdoms**. Regional High Kings share diplomacy through the Crown Council, but there is no single supreme monarch and no permanent Council army. The ancient Canopy Concord supports collective defense.

Forests, cultivated land, knightly Banners, Leshy expertise, and growing clockwork industry shape the region. Ground fields supply ordinary staple grain; elevated agriculture favors valuable or perishable produce. Industrial wealth increasingly competes with old land and lineage.

Free citizens may enjoy substantial prosperity. Much of the population remains enslaved under paternalistic ideals of protection. Comfortable housing, family life, or spending money do not remove that lack of freedom.

## The Imperium

The centralized Imperium inherits a vast serpentine political and technological legacy. Naga hierarchy, specialized peoples, infrastructure, and advanced manufacture support an unusually abundant life for free citizens.

That prosperity rests on profound inequality and exploitation. A free person's access to luxuries says little about an enslaved worker's rights. Kobold engineering, psychic institutions, and ancient Worldspindles are important parts of its world.

## The Scarlands

The Scarlands combine mountains, swamps, extensive caves, concentrated wealth, and rival powers. Vren Blood Courts, Nymbrin guilds, coercive contracts, and local authorities coexist; decentralization does not mean an absence of hierarchy.

Alchemical industry transforms substances and grows living infrastructure, while producing dangerous Hex pollution. Mines promise resources but can encounter terrifying subterranean threats. Freedom is precarious, and skill, allies, strength, or guile may be essential to keeping it.

Sheltered agriculture and imports coexist with the wasteland. Pazara homes are living structures requiring nourishment and skilled care. Clean air, safe work, and access to protection are deeply unequal.

## Travel and the Worldcoil

Ancient Serpentari waterways link important routes. Use depends on actual exposed and accessible segments; they are not an unrestricted shortcut between any two places. Wakecraft and Imperial infrastructure accelerate suitable stretches, while overland routes, couriers, and airships serve other needs.

Distance, terrain, fuel loss, cargo bulk, security, and local rights matter. [Work, money, and trade](/economy/trade/) explains the practical differences.

## Why a Hero matters

Reincarnated humans chosen by a goddess become Heroes with the Shimmer: an interface for advancement, inventory, navigation, messages, quests, and progressive reference knowledge. A Hero enters a world that already has its own people, institutions, and conflicts.

The three central goddesses have distinct concerns: **Arrest** with justice, restraint, and authority; **Surrender** with love, trust, nature, and freely chosen submission; **Baphomet** with revenge, pain, chaos, and betrayed promises. They disagree, and none should be reduced to a simple mana-color alignment.
