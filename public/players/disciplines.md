## Buying ranks

Use the actual tradition's ledger. Native and foreign Craft learning can differ; an awakening requirement is not a rank. Sorcery purchases individual Incantations and alterations; Jestercraft has a different component economy. Earlier ranks and the appropriate tier prerequisites still matter.

SP buys these learned options. TP improves the combat statistics used to apply them; buying accuracy does not itself teach a spell.

## Cooperative magic

Concordance joins Thornguard supply with Bloomkin shaping. A Corvyn familiar can teach patterns and supply compatible foreign-color mana through a Witchbond, without the witch storing foreign mana in her own soul. A Summoner's Accord is a different relationship.

Every participant, medium, reservoir, and action must actually be available. An ally's specialty is not a passive license to use all their skills.

## Memory and Skillprints

An **Echo** is stored experience. **Echoing** copies; **Mindcut** removes and transfers, with medical and personal risks. The law of returning Echoes constrains repeated extraction.

An eligible **Skillprint** may count a compatible Shimmer skill as purchased for zero SP under its actual rules. It does not invent an unknown skill, automatically satisfy every other prerequisite, or provide unlimited duplicate benefit.

Complete rank trees are not supplied for every known tradition. Choose a documented ability or settle the missing detail with your GM before spending points.
