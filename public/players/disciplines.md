## Buying ranks

Use the actual tradition's ledger. Native and foreign Craft learning can differ; an awakening requirement is not a rank. Sorcery purchases individual Incantations and alterations; Jestercraft has a different component economy. Earlier ranks and the appropriate tier prerequisites still matter.

SP buys these learned options. TP improves the combat statistics used to apply them; buying accuracy does not itself teach a spell.

## Cooperative magic

Concordance joins Thornguard supply with Bloomkin shaping. A Corvyn familiar can teach patterns and supply compatible foreign-color mana through a Witchbond, without the witch storing foreign mana in her own soul. A Summoner's Accord is a different relationship.

Every participant, medium, reservoir, and action must actually be available. An ally's specialty is not a passive license to use all their skills.

## Memory and Skillprints

An **Echo** is stored experience. **Echoing** copies; **Mindcut** removes and transfers, with medical and personal risks. The law of returning Echoes constrains repeated extraction.

An eligible **Skillprint** may count a compatible Shimmer skill as purchased for zero SP under its actual rules. It does not invent an unknown skill, automatically satisfy every other prerequisite, or provide unlimited duplicate benefit.

Complete rank trees are not supplied for every known tradition. Choose a documented ability or settle the missing detail with your GM before spending points.


## Orbcraft and field engineering

Kobold engineers combine materials, tools, time, Fluxfluid, mana and Orb bandwidth to make inventions. Ordinary fabrication is not exclusive to Kobolds. Their six areas of expertise are Fabrication, Computation, Transmission, Manatech, Augmentation and Automation. Arcane Fields manipulate gravity and direct heat or kinetic force; red favors heat, blue precision, green kinetic power, white efficiency and black uncontrolled raw power. Kinetic projection has equal opposing recoil. Reallocating bandwidth costs an action in combat; powered gadgets and constructs do not give extra turns or attacks. You can specialize without robots. Ask your GM for finished recipes, ranks and costs before buying or building a device.

## Building and servicing gadgets

Orbcraft uses INT rather than DEX. Foundational technologies grant basic functions, and further research opens new functions and advanced applications. Gadgets are lasting equipment made from actual materials and interchangeable modules. Fabrication ranks I–V allow corresponding module Quality and integrate up to 1/2/4/8/16 gadgets into one machine; there is no limit on gadgets owned from this rule. Compatible higher-grade modules may be purchased or salvaged and installed. Basic conventional weapons can be manufactured two Quality tiers below Fabrication, without granting enchantment. Modules come in miniature, handheld, personal, vehicle and industrial sizes. Compatible transfers cost time; enlarging needs materials, and downsizing is destructive reworking.

Gadgets produce Hex waste, but adequate containment prevents ordinary use from automatically exposing the operator. Slow refueling permits safe personal servicing in suitable conditions; rapid combat refueling releases a burst of Hex under existing status rules. Unfiltered gadgets can expose users during operation. Exact capacity, recipes, costs and status buildup values require a GM ruling. Integrated machines pay for their actual subsystems and never gain extra combat actions merely from installing more gadgets.

## Using technological equipment

Directed weapons such as laser pistols and Arcblades use ordinary applicable weapon attributes, training and handling. Their modules determine equipment capabilities and operating costs. Powered armor and implants use module-derived protection and assistance; implants incur chronic Hex Rejection. Complex machines have Integration requirements primarily based on INT: meeting them enables full hardware output, while exceeding them does not increase it. Partial Integration of at least 25% permits reduced performance at increased fuel cost; more fuel generates more Hex. At half Integration, performance is 75% and mana cost is four times normal. Individual functions do not have extra universal Integration gates. Gadget Quality averages functional module Qualities equally, but individual components retain their stats. Frame strength, material and handling can limit supported output. Exact machine requirements, prices and module stats remain table questions.

### Integration and material expertise

A powerful living Anima can extract more magical effect from mana than a novice or an automated mechanism. Integration bridges living control and fixed hardware capabilities: a proficient operator realizes the machine's intended output efficiently, without exceeding its physical maximum. Compatible purchased mana does not require personal harmonization.

Orbcraft can fabricate machinery from appropriate prepared exotic stock, including purchased or salvaged alloys and Adamantite. It does not inherently teach ore refining or alloy manufacture; those remain qualified Anvilcant capabilities. Wetcraft can complement both through lawful extraction and material preparation, subject to equivalent exchange and real materials, costs and qualifications.

## Modules, submodules and mounts

A module supplies a capability; a submodule optionally improves it. A gadget performs a complete function; an assembly combines gadgets on a frame (or a suitable host frame). Each integrated gadget uses one Mount, and Fabrication I–V supports 1/2/4/8/16 Mounts. Supporting components do not consume separate Mounts. Recipes allow compatible submodule types, normally one of each per primary module. Shared mana, control and Hex containment are possible, subject to real capacity. Gadgets can use compatible personal mana instead of a Codex, but insufficient filtration can expose the operator immediately. Minimal designs are often weak or inefficient; choose upgrades to suit the job. Magic Mirrors supply visual data. Foundational beams have no color-specific bonus; advanced color effects require matching mana and appropriate research, whose details remain under development.


## Prisminds and technological infiltration

A Prismind is an individual intelligence, not a copyable program. Caged Prisminds follow orders and do not grant independent combat turns; uncaged Rogue Prisminds are NPCs with their own actions and personalities, not guaranteed allies or enemies. Dangerous rogue intelligences are called Ciphergeists (Geists). Prisminds learn genuine expertise through neural interfaces with qualified instructors and inherit their master's Integration; a rogue retains its last learned score. Tier I is insect-like, II pet-like, III person-like, IV and V have noncombat intellectual equivalents of INT 100 and 200. They cannot cast magic. Hardware and training still limit their work.

Fusion can accelerate growth by consuming eligible lower minds. Complexity values I–V are 1/4/16/64/256; contributors must be at least two tiers below the result and may be mixed. Sixteen Tier I minds can form Tier III, sixteen Tier II form IV, sixteen Tier III form V; Tier II develops through education. Fusion does not copy individuals. A Prismind can retain learned expertise and memories from contributors; identity and personality vary.

An eligible Kobold directly interfaces with machinery and contests the stronger applicable magic check of its creator or operator. Success sabotages; a margin of 10 or more seizes control. Spend an action releasing a Prismind to attempt infiltration from within 30 feet, even through ordinary walls; magic-resistant barriers block it, but disconnecting the Coilnet does not. Prisminds receive +10 to hacking unless an operator spends an action defending or an onboard Prismind defends without an action. Sabotage lasts three rounds, refreshed rather than stacked on repeat successes. Relative to the target Orb tier, an attacking Prismind two or more tiers lower cannot affect it; one lower reduces computational stats by 10%, equal 25%, one higher 50%, and two or more higher 75%. Seized machinery uses the Kobold's own actions or a rogue's NPC actions. Defensive Orb hardware trades computational performance for security. Ask the GM about check statistics, rounding, ordinary Kobold sabotage, seizure recovery and unfinished construction details.


## Emitters, Focusers and Orbs

Emitters generate Arcane Fields. Choose Standard for general use, Continuous for sustained work, Pulse for bursts, Precision for careful control or Broad-field for distributed output. Focusers shape fields into beams, cones, contained shapes, barriers, manipulation fields or radial effects. Unfocused output is crude. Optional enhancements can trade Power, Range, fuel, Load and control; field range attenuates and color-specific effects need matching mana and appropriate research. The final absolute profiles and recipes require a GM ruling.

An Orb has Processing (the sophistication of a task it can perform) and Bandwidth (how much control it can manage at once). Working reference-size Bandwidth for Quality I–V is 1/2/4/8/16. Basic tasks may use 0, simple automation 1, navigation or tracking 2 and advanced simultaneous calculation 4, subject to the actual design. Processing progresses from simple switches to conditional control, recognition and navigation, diagnostics and simulations, then sophisticated scientific modelling. Give a program ordinary-language instructions that fit its hardware and the programmer's expertise. Programs can be copied; Prisminds cannot. An Orb can run several Gadgets or coordinate with other Orbs if connected and adequate; Processing tiers do not add together. Routine computation is included in equipment operating costs when practical. Accelerators, Parallel Cores, Memory Banks, Logic Cores and Isolation Cores offer distinct specializations. Advanced blue computation favors precision, white efficiency and black overdrive with added risks. A Prismind needs a compatible host modification. Neither programming nor more Bandwidth grants extra combat actions. Exact modifiers and operator capacity remain table questions.


## Cipherlight communication and Prismind travel

Transmitters send, Receivers receive, Transceivers do both, and Magic Mirrors convert imagery. Range belongs to the sender; two-way communication needs a viable path each way. Larger modules transmit farther. Handheld broadcast Range by Quality I–V is 100/300/1,000/3,000/10,000 feet, multiplied by 0.25/1/3/10/30 for miniature/handheld/personal/vehicle/industrial. Throughput I–V is 1/2/4/8/16; simple commands are negligible, continuous control/audio 1, a live Mirror feed 2, detailed sensory stream 4, complex multisensory 8. Directional lenses multiply Range by twenty but create a visible curved Rainbow revealing origin. It can bend around the horizon; interrupting the actual path breaks the link. Weather reduces Range to 75%, 50% or 25% depending on severity. Relays extend networks and may be carried by dedicated machines. These are working equipment ratings; exact costs and enhancement details require the GM.

Prisminds can hop locally within 30 feet or travel through compatible Receiver-range connections, taking twice the local hopping time (four times if stealthy, eight times high-stealth). Transfer is not copying. Each security layer risks discovery. A failed sabotage attempt shunts the mind back to its latest host, skipping closed hosts backward. A required return host out of signal range, completely powered down or destroyed causes the mind to spill at the failed target Orb as an immobile ghostlike mana clump. It cannot move, hack or transmit itself. It survives approximately five minutes per tier unless someone physically touches a suitable Orb to it to restore hosting. Ask your GM about exact transit timing, stealth checks and host compatibility.


## Insulated communication, holograms and acoustics

Magic-resistant material blocks ordinary wireless Cipherlight and local Prismind hopping. A sealed Nullporcelain casing can isolate a Prismind; compatible fiber-optic cables can carry both data and Prisminds through a deliberate physical route, with instant mind transfer even when stealthy. Ordinary sound can relay instructions to an insulated machine but cannot carry the mind itself. Use Line of Light for a curved directional beam's actual path. For Prismind wireless hopping only, each inch of intervening solid material halves effective range; local 30-foot hops ignore ordinary material thickness but not magical barriers. Wireless travel takes twice the local reference time, four times stealthy or eight times high-stealth (working +5 stealth check bonus). Failed hacking stealth checks accumulate -2 for 24 hours; failure by 10 discovers the mind until it reaches an Orb entirely under its control, without automatically clearing timed penalties.

A hologram uses an Emitter, Focuser, Projector enhancement and Orb; Mirrors can supply imagery and speakers sound. Higher Processing enables more complex images. It cannot sense its own surroundings; a real sensor or controller supplies input. Separately powered thermal or kinetic fields can give a projection physical effects but ordinary holograms are intangible. Keep Line of Light clear within field range; loss of generator, path or power interrupts the image. Prisminds can express themselves through avatars. Color research and exact device costs remain table questions.

Quality I Acoustic Sensors serve as microphones, speakers reproduce sound, acoustic transceivers do both, sonic projectors focus it, and advanced acoustic sensors analyze vibrations. Sound weapons and active noise cancellation are possible. Sonic is physical damage and a status; compare weapon damage with equivalent laser pistols, using the actual device's profile. See the conditions guide for Deafened. Detailed acoustic specifications remain table questions.


### Acoustic equipment and surveying

A Quality I Acoustic Sensor serves as a microphone; higher or specialized sensors analyze echoes and vibrations. Speakers produce sound, combined acoustic transceivers handle both functions, and Sonic Projectors focus high-intensity sound. Sonic pistols, rifles, scatterguns, cannons, status-specialist resonators and area emitters use comparable weapon budgets to their laser equivalents. A weapon may use ordinary physical damage, hybrid buildup or pure status according to its actual profile. No automatic armor bypass. Optional Amplifiers, Directional Horns, Resonators, Voice Synthesizers, Acoustic Filters, Spatial Arrays, Dampeners and Counterwave submodules specialize equipment. Advanced blue favors precision/cancellation, green physical vibration, white efficiency and black raw output with increased risk.

Noise cancellation provides a relevant stealth bonus and fully suppresses specified sounds within actual capacity. A low-grade device can conceal conversation; powerful projected silence fields can suppress explosion noise without removing the blast itself. Active acoustic surveying sends a noticeable ping that can reveal its source, map rooms through walls and locate visually concealed people; appropriate noise cancellation can conceal targets. See Counterwave and Echosounding below for working grades; exact action costs and recipes require a GM ruling.


### Counterwave and Echosounding

Acoustic primary modules are Sensor, Speaker and Sonic Projector; an acoustic transceiver combines the first two. Counterwave Quality I–V grants +2/+4/+6/+8/+10 to relevant acoustic Stealth checks. Within actual supported coverage/output, fully suppress cumulative noise classes: I quiet conversations and small devices; II normal speech, footsteps and small mechanisms; III running, shouting and ordinary machinery; IV gunshots, heavy machinery and crashing objects; V explosions and powerful discharges. A projected silence field requires suitable field hardware. Sound cancellation does not remove blast damage or visual evidence.

An Echosounder uses a Sensor, Projector and Orb to map physical geometry from active pings, including through ordinary walls; the ping generally exposes the device. Handheld working Quality I–V ranges are 30/60/120/240/480 feet; higher grades provide progressively more detailed maps. Solid obstructions can impair returns; acoustic insulation and appropriate Counterwave can conceal a target, unlike mere visual hiding. Passive listening does not send a ping. Kinetic constructs make sound when moving and return echoes; ordinary visual holograms do not. While the user has at least half their effective status HP in Sonic buildup, their Acoustic Sensors cannot function until buildup falls below that threshold. Exact opposed checks, range/size, noise field limits, remote/autonomous routing and costs remain table questions.


### Sensors and countersensors

Use Magic Mirrors for imagery, Acoustic Sensors for sound and vibration, Mana Sensors for magical energy and Hex, and Physical Sensors for selected measurements. Mirror lenses specialize magnification, distant vision, night vision, thermal imaging, panoramic views, depth or glare filtration. A basic Mana Sensor can measure the mana stored in a Leywell, Codex or creature, within its comparatively short range and without intervening walls. Handheld working Mana Sensor Quality I–V ranges are 10/20/40/80/160 feet, subject to detectable source strength; specialized analyzers examine colors, Hex/Haze, flux, ley flows, spells or materials. Physical Sensors use actual thermal, pressure, motion, chemical, structural, geological or biological hardware. An Orb can combine independent sensory streams if it has sufficient Processing and Bandwidth; interpretation still requires knowledge.

Specialized holographic equipment can bend light, enable Stealth without ordinary cover and potentially make a target invisible. Countersensors manipulate the relevant information, including acoustic echoes, heat and mana signatures; they can conceal or produce false readings. Quality and target size determine effectiveness: the same device conceals smaller objects more effectively than larger ones. Visual invisibility does not automatically conceal other signatures. Use real field and sensor hardware, actual coverage and the existing Counterwave rules. Advanced colored sensing favors red thermal analysis, blue precision, green motion/structure, white efficiency or black raw range with increased risks. Exact concealment checks, size multipliers and costs require a GM ruling.


### Countersensor resolution and colored Codex equipment

Effective concealment tier is Quality plus equipment size category minus target size category (minimum zero). Compare it to the applicable Sensor Quality: higher concealment automatically defeats that mechanical sense, equal is an even contest, lower favors detection. Natural senses instead use applicable perception/stealth check modifiers, with exact values settled at the table. A wholly artificial eye is a mechanical sense; other independent senses may still reveal the target. Concealment must cover the actual sensory mode and target.

Imperial kinetic weapons can use micro-Codex ammunition; specialized colored ammunition modules are under development. Colored Codex explosives have distinct magical identities: red Burning, blue Freeze, green healing, white temporary trapping fields, black raw magical output and Hex. Use the actual equipment profile and lawful status, healing, field and resource rules. Exact profiles, prices and costs remain table questions.


## Cybernetic augmentation

An installed cybernetic system grants passive attribute bonuses from its total Quality: I **+3**, II **+6**, III **+10**, IV **+14**, V **+18**. The bonus does not need a special power module or spend MP, and does not add ordinary equipment Soul Strain. Cybernetic END can increase applicable MP and the capacity for Soul Strain, but not HP; enchanted END does the opposite. Enhanced Skill never grants additional SP or TP. You can benefit from at most **15 attribute-enhancing items and implants combined**; existing equipment and anatomical limits still apply. Ordinary wearable gadgets can substitute a machine's capabilities for yours but do not raise your attributes directly.

Integrated active functions use actual modules, normal mana expenditure, normal Hex generation and ordinary action and Integration rules. A built-in weapon is not a free attack or automatically twice as mana-efficient. Proper containment handles operating waste as with other technology; it is not automatically poured into your body. The interface still produces chronic bodily Hex independently of operation. Exact chronic rates, displacement, anatomical requirements and treatment remain GM questions. Both divine Gems and mundane Silver can fund implants or enchantments through lawful channels. Implant passive bonuses have equal value per point to Blank enchantments before the working 10% surgery surcharge; active modules add their own costs. Exact prices remain open.



## Masterful integration and machine repair

Your personal Bandwidth is 2 + floor(effective INT / 10), subject to actual machine and connection capabilities. Meeting a machine's Integration requirement grants its full designed output. Exceeding it never raises that output, but actively integrated operation becomes cheaper: at 100%, 200%, 300% and 400% Integration, pay 100%, 75%, 50% and 25% of ordinary MP respectively, interpolated linearly; 25% is the floor. Autonomous operation does not receive this excess-Integration discount. Tool Quality, not a second INT-based proficiency, governs technical tasks alongside applicable knowledge and checks.

Patch a damaged machine with a flux tool, replacement parts and repair time: current structural HP recovers but maximum structural HP decreases. Appropriate green-mana structural healing, or true structural repair by a Nymbrin alchemist capable of making the machine, can restore HP without this penalty. Green structural repair also restores maximum HP lost to previous patches, up to the original intact maximum. Exact repair rates, costs and time require GM adjudication.

## Integrated machines

A piloted or remotely integrated machine uses your actions and hardware statistics rather than gaining its own character levels or training. High-end automatic combat requires one activation action per machine and is designed for about three rounds at full output; direct operation is more efficient. A Hero shares one Shimmer Guard with an integrated machine, based on the greater of their own ordinary maximum HP or the machine's maximum structural HP, preserving the remaining proportion and broken state. Occupied machines retain protection continuously; remotely operated machines retain it until the end of your next turn after a meaningful command. Unlinked autonomous machines have no Guard. Enclosed machines grant complete cover above half structural HP and improved cover at or below half; an enemy can pry open the hatch with a Technique grapple check as difficult as pinning the machine. Status attacks delivered through an occupied shell use its effective status HP and share applicable conditions with the pilot. Direct attacks against exposed pilots use their personal defenses. Exact cover penalties, damage routing and construction costs remain table questions.
