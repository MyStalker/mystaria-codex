Wakecraft is the Tidekin art of **existing water**. It can move, shape, perceive through, and transform lawful water media; it does not simply conjure an unlimited ocean.

## Six paths through the water

| Discipline | What it develops |
|---|---|
| **Wakeforce** | The force and weight of manipulated water. |
| **Longwake** | Working with water across greater separation. |
| **Deepdraw** | Access to hidden water and difficult sources. |
| **Mirrorwake** | Water's remembered impressions and investigative uses. |
| **Reefcalling** | Relationships and influence involving aquatic life and Starspawn. |
| **Saltwake** | Thirst, salinity, and the harsher aspects of the sea. |

Basic hydrokinesis gives Tidekin meaningful utility before advanced specialization. Industrial extraction or seawater purification requires the appropriate specialist, resources, and delivery infrastructure.

## Water in combat

Water attacks can produce physical impacts and Erosion. Ice Spikes are physical piercing attacks and do not automatically add Freeze buildup. A pure Freeze attack has a different status role and potency treatment.

Water, ice, puddles, pressure, actual contact, and material resistance matter. A condition's buildup is not interchangeable with its direct damage. [Conditions](/learn/conditions/) explains the current shared status framework.

## Water outside combat

Irrigation, source acquisition, purification, conveyance, and distribution are different jobs. Specialists may unlock a source that makes farming possible without automatically increasing the throughput of every channel downstream.

Carried water helps in dry regions, but it remains finite. A clever small supply can enable a lawful effect; it does not waive the effect's actual quantity, medium, MP, or range requirements.
