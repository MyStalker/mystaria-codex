Your attributes establish broad potential. Training lets you decide which parts of that potential become exceptional. Skill Points teach you abilities; Training Points improve particular statistics.

## Earning points

For Hero bookkeeping at level **L**, baseline SP and TP income are each **2 × L**, including level 1. Each allocated attribute point invested in Skill adds two SP and a growing amount of TP as you level.

| Character level | TP per invested Skill | Extra trainable HP per invested Skill |
|---|---:|---:|
| 1–10 | 0.5 | 0 |
| 25 | 1 | 0 |
| 50 | 2 | 1 |
| 75 | 2.5 | 1 |
| 100 | 2.5 | 1.5 |

Between these levels the benefits increase gradually. Use your current level for all invested points, then round down total TP. At level 1, investing both attribute points in Skill gives 6 SP and 3 TP. At level 5, three invested Skill points give 16 SP and 11 TP.

Endurance makes you naturally tougher; Skill lets you train yourself to become tougher. You can purchase HP from level 1, normally up to 150% of natural HP. Later Skill expands that ceiling by the amount shown above; it does not grant free HP. Your character manager can calculate the intermediate values.

These are earned totals before purchases and before any separately established exceptional sources. Skill's racial multiplier, enchanted Skill bonuses and the automatic fifth-level increase do not count as allocated Skill investment.

## Improving attributes

At character creation, a level-1 Hero receives **two allocatable base attribute points**, **two SP**, and **two TP**. Each subsequent Hero level grants another two of each; the level-one grant is already included in the 2 × L totals. Base attribute points earned by level L total 2 × L, before automatic milestone increases. Every fifth level also adds **+1 to all five base attributes**. Racial modifiers continue to apply to eligible base increases.

Keep the base value and effective value distinct. A fractional effective attribute can matter; round only after completing the relevant derived calculation.

Ordinary mortals can improve attributes through training, but they do not automatically use the Hero level and reward schedule.

## Spending SP

SP buys learned capabilities: magical Craft ranks, mundane skills, utility, and new options. Check the named ability's actual price and prerequisites. A Craft's rank is not the same thing as a purchased accuracy rank.

Many established supernatural Craft upgrades use **5 SP per rank**. Some traditions differ: Jestercraft includes **3-SP modifiers and 4-SP applications**. Use the individual discipline's rules rather than a universal price assumption.

## Spending TP

TP purchases improve a derived statistic. You may train physical or magical accuracy, evasion, HP, damage, armor, resistance, and other supported statistics. Each statistic can have its own price ladder and benefit schedule.

**Later ranks cost more.** The price belongs to the next rank you are buying, not to your character's level or the number of ranks you happen to buy today. Record each purchase and keep unspent TP visible.

::: detail A worked purchase example
If your table uses a ladder charging 2 TP for each of ranks 1–4, purchasing three ranks costs 6 TP. That is an explanation of rank-band pricing, not a universal price for every statistic. Your GM's selected training menu supplies the actual cost and benefit.
:::

## Mana Capacity training

You can train your maximum MP independently of Endurance from level 1. Each rank gives **+5 maximum MP**, with a maximum of **20 ranks (+100 MP)**. There is no END prerequisite or percentage cap.

| Rank | TP per rank | Cumulative TP | Bonus MP at end of band |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 1–5 | 2 | 10 | +25 |
| 6–10 | 3 | 25 | +50 |
| 11–15 | 4 | 45 | +75 |
| 16–20 | 5 | 70 | +100 |

Add this bonus after your racial mana-capacity calculation. The same reservoir supports Soul Strain; it is not a second pool. Moontouched gain strain capacity only, retaining zero usable natural MP. Drakyn still replenish mana only by consuming Starspawn; Thornguard retain their casting and strain restrictions. Mana Capacity training does not change HP, spell power, Channel Limit, Leywells or recovery methods.

## What the combat statistics mean

| Statistic | What training changes |
|---|---|
| **Physical accuracy** | Your chance to connect with a lawful physical attack. |
| **Magical accuracy** | Your contribution to eligible magical attack checks; individual Crafts still have their own rules. |
| **Evasion** | How difficult you are to hit. |
| **HP** | How much ordinary damage you can withstand. |
| **Mana Capacity** | Your maximum MP and shared Soul Strain capacity, subject to racial rules. |
| **Damage** | How hard an eligible landed attack hits, before the target's defenses resolve it. |
| **Armor or resistance** | Protection against the appropriate incoming threat. |
| **Technique, movement, initiative, and other supported training** | Particular capabilities defined by the table's selected rules. |

::: detail Attribute contributions to accuracy and evasion
These are the training-system foundation formulas. Purchased bonuses are added separately.

| Statistic | Attribute contribution |
|---|---|
| Physical accuracy | floor((effective STR + effective DEX) / 20) |
| Magical accuracy | floor((effective INT + effective DEX) / 20) |
| Evasion | floor((effective DEX + effective END) / 20) |

There is no automatic flat level bonus in this training foundation. Equipment, statuses, and temporary effects apply under their own rules.
:::

## Plan a strength and a supporting investment

Choose what you most want to do well, then buy enough supporting competence to make it work. Accuracy helps a powerful attack reach its target. HP gives a nimble character room for mistakes. Damage gives a heavily protected character a meaningful threat.

You can specialize through attributes, TP, equipment, or a mixture. Those choices need not all point in the same direction: END can support a lightly armored fighter carrying a very heavy weapon, while a different END build spends its capacity on armor.

## Check the menu with your GM

The full training price list and HP-per-rank schedule are still being calibrated. Use one declared menu for your table. Use the selected menu your GM supplies for actual purchases.

When a benefit scales with level, owned ranks use your **current-level** benefit; buying early should not require repurchasing the same rank. Check HP caps before spending on a rank that may be partly or wholly ineffective. Gear changes do not automatically refund previous TP purchases.
