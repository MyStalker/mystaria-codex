Race shapes your anatomy, inherited gifts, cultural expectations, and possible magical traditions. Culture is a starting point for a character, not a compulsory personality.

## Find a character concept

| Race | Body and identity | A useful starting interest |
|---|---|---|
| **Moontouched** | Rabbitfolk with keen senses and no natural MP. | Evasive adventuring; rare chosen Warlocks handle external mana. |
| **Thornguard** | Ratfolk associated with martial Banners and Canopy society. | Knights, guards, rogues, and the mana-supplying side of Concordance. |
| **Bloomkin** | Small mousefolk with precise white-mana shaping. | Skilled craftspeople, Shieldmates, and cooperative Concordance. |
| **Tidekin** | Compact otterfolk with aquatic bodies and Raft traditions. | Wakecraft, waterways, investigation, and travel. |
| **Duskpaws** | Raccoonfolk with black-mana Fraycraft and a culture of salvage. | Clever practical work, locks, decay, and reclaimed Lastwear. |
| **Naga** | Serpentine peoples prominent in Imperial hierarchy. | Psycraft: Psychokinesis, Telepathy, and Divination. |
| **Kobolds** | Small dexterous engineers with strong intellectual gifts. | Orbcraft, Mirrorcraft, mechanisms, and magical interfaces. |
| **Vren** | Batfolk whose social world includes the Blood Courts. | Blood magic, bonds, transformations, shadows, and Celerity. |
| **Corvyn** | Crowfolk with two arms and separate back-mounted wings. | Veilsight, tattoo magic, and Starspawn Witchbonds. |
| **Driders** | Two arms, six spider legs, and four pupil-less eyes. | Imposing anatomy, sacred silk traditions, and devotion to Arrest. |
| **Untouched Serpentari** | Humanoid Serpentari, distinct from the ancient true serpents. | A character shaped by that inheritance without the ancient true form. |
| **Kharak — Rexfolk** | Powerful upright tyrannosaur people with humanoid arms and hands. | Imperial service, Warclans, martial traditions, and personal Tattao. |
| **Centaurs** | Equine-bodied people renowned for smithwork and engineering. | Anvilcant, crafted equipment, and clockwork. |

These thirteen races have established player-stat references. The HP/training starting conversion still needs your GM's selected sheet; do not copy an old Speed score into Skill automatically.

## Other peoples you may meet

**Drakyn** are dragons with hoards, Starspawn predation, Sorcery, and Wyrmcraft. Their biology and magical resource model deserve their own discussion before play.

**Nymbrin** are the fluid alchemists of the Scarlands. Their Wetcraft underpins industry, living buildings, healing, and mutation; a complete player-stat package is not yet established.

**Leshy**, also called Leshen, are plant-bodied people central to cultivation, Nectar, and Beanstocks. They are intended for eventual player support, with incomplete racial mechanics.

**Dirtmoles** is the provisional name for a developing mining people. Earthcraft has established economic uses; a finished racial identity and player package are not supplied.

**Firstshaped** are fully sapient animal-bodied variants, rather than ordinary wildlife. **Hexen** describes a diverse mutated condition affecting different creatures, not one uniform race.

## Bodies change practical choices

Consider hands, legs, tails, wings, size, actual senses, and equipment fit. A body can make a route or tool convenient without secretly multiplying an attribute. Equal effective STR means comparable measurable strength across races; anatomy still changes how it is applied.

## Culture, clothes, and names

Bloomkin Bannerweave, Duskpaw Lastwear, Kobold Working Coats, Tidekin Kin-knots, and Kharak Tattao express very different histories and values. Individual work, upbringing, status, and taste also matter.

Use [society and daily life](/world/society/) for citizenship, service, naming, and speech. Then return to [build a character](/learn/character/) to choose the abilities and equipment that make your own person playable.
