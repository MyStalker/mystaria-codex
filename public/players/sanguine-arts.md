The Vren's Sanguine Arts join blood, identity, predation, and control. Choose disciplines for the abilities you actually want; they are separate learned paths rather than one unlimited vampire package.

## Six disciplines

| Discipline | Its focus |
|---|---|
| **Blood Magic — the Crimson Art** | Shaping blood and its magical applications. |
| **Bloodbond — the Covenant Art** | Bonds, awareness, and the obligations of linked blood. |
| **Nightshape — the Borrowed Beast** | Taking other forms and their particular benefits and constraints. |
| **Umbracraft — Sovereignty of Shadow** | Shadow and darkness. |
| **Beastheart — the Unleashed Hunger** | Predatory physical power and dangerous instincts. |
| **Celerity — the Stolen Moment** | Specialized speed and timing effects. |

The **Blood Boon** is a separate foundational feature with its own scaling and restrictions. Learned forms, resources, duration, and prerequisites still apply to the actual ability used.

## Building a Vren

Decide whether blood, forms, control, shadow, or martial power is your main contribution. Buy the corresponding learned abilities with SP, then support them through appropriate attributes and TP. A named speed effect does not independently waive the shared offensive-action limit.

Some abilities still need their exact HP/training interaction supplied for your table. Preserve the named ability's actual identity; do not translate an old injury figure into an invented number of HP.

## Blood Courts and personal choices

The Blood Courts provide a social and political setting for these arts, with exploitation and concentrated power. Individual Vren can support, resist, escape, or complicate those institutions. Cultural expectation is not a compulsory character motivation.
