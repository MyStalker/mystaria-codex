Material is a practical choice, not a ladder where every exotic substance is universally better. Weight, brittleness, fire, maintenance, and magical behavior can matter as much as strength.

## Equipment materials

| Material | Why use it? | What to watch |
|---|---|---|
| **Boughsteel** | Reliable, enchantment-friendly high-grade carbon steel. | No extraordinary property that solves every threat. |
| **Trufula** | Extremely light, physically tough cultivated wood. | Extremely flammable; slow cultivation uses green-rich Beanstock juice. |
| **Adamantite** | Exceptionally strong, dense natural metal for protection and high-stress components. | Its great weight makes full-sized arms demanding to use. |
| **Nullporcelain / Nullporc** | Mana-nonconductive ceramic for magical protection and extreme-energy infrastructure. | Brittle and poor at flexing; mundane physical threats remain important. Complete insulation blocks wireless Cipherlight and Prismind hopping; an actual fiber-optic cable can provide a route through it. |
| **Skyvein** | Negative-weight living alloy of Pazara and aluminium. | Inertia remains; Vital Essence nourishment and stability matter. |
| **Reprise Bronze** | Shape-memory alloy with prepared alternative forms. | The forms must be designed beforehand. |
| **Oathsilver** | Stable long-term attunement and enchantment work. | Attunement and strain still follow their actual rules. |
| **Bellcopper** | Sonic resonance and vibration transfer. | Poor engineering can transmit dangerous vibration back to the user. |
| **Hearthiron** | Long-duration stored heat, furnaces, and heat banks. | Finite heat depletes under use; insulation matters. |
| **Springsteel** | Exceptional elasticity, spring force, recoil management, and precision machinery. | Stored force needs safe engineering. |
| **Glassweave** | Transparent, physically tough composite for visors and windows. | Sustained crushing/twisting and its actual magical limits still matter. |

## Scarlands living materials

**Pazara** is waxlike, living, semisentient material grown into many Scarlands buildings. Mature structures develop chambers and arteries for nourishment. Alchemists guide changes; starvation and poisoned nourishment threaten the structure. Hardened processed Pazara is called **chitin** and appears in equipment and components.

## Ancient construction

**Serpent Steel**, or **Snakesteel**, is a living structural superalloy of Adamantite, Springsteel, and Pazara. Underground it can obtain nourishment from depleted soil mana. Conventional reforging kills the living component; recovery of valuable metals and reuse of intact structure are different choices.

**Slagstone** is ancient mana-responsive limestone that can liquefy and recement for construction. It is an important stable exception to the ordinary fragility of raw temporary Earthcraft excavation.

## Choose an actual item profile

A material description does not supply every item's HP-system AR, AP, Weight, handling, price, or enchantment permission. Use a finished profile agreed with the GM. Material, Quality, enchantment, and the training of the person using it stay separate.
