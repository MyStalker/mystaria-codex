Mystaria's magic uses five colors, many distinct traditions, and real constraints. To cast something, you need the ability, the appropriate energy, a lawful way to channel it, and whatever the effect acts through.

## The five colors

| Color | Common magical concerns | Environmental association |
|---|---|---|
| **Red** | Heat, destruction, energetic empowerment. | Mountains and volcanoes. |
| **Blue** | Water, perception, affection, transformation. | Oceans and water. |
| **Green** | Life, healing, growth, earth. | Forests and growing ecosystems. |
| **White** | Barriers, light, reinforcement, mental wards. | Plains. |
| **Black** | Fear, darkness, draining, fortune, entropy. | Swamps. |

Colors are not moral alignments. In ordinary conversation, people usually say “red” or “blue” rather than the technical word “mana.”

## What lets you cast?

Check four things:

1. **Knowledge:** the actual learned Incantation, Craft rank, or other eligible ability.
2. **Energy:** real available MP of a usable color and source.
3. **Channel:** how much you can lawfully invest in the action or ritual.
4. **Delivery:** reach, targeting, required material or medium, and any implement.

A large reserve does not automatically allow a large one-action spell. An implement does not teach an unlearned effect. Magic that propels a real stone does not turn the stone's impact into pure energy damage.

## Sorcery and racial Crafts

**Sorcery** uses spoken **Elder Resonance**. You purchase individual **Incantations** and their **alterations**, which change how a learned principle manifests.

**Crafts** have their own disciplines and learned ranks. Wakecraft, Psycraft, the Sanguine Arts, and Anvilcant do different jobs and have different limits. Native access and learning a foreign Craft can have different costs.

## MP and recovery

Natural maximum MP uses the applicable racial capacity and Soul Strain rules, including the +10 capacity compensation before racial capacity multipliers. **Drakyn have a natural maximum MP but restore it only by consuming Starspawn, not by resting. Moontouched have zero natural usable MP**, but receive the +10 compensation for Soul Strain calculations only. Warlocks require actual external mana sources.

Stored industrial fuel, a caster's reserve, a Leywell, biological Vital Essence, and dietary managmins are different resources. [Mana generation and waste](/economy/mana/) explains the wider world economy.

## Channels, implements, and media

One-action mana-costing spells normally use at most half the applicable full Channel Limit, with the approved minimum one-MP handling for an eligible spell; two-action spells may use the full limit. The full universal Craft Mastery schedule is still being settled, so use the channel value your GM supplies.

An implement may contribute its own potency. A medium is the real substance or energy the Craft manipulates: water for ordinary Wakecraft work, fabric for Jester threads, or the applicable elemental source. An empty reservoir or absent medium is a real limitation.

## Rituals and cooperation

Rituals allow larger workings over longer periods, with actual mana supplied during the work. A leader's learned capabilities and channel limits determine the spell; allies contribute real resources rather than granting arbitrary new ranks. Cooperation and long rituals can lose substantial energy to entropy.

An interrupted ritual fails rather than becoming an instant partial-strength attack. Permanent workings and their Soul Strain follow their own rules. Ask your GM about an exact ritual before committing resources to it.


The full Channel Limit uses floor(1 + governing effective attribute/10 + applicable Craft Mastery), subject to Craft-specific exceptions. Warlocks use INT or END and half ordinary throughput, with the compensation before the specialty multiplier. Mastery accrual details still require a GM value.
