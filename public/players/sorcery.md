Sorcery gives a learned magical principle a spoken form. You voice **Elder Resonance**, use a compatible color, and invest actual mana within your channel limits.

## Incantations and alterations

An **Incantation** teaches the base magical principle. An **alteration** changes an eligible expression of that principle. Neither is a generic license to improvise an unrelated spell.

## Before you cast

Check the exact alteration, color, MP, medium, target, action count, and channel capacity. An offensive cantrip is still an attack. A longer ritual increases lawful investment opportunities only when you have the resources and can complete the work.

Some approved Blue, Green, and White alteration selections do not yet have a complete recovered name list. Use the named available entry; missing names are not extra choices to invent at purchase time.
