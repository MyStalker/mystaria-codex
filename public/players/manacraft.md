A Warlock handles **real external mana** rather than drawing on a natural personal MP pool. Moontouched Warlocks are chosen by Surrender; manipulating energy does not mean creating it.

## Start with a source

Willing allies, accessible ongoing magic, and eligible Leywells can supply actual compatible mana. Capacity, access, handling time, and the target's resistance still matter. A properly color-matched conventional Leywell can receive a Warlock's transfer without the ordinary transfer loss; the reservoir's capacity and single-color identity remain.

## What the colors can express

| Color | Foundational expression |
|---|---|
| Red | Energize something real: accelerate, heat, or empower a relevant target or medium. |
| Blue | Adapt or morph physical arrangement; responsive survival adaptations and perception/emotion effects have their own limits. |
| Green | Grow, heal, or repair, including eligible objects and natural matter. |
| White | Organize, clarify, reinforce order, and provide finite protection. |
| Black | Degrade, weaken, exhaust, darken, or introduce entropy. |

These expressions explain the tradition's direction; they are not a price list or free permission for every imagined effect. Actual learned capabilities and resource limits decide a working.

## Develop a specialty

The established Manacraft disciplines cover **Mana Reach**, **Mana Power**, **Mana Harvest**, **Mana Imbuement**, and **Mana Dominion**. Each has learned ranks and prerequisites. Modular **Mana-Shaping**, **Spell-Shaping**, **Soul-Shaping**, and Craft-specific **Esotery** extend the developing structure; their incomplete progressions are not interchangeable with finished purchases.

Spell-Shaping requires active lawful shaping or investment rather than passively sharing every Warlock skill with the party. Soul work addresses distinct components—Anima, Cognum, Corpus, and Spiritus—and especially powerful soul changes require their own completed rules.

## Practical limitations

An absent mana source, protected target, inaccessible reservoir, interrupted working, or inadequate medium can stop a plan. A projected spirit does not gain free mana. Transferring into another creature's natural pool requires a compatible color and genuine capacity.

Ask your GM for the applicable channel and holding values when preparing a Warlock sheet; the complete modular numerical migration remains unfinished. The [magic guide](/learn/magic/) supplies the shared casting checklist.
