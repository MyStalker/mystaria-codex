Starspawn fill much of the ecological place of wildlife, but they are intelligent magical beings. Friendship, territorial conflict, predation, hunting, and exploitation all have consequences beyond a single encounter.

## Starspawn and Recall

Starspawn originate in the Celestial Realm and retain personality and memory across bodies. Defeat and death can be terrifying. Immortality does not make them indifferent to pain, captivity, or being eaten.

Celestial Recall constrains the use of their flesh: unassimilated meat returns with Recall even if detached, cooked, frozen, or stored elsewhere. Living products and nutrients already biologically assimilated have their own established treatment. See [food and farming](/economy/food/).

## Familiar, Accord, and Witchbond

A **Summoner's Accord** and a **Corvyn Witchbond** are different relationships. Witchbonds support teaching, borrowed casting, dreams, and companionship; they do not automatically grant Celestial summoning or a familiar's Named Spell.

What a familiar knows, its willingness, actual mana, distance, and the tradition's limits all matter. A magical companion is not a free source of every spell in its color.

## Mossback families

Mossbacks reproduce through pollination and communal **Blooms**. Several mothers become stationary and vulnerable for about a season, feeding developing seeds with milk; walking sprouts later introduce ordinary feed. Other females help nurse and guard them.

Reproductive maturity around twelve years and full adult physical size around twenty are different thresholds. Nurseries, protected breeding, feed, healers, and later ranching all create real labor and land requirements.

Exact husbandry yields and many combat profiles remain under development. Use a GM-supplied encounter profile; an economic livestock example is not automatically a finished monster stat block.
