# Kobold Fashion in the Gilded Coil

**Status:** CANON for explicitly approved foundation below; follow-up ceremonial and prestige details remain **PROPOSAL / NOT YET CANON**.
**Last author approval:** November 6, 2026.
**Related files:** `02_Races.md`, `11_Visual_Style_Guide.md`, `25_Imperial_Collar_Culture_and_Restraints.md`, `18_The_Gilded_Coil_Imperial_Gazetteer.md`, `10_Canon_Changelog.md`.

## I. Approved character of Kobold fashion — CANON

Kobolds prize technical accomplishment and practical equipment as features of style. The Imperial baseline permits mature and provocative silhouettes, but Kobolds express it through modern-fantasy engineering dress: fitted laboratory coats, short high-low skirts, utility belts, goggles, tool straps, restrained leather or latex accessories and personal instruments. Their approved red Kobold engineer illustration is a principal clothing reference, not an approval of oversized character anatomy. Kobolds are small-bodied; avoid accidentally depicting them as human-sized or seven-foot tall.

**Practicality remains central even among prestigious household specialists.** A distinguished enslaved Kobold may wear an impeccably maintained mechanic's outfit, professor's clothing or other scholarly/technical garments rather than impractical aristocratic costume. Petticoats are a prime example; tailored jackets, waistcoats, short academic coats, layered skirts, decorative cuffs and eyewear are compatible wardrobe options, but their exact prevalence is not prescribed as universal fashion.

**Spectacles are especially common in casual Kobold dress.** Their eyes are naturally poor at long-distance vision, adapted to ancestral cave environments where seeing far was less important. This is a qualitative racial biology statement, not an approved universal visual-acuity formula or mechanical attribute penalty. Some Kobolds imprinted upon keen-sighted peoples, such as the Corvyn, are exceptions; the exact mechanism, extent, timing or statistical effect of such an exception is **UNRESOLVED**. Do not assume imprinting grants every ability or specific DEX/SPD bonuses.

**Free Kobolds often wear markedly more worn, haggard and self-modified equipment than enslaved household specialists.** With no masters imposing uniform-maintenance policies, their laboratory coats are commonly torn, burnt, patched or stained while they focus on projects they regard as more important. This is a cultural tendency, not proof of status or a rule that all free Kobolds are unkempt. Well-maintained uniforms can belong to enslaved specialists serving prestigious households. Individual exceptions are normal.

**Craftsmanship and achievement are sources of prestige.** The author's approval retains the proposed idea of cherished personal tools worn or carried visibly as practical ornaments and reminders of achievement. No universal tool-ranking system, named award, ritual or ownership law has been established by implication.

## II. Existing Orb interface and legal distinctions — CANON

An enslaved Kobold's ordinary functional Orb Collar is a simple smooth metal neck band with **one central colored Orb**, without outward buckle, clamp, exposed lock or ring hardware. It is a tool and interface as well as Imperial identification. Orbcraft-capable Kobolds can interpret a dense information stream through it; a small local aurora during paw-gestures may give untrained viewers a soft headache. This is not the full Coilnet Rainbow. Technicians normally keep a compatible Orb on their person; **free Kobold professionals particularly favor Orb-bearing bracers** rather than obligatory identification collars.

The extremely expensive Mirror, Trance and Coilsting Collars are separate specialized Imperial restraints, not standard additions to every engineer's outfit. A maintained uniform, embroidered household insignia or functional collar does not alter a person's legal rights.

## III. Further ceremonial / social-detail proposals — NOT YET CANON

The following are **unapproved design options**, supplied for the author's review. They must not be silently interpreted as established traditions.

1. **Academic Gala dress:** Formal, well-tailored professor-like attire, embroidered waistcoats, short skirts and petticoats, spectacles, polished tools and occasional working miniatures of an inventor's most notable creation.
2. **The Proud Tool:** An admired and personally significant tool carried as a badge of achievement; workshop peers might look skeptically on purely ornamental tools that their owner cannot operate.
3. **Prestige by maintenance context:** Enslaved specialists may be issued uniformly maintained clothing, while free professionals often wear heavily personalized and repaired garments. The cultural norm is approved; detailed etiquette, institutional requirements and inferred prestige from specific patches are still proposed.
4. **Professor silhouette:** Tailored academic jackets, waistcoats, lace or ribbon accents, petticoats, short skirts, spectacles or magnifiers, satchels and appropriate Orb fittings.
5. **Presentation versus demonstration attire:** Some Kobolds may distinguish immaculate lecture/court dress from battered workshop demonstration dress, treating scorch marks and repairs as credible signs of real experimentation.

## IV. Limitations

No compulsory Kobold fashion laws, numeric eyesight penalty, new racial attribute multipliers, specific imprint transfer mechanism, universal ceremonial rituals, standardized tool-emblems, additional Orbcraft ranks or numerical equipment effects have been approved by this fashion ruling. Existing racial statistics, Craft rules and imprinted-name customs remain authoritative in their owning references. Individual illustrations are not approved merely because they resemble these clothes.
