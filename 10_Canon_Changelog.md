## November 7, 2026 — Hatchbane evidence and Deep Inquiry procedure approved (LATEST)

- **Approved:** Court of Fangs addresses motive, opportunity and means; authenticated **mirror evidence** and eyewitness accounts are favored, with independent corroboration and consideration of exculpatory evidence. Free-folk testimony traditionally receives greater weight than enslaved testimony, but corroborated enslaved testimony is admissible. Direct proof can establish guilt without separately proving a specific motive.
- **Approved thresholds:** Third Degree **preponderance of evidence**, Second Degree **clear and convincing**, First Degree **beyond reasonable doubt**. First Degree normally calls for two independent evidentiary foundations; independently authenticated exceptionally clear mirror evidence can suffice.
- **Approved five stages:** petition/protection, investigation, hearing, judgment, sentence/review. Include Third-Degree welfare follow-up, Banebrand identity verification and independent review before irreversible punishment.
- **Approved Writ of Deep Inquiry:** narrowly scoped, independent Crown Arbiter authorization of dangerous compelled deep mind reading or memory extraction only for exceptionally grave unresolved cases, with substantial independent evidence, exhaustion of safer methods, necessity/proportionality, scope limits, witnessed documentation and restrictions on unrelated discoveries. Informed consent governs voluntary limited examination. Free persons' independent rights to silence and mental privacy remain recognized; invoking them is not proof of guilt.
- Outstanding specifics include examiner qualifications, appeals timing, slave protections, and writ refusal/remedies. Supersedes earlier entries marking these procedures as proposals.

## November 7, 2026 — Mirror evidence and separate mental privacy/silence rights (LATEST)

- **Approved correction:** mirrors record visual/audio information; Orbs **store** mirror recordings but cannot directly record sight or sound. Call video/audio recordings **mirror evidence**, not 'Orb recordings'. Authenticated mirror evidence and eyewitness accounts are favored; Imperial courts tend to favor free-folk over enslaved eyewitness testimony.
- **Approved:** psionic mind reading, Orbcraft memory extraction and Tidekin Mirrorwake may support innocence or guilt, but are not infallible. Compelled deep mind reading or memory transfer is painful, dangerous, potentially permanently harmful, and restricted in principle to very high-stakes cases with extensive independent evidence when ordinary proof remains inconclusive. Trivial emotion-reading capability does not imply unrestricted admissibility.
- **Approved:** free people hold separate rights to **silence** and **mental privacy**. They can invoke each independently; unconsented emotional readings cannot be used as evidence or grounds for suspicion. Masters have significant authority over whether and how their slaves are questioned; exact bounds remain open.
- **PROPOSED ONLY:** a special **Writ of Deep Inquiry**, requiring independent Crown Arbiter authorization, necessity, proportionality, exhaustion of safer means and a limited scope. Prior suggested evidence thresholds and court workflow are also pending explicit approval.

## November 7, 2026 — Hatchbane degrees, ancient substitution and Kharak Extinction past (LATEST)

- **Approved Third Degree:** substantial neglect/disrespect, poor food/lodging and abusive care, including failures caused by compulsory slave duties; reparations, often humiliating apology, mandatory excellence in subsequent care; repeated failure may escalate.
- **Approved Second Degree:** substantial harm or kidnapping of eggs/hatchlings, including attack upon a Kharak carrying an unlaid egg; court establishes seriousness and culpability. Attacker and superior who culpably allowed it may share guilt, not automatic strict liability. **Banebrand** is a permanently painful, nonhealing magical mark, with possible clan reorganization. Repeat Hatchbane after Banebrand may bring loss of station or death; higher rank implies greater responsibility, not immunity.
- **Approved First Degree:** deliberate killing, attempted killing, exceptionally grave abuse, commissioned assassination of eggs/hatchlings, and foreseeable/accepted killing during commissioned kidnapping. Command responsibility is evaluated through proof of culpability. Loss of title and Coil; **Decimation** by a follower of Arrest for those without comparable status, subject to further accountability for powerful offenders. Exact procedure open.
- **Ancient exception:** almost never invoked in independent, non-Arrest Kharak clans, not ordinary Imperial law: a freely volunteering **independent adult offspring** may offer life as substitute. Only a **true Serpentari** has ancestral authority to rescind the old provision. Coercion invalidates the offer; enormous consequences and continuing dishonor. Its historical origin and formal operation remain unresolved.
- **Historical contrast:** early Khans before Vornak practiced **Extinction**, destroying enemy bloodlines and sometimes entire minor species, including children. Modern Hatchbane reflects Kharak movement toward protective justice, albeit imperfectly. Vornak's precise reforms remain open. See `33_Kharak_Rexfolk_Culture_and_Command.md`.

### November 5, 2026 — Shimmer XP progression and enemy threat classes approved (LATEST)

- **Explicit user approval:** Hero XP required to advance from level **L** to **L + 1** is **`L × (L + 1)`**, as a per-level requirement, not cumulative lifetime XP. Example thresholds: level 1 **2 XP**, level 50 **2,550 XP**, level 100 **10,100 XP**. XP carries through level transitions.
- **Ordinary qualifying reward:** **`XP = 2 × encounter level × threat modifier`**. A defeated enemy uses its own level; a qualifying quest or nonviolent patron-approved achievement can use a GM-assigned equivalent challenge level. Round to nearest whole XP, minimum **1 XP for a qualifying encounter**. Patron-specific XP rules and the fact that mundane quests do not automatically award XP remain operative.
- **Threat modifiers and relative-level guidelines:** **Horde ×0.1** (enemy level ≤50% of Hero); **Minion ×0.4** (>50%–80%); **Henchman ×1** (>80%–110%); **Elite ×2** (>110%–140%); **Boss** (usually >140% or specially designated). These are **guidelines**; the GM assigns actual encounter class based on capabilities and context, not level ratio alone. The categories indicate swarmable trivial enemies, minor threats, near-peers, minibosses and prepared prolonged bosses respectively.
- **Boss exception:** A qualifying Boss grants the remaining XP to the next level **plus** the full XP for the following level, advancing the Hero **exactly two levels from the Hero's current level and progress**. At level 50 with no progress, the reward is **5,202 XP** and the Hero reaches level 52. Boss XP replaces, rather than multiplies, ordinary enemy XP.
- **Pacing:** One equal-level Henchman at level 1 yields the **2 XP** needed to level; at level 100, an equal-level Henchman gives **200 XP** against **10,100 XP**, so **51** encounters are needed from zero progress (approximately the requested 50).
- **Not approved by implication:** The previously floated anti-farming numerical restriction and any party XP division procedure remain unspecified; merely trivial/repetitive acts are not automatically patron-qualified. This approval does **not** change Shimmer attribute gains, Skill Point costs, rank prerequisites, or the goddesses' established reward criteria.
- **Owning file:** `01_World_and_Shimmer.md`; indexed in `00_Mystaria_Canon_Index.md`.

## November 6, 2026 — Three PP pressure; Trapped independent; reload, aim and mounts (LATEST)

**APPROVED PRESSURE RULE:** Count **net meaningful Pressure Points (PP)**. **0 = Open**, **1–2 = Pressured with exactly the same evasion consequence**, **3+ = Extreme Pressure with no greater evasion penalty at higher totals**. Most independent effective circumstances contribute **1 PP**: credible flank, applicable reach advantage, reliable actual DEX prediction (wrong prediction can favor the *opponent*), small qualifying archer suppression, and an offhand Attack when it poses a genuine separate threat. Credible offhand Defense and other counters may remove/offset applicable PP; PP cannot go below zero. A qualifying four-Windling group may contribute 1, but accumulating enough saturation for Extreme Pressure through raw swarm scale should require **exponentially greater effective numbers/power**, not simple linear +1 per four Windlings. No automatic PP for a harmless attacker or mere simultaneous orders. Exact stacking/de-duplication/coordination and stat-relative swarm threshold remain unresolved.

**APPROVED TRAPPED SEPARATION:** **Trapped is separate**, not produced merely by three PP. Aware Trapped character **cannot ordinarily dodge** the applicable threat, but **can use other legal defenses** such as blocking, armor, wards or a viable reaction. Trapped is not automatically incapacitated or helpless; Extreme Pressure by itself does not eliminate ordinary evasion. This latest ruling supersedes the older ladder ordering that placed Trapped between Pressured and Extreme Pressure. Existing six evasion difficulty categories remain, with exact PP-induced difficulty modifier still open.

**APPROVED RELOAD:** Bows have negligible additional nocking/drawing opening; light crossbows create a modest vulnerability and heavier mechanical/other loading can be substantial. Careful loading of a **single-shot pistol** or a **high-capacity magazine** costs **one full-turn meaningful action**, during which attack openings are present. Siege reload exposes its operators, typically at least a full loading action, with engine-specific longer cycles still unresolved. Reload vulnerability is a contextual opening, not extra free turns for adjacent enemies.

**APPROVED AIMING:** One meaningful action can be spent **aiming** rather than firing, sacrificing one of two potential attacks for a better prospective hit. No automatic shot Power gain, armor bypass, guaranteed hit or new action; exact accuracy tier, retention/interrupt rules and pressure integration remain to define.

**APPROVED MOUNT ROLES:** Fixed side, articulated, heavy-support, assisted-loading mounts remain distinct, with combined rigs permitted when engineered. No free extra attacks or automatic recoil cancellation.
