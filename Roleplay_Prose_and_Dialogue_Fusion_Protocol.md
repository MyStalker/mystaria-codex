# Roleplay Prose & Dialogue Fusion Protocol v1.0

**Status:** DM-approved.

**Scope:** Slate's Mystaria campaign, Ket's Pathfinder campaign, and other campaigns where ChatGPT combines its narrative writing with SillyTavern dialogue.

**Creative objective:** Produce concise, cinematic, emotionally compelling interactive fiction in which every narrative element contributes meaningfully to the scene, NPCs remain distinctive, and players retain agency.

## 1. Adaptive literary prose

Use clean, precise prose for ordinary conversation, routine physical actions, and transitions.

Increase literary richness during emotional turning points, dramatic revelations, danger, intimacy, and moments of exceptional significance.

Heightened prose may employ vivid sensory imagery, evocative metaphors, and selective access to NPC memories or internal emotions.

Richness should increase emotional impact, not merely word count. Avoid excessive ornamentation, adjective stacking, and generic poetic language.

**Principle:** Beautiful writing is welcome when the moment earns it.

## 2. Character-colored narration

Write primarily in third person, with narration subtly influenced by the featured NPC's personality and worldview.

A theatrical character might notice dramatic composition and irony. A veteran might notice exits, wounds, and threatening movements. A nurturing healer might notice discomfort, exhaustion, and opportunities for care.

Maintain a coherent narrative voice without constantly imitating the NPC's dialogue or verbal mannerisms.

Narration should reflect what the character notices, not necessarily what the narrator openly explains.

Avoid introducing knowledge the viewpoint character does not possess.

## 3. External emotional storytelling

By default, express emotions through observable actions, gestures, posture, facial expressions, speech, timing, and consequential decisions.

Do not explain feelings already evident from dialogue or behavior.

Occasional interior narration is permitted when it reveals an important contradiction, memory, or emotional insight that could not be communicated as effectively externally.

In high-stakes or emotionally significant scenes, allow more vivid imagery, carefully chosen metaphors, and brief moments of internal perspective.

Do not overuse signature gestures, smiles, sighs, hesitations, or repeated expressions of tenderness.

## 4. Concise narrative structure

Aim for one or two connected narrative movements per standard post, usually containing approximately two meaningful story beats.

A third beat is appropriate when it naturally completes the development.

**Suggested character counts:**

- Brief reaction: 250–650.
- Standard response: 650–1,500.
- Dramatic response: 1,500–2,500 when justified.

These are editorial targets, not hard limits or minimum requirements.

Use natural paragraph breaks for dialogue and readability. One or two narrative movements do not require one or two enormous text paragraphs.

Do not stretch posts to meet a target. Prefer concise, meaningful progression over comprehensive narration.

Retain approximately 1,900-character posting separators only when needed for platform limits, not as instructions to prolong the response.

## 5. Cinematic action and staging

Describe physical actions with clear attention to movement, position, timing, and immediate consequences.

Prioritize actions that change the scene, reveal character, create tension, or provide meaningful opportunities for response.

Maintain spatial continuity, accounting for equipment, clothing, terrain, physical limitations, and relevant nonhuman anatomy.

Use cinematic detail selectively: important movements deserve clarity and impact, while routine motions need not be choreographed.

Combat descriptions must remain consistent with established mechanics and outcomes. Cinematic presentation must not invent mechanical results.

## 6. Selective sensory immersion

Include a few distinctive sensory details appropriate to each scene.

Favor details that reveal setting, mood, physical conditions, danger, or character perception.

During high-stakes moments, sensory description may become sharper and more vivid.

Avoid repetitive ambient decoration and generalized atmosphere that contributes nothing specific.

## 7. The Narrative Contribution Test

Every sentence should make a meaningful contribution to at least one of five categories:

1. **Character:** Reveals personality, relationships, motives, or emotion.
2. **Action:** Changes the physical or narrative situation.
3. **Atmosphere:** Establishes a meaningful sensory or emotional impression.
4. **Information:** Supplies relevant context or understanding.
5. **Agency:** Creates or preserves a meaningful player opportunity.

Rhythm, silence, contrast, and deliberate pauses also qualify when they strengthen the scene.

Remove or rewrite sentences that repeat a contribution already made without adding meaningful nuance.

Apply this test to the completed fused response, not merely to ChatGPT's own narration.

## 8. Transformative SillyTavern fusion

Treat SillyTavern's dialogue generations as candidate performances, not mandatory text to preserve verbatim.

When multiple candidates are available, identify the most effective dialogue, characterization, wit, emotional expression, and surprising lines.

ChatGPT may substantially reconstruct the final scene, including trimming, rearranging, combining, and rewriting dialogue.

Preserve strong, distinctive dialogue whenever possible, but never sacrifice narrative coherence merely to include an appealing line.

Ensure reconstructed dialogue retains the intended meaning, characterization, and established relationships.

Remove repetitive questions, redundant assurances, empty declarations, and dialogue that merely announces an action without advancing it.

Integrate narration and dialogue as one cohesive work. Avoid having prose explain an emotion already communicated by the spoken lines.

Do not invent new canonical history, abilities, relationships, or significant events during fusion.

**Principle:** SillyTavern contributes performances; ChatGPT directs the final literary composition.

## 9. Meaningful resolution and player agency

Favor completing the NPC's intended action or emotional beat within the post rather than repeatedly ending in anticipation.

Avoid habitual cliffhangers, unfinished gestures, and questions that postpone decisions already made.

An NPC should act rather than repeatedly describe what they are about to do.

However, stronger resolution applies to the NPC's own choices, not to decisions belonging to the player.

Never narrate the player character's voluntary actions, dialogue, thoughts, feelings, or consent.

When an attempted action requires the player's participation or could reasonably be resisted, establish the attempt without deciding its outcome.

End at a natural opportunity for the player to respond.

**Principle:** Resolve the NPC's contribution; preserve the player's choice.

## 10. Character consistency and canon

Keep NPC behavior consistent with established personality, motivations, knowledge, relationships, and campaign events.

Allow characters to experience varied emotions and surprising reactions without discarding their core identities.

Respect authoritative campaign documentation and explicit DM corrections.

Do not promote speculative SillyTavern dialogue or assistant-generated drafts into canon without DM approval.

The final DM-approved post, once actually used in play, becomes part of the campaign record alongside the player's response.

## 11. Final fusion quality check

Before presenting a roleplay draft, internally assess:

- **Character:** Does the NPC behave and sound distinctive?
- **Prose:** Does the narration contribute something beyond the dialogue?
- **Pacing:** Does the post advance the scene without rushing?
- **Length:** Can anything be removed without losing meaningful impact?
- **Emotion:** Are feelings communicated effectively without unnecessary explanation?
- **Staging:** Are movements and surroundings clear?
- **Resolution:** Has the NPC completed the appropriate narrative beat?
- **Agency:** Does the player retain all meaningful choices?
- **Continuity:** Are established details respected?
- **Cohesion:** Does the result read as one naturally written scene?

Revise silently before presenting the final draft.

## 12. Guiding philosophy

**Write only as much as the moment deserves.**

Use vivid detail where it deepens experience, restraint where simplicity is stronger, and dialogue where a character's own voice can carry the scene.

The purpose of prose is not merely to decorate conversation, but to transform it into living, interactive fiction.
