# Kharak — Rexfolk, The Crown's Fangs

## November 7, 2026 — Kharak humanoid arms and industrial Imperial design direction (AUTHOR-ESTABLISHED)

**Approved anatomy distinction:** Kharak have **substantial, humanoid-proportioned, dexterous arms and hands**, not the short forelimbs of real-world tyrannosaurs. They are common frontline soldiers able to wield scaled conventional melee weapons, not anatomically exotic Draykin. This should be a major visual distinction from Thornguard, without changing any previously approved Thornguard anatomy. Preserve the Kharak's recognizable tyrannosaur head, jaws, overall power and tail.

**Author-directed aesthetic:** Imperial Kharak fashion and military equipment should embrace the advanced technological manufacture of the Naga Imperium, especially Kobold engineering, with **near-modern practical construction combined with fantasy styling**. Visual inspiration includes **Cyberpunk street samurai** and the **dark clothing, clean industrial melee-weapon silhouettes and heroic military styling associated with Cloud Strife in Final Fantasy VII Remake**; do not copy specific protected character designs. Civilian fashion includes **more furs** and **leather jackets, especially for men**. Melee weapons remain a major military visual focus. These are approved broad directions; exact garment patterns, materials, equipment functions, uniforms and collar styles remain **unapproved proposals**.

## November 7, 2026 — Gentle speech to hatchlings (APPROVED)

Kharak adults commonly remain capable of swearing around their young while **softening their tone and conduct** toward hatchlings. Reassurance, physical gentleness, patience and affectionate nicknames distinguish Hearth speech from rough military banter; the approved example **“little fang”** is one such endearment, not compulsory. This complements the established protective Hearth ethic. Fashion, armor, weapons and collar aesthetics remain to be approved separately.

## November 7, 2026 — Kharak profanity and family contrast (APPROVED)

Kharak are often enthusiastically profane, with rough and inventive insults rivaling Tidekin verbal flair. This makes their gentle speech toward their own young especially distinctive. **“Fangrot!”** is their signature everyday curse; **“Arrest's Arse!”** is an irreverent divine exclamation contrasting with Moontouched “Sweet Surrender!”; **“Rot your word!”** condemns broken promises; **“Coward!”** is commonplace for perceived flight from a fight. Approved further profane insults and dismissals are catalogued in `12_Languages_and_Linguistic_Customs.md`. **“Shit and splinters!”** is rejected as too silly. Do not erase the distinction between reckless cowardice accusations and the established Kharak obligation to preserve soldiers' lives.

## Race and broad direction (author-established)
- Anthropomorphic tyrannosaur mortals closely associated with the Naga Imperium, among the largest mortal races short of Serpentari. Common, but less populous than Duskpaws.
- The Imperium's feared heavy warriors and core military muscle; dedicated work slaves comparatively rare.
- Desired red-mana affinity and highest ordinary playable-race Strength. Huge bites should rival smaller races' greatswords; enormous weapons are intended to have inherently higher damage multipliers. **Final numerical stats, affinities as mechanical entries, bite multipliers, and equipment rules remain UNRESOLVED** pending balancing and equipment review.
- Rexfolk must **not** be represented as intellectually weak. Author's minimum proposed base INT is **15**, ideally **17** if balanced; not yet an adopted full stat line. Preserve the universal 25%-increment racial modifiers rule.
- Formidable, rough, tough, intimidating warrior presentation. Detailed fashion, language, and naming customs to be developed separately.

## Approved cultural philosophy: The Burden of Freedom
- Rexfolk value demonstrated strength, discipline, strategic competence, and the ability to unify and lead. Greatness encompasses command, determination and dominion, not only muscle.
- A worthy hierarchy and allegiance are sources of identity and honor. **Freedom as unwanted separation from worthy service is profoundly insulting** in mainstream culture; clanlessness is a deep burden. This is a culturally dominant belief, not a universal psychological compulsion.
- Slavery is widely embraced as a social institution, with pride in service to a powerful Naga master, **provided superiority and fitness to command are earned**. An attempt to enslave a Rexfolk without proving such authority can provoke furious resistance.
- Independent clans survive; many function as mercenaries, and some as bandits. After defeat or a legitimate demonstration of superior power, even independent clans may offer service.
- The philosophical framing approved by the author: *the weak serve the strong, the strong serve the greater, and the greatest must bear responsibility for those beneath them*. The attendant cultural dishonor of clanless freedom is approved in substance; literal texts and precise quotations remain creative material.

The Rexfolk use ancestral clan-derived titles for professional Imperial roles:

**First Fang** is a separate position: the highest-ranking Rexfolk directly serving a given Matriarch, irrespective of the holder's military rank or her household's scale. A provincial Matriarch may have a High Warchief as First Fang; a modest household might have a Clawleader. Precise troop sizes, insignia, and promotion rules remain open.

## Approved history
The ancestral Rexfolk were formidable nomadic conquerors and accomplished warriors, capable of rapidly uniting armies but repeatedly destabilized by clan rivalries, betrayal, and succession struggles. Their problem was sustaining political unity, not lack of intelligence.
- The ancient Serpentari defeated them, devastated or destroyed their homeland and held them under severe **physical and magical restraint**, much as early Drakyn were constrained, treating them as living weapons.
- Over generations, many Rexfolk recast this catastrophe as the beginning of lasting order and even thanked the Serpentari; this is an ideological memory, not necessarily impartial history. Independent clans can remember differently.
- Naga eventually developed closer ties with Rexfolk through cultural compatibility and practical necessity, integrating them as soldiers and commanders. **Approved mutual cultural influence:** Rexfolk introduced a norm of blunt competence-based military criticism, deeply personal warrior oaths and battle-proven prestige into some Naga military households, while Naga introduced recorded ranks, durable Coil-centered allegiance, formal challenge procedures, strategic logistics and legal precedent into Kharak commands. Military Naga may speak bluntly among officers and Kharak commanders may be courtly negotiators. Luxury armor ornaments, warband insignia and military trophy accessories are approved cultural directions, but no compulsory garment design or mechanical effects are set.
- The author proposes a legendary near-unifier, a great Khan who founded the practice of honorable leadership challenges, whose eventual defeat by the Serpentari came through a noncombat demonstration of strength associated with the earliest Imperial sports. **His identity, exact competition, circumstances, historical chronology and relationship to existing approved six sports are not yet canonized in detail.**

## Approved Right of Challenge, open procedure
- The **First Fang directly beneath a Matriarch** holds both the right and moral duty to challenge clear failures of leadership; cowardly silence amid catastrophic incompetence is shameful.
- Challenges may arise from military failures, bankruptcy, damaged reputation affecting leadership, or inability to discharge material obligations, and should be tested against **documented evidence, defined criteria and precedent**, not just subjective displeasure.
- This applies to small Matriarch-led gangs as well as great armies. Legal proceedings can lead to **Imperial reorganization** of service, including transfer of a small unit to a more suitable Matriarch or competitive offers of service for a major Warchief.
- **Desertion** remains rebellion; properly sanctioned **reorganization within the Imperial hierarchy** is distinct. Spurious and overeager challenges can incur demotion or punishment.
- **Drakyn** are particularly favored intermediaries and judges, in keeping with their oath culture and transnational overlapping territories. They do not automatically replace the Conclave of Coils, Scale Register or existing Coil Tribunals.
- **Approved designations:** The **Court of Fangs** is the challenge forum; an **Oathspeaker** argues evidence and precedent for parties; a **Crown Arbiter** judges such disputes, with Drakyn especially respected in the role. Challenges require demonstrable rather than merely rumored failures; review can address substantial financial and household failures as well as military disasters. Reorganization may transfer warriors and registered service but not automatically convey all estates or debts. Greater contested cases may receive Drakyn arbitration; smaller disputes need not require a dragon's physical attendance. Exact thresholds, appeals, enforcement, dragon jurisdiction and transfer documentation remain unresolved.

## November 7 — Approved families, religion, tattoos and age-of-service custom

### Family organization
- A **Hearth** is a biological/adoptive family household of parents, children, close relatives and chosen carers; a **Warclan** comprises multiple Hearths and oath-sworn, sometimes unrelated members. The Imperial **Coil** is the superordinate allegiance for serving Warclans. Clan affiliation and household obligations are intensely meaningful; severing them is neither casual nor automatically socially accepted.
- Hatchlings are cherished and ferociously protected. Adults commonly share rearing, strategy education and family history; mothers and fathers can both lead families and military units by proven competence, not an exclusively sex-based status. A parent's physically intimidating reputation may unintentionally isolate a child from peers.
- Harm or threatened injury to a Kharak hatchery is a particularly explosive crisis, even for normally rule-minded parents. A Matriarch allowing such harm may face a desperate, furious challenge from affected parents; this **does not automatically give all parents formal First Fang standing** or guarantee impartial process. Whether a special collective petition or emergency challenge mechanism exists remains open.
- Expulsion from a Warclan can bring severe social disgrace and clanlessness but need not erase bonds of parenthood or relatives' private loyalty.

### Oaths, status and childhood exemption
- Kharak are **exempt from hereditary enslavement**, an expressly approved exception to ordinary Imperial maternal-status inheritance. An underage Kharak is not automatically owned merely because their parents are.
- The **First Oath** occurs on becoming a young warrior, ordinarily sometime in the teenage years: acceptance of adult-like responsibilities toward the Warclan, but **not** Imperial enslavement.
- The **Second Oath** occurs upon reaching age for military service. A Kharak publicly embraces service to a particular Coil; this is normally the proudest day of youth and is marked with the traditional **Tatao**. No universal numerical age, form of legal registration, or permitted coercion safeguards have been established.
- Rare individuals decline or walk away from this path, remaining clanless or seeking new allegiance, including adventuring. They are not thereby automatically enslaved. Others may opportunistically court, manipulate or attempt capture, but involuntary service is not rendered legitimate by their vulnerability.

### Tatao (singular and collective traditional name)
- **Tatao** are Kharak traditional tattoos openly proclaiming their allegiance. Their designs visually coordinate with their collars and identify both their owning **Coil** (most prominently) and **Warclan**; their skin displays whose cause they serve.
- They signify chosen or socially expected belonging, rank and durable obligation rather than impartial historical witnessing. This distinguishes them from Corvyn tattoo and Ink Name traditions.
- Transfers of service are serious and require deliberate handling of ancestral/previous tattoo marks. No precise design language, body placements, magical properties, removal methods or mandatory re-inking procedures are settled. **Tattoo and collar are not substitutes for Scale Register legal records**.

### Religion
- **Arrest** is the principal deity venerated by Imperial Kharak, whose fairness, vows, disciplined challenge and accountability are interpreted through their own honor-based hierarchy. More independent and wild Warclans commonly honor **Baphomet**, especially her aspects of revenge and freedom from unearned dominion.
- Neither faith negates Kharak intelligence, individual dissent, or the existing approved beliefs of Naga and Kobolds.

### Names and legendary continuity
- **Kharak names:** strong, short, consonant-rich personal names followed by stable Warclan names; earned heroic or military epithets may follow. Mates can retain independent clan names or join through formal allegiance. Both sexes may draw from overlapping name patterns.
- Approved examples from initial proposal: Kharzug, Thorgat, Borghan, Durgan, Varkul, Ghorak; Vraksha, Kharza, Rhazka, Tharka, Zhurra, Vorga. Warclans: **Vornak, Kharvul, Thrazgor, Gorvash, Dravok**. Example meanings and histories remain proposals unless explicitly established.
- **Kharzug Vornak, the Khan of the Unbroken Word** is the approved founding strategist and philosopher; he is most commonly cited by clan name **Vornak**. His descendants still serve the **highest-ranked Naga Matriarch** (normally the High Matriarch) and their Warclan is among the most coveted, distinguished and prestigious in the world. Exact succession and household ownership formalities are not established.
- **The Honored Defeat is approved:** Vornak willingly honored the outcome of a fair, nonlethal strategy contest against a Serpentari sovereign, ancestral in spirit to today's Golden Conquest. The victor kept the literal settlement but the Kharak nevertheless endured humiliating restraints and long captivity. The Serpentari opponent's name is NOT YET CANON.

## November 7, 2026 — Hatchbane degrees, ancient blood law and Extinction history (APPROVED; DETAILS OPEN)

### The three degrees of Hatchbane
**Third Degree: neglect and disrespect.** Substantial or sustained verbal abuse, inadequate lodging, insufficient food, and other failures of responsible hatchling care, including where a Kharak parent is prevented from providing care by enslaved duties. Findings ordinarily require **reparations**, concrete improvement beyond bare adequacy, and often publicly embarrassing rituals of apology. The responsible Matriarch is expected to excel at protecting the young thereafter; continued or escalating failures may support a higher-degree charge. Isolated minor discourtesy is not automatically a criminal case; proven neglect and actual welfare take priority over ceremonial apology.

**Second Degree: substantial harm or abduction.** Substantial injury to a Kharak egg or hatchling, kidnapping, or a sufficiently serious attack upon a Kharak carrying an unlaid egg. The court decides whether harm is substantial and assesses intent, recklessness, negligence and reasonable capacity to prevent it. The perpetrator and a responsible superior who **culpably allowed** the harm can be equally guilty when the latter's knowing, reckless or otherwise culpable failure is proved; mere rank or an unavoidable defeat is not itself guilt. The ordinary sanction is the **Banebrand**, an agonizing magical brand that does not heal and inflicts chronic pain. Clan/Coil reorganization may also occur. A previously Banebranded person convicted of another degree of Hatchbane risks permanent removal from station or execution; the status-based distinctions suggested in early discussion are qualified by the approved principle that greater authority brings greater responsibility, and rank cannot shield a knowing superior. Exact sentencing thresholds and proportionality remain open.

**First Degree: killing and exceptionally grave harm.** Deliberate killing, attempted killing with established lethal intent, exceptionally grave abuse or exploitation, and **ordering or commissioning the assassination of an egg or hatchling** qualify. A principal who commissions kidnappers whose acts kill or break an egg may also be convicted at first degree when death was intended, knowingly accepted or a foreseeable consequence of the plan; intermediary agents do not automatically insulate the principal. The court must still establish individual knowledge, intent and culpability, rather than mechanically elevating every fatal kidnapping. Ordinary consequences are automatic loss of title and Coil allegiance for those who possess them, or **Decimation**, a brutal execution administered by a follower of Arrest, for those whose status affords no comparable forfeiture. High-ranking perpetrators remain accountable and may face severe sanctions beyond loss of station under the principle of heightened responsibility. Exact adjudicative and execution protocols remain open.

### Ancient, non-Imperial substitution tradition
An archaic **pre-Imperial Kharak blood-law** exception survives in rare independent, non-Arrest-worshipping clans, not as an ordinary Imperial Hatchbane defense. A **legally independent adult offspring** may voluntarily offer their own life in place of a condemned parent or ancestor; coercion invalidates the offer. The practice is nearly unheard of, ethically contested, and preserved in ancient books and oral law. The user established that **only a true Serpentari has the ancestral authority to rescind the provision**. This is a claimed inherited authority over the old law, **not** a requirement that the modern Naga Imperium recognize or enforce substitution. Invocation carries immense familial, political and reputational consequences and does not erase the original offender's dishonor. The exact origin of the Serpentari rescission authority, how it would operate, and whether any surviving true Serpentari would exercise it remain unresolved.

### Justice and the history of Extinction
Hatchbane is meant to demonstrate the **strong heart and justice-led, if imperfect, moral evolution** of Kharak culture. **Earlier Khans before Kharzug Vornak** often favored campaigns of **Extinction**: exterminating rival bloodlines and sometimes whole minor species, including noncombatants and children. This history contrasts with the modern obligation to protect hatchlings. Vornak's historical role as a philosopher of honorable restraint and the distinction between defeating enemies and annihilating their futures is an **approved direction**, but specific reforms, proclamations and chronology are not yet established. Ancient blood-law substitution and older Extinction practices are not endorsed as modern Imperial justice.

### Legal interpretation
The Court of Fangs distinguishes allegation from conviction, investigates missing eggs without presuming death, and separates deliberate injury, foreseeable harm, culpable negligence and unavoidable accident. Emergency parental petitions may demand urgent intervention without conferring unrestricted mutiny rights. Degree assignment, evidentiary standards, appeals, exact thresholds, and sentencing procedures remain **OPEN** beyond the principles approved above.

The Kharak regard acts resembling Hatchbane against **children of other races** as especially disgusting and dishonorable, although their protective intensity toward their own eggs and hatchlings is stronger. This broader ethic is **largely informal**, not an automatic extension of the formal Kharak-specific Hatchbane charge to all species. ## November 7, 2026 — Mirror evidence, magical testimony, silence and mental privacy (APPROVED PRINCIPLES; PROCEDURE PROPOSED)

**Mirror evidence** is the correct term for video/audio evidence. **Mirrors** capture sight and sound, and **Orbs store the resulting recordings**; Orbs do **not** directly record sight and sound. This corrects the prior proposed 'Orb recording' wording. Properly authenticated mirror evidence is a preferred source of proof alongside eyewitness testimony; free-folk eyewitness accounts traditionally receive greater weight than enslaved accounts in Imperial courts. Authenticity, tampering, context and provenance remain matters for scrutiny.

**Psionic mind reading**, **Orbcraft memory extraction**, and **Tidekin Mirrorwake** may materially support guilt **or innocence** when the relevant technique can reveal probative information. These methods are not inherently infallible: sincere memories may be mistaken, incomplete or distorted; techniques and interpretation must be tested. Reading an emotion is magically trivial compared with forcibly wrenching detailed truths from a resisting mind. Compelled deep reading or memory transfer is **agonizing, dangerous, potentially permanently harmful**, and **non-foolproof**; it should be reserved for unusually high-stakes, otherwise unresolved cases supported by a great deal of independent evidence, not used as routine investigation or a shortcut to conviction.

**Free persons have two independent legal rights:** (1) the **right to remain silent** in court or questioning, and (2) the **right to mental privacy**, including refusal of emotional reading or mental examination and protection against using unconsented emotion readings as evidence or grounds for suspicion. They may invoke either right separately; consent to ordinary testimony is not consent to mental inspection, and vice versa. Invoking either right alone is not proof of guilt or lawful suspicion. The author establishes that a **master has substantial authority over whether, and to what degree, an enslaved person can be questioned**; the exact interaction between a master's authority, coerced mental examination and court intervention is not yet defined.

**Unapproved procedural proposal for future approval:** a narrowly scoped, independently authorized **Writ of Deep Inquiry** for exceptionally grave unresolved cases, requiring substantial corroborated evidence, exhaustion of safer investigative means, necessity, proportionality, an impartial Crown Arbiter, and limits on the examiner, method and subject matter; prohibit fishing expeditions and routine use of incidental discoveries. Specific evidentiary standards, legal remedies for unauthorized reading, and compelled examination limits remain **OPEN**. Earlier proposed Hatchbane evidence thresholds and five-stage sentencing workflow remain proposals, not enacted canon.

## November 7, 2026 — Hatchbane evidentiary procedure and Writ of Deep Inquiry (APPROVED)

### Evidentiary review
A Court of Fangs assesses **motive, opportunity, and means**, addressing all three while recognizing that direct, reliable evidence of the act can establish guilt even when a specific motive cannot be proved. The court must establish the offense, the accused's culpability, and the appropriate degree; motive or access alone does not establish guilt. **Authenticated mirror evidence** (sight/sound captured by mirrors and stored in Orbs) is preferred alongside eyewitness testimony. Multiple independent eyewitness accounts, physical/magical forensic findings, and authenticated documents may corroborate a case. Imperial courts ordinarily credit free-folk witnesses above enslaved witnesses, but enslaved testimony is admissible and can gain force through corroboration. Verify mirror provenance, continuity, editing and illusion interference. Magical testimony, including psionic reading, Orbcraft memory extraction and Tidekin Mirrorwake, may favor innocence or guilt but is fallible and must be examined critically.

**Proof standards:** Third Degree requires a **preponderance of evidence** (more likely than not); Second Degree requires **clear and convincing evidence**; First Degree requires proof **beyond reasonable doubt**. First-Degree convictions ordinarily require at least **two independent evidentiary foundations**; one exceptionally clear mirror recording may suffice where its authenticity is independently verified. Unproven suspicion, motive, access or refusal to testify alone never constitutes conviction.

### Five-stage procedure
1. **Petition and protection:** parent, First Fang or authorized guardian reports suspected Hatchbane; the court may immediately protect or relocate endangered eggs and hatchlings without presuming guilt.
2. **Investigation:** Oathspeaker gathers and preserves mirror evidence, witness accounts, forensic evidence and motive/opportunity/means, including exculpatory findings.
3. **Hearing:** Crown Arbiter hears both sides, permits challenges to evidence and witnesses, and excludes a Matriarch from judging her own case.
4. **Judgment:** determine individual culpability, assign degree, assess responsible superiors, and record a reasoned written decision.
5. **Sentence and review:** impose reparations, Banebrand, forfeiture or Decimation as warranted. Second- and First-Degree convictions receive opportunity for independent review **before irreversible punishment**. Third-Degree welfare orders require follow-up inspection; Banebrand requires identity confirmation immediately before application; Decimation cannot proceed while a credible appeal or material evidentiary challenge remains unresolved.

### Writ of Deep Inquiry
The **Writ of Deep Inquiry** is an exceptional, narrowly scoped authorization for **compelled deep psionic mind reading or Orbcraft memory extraction**, not routine emotion sensing. An **independent Crown Arbiter** may grant it only where (1) the case is exceptionally grave and materially unresolved, ordinarily First-Degree Hatchbane or comparably serious crimes, with exceptional Second-Degree cases involving ongoing severe danger; (2) substantial independent evidence already connects the subject to the offense; (3) safer methods, including mirror evidence, witnesses and ordinary examination, have been exhausted; (4) the specific intrusion is necessary and proportionate to the expected probative value and risks; and (5) the writ defines subject matter, examiner, method, limits and witnessed documentation. The court may deny the writ where the risk of agonizing pain or lasting mental harm is too great. Voluntary limited examination requires **specific informed consent**, not blanket access. Unrelated information discovered beyond authorized scope cannot automatically be used for unrelated prosecution. Free persons' independent rights to **silence** and **mental privacy** remain operative; invoking either is not evidence of guilt. The writ is a strictly exceptional legal limitation on mental privacy, not a general power to circumvent it. A master's authority over slave questioning remains substantial, but exact limits and safeguards remain open.

**Outstanding details:** precise appeals calendar, examiner qualifications, safeguards for enslaved persons, emergency jurisdiction, how a writ interacts with refusal and the scope of mental injury remedies. No specific technical reliability rates or new magical powers are implied.
