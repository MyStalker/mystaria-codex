## November 18, 2026 — Water erosion and Air amplification (LATEST AUTHOR DIRECTION; EXACT RESOLUTION OPEN)

**Explicit exceptions to the shared 0.5× SR Pressure Point:** **Water** and **Air** do not use the automatic one-self-PP band. Continue the approved **Status Resistance = (effective END)²**, lawful elemental delivery, separate accumulating magnitudes, three-turn statuses refreshed on valid matching-element reapplication, and 0.5×, >1× and ≥4× SR thresholds. Other elements retain the prior Pressure Point baseline unless separately revised.

**Water, magical erosion:** Water is the **Magic Resistance (MR) erosion counterpart to Acid's approved Armor Rating (AR) erosion**. Water's magnitude-based status reduces MR rather than forcing movement or imposing the ordinary 0.5× PP. The author's direction is erosion with rising severity; exact amount at 0.5×, >1× and 4× SR, floor, rounding, persistence/recovery and whether protected surfaces/items have separate MR are **NOT YET DETERMINED**. Do not silently assume a percent or numerical MR reduction or make wetting alone ignore ordinary protection.

**Air, elemental fanning and detonation:** At **0.5× SR and above**, **half of current Air Magnitude increases the effective magnitude of each other active elemental condition** on that target. At **more than 1× SR**, **all other elemental magnitude is multiplied by 2 for resolving its final effects**; preserve the Air half-magnitude contribution as well, without self-amplification or recursive loops. At **4× SR or above**, **all elemental magnitude is expended at quadruple power in an explosive attack that deals damage to the target**, after which **every elemental magnitude condition ends**, including Air. The intended explosion's numerical damage conversion, whether its source magnitude includes one or multiple elemental pools and exactly how the ×4 applies, interaction order with the x2 and half-Air modifiers, defensive routing, splash area (if any), and automatic versus triggered timing are **OPEN**. Do not equate quadruple Power with four direct injury stages. No generic free extra attack is authorized outside the specified culmination.

**Elemental outcome map:** Fire: from 0.5× one self-PP; above 1× one injury stage every second round spent Burning; from 4× two injury stages every round (Engulfed). Ice/cold: 0.5× one PP, movement/freezing or incapacitation at higher tiers **proposed only**. Lightning: 0.5× one PP, action interruption/stun/drop-item outcomes **proposed only**; genuine conductive-metal exception remains but is unquantified. Water: MR erosion without default PP. Air: half-Air contribution / x2 other-element effects / 4× expenditure explosion without default PP. Toxic/hex: 0.5× one PP, poisoning/MP interference **proposed only**. Acid: 0.5× one PP, qualifying AR erosion never below 0; strength and escalation undecided. Do not mistake a proposal for an approved higher-tier consequence.

## November 18, 2026 — Universal elemental status mirror (AUTHOR DIRECTIVE; INDIVIDUAL EFFECTS UNDER REVIEW)

**Scope:** Extend the latest approved Burning/Fire *accumulation framework* to all established eligible elemental energy effects, including ice/cold, lightning/shock, water, air, toxic/hex, and acid/corrosion. These are distinct statuses, not newly approved independent spell lists or new mana colors. Fire remains the calibrated benchmark. The mirror establishes common **delivery, accumulated magnitude, Status Resistance thresholds, three-turn duration, reapplication/refresh and cleansing rules**, not identical physical consequences for every type.

**Shared resolution:** A legal attack or other effect that actually delivers a particular element adds only its eligible delivered elemental Power to that element's existing Magnitude. The target tracks that element's condition for **three turns**. Every further legal matching-element application **adds Magnitude and refreshes the same condition's remaining duration to three turns**, at every severity, just as approved for Burning Edge. Different elements retain separate conditions and magnitudes, barring an expressly approved interaction. Do not import a generic Contact Factor, make an extra attack, or treat mere armor/shield contact as automatic bodily contact. Mundane and magical interventions may cleanse, alter, or intensify a condition when physically and magically appropriate.

**Common severity bands:** Below **0.5 × Status Resistance**, a trace state with no inherent mechanical penalty; from **0.5 × SR**, **one meaningful ongoing self-Pressure Point** (not one additional PP per turn); **above 1 × SR**, the element's principal substantial consequence begins; from **4 × SR**, an intensified consequence. For Fire alone the named thresholds are Smoldering / pressure-producing Smoldering / Burning / Engulfed and the final band doubles ongoing Burning damage. **Do not automatically interpret the >1× threshold as HP damage, or the 4× threshold as double damage, for non-fire elements**. Their physical outcomes must be expressly approved individually. This common skeleton supersedes older unapproved three-rank elemental severity and lightning-stun duration assumptions where conflicting.

**Element-specific review docket (NOT YET APPROVED AS EFFECT MECHANICS):**
- **Ice/cold:** freezing, restricted movement, bodily cold injury or weakening susceptible armor are candidate identities; exact threshold effects, immobilization, protection, thawing and injury remain open. Preserve approved Hearthiron ice vulnerability.
- **Lightning/shock:** interruption, stun/temporary loss of action and dropped items/Trapped-like evasion are older *proposals*, not automatic threshold effects. Lightning can be specially advantaged by a real conductive-metal pathway; quantitative bonus remains open, and real insulation still matters.
- **Water:** wetting, movement/displacement and heightened lightning exposure are candidate interactions, not automatic control or damage. Earlier 5/15 m forced movement values remain unapproved.
- **Air:** force/displacement and staggering are candidate identities; older 10/30 m movement values remain unapproved.
- **Toxic/hex:** poisoning, debilitation, MP/Leywell or magical-protection interference are candidates and require lawful delivery and individual approval. A magical hex is not automatically the same medium as a biological toxin.
- **Acid/corrosion:** **approved identity:** qualifying acid attacks can lower **Armor Rating, never below AR 0**, without inherently reducing END. Its magnitude-to-AR-loss curve, target material, stacking, persistence, restoration, and potential bodily injury require separate approval.

**Unresolved:** The exact Status Resistance equation, calibrated per-tier elemental Power for non-fire effects, any element's >1× and 4× consequences other than fire, status names, tick and expiry timing, cross-element interactions, special environmental magnitudes, cleansing actions, and non-fire specific Resistance application. Do not promote these proposals into finished combat effects without the author's review.

## November 18, 2026 — Status Resistance, Burning Edge and fire injury cadence (LATEST AUTHOR APPROVAL)

**Status Resistance (SR) = (effective END)²**, including the full value of eligible equipment END and the usual effective-attribute modifiers. Elemental Magnitude is now **the total eligible elemental Power actually delivered to and accumulated on a target in an element-specific status condition lasting three turns**, not the previous standalone elemental intensity/contact ratio. Each subsequent lawful hit of that element increases the accumulated magnitude. Do not include ordinary physical weapon Power unless it genuinely delivers elemental energy; preserve shields, insulation, material defenses, actual real-element pathways and specific Resistances. No extra offensive attack, separate Speed-based ignition roll, second END-based severity roll, or universal Contact Factor is created.

**Fire thresholds and injury:** Below **0.5 × SR**, Smoldering has no mechanical effect. At **0.5 × SR** or higher, the target takes one ongoing self-Pressure Point in total. Above **1 × SR**, the target is Burning and takes **one injury stage every second round spent Burning**. At **4 × SR** or higher, the target is Engulfed and takes **two injury stages each round**. Apply these stages through the normal injury and Shimmer Guard progression. The threshold crossing determines whether fire can inflict ongoing injuries. Each tick then applies its stated stages directly, without another attack roll or Impact-to-Defense comparison.

**Duration and environment:** An elemental condition lasts **three turns** unless cleansed earlier. Real **mundane or magical interventions** can cleanse or worsen an active condition: e.g., dousing fire with water extinguishes it, oil can make fire worse, and metal armor can strengthen lightning when a genuine conductive path exists. This is not permission to reinstate the old universal Contact Factor. **Fire refresh:** An eligible Burning Edge hit adds its delivered Fire Power to the existing Fire Magnitude and resets its remaining duration to **three turns**, whether the target is **Smoldering**, **Burning**, or **Engulfed**. Extinguishing clears the condition early. Refreshing the duration preserves the rounds already spent Burning. The exact within-turn tick and expiration boundaries, refresh for other elements, environmental adjustments and cleansing action costs remain open.

Find the approved Tier I–V Fire Power ladder in [Equipment and Economy](09_Equipment_and_Economy.md). **Balance constraint:** Burning Edge tiers I–V should remain equally effective relative to same-level/similarly equipped peers even while absolute elemental Power increases with progression. Tier VI Mana Blade remains the intentionally exceptional, short-ammunition peak option. Its 24 MP per five-round activation and 60-MP Leywell remain approved. The weapon's five-round powered *activation* is distinct from the three-turn *target status*. Tier VI Fire Power remains open.

# Mystaria — Magical Media and Contested Spellcasting

**Status:** Author-approved qualitative framework, November 13, 2026. Quantitative MP/Power formulas and individual technique revisions remain pending. This document clarifies universal philosophy without changing existing SP prices, ranks, prerequisites or Channel Limit equations.

## November 14, 2026 — Turn-scale Channel Limit and prepared magical defense

**Channel Limit is the caster's shared magical throughput for the turn.** On their own turn, the caster allocates capacity between any offensive magic and **at most one specific prepared reactive defensive spell**. Medium recovery, contests and other applicable channeling also draw from the same limit. A prepared defense uses the capacity reserved on that turn, even if activated off-turn. **A caster cannot borrow from their next turn's Channel Limit.** Actual MP expenditure draws from the usual reserve at the spell's normal cost. Refreshing Channel Limit does not restore MP. Existing attribute-based Channel Limit calculations still apply.

**Declare the spell and its narrow trigger on the caster's own turn.** Valid examples are “the next attack against this specified target” or “the next attack by this specified attacker.” A trigger cannot be based on whether an attack would hit or on a generic watch for the first attack that hits or attempts to hit any ally. When the declared event occurs, the caster may activate the prepared spell at a legitimate response opportunity, provided the required physical medium, access and other spell prerequisites are still available. The spell uses normal MP costs. The prepared defense expires at the **beginning of the caster’s next turn**, whether used or unused. Protection beyond that point requires casting a separate spell with sufficient duration or eligible maintenance. Exact response timing and priority, and the interaction with complete spell seizure's prior preparation or next-action forfeiture, remain unresolved.

**Mixed physical and magical action:** A character may combine otherwise legal normal physical actions and magic in one turn, but their **applicable Channel Limit is halved, rounded down** and they may make **no more than one attack total** that turn. This does not grant bonus actions, and it is not permission to stack a physical strike and damaging spell as two independent attacks. Maneuver qualification, timing of commitment to mixed actions, and interactions with specialist rules remain unresolved.

**Mixed-action example:** A Bloomkin may fire a crossbow as their one attack and prepare an eligible magical shield for a nearby Thornguard. “The next attack targeting my knight” is a valid trigger. “The next attack that hits my knight” depends on the outcome and is invalid.

**Example:** A Wakewright with access to suitable liquid water could use a legal spell to place an ice wall on their turn, or prepare a specific defensive spell to raise it when the next attack against a named target triggers it. The wall placed in advance can change the battlefield and can be seen and maneuvered around. Either use requires an eligible technique, sufficient medium and its normal MP cost.

Other illustrative defenses include a pyromancer contesting incoming flame and a Naga using soothing Cipherlight to suppress aggressive intent, subject to successful casting and the target's resistance. These examples grant no new techniques. Each use requires its own approved prerequisites, costs and legal timing.

**Preserve existing specialized exceptions:** Drakyn Wyrmcraft Overchannel and Serpentbreaker intentionally affect Channel Limit; approved Sorcery surcharge and metamagic exceptions remain governed by their own dossiers. Warlock Manacraft uses external mana and its own Handling Capacity rules: a blanket copy of ordinary soul-channel calculations onto Warlock handling is NOT authorized. Approved MP/SP/rank costs, passive item effects, Guard/Double Strike rules and other action restrictions remain in force until directly revised.

## Environmental media are Craft-specific

Every magical Craft requires its own suitable medium, tool, or environmental condition. Greater mastery expands what a practitioner can accomplish, but **does not universally expand access to the medium**. Environmental denial, interference, and removal of required equipment must remain viable tactics even against highly experienced casters.

- **Wakecraft:** Requires accessible **liquid water** to manipulate and maintain water-based constructs. Skills such as Deepdraw allow recovery of liquid from difficult sources, including minute bodily moisture (sweat, urine, and other available fluids), at appropriate cost and with appropriate ability. Water may be frozen or vaporized, but no longer remains a directly manipulable medium in those states. Specific trained techniques may recover vapor as liquid or melt ice to regain control. A spell can require both MP and a volume of water.
- **Pyromancy (future racial Craft):** Requires a qualifying existing flame, as small as a match even for a novice's amplified flame. Individual techniques may demand more existing fire, and may consume it. An identity-defining option is inhaling a great blaze, extinguishing it into smoke, and releasing a concentrated heat projectile. Smoke is specialized ammunition reserved for appropriate learned smoke techniques. Pyromancy's exact skill branches, costs and projectile rules remain undeveloped.
- **Naga Psycraft:** Requires functioning focusing **gems** (not computational Orbs). Uses extremely bright, information-bearing **Cipherlight**, the established canon spelling, which can penetrate some materials and eyelids. More external light does not amplify Psycraft. Blocking eyesight can improve resistance at the expense of situational visual awareness; it is not automatic immunity. Appropriate sensory contact, rather than exclusively open-eye sight, can deliver effects. Target resistance persists. Specific Psycraft rank identities and effects require a separate redesign, especially Psychokinesis; do not silently delete the existing approved catalogue.

Existing matter can remain after a spell: launched water remains water, and propagated flame remains flame while supportable. Spent magical energy becomes depleted and later recycled; matter is not generically consumed with MP. Casting requirements may specify a minimum physical volume of medium; definitions of units and thresholds are pending.

## Magical contests: one Power statistic

When compatible casters contest the same ongoing spell or medium, use **existing effective spell Power**, not a separate Control statistic.

- Challenger Power **below** the original effective spell Power: fails to seize control.
- Challenger Power **at least equal** to original spell Power: **medium seizure**. The opponent's control of that medium ends. The challenger may manipulate or deny that medium according to their own Craft. On the turn they acquire medium control, **they cannot cast an additional spell**.
- Challenger Power **at least twice** the original effective spell Power: **complete spell seizure**. The challenger commands the existing spell as if its caster, within applicable Craft compatibility and without replenishing its remaining duration, resources, or medium. This requires giving up an action on the prior turn to prepare **or** surrendering their next action. Exact reactions/turn sequencing in contested initiative remains to be reconciled with combat rules.

A challenger can commit additional MP to raise the Power of the contest attempt within their ordinary casting/channel constraints. That expenditure is not recoverable for later spells, including when the attempt fails. There is no repeat bidding exchange as a default procedure. Seizing a medium is not free acquisition of the original caster's special technique; a subsequently cast new spell pays its normal activation cost. Winning a psychic duel against a third party does not automatically bypass that person's innate resistance.

### Craft-specific consequences of medium seizure

- **Wakecraft:** Seized water can be moved aside, where it may remain accessible to the original caster, or be frozen/vaporized to deny direct control. The opponent may spend their action and appropriate mana to recover it as liquid if their learned techniques allow. Melting/recovery uses channel capacity in the current turn; exact amount depends on later numerical spell rules. If an active water dragon is completely vaporized, the original maintained spell **ends**; recondensing its water does not automatically revive that spell.
- **Pyromancy:** A pyromancer seizing another's fire can inhale/extinguish it into smoke, which may then serve as ammunition for specialized learned smoke magic. When an active flamethrower loses its viable fire (for example through total immersion), its maintained spell ends.
- **Psycraft:** Ordinary successful contests dispel the opposing psychic effect rather than creating a parallel physical substance to move aside. In a third-party mental contest, a friendly psychic may bolster the target's will and reduce the effective Power of hostile influence, even if the target cannot fully escape domination. The target's resistance still applies. A temporary interruption of the Cipherlight stream need not automatically end an established mental effect; the caster may redirect/resume the stream, but destruction of the required focus ends it. Exact persistence and intervention timing are discipline-specific pending rules.
