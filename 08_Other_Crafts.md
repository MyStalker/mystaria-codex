# Mystaria — Other Racial Crafts and Special Magic

## November 6, 2026 — Kobold Orbcraft direct interfaces (CANON)

Computational **Orbs** process encoded information and **Cipherlight**. **Magic Mirrors** are required to convert visual pictures **into Cipherlight and vice versa**. Both compatible Orbs and Magic Mirrors can issue controls to Kobold machinery. Kobolds capable of Orbcraft can directly interpret and manipulate the stream from an Orb **without** a Mirror showing a picture or text screen; the stream is meaningful to their minds.

The approved **Orb Collar** is primarily an engineering tool, not merely an ornament: a smooth metal neckband with **one central colored orb** and **no visible rings, locks, buckles or clamps**. Manipulating that orb with paw gestures creates a small aurora of dense data. An ungifted person staring straight at that localized display experiences a soft headache, whereas an Orbcrafter perceives rich information far beyond a static image. Technical Kobolds commonly carry or wear an Orb; free Kobolds especially favor Orb **bracers** instead of collars.

Extremely intense **Coilnet Rainbows** can still strain even the gifted, while the old warning against ordinary direct Cipherlight exposure continues to apply to ungifted observers. No exact visual exposure thresholds, Orbcraft skill ranks, SP costs, or new equipment-power costs have been established. The special retinal calibration for **Mindprism** is unchanged. See `18_The_Gilded_Coil_Imperial_Gazetteer.md` and `25_Imperial_Collar_Culture_and_Restraints.md`.

## Concordance — established effects

## 19. Concordance — Shared White-Mana Magic

Concordance is a paired white-mana discipline belonging to the Thornguard and Bloomkin. Thornguard possess large reserves of mana but cannot independently shape or release it; Bloomkin have exceptional shaping precision but lack meaningful reserves. Together they can produce **Aegis Mantle** shields, **Living Bastion** structural reinforcement, **Gentle Restraint** bindings, **Shared Burden** injury redistribution, and **Sacred Sanctuary** settlement defenses. Forced pairings produce weaker, unstable effects. Genuine cooperation, trust, and consent increase reliability and power. The secret **Perfect Promise** is a legendary form of Concordance attainable only when both partners willingly recognize each other as equals.

## Corvyn Fivefold Witchbond — established mechanics

## 20. Corvyn Witchcraft — The Fivefold Witchbond

Witchcraft is the Corvyn's distinctive magical tradition, combining black mana, enchanted tattoos, ritual magic, and spiritual partnerships with Starspawn familiars. **Corvyn can learn spells of all five colors through their familiars, but cannot store foreign mana inside their souls.** Black mana remains their native energy. Shaping red, blue, green, or white mana is otherwise difficult even when they can obtain it.

When a Corvyn forms a **Witchbond**, the Starspawn familiar teaches magical patterns through shared dreams, emotions, impressions, and fragments of Starsong. The witch learns to reproduce those patterns through gestures, rituals, and **Living Ink** tattoo circuits. To cast a foreign-color spell, mana is borrowed directly from the corresponding familiar or a compatible external source. It passes through the witch's enchanted tattoos without being stored within her soul.

| Familiar affinity | Witchcraft taught | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Red | Flames, offensive enchantments, physical empowerment | Cinder Hex: ignites a marked target |
| Blue | Illusions, transformations, sensory manipulation | Mirrorveil: creates a magical illusion |
| Green | Healing, herbalism, regeneration, biological alterations | Witch's Remedy: healing via enchanted herbs |
| White | Protective charms, purification, stabilization | Silver Ward: protects from curses |
| Black | Curses, entropy, sympathetic magic, spirit manipulation | Widow's Kiss: weakening effigy curse |

Golden Nightfoxes are prestigious because their black mana harmonizes with a Corvyn witch's natural gifts and can teach sophisticated hexes, entropy effects, and magical detection. Foreign-color familiars greatly broaden a witch's repertoire but require ongoing companionship and an available mana source. **Fivefold Witches** have learned magic from familiars of all five colors; they are exceedingly rare.

Witchbonds differ from Summoner Accords: they do **not** grant the ability to summon a familiar from the Celestial Realm or invoke its Named Spell. They allow cooperative teaching, emotional awareness, ritual stabilization, and borrowed casting. Their limits include inefficiency with foreign mana, backlash from faulty tattoo circuits, dependence on willing familiars, restricted mana transfer over long distances, and the fact that a familiar can teach only techniques it understands. Familial Starspawn partnerships may continue across mortal generations, making old familiars vital teachers and custodians of forgotten magic.

# PART V — STARSPAWN, FAMILIARS, AND SUMMONERS

## Summoners and Starspawn-linked spellcasting

## 23. Summoners — Keepers of the Celestial Accord

**Classification:** Magical Adventurer Class. **Magical Affinity:** Any of the five mana colors. **Eligible Races:** All magically capable races except Moontouched. **Primary Abilities:** Starspawn summoning, healing, and Named Spell invocation. **Source of Power:** Mana and spiritual agreements called Accords. **Divine Allegiance:** Any goddess, or none.

### Origins and History

Summoners are magical practitioners who establish spiritual partnerships with **Starspawn**, the immortal magical creatures originally created by the gods during the God War against the Serpentari. During the war, divine followers developed methods of coordinating Starspawn armies through magical bonds. These techniques eventually evolved into the modern art of Summoning. Unlike conventional sorcerers, Summoners specialize in channeling their mana through other living beings rather than directly manipulating the environment.

### The Celestial Accord

The foundation of Summoning is the **Accord**, a spiritual agreement connecting a Summoner's soul to a Starspawn's immortal Astral Heart. Accords can be established through several methods:

- **Friendship:** A Starspawn willingly bonds with someone it trusts.
- **Victory:** A defeated Starspawn agrees to serve a worthy opponent.
- **Exchange:** Both parties negotiate a mutually beneficial arrangement.
- **Salvation:** A rescued Starspawn forms a bond through gratitude.
- **Dominion:** Magical coercion forces an unstable, potentially resentful bond.

Every Accord is unique, reflecting the Starspawn's personality and its relationship with the Summoner. Starspawn retain intelligence, memories, and individuality. They understand mortal speech, although most cannot speak themselves, instead communicating through body language and Starsong.

### Summoning Magic

Once an Accord is established, the Summoner can expend mana to transport their Starspawn companion from the Celestial Realm or another location in Mystaria. Summoning costs depend upon the creature's size, power, distance, and the strength of their spiritual connection. Summoners can also transfer mana into companions to accelerate healing, restore stamina, and sustain magical abilities. Their signature technique is the **Named Invocation**. By calling a bonded Starspawn's recognized name and channeling mana through their Accord, a Summoner can activate its unique **Named Spell**. These powerful abilities vary enormously between species, ranging from devastating elemental attacks to protective barriers and restorative effects. Named Spells require substantial mana and may exhaust both participants.

### Relationships with Starspawn

Successful Summoners are often skilled caretakers, negotiators, and companions. Starspawn possess preferences, fears, loyalties, and ambitions. Some appreciate affection and companionship, while others demand challenges, food, or exploration. Trust strengthens an Accord, improving cooperation and magical efficiency. A resentful Starspawn may refuse commands, deliberately misunderstand instructions, or resist its Summoner. Because Starspawn possess immortal Astral Hearts, they can maintain relationships across multiple mortal generations. Unlike Corvyn Witchbonds, which allow familiars to teach spells of their mana affinity, **Accords emphasize summoning, healing, and invoking the Starspawn's own abilities**.

### Strengths and Weaknesses

**Strengths:** Exceptional versatility, access to multiple Starspawn abilities, ranged and defensive options, magical healing, and coordination of several companions.

**Weaknesses:** Heavy mana consumption, dependence on bonded Starspawn, limited simultaneous summons, vulnerability when companions are unavailable, and the difficulty of managing independent personalities. Summoners cannot permanently resurrect destroyed Starspawn bodies. Companions undergoing Celestial Recall must recover before returning. Summoning requires active mana shaping, excluding Moontouched from the profession.

### Their Place in Mystaria

Summoners serve as adventurers, monster handlers, caravan guards, explorers, and military specialists. Their greatest strength is not necessarily personal magical power, but their ability to develop effective partnerships with creatures possessing extraordinary abilities.

*“A beast commanded may fight for you. A companion trusted will choose to stand beside you.”*
