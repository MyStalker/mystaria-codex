# Mystaria — Approved Racial Naming Conventions and Common Namebook

## November 6, 2026 — Bloomkin heroic epithets supplement existing naming (CANON)

Bloomkin commonly use elaborate, self-awarded heroic **presentation titles** in knightly and aristocratic circles, e.g. “the Hope of the Canopy,” without replacing their approved short personal names and craft-derived family names. Such extravagant introductions are colloquial and expressive, **not a separate legal title system or evidence of the feats claimed**. Their Thornguard partnerships and Bloomkin-led **Bannerweave** are detailed in `31_Bloomkin_Bannerweave_Fashion.md`. The previously approved 30 Bloomkin name examples remain unchanged.


**Status:** CANON, explicitly approved by the author November 5, 2026 (all 15 groups and all 450 proposed examples).  
**Authority:** Latest author approvals; `00_Mystaria_Canon_Index.md`, `10_Canon_Changelog.md`, `02_Races.md`, `12_Languages_and_Linguistic_Customs.md`.  
**Scope:** Cultural name construction, example name pools and NPC/character naming guidance; **no new mechanical powers or historical NPCs**.

## How to use this namebook

The 450 approved entries are **reusable name examples**, not a census of 450 established named characters. Family names, dynastic forms, achievements, private names and titles illustrate permitted patterns. They do **not** automatically establish a historical house, actual named noble, specific workshop, new Drider Court, judicial event, family tree, magical true-name rule or social entitlement. Names can be reused, mixed or adapted by a GM; use the approved conventions to construct more. Cultural upbringing, migration, enslavement and individual preference may alter usage. These are cultural patterns, not compulsory racial accents or rigid naming laws. The linguistic reference's Roadspeech, Imperial Coil Speech, Canopy Cant and Red Cant remain operative.

**Approved clarifications:** Naga inherited syllables vary flexibly rather than remaining letter-for-letter fixed; Kobolds have birth names before First Devotion; Corvyn have birth names before any earned Ink Name.

## Canon boundaries

- Firstshaped naming follows the **parent race**, not a new independent racial language or obligatory animal-form epithet.
- Driders remain extremely rare and all female. This name pool does not add 30 Driders to the population or create 15 distinct Verdict institutions.
- Drakyn and Untouched Serpentari ceremonial names have **no established true-name magical vulnerability**.
- A Kobold may earn an achievement name through accomplishment without thereby becoming free; First Devotion does not erase their birth name.
- Corvyn Ink Names and Drakyn hoard titles are earned, not automatic; common names remain valid without one.
- Centaur demographic proportions are unchanged; the examples' 27/3 mare/stallion balance is illustrative.
- No additional language ancestry, skill, SP cost, social rank, or historic event is canonized merely by an illustrative name.
