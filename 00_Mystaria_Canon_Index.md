## Hatchbane evidentiary procedure and Deep Inquiry writ — November 7, 2026

**CANON:** Court of Fangs assesses motive/opportunity/means and authenticates **mirror evidence**; Third/Second/First Degree require preponderance, clear-and-convincing, and beyond-reasonable-doubt proof respectively. Five stages: petition/protection, investigation, hearing, judgment, sentence/review. Exceptional compelled mind/memory examination requires an independently authorized, narrow **Writ of Deep Inquiry**, substantial prior evidence, exhaustion of safer methods and proportionality; free persons retain separate silence and mental privacy rights. See `33_Kharak_Rexfolk_Culture_and_Command.md`. Detailed appeals and slave safeguards remain open.

## Mirror evidence and mental privacy in Imperial courts — November 7, 2026

**CANON:** Mirrors capture sights and sounds, while Orbs store recordings; legal video/audio is called **mirror evidence**, not Orb recordings. Psionic mind reading, Orbcraft memory extraction and Tidekin Mirrorwake may support guilt or innocence but are fallible, and compelled deep probing is dangerous and exceptionally restricted in principle. Free persons have **independent rights to silence and mental privacy**; nonconsensual emotion reading cannot be used against them as evidence or grounds for suspicion. Masters exercise substantial authority over slaves' questioning. Detailed **Writ of Deep Inquiry** safeguards and Hatchbane evidence thresholds remain **proposals**, not canon. See `33_Kharak_Rexfolk_Culture_and_Command.md` and `10_Canon_Changelog.md`.

## Kharak protection of other races' children — November 7, 2026

**CANON:** Acts resembling Hatchbane against children of other species are broadly despised by Kharak as a matter of **informal honor**, without automatically constituting the same formal Kharak offense. This moral development contrasts with early Khans' Extinction campaigns; Vornak formalized a reform movement developing over generations, rather than inventing compassion alone. See `33_Kharak_Rexfolk_Culture_and_Command.md`.

## Hatchbane degrees and Kharak historical Extinction — November 7, 2026

**CANON:** Third-Degree Hatchbane concerns substantial neglect and mistreatment with reparations and improvement; Second Degree concerns substantial injury/kidnapping and culpable failure to protect, ordinarily punished by the permanent painful **Banebrand**; First Degree includes deliberate killing, commissioned assassination and culpably foreseeable lethal kidnapping, with forfeiture of title/Coil or **Decimation**. Ancient adult-offspring life substitution survives only as a nearly unused non-Imperial, non-Arrest tribal blood-law exception; only a true Serpentari may rescind that ancient provision. Earlier Khans practiced **Extinction** of rival bloodlines and some minor species, including children; modern Kharak protection of hatchlings represents imperfect moral reform. Details and boundaries: `33_Kharak_Rexfolk_Culture_and_Command.md`.

## Approved Hero Gem wealth, resale and threat rewards — November 5, 2026

`09_Equipment_and_Economy.md` owns the **approved typical-Hero Gem progression**: target equipment purchase-value `W(L) = 3 + 3997 × ((L−1)/99)^2.3` for levels 1–100, from **3 Gems at level 1** to **4,000 Gems in currently owned equipment at level 100**, with a typical Hero replacing their equipment at each quality tier. **30% of lifetime Gem earnings** is the typical consumable allocation; Gem Shops buy back **their previously sold items at 50% of original price**. The reference full-replacement milestones are levels 10/25/50/75/100; expected resale losses total **~1,511 Gems**, yielding **~7,872 lifetime Gems earned** and **~2,362 spent on consumables** by level 100. **Gem threat modifiers match the unchanged canonical XP modifiers**: Horde ×0.1, Minion ×0.4, Henchman ×1, Elite ×2; Bosses receive a special **two-level Gem-budget award** (without changing the existing two-level XP rule). Reference calculations, limits, and open payout rounding details are in the owning file. This is a typical economic target, not an automatic Gem award or item-use level gate.

## Approved Shimmer XP and encounter threat — November 5, 2026

`01_World_and_Shimmer.md` owns the newly approved **XP to next level = L × (L + 1)** curve, where **L is the current Hero level**. Qualifying non-Boss enemy or equivalent patron-approved quest XP is **2 × encounter level × threat modifier**, rounded to whole XP (minimum 1 XP for a qualifying award). **Horde ×0.1** (suggested enemy level ≤50% of Hero), **Minion ×0.4** (>50–80%), **Henchman ×1** (>80–110%), **Elite ×2** (>110–140%), and **Boss** (usually >140%, or specially designated) awards exactly **two full level advances** from the Hero's current XP progress instead of the ordinary formula. The GM assigns actual threat roles based on encounter capability; relative level bands are guidance. A level-1 Hero needs 2 XP, usually one equal-level Henchman; level 100 needs 10,100 XP, approximately **51 equal-level Henchmen**. Existing patron XP qualification, mundane quest limitations, stat growth and SP costs remain unchanged. No numeric anti-farming penalty or party split is approved.

## Approved Drider anatomy revision — November 4, 2026

Driders have dark skin reminiscent of a drow elf, pointed ears, **two arms**, and **six long spider-like legs** supporting an enormous arachnid lower body. They have **four solid-color eyes with no pupils**; eye color varies between individuals. Their long legs and imposing bodies make them **one of Mystaria's largest mortal races**. `02_Races.md` owns the revised description; `01_World_and_Shimmer.md` removes the obsolete Four-Arm Coordination benefit, and `11_Visual_Style_Guide.md` records the illustration requirements. Earlier four-armed Drider references are superseded. This ruling does not revise Arrest's own anatomy or establish numerical racial stat changes.

## Read this before adjudicating a rule

**Canon precedence:**

1. The user's **latest explicit approval or correction** governs the subject, even when an older master compendium or generated guide disagrees.
2. A dedicated approved canonical source (e.g. Warlock rank manuscript or revised Jester Craft) controls its own details over short summaries.
3. The November 1 `Mystaria_Canon_Compendium.md` supplies background canon **only insofar as no later decision contradicts it**.
4. An assistant's unapproved proposal or recommended formula is **not canon**, even if fully developed or contained in this library.
5. Missing exact mechanics, absent named ranks and unresolved count conflicts must be labeled **UNKNOWN/UNRESOLVED**; never infer them just to make the documents look complete.

**Status key:** **CANON** = explicitly approved and operative; **CANON WITH SOURCE GAPS** = approved but some exact recorded details inaccessible; **UNDER REVISION** = older approved content flagged for potential replacement; **PROPOSAL** = presented, not canon.

## November 6, 2026 — Shield architecture approved, durability damage tests pending

**LATEST USER-APPROVED SHIELD SYSTEM DESIGN:** Shields provide **their own independent defensive capacity**, governed by shield shape, material, craftsmanship and later enchantment; do **not** simply add shield protection to the wearer's body Armor Rating or END². Shields track **three accumulated durability losses before breaking** (names: **Intact 0/3 → Dented 1/3 → Cracked 2/3 → Broken 3/3**; the previously suggested state **Strained** is explicitly replaced by **Dented**). No reduction in protection, obstruction, blocking performance or other shield function at 1/3 or 2/3; **Broken shields are useless until repaired**. Exceptionally powerful attacks may cause multiple durability losses in a single impact; weak attacks below the shield's meaningful defensive threshold should not automatically chip away durability. Shield durability damage, breakthrough/overflow and shield-defense numerical formula still require testing and approval.

**APPROVED SHIELD POSITIONING:** A properly handled, intact shield makes it harder to attack an actually shielded fighter's exposed body regions **other than the shield itself**. Recognized shape roles: **buckler** (quick parries/minimal static coverage), **standard shield** (balanced), **tower shield** (broad coverage, more cumbersome); proposed ordinary baseline shield obstruction **+1 / +1 / +2 six-tier hit-difficulty categories** respectively, only for plausible defensive angles while able to operate the shield. This is not universal body AR and is not automatically additive to other benefits representing the same obstruction. Shield handling has its **own STR/DEX requirements**, not yet numerical. Shield can physically intercept attacks and take its own durability damage; avoid simultaneously claiming all passive, active and evasive benefits for the same movement.

**LATEST AUTHOR-APPROVED EXTREME PRESSURE EXCEPTION:** At **net 3+ meaningful PP (Extreme Pressure)** the defender **cannot actively block/parry with a shield or claim the shield's maneuver-based / active obstruction hit-difficulty benefit**. Shield material remains in the world and physically intercepts attacks that actually strike its existing position. This is a shield-specific limitation and **does not generally equate Extreme Pressure with Trapped, or prohibit other defenses**; **Trapped below Extreme Pressure** may still permit physically credible shield responses. Shields need **their own future enchantments**, likely shared with or closely analogous to armor enchantments; no new rune abilities, prices, Soul Strain or slots approved. ## November 8, 2026 — APPROVED shield overflow: remaining durability absorption

**LATEST USER-APPROVED OVERFLOW FORMULA:** For a shield that successfully intercepts a physical blow, determine durability loss using the ongoing **test baseline** `Shield Impact = Incoming Power / Shield Rating²` with bands below 1 = 0; 1–<2 = 1; 2–<3 = 2; 3+ = 3. Count remaining durability **before** this strike: `R = 3 − existing losses` (Intact 3, Dented 2, Cracked 1). **Only if this blow causes Broken (3 accumulated losses)**, pass `Overflow Power = max(0, Incoming Power − R × Shield Rating²)` toward the defender's **torso**; otherwise overflow is zero. Resolve torso overflow once against effective END² plus penetrated torso AR² and ordinary injury/Shimmer rules. This **supersedes** the previous proposed subtraction of only `Shield Rating²` regardless of previous durability. Dented/Cracked shields still perform identically to Intact shields until Broken; R measures destruction capacity, not a weakened blocking stat. **The numerical shape Shield Ratings 30/50/75, shield durability-loss thresholds, and STR/DEX minimums remain calibration baselines, not final approved equipment statistics.** User also directed playtesting to monitor whether even this shield arithmetic creates an undesirable calculator burden; avoid turning combat into frequent wound-calculator interruptions.
