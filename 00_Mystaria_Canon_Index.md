## Approved Drider anatomy revision — November 4, 2026

Driders have dark skin reminiscent of a drow elf, pointed ears, **two arms**, and **six long spider-like legs** supporting an enormous arachnid lower body. They have **four solid-color eyes with no pupils**; eye color varies between individuals. Their long legs and imposing bodies make them **one of Mystaria's largest mortal races**. `02_Races.md` owns the revised description; `01_World_and_Shimmer.md` removes the obsolete Four-Arm Coordination benefit, and `11_Visual_Style_Guide.md` records the illustration requirements. Earlier four-armed Drider references are superseded. This ruling does not revise Arrest's own anatomy or establish numerical racial stat changes.

## Approved official-art collar convention — November 4, 2026

In official Mystaria art, slave races should **usually wear visible collars**, with exceptions appropriate to the character, narrative context or explicit art direction. `11_Visual_Style_Guide.md` owns this presentation default; it does not make every individual enslaved or change racial mechanics.

## Read this before adjudicating a rule

**Canon precedence:**

1. The user's **latest explicit approval or correction** governs the subject, even when an older master compendium or generated guide disagrees.
2. A dedicated approved canonical source (e.g. Warlock rank manuscript or revised Jester Craft) controls its own details over short summaries.
3. The November 1 `Mystaria_Canon_Compendium.md` supplies background canon **only insofar as no later decision contradicts it**.
4. An assistant's unapproved proposal or recommended formula is **not canon**, even if fully developed or contained in this library.
5. Missing exact mechanics, absent named ranks and unresolved count conflicts must be labeled **UNKNOWN/UNRESOLVED**; never infer them just to make the documents look complete.

**Status key:** **CANON** = explicitly approved and operative; **CANON WITH SOURCE GAPS** = approved but some exact recorded details inaccessible; **UNDER REVISION** = older approved content flagged for potential replacement; **PROPOSAL** = presented, not canon.

## Primary rules that avoid the largest continuity mistakes

- **Drakyn**, not prior placeholder dragon racial names. Dragon elements arise **in the egg**. Their harvesting changes their current mana color and ejects an older pool into ambient mana.
- **Jestercraft is NOT inversion/Recompense.** A sentient speaking Regalia grants six free foundations, physical Stitches and distinct Cut/Frayed states; Pincushion reverses currently Frayed once/day. Both Hemweaving ultimates may be purchased. Hollow Jesters survive as a lore possibility.
- **Warlocks are not ordinary Sorcerers.** Chosen Moontouched manipulate raw external mana and already-shaped spells; their natural mana pool stays at zero. **Current five skill trees are the November 4 replacements**, not the prior November 3 trees; see `04_Warlock_Manacraft.md`.
- **The Centaur race and Celestite are canon, but the eight-discipline clockwork/Socketwright expansion is still a proposal.** Do not silently bill all Centaur masters 200 SP.
- **Black Sorcery alterations were explicitly approved. Red detailed revisions were explicitly approved.** The former Red 26-purchase ledger is **superseded by the approved 25-alteration resolution (90/60 SP)**. Inaccessible Green/White/Blue final selected names remain open documentation problems, not permission to invent changes.
- **Equipment material, enchantment, Gems, MP and Soul Strain are different mechanisms.** Celestite is **outside normal equipment quality** and destroys a wielder's experience/soul per stroke.
- **Old compendium Shimmer growth of 1 attribute point per level is superseded.** Use 2 base points per level plus +1 all five every fifth level.

## November 5, 2026 — Speed, pressure and evasion

The approved Speed design in [`22_Combat_Endurance_and_Shimmer_Guard.md`](22_Combat_Endurance_and_Shimmer_Guard.md) uses **six hit-difficulty categories (Certain / Easy / Average / Hard / Very Hard / Impossible)** based on `(defender effective SPD + 10)/(attacker effective SPD + 10)`, adjudicated in four pressure situations (**Open / Pressured / Trapped / Extreme Pressure**). Evasion is separate from Resistance, does not by itself add attacks, and can fully negate a hit; rarer glances mitigate roughly half the **injury states**. Armor penalizes SPD (rates unresolved), with area denial and coordinated attackers as counters. Extreme mismatches permit **Shimmer Guard projected evasion paths** as a conditional DM tool; exact numerical benefit is unresolved. Previous percentage dodge model **not for normal gameplay**, only rare hidden balance testing. **Raising every racial starting stat by 10 is merely a PROPOSAL**, not canon; retain the existing combat-only +10 and all original magic range/MP formulas.

## November 5, 2026 — Approved racial stat +10 rebase (latest)
