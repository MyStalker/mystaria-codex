# Leshen (Leshy) — Foundational Racial and Economic Canon

**Status: AUTHOR APPROVED November 21, 2026 — foundational identity, ecology, geography and social role only.** The playable-race rules and Leshy Craft will be developed later; this is not a completed mechanical race dossier.

## Identity and body
- **Leshen**, commonly called **Leshy**, are extremely small sapient plant people, approximately **2.5 feet tall**, currently the smallest known Mystarian race.
- They do not eat ordinary meals to sustain themselves: **photosynthesis** supports their nourishment. Detailed needs for light, water, minerals, soil, shelter and managmin nutrition remain OPEN; do not infer immunity to dehydration or starvation in darkness without later approval.
- They have an affinity for **manipulating plants** through a yet-to-be-developed Leshy magical **Craft**. The Craft's name, resources, effects, ranks, SP/MP costs, limits, productivity, and combat application are **OPEN**. Do not invent growth multipliers, crop yields, or botanical production miracles.

## Geography and economics
- Leshy origins and primary homeland lie in the **Canopy Kingdoms**, especially botanical, agricultural and forest communities.
- Leshy horticulture, in cooperation with existing clockwork harvesting, is potentially a significant Canopy agricultural advantage; the exact scale is **unquantified**. Canopy food prices may prove closer to the Imperium's Worldspindle-aided prices than previously expected, **subject to economic calibration**, not a set exchange or price rule.
- A consequential minority of Leshy live, work or are held in **Scarlands** agricultural or alchemical enclaves. The exact population distribution remains **OPEN**.
- Canopy foodstuffs, medicinal herbs, rare plants and botanical knowledge support significant trade with Scarlands demand, including Nymbrin alchemy. The Scarlands should not be treated as incapable of all agriculture: local sheltered production and imports coexist, at varying costs. Specific trade volumes, monopolies and treaties remain OPEN.
- Scarlands Leshy are unusually coveted for botanical cultivation and potentially rare potion ingredients. Enslaved or held Leshy can be especially valuable outside the Canopy, but no slave prices, ranks or universal legal conditions are approved.

## Haze, protection and captivity
- Leshy are **sensitive to ambient Hex/Haze**. It does not automatically or rapidly kill them; it **disrupts or ravages their plant magic more than is typical**. Mechanical thresholds, resilience, exposure and restoration rates remain **OPEN**. Use existing general Haze rules as applicable until an explicit racial exception is defined.
- In the Scarlands, valuable Leshy are frequently kept as secured **treasures in enclosed greenhouses** or botanical facilities, including potentially **subterranean gardens within its extensive cave environments**. They may be concealed and physically restricted. Exact prevalence, ownership law and infrastructure are OPEN.
- Scarlands horticulture can use sheltered greenhouses, underground enclaves and import routes; whether lighting and purity are magically supplied and at what cost remains OPEN.

## Canopy protection orders and political tension
- Small **Thornguard knightly orders** may specialize in protection, escort and recovery of Leshy targeted by abduction, trafficking and exploitation, including pursuit across borders. Certain groups may also seek the return of Leshy who have fled what nobles consider their guardianship. The tension between actual rescue and controlling ownership is intentional.
- Such orders can include genuinely emancipatory protectors and paternalists who consider noble custody necessary. The **specific order names, membership, charters, jurisdictions and treatment of voluntary emigrants remain OPEN**. 'Knights of the Verdant Oath' is an **unapproved working name**, not a confirmed institution.
- Leshy are independent agents with their own communities, traditions and choices. **Their ordinary legal status within the Canopy is not yet decided**; do not assert all Leshy are free, enslaved or automatically noble wards.

## Playability and missing mechanics
- Leshy are approved as a **race intended for eventual player-character support**; no finished racial stats, attribute table, capabilities, progression, Craft ranks, costs, resistances, equipment adaptations, or full cultural dossier are created here.
- Respect the existing race roster and canonical mechanics; new full playable rules need a separate author-approved integration pass.

## November 21, 2026 — Photosynthetic inputs and artificial daylight (AUTHOR APPROVED)

- Leshy **require sunlight/light, water, and soil** to sustain their photosynthetic way of life; their nourishment is **not** resource-free. Their exact amounts, tolerance of darkness, mineral requirements, soil quality, and managmin nutrition remain OPEN.
- Scarlands alchemists **have successfully developed controlled artificial sunlight** for enclosed growing environments, including subterranean greenhouses. These environments can sustain photosynthetic Leshy where ordinary daylight is absent, provided their other needs are met. Exact construction methods, energy costs, mana/Hex effects, and comparative quality of artificial light are OPEN.
- The sun-averse **Vren** particularly favor the practical advantage of protected underground artificial-daylight cultivation. This does not establish that Vren personally tolerate direct exposure to this artificial light, or bypass their canonical sun vulnerability; engineered separation/shielding remains an OPEN practical design question.
- Haze particularly impairs Leshy magic, without implying instant or accelerated unavoidable death. Haze mitigation and greenhouse infrastructure need later calibration.

## November 21, 2026 — Canopy locomotion and urban tree cultivation (AUTHOR DIRECTION)

- Leshy have **long, reaching, flexible vines extending from their bodies**. These permit distinctive locomotion and mobility among the Canopy Kingdoms' enormous treetops, as well as useful grip and strength **beyond what their approximately 2.5-foot stature suggests**.
- The **Canopy Kingdoms' cities and settlements are built among the canopies of massive trees**. Urban Leshy may cultivate, protect and maintain the living trees, decorative greenery and hanging gardens, and employ botanical talents in household service. Their work is not limited to rural agriculture.
- **Mechanical reach, lifting strength, vine count, movement Speed, combat uses, spell area, mana costs and vertical mobility limits remain OPEN** for later race/Craft development. Do not infer a grapple attack, unrestricted flight, or a numeric Strength modifier from this description.

## November 21, 2026 — Equatorial treetop agriculture and proposed cultivation capabilities (AUTHOR DIRECTION)

- The **Canopy Kingdoms are located on Mystaria's equator**. Their agricultural environment supports **year-round growing and cultivation**, rather than a mandatory temperate winter shutdown. **Continuous sunlight is not implied**: rain, clouds, water availability, crop-specific maturation, pests, nutrient cycles, and overlapping harvest schedules remain real considerations.
- Typical Canopy farming operates **in the giant treetops**, not primarily on the dangerous, dark forest floor. Leshy have cultivated and integrated **grains such as wheat, vegetables, and root crops including potatoes into living branches** and associated sunlit growing spaces. **Soil is brought up from the forest floor** to provide growing media where needed. The exact botanical mechanism linking crop roots and host branches, substrate mass, water/nutrient circulation, tree health and structural carrying capacity remain **OPEN**.
- The forest floor is **dark and dangerous**; farming, habitation, transportation and logistical facilities in the canopy reflect that environmental constraint.
- **Design target for eventual Leshy Craft, not finalized mechanics:** Leshy magic can act as a substitute for much conventional fertilizer/soil-quality remediation, permitting crops to grow in poor soil. Exceptionally powerful Leshy might cultivate **watermelons in desert sand**. This is an extreme **author-proposed capability example**, not an unlimited water/nutrient/energy waiver or numeric crop multiplier.
- **Provisional productivity benchmark, not canonized skill math:** one Leshy could plausibly perform **planting and plowing/site preparation** comparable to **at least five conventional workers**, if appropriate for the Craft's final form. Their talents also encompass **pest and blight management**. They have a comparative **weakness in harvesting**, retaining the need for other workers and clockwork harvesters. Do NOT multiply all farm productivity by five or infer that Leshy automatically replace five full-time harvest laborers.
- **Unresolved:** plant fusion's biological constraints, yield ceilings, time/mana requirements, severity/limits of blight removal, pest scope, water needs, soil requirements, seasonal planting cycles, machine adaptation to elevated farms, and harvest bottlenecks. No new playable Craft costs or universal agricultural multipliers approved.

## November 21, 2026 — Living-tree growing medium and direct watering (AUTHOR CLARIFICATION)

- In the Canopy's elevated farms, the **living host tree itself functions as a suitable growing medium for most compatible branch-integrated crops**. It can replace imported soil for those crops, but this does **not** mean every plant can grow without an appropriate medium or that host-tree resources are limitless.
- **Agricultural crops must still be watered directly**; association with a living tree does not automatically satisfy their water demand. Irrigation, collection, pumping, storage, distribution and drought vulnerabilities remain major infrastructure/operating questions.
- Imported forest-floor soil remains useful or necessary for crops that are not compatible with direct tree-supported growth and for specialized agricultural installations. Quantitative crop compatibility, water volumes, root-host nutrient exchange and any harm to host trees remain OPEN.
