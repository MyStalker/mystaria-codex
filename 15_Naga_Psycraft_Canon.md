## Approved source manuscript (tradition renamed Psycraft)

Approved Naga Racial Magic · The Gilded Coil

“The Serpentari ruled through overwhelming power. The Naga were created to rule without needing to lift a finger.”

The Naga possess blue mana as their predominant natural affinity, with a unique psychic magical tradition called Psycraft, sometimes known as the Sovereign Mind.

Unlike the destructive magic of red mana or the curses of black mana, Psycraft is the art of imposing one's will upon the world through thought.

Its practitioners develop three disciplines:

- Psychokinesis — mastery over matter and movement.
- Telepathy — mastery over thought, emotion, and perception.
- Divination — perception of possible futures.

Together, these disciplines make the Naga formidable rulers, investigators, combatants, athletes, and manipulators. They also help explain how a relatively small population of Naga maintains such an enormous Empire.

Most Naga are naturally blue-affined. Rare Naga may possess a different dominant affinity or unusual secondary affinities, preserving the established possibility of individual variation.

However, blue is now the ancestral and culturally defining mana of the Naga.

Their souls are especially suited to perceiving and influencing patterns: patterns of movement, patterns of thought, and patterns of causality.

This is an extension of blue mana's established association with adaptation, transformation, and perception.

### Why the Serpentari Created Psycraft

The Naga were engineered as Imperial overseers long before the modern understanding of magic had been fully systematized.

Their Serpentari creators understood how to wield all five mana colors, but the specialized psychic abilities engineered into the Naga predate many of the classifications and techniques used by modern magical scholars.

Psycraft was designed to solve three problems:

1. Administration: A Naga overseeing hundreds of workers needed to supervise complex operations without physically performing every task.
2. Authority: An overseer needed to understand the emotions, intentions, and potential disloyalty of those under her command.
3. Anticipation: Imperial infrastructure demanded someone capable of recognizing failures before they became catastrophes.

Psychokinesis addressed the first problem, Telepathy the second, and Divination the third.

The Serpentari did not create the Naga to be the strongest conventional spellcasters. They created them to be extraordinarily effective supervisors.

Modern Naga have transformed this inheritance into a sophisticated magical culture.

## II. The Innate Gift — The Sovereign Sense

Every blue-affined Naga possesses some degree of innate psychic sensitivity, even without formal Psycraft training.

This is a free racial magical ability, not a purchased Skill Rank.

At its most basic, the Sovereign Sense permits three minor effects:

The Unseen Touch

Apply a small amount of force to a nearby object. A young Naga might pull a cup closer, turn a page, or close an unlocked door without touching it.

The Felt Heart

Sense the general emotional atmosphere of someone nearby: fear, affection, anger, contentment, or distress. The Naga does not automatically understand the cause.

The Moment's Warning

Occasionally experience a vague premonition immediately before something significant happens. This might manifest as unease, a sudden instinct, or a fleeting impression.

These abilities are subtle and cannot replace trained techniques. Active use consumes blue mana.

A level 1 Naga begins with 7 effective Intelligence and 10 effective Endurance, providing 10 natural MP before equipment Soul Strain.

Naga therefore possess respectable initial mana reserves, although their Intelligence is not inherently as high as that of Bloomkin or Kobolds. Their extraordinary potential comes from dedicated training, unusually long lives, and skilled control.

# III. The Three Disciplines of Psycraft

Each discipline is a broad five-rank Skill. For Heroes, each rank costs 5 Skill Points, meaning full mastery costs 25 SP.

Naga Heroes still begin with zero Skill Points and earn them through divine quests. Ordinary non-Hero Naga develop expertise through conventional training and practice rather than the Shimmer's Skill Point system.

## 1. Psychokinesis — The Unseen Coil

The discipline of force, movement, and physical control

Psychokinesis allows a Naga to exert physical force through blue mana without directly touching an object.

At low proficiency it resembles an invisible hand. At higher levels it becomes a sophisticated method of manipulating momentum, restraining enemies, and reshaping one's surroundings.

The technique does not create unlimited force. Moving heavy objects, accelerating them rapidly, or resisting strong opponents consumes substantially more mana.

### Five Ranks of Psychokinesis

| Rank | Technique            | New capability                                                                                                                                                               |
| ---- | -------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| I    | Invisible Grasp      | Lift, push, pull, carry, or hold objects with deliberate psychic force. Enables practical remote manipulation beyond the Sovereign Sense's tiny movements.                   |
| II   | Phantom Fingers      | Perform intricate manipulation: operating tools, turning locks, threading needles, writing, and handling complicated mechanisms without physical contact.                    |
| III  | Kinetic Weave        | Shape invisible force into temporary surfaces, restraints, and barriers. A Naga can catch falling objects, bind an opponent, or deflect physical attacks.                    |
| IV   | Momentum Sovereignty | Seize and redirect existing movement. Stop a charging opponent, reverse a projectile's course, or redirect the momentum of a collapsing object.                              |
| V    | Imperial Burden      | Exert immense, precisely directed forces upon large physical structures. Lift heavy architectural components, deform fortifications, or suspend massive objects temporarily. |

### Everyday Applications

Psychokinesis is deeply integrated into Naga civilization.

A Naga architect may direct heavy building materials while Kobold machinery performs the actual construction.

A wealthy Matriarch might conduct household business without ever picking up a pen or opening a door herself.

Naga musicians can manipulate unusual instruments through invisible force, creating performances in which strings, bells, and floating objects move without visible contact.

This ability is particularly significant for Firstshaped Naga, who possess no hands or arms whatsoever.

An accomplished Firstshaped Naga can operate tools psychokinetically without changing her anatomy. She still has no physical hands, and her abilities cease to function when her magic is suppressed.

### Combat Potential

Psychokinetic specialists are formidable battlefield controllers.

They can knock opponents from elevated positions, seize their weapons, immobilize vulnerable enemies, and redirect projectiles.

A highly trained Naga could even fight while reclining comfortably, manipulating weapons and the battlefield from a protected position.

However, telekinetically moving a weapon is not inherently more powerful than swinging it physically. The technique is governed by Intelligence, available mana, concentration, and the resistance of the target.

At the highest levels, Psychokinesis becomes capable of spectacular feats, although continuous large-scale manipulation is prohibitively expensive without considerable mana support.

Defining strength: Physical control and extraordinary utility.

## 2. Telepathy — The Inner Coil

The discipline of emotion, communication, enchantment, and mental authority

Telepathy is the most culturally influential Psycraft discipline.

It allows a Naga to perceive aspects of another mind and eventually influence how that mind interprets the world.

At lower levels, Telepathy makes Naga extraordinarily perceptive. At higher levels, it becomes one of Mystaria's most sophisticated forms of mental enchantment.

Telepathy is not automatically perfect mind reading. The depth of access depends on proficiency, resistance, and the nature of the connection.

### Five Ranks of Telepathy

| Rank | Technique            | New capability                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| ---- | -------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| I    | Heartreading         | Discern specific emotions, emotional contradictions, and changes in another creature's mental state. Recognize fear beneath politeness, hidden excitement, or growing resentment.                                |
| II   | Whispering Mind      | Transmit thoughts, images, emotional impressions, and simple messages directly between minds. Mutual communication is possible with a receptive participant.                                                     |
| III  | Velvet Enchantment   | Gently influence another person's emotional state or perceptions, encouraging calm, trust, uncertainty, distraction, or apprehension. Can also perceive exposed surface thoughts through a contested connection. |
| IV   | Sovereign Suggestion | Introduce a compelling suggestion into another mind, making a particular choice or action seem unusually natural or desirable. Strongly conflicting suggestions are resisted more effectively.                   |
| V    | The Perfect Crown    | Establish deep temporary control over a successfully subdued mind, directing its perceptions, decisions, and deliberate actions while the connection is maintained.                                              |

### The Different Forms of Mental Influence

Telepathy has several distinct applications.

Empathy allows a Naga to understand how someone feels without requiring their cooperation.

Communication allows willing individuals to exchange thoughts, images, and intentions without speaking.

Enchantment changes the emotional or perceptual conditions influencing a decision.

Suggestion attempts to redirect a person's choices without necessarily overwhelming their personality.

Domination attempts direct control over decisions and conscious behavior.

These forms of influence are not equally difficult.

Reading obvious fear from a nervous visitor is considerably easier than convincing a loyal bodyguard to betray someone they love.

### Limits on Mental Control

Telepathy must not make the Naga automatically unbeatable.

- Intelligence and trained mental discipline influence resistance.
- Strong emotions and deeply held beliefs are difficult to override.
- A hostile target can recognize and resist a sufficiently intrusive connection.
- Sustained mental domination requires concentration and continuous mana expenditure.
- A dominated individual retains their underlying identity and may remember fragments of the experience.
- Telepathy cannot permanently rewrite a soul or guarantee lifelong obedience.

Even The Perfect Crown represents temporary control, not automatic ownership of another person's mind.

The most dangerous Grandmasters may overcome lesser minds with frightening ease, but an equally formidable opponent remains a serious challenge.

### Telepathy and Starspawn

Naga can sense the emotions of sufficiently accessible Starspawn minds.

Higher-ranking telepaths may exchange imagery or simple concepts with receptive Starspawn, although mental structures vary considerably between species.

This does not mean every Starspawn can speak mortal languages. Nightfoxes, for example, remain incapable of ordinary speech; sharing an emotional impression or image is not the same as speaking a sentence.

Telepathic interaction can also be complicated by the unique nature of Astral Hearts.

### The Social Consequences

Telepathy creates an unusually complicated culture.

Naga aristocrats consider emotional perceptiveness an essential accomplishment.

A refined Matriarch is expected to notice her guests' discomfort without requiring them to say anything.

Courtship may involve delicate exchanges of emotion and mental imagery.

Among lovers or trusted companions, voluntary telepathic communication is considered profoundly intimate.

But the same gift has darker applications.

A powerful Naga might soothe a distressed slave without addressing the conditions causing that distress.

Another might subtly encourage loyalty during disciplinary proceedings.

A particularly unscrupulous Matriarch could use Sovereign Suggestion to influence political rivals, witnesses, or servants.

The Empire's sophisticated courts consequently face a difficult question:

How can consent be established when one party possesses the ability to influence another party's desires?

Defining strength: Social influence, communication, investigation, and mental control.

## 3. Divination — The Forked Eye

The discipline of possibilities, anticipation, and uncertain futures

Divination is the rarest and least understood of the three disciplines.

Unlike Psychokinesis and Telepathy, it does not simply manipulate something currently present.

Instead, Divination allows a Naga to perceive probable consequences of existing circumstances.

Its practitioners describe the future as a branching network of possibilities.

Most paths disappear as circumstances change. Others become increasingly likely until they are almost inevitable.

A diviner cannot simply choose which future will occur.

However, by understanding the available possibilities, she may influence events toward a desired outcome.

### Five Ranks of Divination

| Rank | Technique            | New capability                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| ---- | -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| I    | Premonition          | Receive brief impressions of immediate danger, impending movement, or likely events in the next few moments.                                                                                                                               |
| II   | Forked Choice        | Examine a small number of probable immediate consequences of a contemplated action, such as taking one route instead of another.                                                                                                           |
| III  | Unfolding Moment     | Observe a likely sequence of events several minutes into the future, particularly when focused on a familiar situation or specific opponent.                                                                                               |
| IV   | Threads of Tomorrow  | Study the probable consequences of a particular plan or event over hours or days, identifying dangerous turning points and opportunities to intervene.                                                                                     |
| V    | The Thousand Futures | Perceive several competing futures surrounding a significant event and identify actions most likely to alter its outcome. Particularly elaborate readings may extend days or occasionally weeks ahead, with rapidly declining reliability. |

### Divination Is Not Certainty

The discipline has several important restrictions.

The future changes when people make decisions. Seeing a possible outcome often changes the decisions that lead to it.

More participants create greater uncertainty. Predicting a single person's next movement is easier than predicting an entire battle.

Unfamiliar information limits accuracy. A diviner cannot reliably predict decisions involving events, people, or information she has no meaningful connection to.

Unusual magical interference can distort predictions. Powerful Starspawn, rival diviners, divine intervention, and unpredictable magical events create especially unstable outcomes.

Longer predictions are less reliable. A skilled diviner may accurately anticipate an attack one second ahead but struggle to predict who will rule the Empire next year.

Divination therefore deals in possibilities rather than destiny.

It also does not grant the ability to create or travel through Surrender's temporary alternative timelines. Those are distinct divine phenomena.

### Practical Applications

A Naga duelist might glimpse her opponent's next attack.

A merchant could identify which trade agreement is most likely to collapse.

A military commander might recognize that an apparently favorable engagement conceals a dangerous weakness.

A Kobold engineer working alongside a diviner could investigate an unstable Worldspindle, using predictive insights to identify which component is likely to fail next.

The strongest diviners become indispensable political advisers.

However, Naga aristocrats have learned that diviners sometimes disagree violently about what will happen.

Two experts may perceive different futures because they are considering different decisions.

Neither is necessarily lying.

Defining strength: Anticipation, strategic planning, survival, and recognizing opportunities.

## IV. Comparing the Three Disciplines

| Discipline    | Everyday usefulness                     | Combat specialty                  | Greatest weakness                                           |
| ------------- | --------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- |
| Psychokinesis | Tool use, construction, transport       | Physical control and defense      | Heavy objects and sustained force consume considerable mana |
| Telepathy     | Communication, diplomacy, investigation | Confusion, suggestion, domination | Strong-willed minds and protective enchantments             |
| Divination    | Planning, risk assessment, prediction   | Evasion and anticipation          | Uncertainty and changing circumstances                      |

Each discipline can support a successful Hero without requiring mastery of the others.

A Psychokinesis specialist can become a formidable warrior or adventurer. A Telepathy specialist can accomplish remarkable things through negotiation and enchantment. A Divination specialist can survive danger and solve problems through superior foresight.

Combining the disciplines provides exceptional versatility.

For example, a telepathic diviner can recognize that someone is about to betray her, anticipate how they intend to act, and influence the decision before it occurs.

A psychokinetic diviner can predict an incoming projectile and redirect it.

A psychokinetic telepath could coordinate a construction team through mental communication while personally adjusting delicate machinery.

# V. Psycraft in the Gilded Coil

Psycraft fundamentally shapes Naga society rather than functioning merely as a set of combat powers.

## The Three Great Academies

Three prestigious schools are associated with different Imperial cities.

The Academy of Invisible Hands — Kheth-Vael

Trains psychokinetic engineers, artisans, and competitive athletes.

Its most famous students demonstrate precise manipulation of extremely delicate mechanisms or perform elaborate physical feats without touching their surroundings.

The Chamber of Quiet Crowns — Sathryx

Specializes in Telepathy, diplomacy, investigation, and mental defenses.

Its graduates are highly sought after as political advisers, judicial examiners, and household governors.

The Observatory of Forked Stars — Velmora

Trains diviners who specialize in environmental forecasting, strategic planning, and anticipating magical infrastructure failures.

The Observatory's predictions are closely monitored by the Conclave of Coils.

## Psycraft and Manatech

Kobold engineering and Naga psychic abilities are complementary, not interchangeable.

A Naga psychokinetic can manipulate a delicate mechanism, but a Kobold artificer understands how to build and repair it.

A Naga telepath can communicate silently with a nearby companion, but the Coilnet can transmit messages between distant cities.

A Naga diviner can anticipate a possible mechanical failure, but a Grand Orb can calculate thousands of complex variables and preserve accurate technical records.

Neither population can easily replace the other.

Some Kobold Workshops even produce specialized Crown Mirrors, magical interfaces that allow Naga psychics to interact directly with computational Orbs through mental commands.

A sufficiently skilled Naga might study an animated architectural diagram, mentally manipulate its structure, and communicate modifications to a Kobold engineer without uttering a word.

This makes Imperial administration extraordinarily efficient.

It also gives aristocratic Naga an additional reason to value Kobold technical expertise.

## Psycraft and Slavery

Psychic magic helps explain how the Naga maintain control despite being vastly outnumbered.

A Matriarch who can sense emotional distress may identify unrest before it develops into open rebellion.

Telepathic communication makes private instructions and covert coordination easier.

Psychokinetic restraint can help capture dangerous individuals without requiring a large physical confrontation.

Diviners can identify likely vulnerabilities in security arrangements.

However, Psycraft does not allow a handful of Naga to effortlessly dominate millions of minds.

Telepathy has finite range, mana costs, resistance, and concentration limits. Most Naga cannot maintain powerful control over even one resistant individual indefinitely.

The Empire still depends on its laws, Scale Register, physical restraints, economic structures, professional security forces, and relationships of genuine loyalty.

The psychic abilities of its rulers reinforce these institutions; they do not replace them.

This creates a particularly important social distinction.

A Naga may sincerely care about her enslaved companions and use Telepathy to make them feel comfortable, yet never consider allowing them to leave.

Among the Unbound Daughters, some reformers specifically condemn the use of emotional enchantments to manufacture obedience.

They argue that kindness produced by a psychic command is not genuine affection, and that a person's apparent contentment cannot be trusted when their emotions have been deliberately manipulated.

Reading an opponent's surface thoughts could be considered a legitimate tactic in one competition but cheating in another.

This gives the Mirrorwardens an entirely new category of sporting crimes to investigate.

## VII. Three Defining Weaknesses

Psycraft has three universal limitations that prevent it from making other magical traditions irrelevant.

First, the mind must maintain control. Complex Psycraft requires concentration. Severe pain, emotional shock, fatigue, and competing demands can interfere with a technique.

Second, power requires mana. Even the most talented Naga remains limited by their available blue mana. Their natural MP equals effective Endurance before equipment strain, and follows the established Short Rest and Long Rest regeneration rules.

Third, psychic authority is contestable. Other minds can resist Telepathy, physical forces can overcome Psychokinesis, and unforeseen decisions can invalidate Divination. Higher Intelligence improves precision and success, but does not guarantee victory.

These constraints are especially important for Naga Heroes.

Their initial attributes are respectable rather than overwhelming. A Naga must develop her talents through investment, training, and experience to reach the extraordinary heights of Psycraft.

## VIII. What Psycraft Adds to Naga Culture

Psycraft gives the Naga several traits that distinguish their magical culture.

They become a culture where physical stillness is not necessarily passivity, where a silent conversation may convey more than a spoken argument, and where political leaders are expected to anticipate problems before those problems occur.

A Naga Matriarch might entertain guests while simultaneously rearranging objects throughout her chamber, sensing their emotional responses, and considering three possible outcomes of the conversation.

None of these activities requires her to stand up.

To foreign visitors, this can make Naga aristocrats seem impossibly composed.

To the Naga themselves, it is simply good manners.

Their society also has distinctive taboos: intrusive emotional probing during courtship, unauthorized reading of private thoughts, psychic tampering with testimony, and attempts to influence another Matriarch's decisions could all provoke serious scandal.

The deepest irony is that Naga culture celebrates absolute mastery over the self, yet its most sophisticated magical discipline is devoted to influencing the minds of others.

The Serpentari gave the Naga psychic power to oversee an Empire. The Naga turned that gift into an entire philosophy of civilization.
