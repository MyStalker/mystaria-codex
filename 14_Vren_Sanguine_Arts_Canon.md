“The Naga command your mind. The Tidekin command the water around you. We command something far more intimate, darling. The blood that keeps you alive.”

Vren magic should feel predatory, elegant, supernatural, and occasionally horrifying. Unlike the Naga's Psycraft or the Tidekin's Wakecraft, the Vren do not develop a single magical technique into increasingly sophisticated applications.

Instead, their magic consists of six independent Disciplines, each representing a different expression of their vampiric nature.

A Vren may become a terrifyingly fast duelist, a nocturnal shapeshifter, a master of blood contracts, or a berserker who can tear through armored soldiers.

Their collective magical tradition is known as the Sanguine Arts, while its six branches are formally called Disciplines of the Night.

## I. The Six Disciplines


1\. Blood Magic — The Crimson Art

Grants a blood-manipulation cantrip

Manipulates blood as a physical substance, weapon, magical reagent, and instrument of curses.


2\. Bloodbond — The Covenant Art

Grants a blood-connection cantrip

Establishes supernatural connections through exchanged blood, enabling communication, contracts, compulsions, and control.


3\. Nightshape — The Borrowed Beast

Grants a minor shapeshifting cantrip

Allows Vren to assume the forms of nocturnal predators, from small bats and wolves to the physical likenesses of formidable Starspawn.


4\. Umbracraft — The Sovereignty of Shadow

Grants a darkness-manipulation cantrip

Manipulates shadow, conceals movement, creates tangible darkness, and eventually turns the battlefield into a kingdom of night.


5\. Beastheart — The Unleashed Hunger

+5 effective Strength per rank

Enhances physical power and unlocks increasingly dangerous states of supernatural fury, exchanging self-control for ferocity.


6\. Celerity — The Stolen Moment

+5 effective Speed per rank

Grants extraordinary physical acceleration, supernatural reflexes, and eventually the ability to perform an action instantaneously.

The approved structure has four cantrip-granting Disciplines and two passive-stat Disciplines. This keeps permanent attribute bonuses confined to two disciplines.

Every Discipline has five ranks, costing 5 Skill Points per rank, or 25 SP to master.

## II. Blood Boon — The Awakening Blood

Innate Vren Ability · Free · Scales with Hero Level

Blood Boon temporarily enhances a Vren's physique, reflexes, stamina, and mental focus.

Unlike Beastheart or Celerity, Blood Boon does not directly increase Shimmer attributes. Instead, it improves how effectively the Vren can use their existing physical capabilities.

Its strength and MP cost increase with the Vren's character level.

### Level Scaling

**Authoritative activation cost:** `ceil(Hero Level / 25)` MP, subject to the normal Channel Limit.

| Hero level | Activation cost | Blood Boon development |
|---|---:|---|
| 1–24 | 1 MP | Sharper reflexes, stronger muscular coordination, improved physical effort and concentration |
| 25 | **1 MP** | Greater resistance to fatigue, physical distractions, and disrupted concentration begins |
| 26–39 | 2 MP | Greater resistance to fatigue, physical distractions, and disrupted concentration |
| 40–49 | 2 MP | Blood Boon can now be shared through established Bloodbonds |
| 50 | **2 MP** | Enhanced muscular efficiency, sustained exertion, and stability under injury or physical pressure begins |
| 51–74 | 3 MP | Enhanced muscular efficiency, sustained exertion, and stability under injury or physical pressure |
| 75 | **3 MP** | Extraordinary coordination of physical movement and mental concentration, even during chaotic combat, begins |
| 76–99 | 4 MP | Extraordinary coordination of physical movement and mental concentration, even during chaotic combat |
| 100 | **4 MP** | Exceptional physical efficiency and predatory focus, with further improvement as the Hero gains levels, begins |
| 101+ | `ceil(level / 25)` MP (5 MP at levels 101–125) | Exceptional physical efficiency and predatory focus; continuing improvement as the Hero gains levels |

**Approved clarification — November 4, 2026:** The formula governs activation costs at every level, including exact multiples of 25. Cost bands are 1–25 = 1 MP, 26–50 = 2 MP, 51–75 = 3 MP, 76–100 = 4 MP, and so on. Developmental milestones at levels 25, 40, 50, 75, and 100 remain unchanged. The original revision's cost ranges were inconsistent with the formula and are superseded **only as to their MP values**.

### Duration and Restrictions

Blood Boon lasts one minute, requiring no additional upkeep during that minute.

It cannot stack with itself, but it can operate alongside other Disciplines. A Vren might activate Blood Boon before entering Beastheart's rage or using Celerity to pursue an opponent.

Blood Boon does not eliminate sunlight vulnerability, replace the need to consume blood, or automatically heal injuries.

### Level 40 — The Shared Boon

At level 40, a Vren learns to extend Blood Boon through an existing supernatural Bloodbond.

Crimson Communion

A Vren may temporarily grant Blood Boon's benefits to another creature connected to them through Bloodbond.

The recipient does not need to be a Vren or possess black mana.

The granting Vren supplies the MP required to activate the borrowed boon, paying the normal Blood Boon cost separately for each recipient.

The shared effect lasts up to one minute, or until the sustaining Bloodbond is severed.

Each granted boon is a separate activation, respecting the Vren's Channel Limit. It is not necessary for the recipient to generate or store mana.

A Vren must possess access to an established Bloodbond to use this new application. Bloodbond Rank I is sufficient; more advanced bonds may be better suited to maintaining connections over distance.

A Vren might share heightened reflexes with a bodyguard, improve a companion's endurance during a dangerous escape, or sharpen a trusted servant's focus during delicate work.

This gives Bloodbond additional value beyond coercion and contractual obligations.


**Additional interpretation:** Blood Boon improves responsiveness, exertion, stamina and concentration without changing Shimmer attribute totals, granting regeneration, negating sunlight, or removing blood dependence. It requires functional circulation and available MP. The prior flat **2 MP** activation at all levels is superseded by the level-based formula above.

## III. The Six Disciplines — Five Ranks Each

## 1. Blood Magic — The Crimson Art

Blood manipulation · Curses · Blood weapons · Predatory feeding

Blood Magic is the oldest and most recognizable of the Vren Disciplines.

It manipulates blood itself, using black mana to control its movement, physical structure, and supernatural connections.

Where Tidekin manipulate water as a substance, Blood Magicians manipulate blood as both a substance and a vessel of living identity.

### Cantrip — Crimson Thread

Manipulate tiny quantities of exposed blood without spending MP.

A Vren can move droplets across a surface, identify their own blood, separate a small bloodstain from water, or form decorative patterns using a trace of blood.

The cantrip cannot cause bleeding, harm living tissue, or manipulate blood inside another person's body.

### Five Ranks (current canon)

| Rank | Technique            | Ability                                                                                                                                              |
| ---- | -------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| I    | Bloodweaving         | Manipulate exposed blood into streams, threads, projectiles, and grasping tendrils.                                                                  |
| II   | Crimson Forging      | Solidify blood into temporary weapons, armor, implements, and barriers.                                                                              |
| III  | The Red Thirst       | Extract blood from exposed wounds or accessible vessels, drawing it toward the Vren for feeding or manipulation.                                     |
| IV   | Blood Temper         | Superheat blood until it boils, or rapidly chill it toward freezing.                                                                                 |
| V    | Sovereignty of Veins | Directly manipulate circulating blood inside a living creature, controlling its flow, pressure, and movement through a contested magical connection. |

### Rank IV — Blood Temper


Blood Temper allows a Vren to manipulate the thermal behavior of blood through black-mana disruption of its physical stability.

Boiling Blood rapidly agitates blood, producing intense heat and internal injury.

Cold Blood drains heat from blood, making it sluggish and potentially causing freezing, stiffness, and circulatory failure.

Exposed blood is easier to manipulate, but the technique can affect blood inside living creatures through a successful magical contest.

A weak opponent may suffer catastrophic injury. A stronger opponent can resist the effect, limiting its severity or forcing the Vren to maintain the connection.

Blood Temper also has practical uses. A Vren might preserve collected blood by chilling it, sterilize blood-contaminated equipment through controlled heating, or create frozen blood weapons.

This is not general-purpose red-mana pyromancy or blue-mana cryomancy. The temperature manipulation applies specifically to blood.

Rank V remains more dangerous because it grants comprehensive control over circulation and internal blood movement rather than only temperature.

### Grandmaster Potential

A Grandmaster Blood Magician becomes an exceptionally dangerous opponent even without other Disciplines.

They can forge weapons from their own blood, attack through exposed wounds, temper blood through precise heat and cold effects, and eventually manipulate an opponent's circulation.

However, Sovereignty of Veins is not an automatic death sentence. A healthy, resisting opponent can fight the connection through magical defenses, Endurance, and resistance. Powerful internal manipulation requires substantial mana and sustained control.

Blood Magic also does not grant green-mana healing. Coagulating a wound can temporarily stop bleeding, but it does not regenerate damaged tissue.

Vren careers: Assassins, battlefield specialists, cursewrights, blood alchemists, physicians specializing in blood analysis, and Blood Court executioners.

## 2. Bloodbond — The Covenant Art

Blood connections · Ritual promises · Mental influence · Long-term obligations

Bloodbond is the Vren equivalent of a mentalist tradition, but it operates very differently from Naga Telepathy.

A Naga Psychic manipulates thoughts, emotions, and perceptions directly.

A Vren Bloodbinder establishes a supernatural connection through blood, then influences a person's body and decisions through that connection.

Bloodbinders generally cannot read minds or sense emotions. Instead, they understand a person's physical state, intentions expressed through bodily action, and the obligations established through blood.

### Cantrip — Pulse Echo (revised; 0 MP)

Pulse Echo now detects blooded creatures regardless of whether they consent.

The Vren instinctively perceives nearby heartbeats, circulation, and basic physiological rhythms through black-mana resonance.

This does not require an established Bloodbond.

A Vren could detect a frightened intruder hiding behind a curtain, recognize an injured person's irregular heartbeat, or notice a second living presence in a seemingly empty room.

Its limitations remain important:

- Pulse Echo senses physiology, not emotions or thoughts.
- Physical obstacles, magical wards, and unusual anatomy can interfere with detection.
- It does not automatically locate every living creature or reveal its identity.
- It cannot read the heartbeat of a creature that has no functional blood circulation.

Established Bloodbonds allow more sophisticated and longer-lasting awareness than the basic cantrip.

### Five Ranks

| Rank | Technique        | New capability                                                                                                                                                               |
| ---- | ---------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| I    | Blood Compact    | Establish a short-term connection through exchanged blood, allowing participants to sense each other's presence and physical condition.                                      |
| II   | Shared Pulse     | Transmit simple intentions, warnings, physical sensations, or coordinated reflex cues through an established bond without speaking.                                          |
| III  | Vowscar          | Create enduring blood oaths that recognize agreed obligations, reveal breaches, and activate mutually established magical consequences.                                      |
| IV   | The Red Leash    | Interfere with a connected target's bodily impulses and motor responses, forcing hesitation, movement, restraint, or other limited physical actions through a resisted bond. |
| V    | Crimson Covenant | Establish an exceptionally powerful, enduring bond capable of imposing conditional magical compulsions and enforcing complex obligations over long periods.                  |

### Short-Term and Long-Term Bonds

Bloodbond creates two broad types of relationship.

Short bonds are temporary connections used by companions, soldiers, lovers, and conspirators.

A pair of bonded Vren might coordinate an ambush without communicating verbally. Another pair might use Shared Pulse to recognize when one has been injured.

Long bonds are durable magical relationships often formalized through Blood Court contracts.

They are frequently used for marriages, alliances, political service, debt agreements, and ownership arrangements.

A Vren noble might promise a subordinate protection in exchange for a decade of service, establishing a blood oath that magically recognizes violations of the agreement.

Such contracts are not inherently fair.

At Rank IV, Bloodbinders also acquire techniques capable of forcibly interfering with an unwilling person's body. These require an accessible blood connection and a successful contest against resistance.

At Rank V, especially powerful Bloodbinders can attempt coercive long-term bonds. These are curses of obligation rather than legitimate consensual agreements, and can be resisted, challenged, or broken through sufficiently powerful magic.

Baphomet is particularly fascinated by the distinction between a promise freely given and a promise extracted under pressure.

### The Blood Courts

Bloodbond becomes a central institution of Vren civilization.

A respected Bloodbinder may have tremendous political influence without possessing extraordinary physical strength.

Blood Court officials are particularly concerned with contract authenticity, magical coercion, and the interpretation of deliberately ambiguous agreements.

Vren careers: Diplomats, contract enforcers, investigators, military coordinators, negotiators, household governors, and political manipulators.

## 3. Nightshape — The Borrowed Beast

Shapeshifting · Nocturnal predators · Infiltration · Starspawn mimicry

Nightshape allows Vren to abandon their humanoid bodies and assume predatory forms.

These forms draw inspiration from bats, wolves, and the many nocturnal Starspawn inhabiting Mystaria.

Since Starspawn constitute Mystaria's wildlife, wolf and bat shapes are understood as predatory forms and ancestral body patterns, not evidence of separate populations of ordinary mundane wolves or bats.

### Cantrip — Borrowed Features

Make minor, harmless changes to physical appearance without spending MP.

A Vren might alter their pupils, subtly reshape their ears, change their fur pattern, or temporarily accentuate their fangs.

The changes are superficial and cannot provide meaningful combat advantages.

### Five Ranks

| Rank | Technique            | New capability                                                                                                                                                              |
| ---- | -------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| I    | Little Bat           | Transform into a much smaller bat-shaped creature, capable of flight, squeezing through openings, and avoiding attention.                                                   |
| II   | Wolfskin             | Transform into a wolf-shaped quadrupedal predator, gaining the locomotion, senses, and natural weapons appropriate to that form.                                            |
| III  | Chimeric Shape       | Selectively transform portions of the body, combining useful traits without abandoning the humanoid form.                                                                   |
| IV   | Astral Masquerade    | Assume the physical likeness of a personally studied Starspawn, reproducing appropriate nonmagical anatomy and movement.                                                    |
| V    | The Perfect Predator | Assume exceptionally powerful predatory forms, transform seamlessly between learned shapes during movement, and exercise precise control over the transformation's anatomy. |

### Becoming a Starspawn

At Rank IV, a Vren might imitate a Nightfox.

They could reproduce its black fur, physical proportions, foxlike movement, and golden markings if they had studied an appropriate specimen.

However, they would not acquire an Astral Heart, Celestial Recall, Moonless Star, or the ability to communicate through Starsong.

The transformation copies physical anatomy, not the creature's soul or unique magical abilities.

At Rank V, a Nightshaper can assume larger and more elaborate forms, including formidable predatory bodies.

However, the transformation remains subject to mana expenditure, available physical space, and the limits of the Vren's control. It is not unrestricted size-changing.

### A Particularly Vren Application

Vren nobles sometimes use Nightshape during courtship.

A courting pair might fly through moonlit gardens as little bats or hunt together in wolf-shaped bodies.

Naturally, certain aristocrats insist that selecting the correct Starspawn form for a romantic encounter is an important expression of cultural refinement.

Vren careers: Scouts, infiltrators, spies, assassins, trackers, hunters, and explorers.

## 4. Umbracraft — The Sovereignty of Shadow

Darkness manipulation · Concealment · Shadow constructs · Nocturnal territory

Umbracraft is the manipulation of darkness through black mana.

Where Blood Magic controls a physical substance, Umbracraft manipulates the absence of light as a supernatural medium.

At higher ranks, darkness becomes something the Vren can shape, traverse, and temporarily give physical form.

### Cantrip — Shadeplay

Manipulate tiny shadows without spending MP.

A Vren might make a candle's shadow dance, deepen the shade beneath their fingers, or produce decorative silhouettes.

Shadeplay cannot conceal a person or extinguish meaningful illumination.

### Five Ranks

| Rank | Technique         | New capability                                                                                                                      |
| ---- | ----------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| I    | Veil of Gloam     | Manipulate existing shadows to obscure the Vren's silhouette, movement, and appearance, improving concealment in dim environments.  |
| II   | Light Hunger      | Create a localized region of magical darkness that suppresses ordinary illumination and obstructs sight.                            |
| III  | Shadowforge       | Condense darkness into temporary physical constructs such as grasping tendrils, blades, barriers, and restraints.                   |
| IV   | Through the Seam  | Travel rapidly between connected areas of darkness, passing through shadows as though they form a hidden pathway.                   |
| V    | Court of Midnight | Establish a sustained territory of supernatural darkness whose shadows can be shaped into defenses, prisons, weapons, and passages. |

### The Court of Midnight

A Grandmaster Umbracrafter can turn an illuminated chamber into a terrifying landscape of living darkness.

Shadows stretch into walls. Corridors become obscured. Escape routes disappear behind dark barriers.

The Vren can manipulate the territory to restrain opponents, conceal allies, and dominate the battlefield.

However, Court of Midnight is expensive to maintain, especially in brightly illuminated environments.

Sunlight remains particularly dangerous.

A properly sustained darkness field can provide shelter from direct sunlight, but the Vren has not become immune to it. A disruption in the field can expose them immediately.

Umbracraft cannot permanently abolish the Vren's daylight vulnerability.

Vren careers: Assassins, prison wardens, night guards, spies, defensive architects, and military specialists.

## 5. Beastheart — The Unleashed Hunger

Permanent Strength enhancement · Fury · Pain suppression · Berserk combat

Beastheart embraces the predatory instincts that civilized Vren are usually expected to suppress.

Unlike the previous four Disciplines, Beastheart grants a permanent attribute bonus.

Passive Bonus

**Passive: +5 effective STR per rank**

Applies to effective Strength after racial multipliers. Maximum +25 STR at Rank V.

The passive bonus is always active, even when the Vren is calm.

The discipline's active abilities allow them to enter increasingly powerful states of rage, sacrificing precision and judgment for overwhelming physical aggression.

### Five Ranks (current canon)

| Rank | Technique           | Ability                                                                                                                        |
| ---- | ------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| I    | First Hunger        | Enter a predatory rage voluntarily, enhancing aggressive physical exertion while making delicate concentration more difficult. |
| II   | Pain Is Prey        | Continue fighting despite pain, injury, and physical distress while raging.                                                    |
| III  | Fight or Flight     | Automatically enter a defensive or aggressive frenzy when seriously wounded or overwhelmed by panic.                           |
| IV   | Predator's Fixation | Designate a particular target during rage and retain an unbreakable predatory focus upon it.                                   |
| V    | The Deathless Beast | Become temporarily unkillable while fully enraged, surviving injuries that would otherwise cause death.                        |

### Rank III — Fight or Flight



The inner beast begins acting as an independent survival instinct.

When the Vren suffers a significant wound or experiences overwhelming fear, Beastheart can activate reflexively.

The Vren enters one of two instinctive responses.

Fight: The beast reacts to the source of danger with immediate aggression. It prioritizes attacking, overpowering, or driving the threat away.

Flight: The beast prioritizes survival, enhancing the Vren's determination to escape, evade attacks, and reach safety.

The response requires available MP but no deliberate decision or ordinary activation action. It can therefore trigger even when the Vren is startled, panicking, or temporarily unable to concentrate.

The Vren retains limited awareness, but instinct temporarily takes priority over reason.

This makes Beastheart uniquely valuable against fear-inducing magic and surprise attacks.

### Rank IV — Predator's Fixation

At Rank IV, a Vren gains the ability to give their rage a purpose.

Upon entering rage, or during a moment of deliberate focus while raging, the Vren can designate an individual as their prey.

Once selected, the inner beast maintains an extraordinary awareness of that target.

The Vren can recognize the target through distractions, crowds, darkness, and other confusing conditions. If the target escapes immediate sight, the beast retains its direction and recent trail for as long as the rage lasts.

This is supernatural target fixation, not omniscient tracking or unrestricted remote detection.

The Vren cannot know the target's exact location across an entire continent, but ordinary distractions will no longer make them forget whom they are pursuing.

An additional advantage is that the beast becomes less inclined to attack unrelated creatures while the chosen target remains accessible.

That makes Rank IV an important step toward controlling the dangers introduced by earlier Beastheart ranks.

### Rank V — The Deathless Beast



At its highest rank, Beastheart becomes a terrifying supernatural survival ability.

While The Deathless Beast is active, the Vren cannot be killed by bodily injury.

Blades can pierce their heart. Bones can shatter. The Vren can sustain wounds that would unquestionably kill an ordinary creature.

Their black mana temporarily holds their vital processes together through sheer supernatural predatory force.

They do not become invulnerable. The injuries remain real, and limbs or bodily functions can still be impaired.

The distinction is that fatal injuries cannot end their life while the state persists.

Deathless Rage — Mechanical Rules

- Requires entering Beastheart's highest state of rage.
- Costs at least 1 MP to activate, with 1 MP upkeep per combat round.
- Lasts at most one minute per activation.
- Cannot be killed by physical trauma or otherwise lethal bodily damage while maintained.
- Can still be restrained, imprisoned, incapacitated through appropriate supernatural means, or separated from an objective.
- Does not regenerate lost limbs or heal accumulated injuries.
- If the rage ends while fatal injuries remain untreated, death follows immediately.
- Suppressing the Vren's magic can terminate the effect.

The Deathless Beast becomes most terrifying when combined with Predator's Fixation.

A Vren could pursue a chosen enemy despite suffering catastrophic wounds, refusing to stop until the target is defeated, the rage expires, or the Vren's magic is suppressed.

The cost creates dramatic consequences.

A Grandmaster might win an otherwise impossible battle, only to collapse afterward from injuries their supernatural fury kept from killing them.

An ally capable of stabilization—or a carefully prepared emergency plan—may be essential.

The Beastheart Grandmaster is not immortal. They are temporarily beyond death, and the distinction matters enormously.


**Vren careers:** Soldiers, gladiators, bodyguards, shock troops, monster hunters, and professional combatants.

## 6. Celerity — The Stolen Moment

Permanent Speed enhancement · Supernatural movement · Instantaneous action

Celerity is perhaps the most spectacular of the physical Disciplines.

Its practitioners accelerate their movements through black-mana manipulation of their own bodies.

They can cross rooms before an opponent finishes speaking, dodge attacks that seem impossible to avoid, and execute physical maneuvers with astonishing speed.

Passive Bonus

**Passive: +5 effective SPD per rank**

Applies to effective Speed after racial multipliers. Maximum +25 SPD at Rank V.

### Five Ranks (current canon)

| Rank | Technique          | Ability                                                                                                               |
| ---- | ------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| I    | Quickening Step    | Perform brief bursts of supernatural acceleration.                                                                    |
| II   | Broken Motion      | Change direction and redirect momentum during accelerated movements.                                                  |
| III  | Between Heartbeats | Dramatically accelerate perception and reaction, allowing the Vren to follow movements normally too fast to perceive. |
| IV   | The Vanishing Step | Move so quickly during a brief burst that ordinary observers struggle to track the Vren.                              |
| V    | Zero Interval      | Complete one otherwise physically possible action instantaneously.                                                    |

### Rank III — Between Heartbeats



Between Heartbeats changes how the Vren experiences time.

Their conscious perception accelerates dramatically, allowing them to observe and understand movements that would appear instantaneous to an ordinary observer.

A Vren with Between Heartbeats could:

- Follow the movement of an opponent using Celerity.
- Recognize an incoming projectile that would ordinarily be too fast to observe.
- Identify the direction and apparent sequence of an exceptionally rapid attack.
- Understand a Zero Interval action as a discernible sequence through supernatural perception, despite its absence of measurable elapsed time.

The rank does not grant additional Speed beyond Celerity's existing passive +5 SPD per rank.

The Vren can perceive an attack without necessarily possessing the physical capability to avoid it.

A character with Rank III Celerity might clearly witness a Grandmaster's instantaneous sword strike but still be unable to move their body quickly enough to intercept the blade.

This makes Celerity combat more interesting.

A sufficiently perceptive Vren can recognize the difference between ordinary movement, supernatural acceleration, and a true Zero Interval action.

However, successful defense requires additional Speed, appropriate abilities, anticipation, or a prepared countermeasure.

At Rank V, Zero Interval remains a genuine instantaneous action, subject to its previously established mana cost and other restrictions.

### Rank V — Zero Interval

This is Celerity's defining ability.

A Vren can perform a single action instantaneously.

They might draw and strike with a weapon, move across a short distance, catch a falling object, or intervene in an attack that is already underway.

Zero Interval — Rules

- Minimum activation expenditure: 5 MP.
- The action itself is instantaneous.
- Only one action is completed; the ability does not create additional actions or an extra turn.
- The action must be physically possible for the Vren, including any applicable physical or magical resistance.
- Performing a second instantaneous action requires a separate activation and ordinary opportunity to act.
- It does not reverse time, rewrite an event already completed, or allow unlimited-distance movement.

A Rank V Vren duelist could potentially strike before an opponent perceives the beginning of their movement.

Against another Celerity Grandmaster, however, the contest becomes extraordinarily interesting.

Both participants can react at supernatural speeds, and the outcome depends on their timing, Speed, strategy, and available mana.

A Naga diviner might foresee the strike without necessarily possessing the physical means to avoid it.

A Warlock might attempt to suppress the supernatural acceleration.

A Wakewright might prepare a defensive water barrier that responds to the physical disturbance.

Celerity is devastating, but not unbeatable.

Vren careers: Duelists, assassins, messengers, scouts, couriers, thieves, emergency responders, and elite guards.

## IV. Stat Balance — The Level 100 Test (source example)

Your restriction is particularly useful because it prevents the Vren's passive disciplines from overwhelming the established Shimmer attribute system.

## V. How the Disciplines Interact

The Disciplines are independent, but their interactions create some particularly dangerous Vren specialists.

| Combination              | Result                                                                                                              |
| ------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Blood Magic + Bloodbond  | Extract a blood sample and establish a magical connection through it, supporting curses or contested compulsions.   |
| Blood Magic + Beastheart | Fight aggressively with blood-forged weapons while drawing upon predatory rage.                                     |
| Blood Magic + Celerity   | Manipulate blood during exceptionally fast movements, delivering prepared blood weapons or curses with great speed. |
| Nightshape + Celerity    | Achieve astonishing speed in bat, wolf, or Starspawn-like forms.                                                    |
| Nightshape + Beastheart  | Become an especially dangerous feral combatant whose animal form exploits supernatural strength.                    |
| Nightshape + Umbracraft  | Disappear into shadow, transform, and emerge in a completely different physical form.                               |
| Bloodbond + Beastheart   | Use a trusted blood connection to help calm or redirect an ally overwhelmed by rage.                                |
| Bloodbond + Celerity     | Coordinate sudden attacks through exchanged physical impulses, allowing extraordinarily precise teamwork.           |
| Umbracraft + Celerity    | Cross a battlefield in the blink of an eye while using shadows to conceal the movement.                             |

A Vren with all six Disciplines becomes terrifyingly versatile, but mastery of all six requires 150 Skill Points, earned through divine quests.

This ensures complete mastery remains an exceptional accomplishment.

## VI. Vren Society and the Disciplines

The Six Disciplines should be reflected directly in the aristocratic structure of the Blood Courts.

Each Discipline has an associated professional tradition, although individual Vren may study whichever they prefer.

| Discipline  | Traditional prestige in the Blood Courts                                    |
| ----------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Blood Magic | Ritualists, executioners, and supernatural physicians specializing in blood |
| Bloodbond   | Judges, diplomats, contract scholars, and aristocratic governors            |
| Nightshape  | Spies, hunters, scouts, and clandestine courtiers                           |
| Umbracraft  | Night wardens, infiltrators, and protectors of noble estates                |
| Beastheart  | Warriors, gladiators, and elite military champions                          |
| Celerity    | Duelists, couriers, royal guards, and prestigious assassins                 |

### The Beast and the Aristocrat

The Blood Courts place tremendous value on elegance, restraint, and control.

Consequently, Beastheart occupies a socially complicated position.

Almost everyone respects its extraordinary physical power.

However, openly losing control is considered vulgar among many aristocratic families.

A noble who masters Bloodbond might be praised for manipulating a rival into surrendering their estate.

A noble who masters Beastheart might be criticized for breaking that rival's furniture during an argument.

Both acts may be equally malicious, but the Blood Courts are not especially famous for their consistency.

### Bloodbound Romance

Bloodbond adds a distinctive romantic tradition.

Vren lovers sometimes exchange a small quantity of blood to establish an enduring voluntary connection.

Through this bond, they can sense each other's physical presence and communicate private signals.

The ritual is considered far more intimate than exchanging jewelry.

In particularly traditional Blood Courts, aristocrats may regard an unbonded romantic relationship as unserious.

Naturally, this leads to enormous scandals when someone establishes overlapping romantic blood oaths.

### The Midnight Duels

Celerity and Blood Magic have encouraged a prestigious dueling culture.

Unlike the Naga's elaborate team sports, Vren duels emphasize personal style, deadly precision, and the ability to decisively defeat an opponent.

A particularly famous duelist might end a match with one instantaneous strike.

The crowd may not even witness the movement.

Spectators then spend the remainder of the evening debating whether the victory was sufficiently elegant.

## VII. Shared Restrictions and Hero Rules

These Disciplines remain subject to Mystaria's established magic system.

Vren are naturally black-mana users. Their Disciplines express the properties of that affinity through blood, entropy, curses, darkness, and supernatural vampiric physiology.

They do not automatically grant additional mana colors.

Cantrips use trace mana and cost 0 MP. Meaningful supernatural effects require MP. A character's Channel Limit remains `ceil(effective INT ÷ 5)`, and sustained techniques follow the established activation and upkeep rules.

Nightshape cannot grant Starspawn immortality or Named Spells. Blood Magic cannot perform green-mana regeneration. Bloodbond cannot simply read thoughts like Psycraft Telepathy.

Sunlight remains a severe weakness, and Vren still require blood for their continued well-being.

Vren Heroes begin with 0 Skill Points. Additional points come exclusively from divine quests, and every Discipline rank costs 5 SP.

Their free Blood Boon means even a starting Vren without a purchased Discipline possesses an active supernatural ability.

Canon preserves the distinction between Bloodbond and Psycraft Telepathy.

Naga influence minds through perception, emotion, and psychic suggestion. Vren influence people through supernatural relationships established by blood, bodily control, and binding obligations.

That distinction makes the Vren's magic feel much more personal—and often considerably more unsettling.

A Psychic may know that you are afraid.

A Bloodbinder may know exactly when your heart starts beating faster, whose blood you have sworn yourself to, and what will happen if you break your promise.
