## 1. Damage and Endurance: quadratic comparison (CANON)

For physical and other applicable opposed attacks, compare effective attack and defense attributes using a **+10 combat-only offset**. Damage power and defensive resistance remain quadratic:

```text
Unarmed Attack Power = (effective STR + 10)^2
Natural Defense Power = (effective END + 10)^2
Impact Ratio = Attack Power / Defense Power
```

The +10 does **not** increase real Shimmer attributes, mana reserves, craft limits, skill prerequisites or costs. Endurance resists virtually all forms of hostile assault, including ordinary physical, magical and psychic attacks; special technique rules still govern their applicability. When an attack is not driven by STR (e.g. a gun), use the relevant independent attack rating. Existing racial modifiers apply to effective attributes before combat formulas.

Example: 1 effective STR gives 121 baseline power, 10 effective STR gives 400; the latter is about 3.31 times the former, not 100 times.

### Impact ratios (CANON)

| Impact ratio | Effect | Injury-state advancement |
|---|---|---:|
| 0 through **0.75**, inclusive | Superficial | 0 |
| Above **0.75** through **1.75**, inclusive | Solid | 1 |
| Above **1.75** through **3.0**, inclusive | Devastating | 2 |

Equal attack and defense give ratio 1 and one meaningful injury; an ordinary evenly matched unprotected combatant is incapacitated after approximately three solid damaging strikes.

## 2. Injury progression, Hero intervention and Legends (CANON)

Normal combat has three meaningful injury advancements: **Wounded** (first), **Gravely Wounded** (second), **Incapacitated** (third). They normally apply respectively **−5%, −10% total, and −15% total** to effective attributes; incapacitated characters ordinarily cannot act, so the final penalty matters only if an ability allows action. Apply injury penalties to actual effective attributes **before** adding the combat-only +10. The exact handling of distinct simultaneous bodily and psychic wounds is **UNRESOLVED**.

**Heroes and Legends** gain **two extra, low-penalty Shimmer Guard injury stages**. In the current adopted ordering this makes: Uninjured → Wounded (−5%) → Guard I (still −5%) → Guard II (still −5%) → Gravely Wounded (−10%) → Incapacitated (−15%). Each normal solid attack advances one state; overwhelming damage advances at most three, so a fully healthy Hero or Legend **cannot be incapacitated by a single attack unless the DM expressly decides otherwise**. These stages represent exceptional ability to continue despite real injuries, **not erasure of trauma**.

When a **Hero** crosses beyond the first wounded state and Shimmer Guard manifests, their eyes visibly emit the Shimmer's signature **purple light**. The Shimmer can flawlessly diagnose a Hero's personal injuries and suppress pain to let the Hero function where most mortals falter. A Hero may **turn off or conceal the visible Shimmer Guard glow**, but experiences the full corresponding pain. Turning off the glow **does not change the numerical injury penalties**; extreme pain may be adjudicated situationally, without a new fixed penalty.

**Legends** are renowned mortals or monsters whose reputation and mortal belief create their own Shimmer Guard. They gain the **two additional injury states and the visible Guard**, but **no other Hero/Shimmer bonuses**, including no guaranteed pain suppression or personal Shimmer diagnostic interface. Belief sustains this power; attack on a Legend's *legend*, including thorough humiliation or loss of renown, can temporarily or permanently disable their Shimmer Guard. **A Legend whose Guard vanishes can collapse immediately if their accumulated injuries exceed ordinary mortal capacity.** How reputation is measured, and thresholds for disabling the Guard, are **UNRESOLVED**.

### Healing, rest, and prognosis (CANON)

**Superficial injuries heal during a long rest.** Meaningful injuries may take **weeks or months** to heal naturally, and some would be permanent without magical intervention. Because magical healing is **standard medical practice** in Mystaria, untreated permanent damage is comparatively rare where appropriate care is available. A long rest does **not** automatically heal meaningful injuries.

**Heal burn** is strain inflicted **on the recipient** by healing magic, cumulative with ordinary Soul Strain. Approved target percentages: **superficial healing 5%**; **first ordinary injury state 20%**; **second injury (first extra Guard stage) 10%**; the further Guard-stage progression adds **5 percentage points per further Guard injury** (third injury / second extra stage: **15%**). Ordinary penalty-causing injury-state healing is approximately **20% per state**. Burn is charged for injuries actually healed, not for unhealed stages. Up to **two short rests per day** can remove some heal burn; a **long rest removes all heal burn**. Exact short-rest recovery percentages, strain caps and the precise mana-reserve conversion of percent heal burn are **UNRESOLVED**. Neither the Shimmer Guard buffer nor ordinary rest provides free magical tissue regeneration.

## 3. Armor (working combat formula, scaling NOT finalized)

Retain the **working** additive-in-power armor formula:

```text
Effective Defense Power = (effective END after injuries + 10)^2 + (Armor Rating after penetration)^2
Impact Ratio = Attack Power / Effective Defense Power
```

Mundane armor usually applies only where physically present; mundane armor need not resist psychic or magical assaults, while specific enchantments or materials may. **Numerical armor ratings, penetration percentages, how armor quality and Centaur crafting scale, and final integration with equipment-tier bonuses remain an UNFINISHED PROPOSAL.** Do not canonize past sample armor numbers as equipment quality rules. Preserve approved equipment cost, Soul Strain and Centaur equipment affinity until expressly revised.

## 4. Firearms, recoil and materials (CANON principles; thresholds unresolved)

Firearm damage is set primarily by **weapon construction, propellant and ammunition**, **not** directly by the shooter's Strength. Each gun should have an intrinsic **Damage Rating** and a **Strength Requirement**, their relationship balancing the weapon's power against handling demands. Being below its STR requirement makes aiming, control and recoil increasingly difficult; an extreme deficit prevents free use. Far-above-required STR can reduce felt recoil nearly to nothing. **Exact strength ratios/cutoffs are UNRESOLVED**; numbers suggested during brainstorming are proposals only.

Extremely heavy guns may be fired using a **brace, ground rest or mount**, but can be unsuitable for a mobile melee brawl. Magic such as Jester ribbons may offset the **weight/movement requirement** without automatically cancelling the gun's **recoil**. If extreme recoil actually causes injury, it is **severe and advances the wielder by one injury state** (not merely superficial harm). When the recoil injury occurs, and its threshold, remain unresolved. Specialized magic that truly absorbs impact must have an applicable, established effect.

**Adamantite** is the authoritative spelling for the exceptional metal; the alternate spelling “Adamantine” is not adopted. **Crownsalt** is the established powerful propellant; **gunpowder** is also an acceptable ordinary term for Crownsalt. Even **Trufula** guns can have enormous recoil by real-world standards; maintain established special noncombustive-propellant compatibility for Trufula frames rather than implying ordinary fire is safe. Adamantite and reinforced designs can be extraordinarily heavy; powerful propellant exists to challenge Adamantite armor, dragon scales and other formidable defenses.

## 5. Combined attacks (quadratic principle; implementation still provisional)

In a genuinely synchronized attack, weaker attackers can combine offensive **power**, potentially overwhelming a superior opponent (including Naga Psychics joining forces against a Serpentari). A prior suggested rule was `A_group = sqrt(sum(A_i^2))`, with a coordination factor for imperfect synchronization; these **exact aggregation and coordination numbers remain PROPOSAL** until approved. Separate, unsynchronized attacks are not automatically one combined strike. Splitting power among targets remains under design.

## November 5, 2026 — Power, Resistance, Defense and Evasion terminology (CANON)

- **Power** is the general numerical strength/output of an attack, spell, weapon or other scaling effect. It does **not** always mean damage: an effect can use Power for damage, a spell's strength, overcoming protection or another function explicitly defined by that ability.
- **Resistance** is the character's **effective END (Endurance)** as a baseline statistic, with the usual racial/equipment/injury adjustments that actually affect effective END. The combat-only +10 is **not** included in the Resistance statistic. Thus **Natural Defense Power = (Resistance + 10)²**.
- **Specific Resistance** is a convenient description for situational adjustments against a particular effect or damage type (for example, fire resistance, psychic resistance, piercing resistance). Its actual modifier and scope must come from an established spell, material, hazard or ability; **this terminology does not authorize blanket new resistances, stacking rules or bonus values**. Unless a rule explicitly changes effective END generally, a specific resistance modifier applies only to its named threat.
- **Defense** means the result of physically, magically or mentally withstanding an effect. Armor, wards and barriers may contribute separately to the overall defensive calculation as their established rules allow; **Defense has no role in evasion**.
- **Evasion** is avoiding an attack or exposure entirely, resolved separately using relevant speed/dexterity/positioning or established skills. A successful dodge is not a higher Defense score.

**Calculation vocabulary:** For an ordinary Strength-based weapon strike, `Power = ((effective STR + 10) × weapon modifier)^2`. For natural protection, `Defense Power = (Resistance + 10)^2`. `Impact Ratio = Power / Defense Power` when no other applicable defenses or modifiers intervene. Preserve the existing working armor model `Defense Power = (Resistance + 10)^2 + (effective armor rating)^2` for covered physical hits; final armor ratings and penetration remain **UNRESOLVED**. Firearm Power comes from the weapon/ammunition rather than the shooter's Strength. Spell Power depends on that spell's established scaling parameters, not an automatic conversion from STR.

**DM modifier order (organizational guidance, not a new stacking rule):** (1) establish whether the effect connects or is evaded; (2) identify the effect and its Power source (including weapon, implement, ammunition and known modifiers); (3) start with effective END as baseline Resistance and apply only relevant type-specific adjustments; (4) evaluate actual armor coverage, barrier effects, and vulnerabilities as separately applicable; (5) use the Impact Ratio thresholds only when the effect inflicts an injury-type attack. Record modifiers by their source and type instead of folding unrelated effects into one arbitrary END change. **The exact order for applying oil vulnerability, fire-resistant armor, barrier absorption, resistance stacking and spell-specific bonuses remains UNRESOLVED** until the underlying rules are set.

**Illustrative scenario, not a ruling on numerical effects:** A Sorcerer using an implement casts **Ignis** at a Thornguard in plate armor, coated in lantern oil and protected by a barrier. First decide whether Ignis connects. Determine Ignis's Power under its actual spell rules and the implement's approved effect, then apply any proven fire-specific Resistance, armor protection that really works against the spell, the actual barrier mechanism, and any oil-induced vulnerability. Do not assume that plate stops fire, that lantern oil changes effective END, or that all barriers merely increase END. Specific numerical modifiers, spell damage scaling and their precedence are **not yet canonical**.

This terminology clarification supersedes uses of “attack rating,” “effective Endurance” and “Defense” as interchangeable names for Power, Resistance and evasion in older summaries. It does **not** change the quadratic law, the +10 offset, the injury thresholds, or existing Sorcery skill/MP/SP costs.


## November 5, 2026 — Speed and Evasion: approved design direction

**Status:** **CANON for the design philosophy, difficulty ladder, pressure states, no-extra-attacks restriction, and Shimmer prediction feature.** Specific procedural modifiers, numerical armor SPD penalties, and a full hit-resolution algorithm are **UNRESOLVED**. Do not treat hypothetical frequencies as canon.

### Core principles

- **STR, DEX, INT** are the primary scaling attributes for thematic skill output. General-purpose, Skill-Point-purchased STR/DEX skills are planned for future development; **no names, SP prices, rank effects, or purchases are established here**. Specific magic can also scale with END under its existing rules.
- **END / Resistance** is the universal protection against landed harm. **SPD / Speed** helps a combatant evade and land attacks on fast opponents, with emphasis on **defensive** use; DEX remains distinct for accuracy and technique. Speed never independently grants extra attacks within an action. Special skills or magic that might alter action count are speculative, not granted.
- Evasion is separate from Defense, entirely negating a successfully dodged attack. **Glancing blows** are permitted as an intermediate result when an attack cannot wholly miss or wholly connect; they reduce the resulting **injury states by approximately half, NOT Power**. Exact rounding for odd injury counts remains **UNRESOLVED**. Glances should be less common than complete dodges. Targeted, high-quality attacks particularly invite evasion; armor/Resistance are especially dependable against repeated weaker strikes.
- Different combat circumstances are the principal counter to superior Speed: **area attacks, multiple coordinated enemies, constraints on escape paths, terrain and setup**; even extraordinary Speed does not guarantee absolute immunity in all situations. Extreme disparity may make a direct ordinary attack impossible in the *current circumstances* until the attacker changes those circumstances. Armor **directly penalizes SPD**, but numerical values and equipment-quality scaling remain an **UNFINISHED PROPOSAL**, coordinated with Centaur crafting.
- The design benchmark is that, against **one ordinary melee opponent in open conditions**, equal extra-stat investment in SPD versus END should yield **roughly comparable overall injury avoidance/mitigation**. END should be more consistent across multiple smaller attacks and area exposure; SPD should be strongest at evading individual dangerous hits. This is a balance goal, not an approved probability system.

### Approved baseline Evasion Ratio and difficulty ladder

The ratio compares defender and attacker **effective SPD**, using the combat-only offset; actual ability to dodge additionally depends on situation and the attack's nature.

```text
Evasion Ratio = (Defender effective SPD + 10) / (Attacker effective SPD + 10)
```

| Evasion Ratio | Difficulty to land a direct attack | Interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| Below **0.50** | **Certain** | A straightforward strike should ordinarily connect unless circumstances alter it |
| **0.50–0.79** | **Easy** | Defender needs favorable circumstances to evade |
| **0.80–1.24** | **Average** | Neither side has a major Speed advantage |
| **1.25–1.49** | **Hard** | Defender usually evades if aware and free to move |
| **1.50–2.99** | **Very Hard** | A direct hit normally needs a created opening |
| **3.00 and higher** | **Impossible** | Ordinary direct attacks cannot connect under unchanged circumstances |

The displayed decimal bands are human-friendly approximations; **exact treatment of gaps between hundredths is unresolved**. For human adjudication, compare to the shown boundaries and avoid needless precision. Difficulty is a default **before contextual adjustments**. **Certain does not bypass armor/Resistance** and **Impossible is not absolute invulnerability**.

### Four pressure states: open, pressured, trapped, extreme pressure

- **Open:** clear escape routes; the defender can benefit from their normal Speed advantage.
- **Pressured:** crowding, distraction, an ally flanking, partial confinement, or converging threats; evasion is harder and glancing blows become plausible.
- **Trapped:** meaningful escape routes are denied or attack coverage prevents a clean ordinary dodge; armor, barriers, Resistance and applicable magic become crucial.
- **Extreme Pressure:** unusually intense saturation such as a coordinated army of archers and swordsmen, overwhelming numbers and multiple overlapping attacks. The target is severely constrained **but never automatically helpless**: creative magical mobility, cover, disruptions, defensive techniques and favorable opportunities may still matter. Do not assume this state automatically makes every attack hit or authorizes unlimited group power.

Pressure changes the **circumstances** of an attack, not END/Resistance itself. The precise number of difficulty steps or die-less success procedures for each state is **UNRESOLVED**. Flanking by one ally and being targeted by a full formation should not be treated as equally oppressive.

### Shimmer prediction — approved feature, conditional DM intervention

For Heroes and Legends confronting overwhelming Speed discrepancies, **Shimmer Guard can display predicted evasion paths** (for Heroes, Shimmer-style purple path projections; Legends' manifestation follows their own Guard). This is an in-world sensory/anticipatory effect and a DM-facing mechanical aid when Speed disparities make ordinary attacks difficult to adjudicate or play. It can convey plausible movement/escape paths or openings and support adjudication of an otherwise impossible direct attack; it **does not** automatically boost SPD, grant extra actions, guarantee a hit, disclose certain future events, or override an opponent's abilities. Its numeric activation threshold, predictive certainty, and any explicit accuracy adjustment are **UNRESOLVED**. It should be used sparingly, when needed to preserve satisfying tactical counterplay, not treated as omniscience or ordinary always-on aim assistance.

### Rejected for routine play: percentage evasion model

The author explicitly **dislikes the prior Proposal Three / Bounded Evasion Curve** as a player-facing or GM-facing operational mechanic. Percent dodge frequencies may be used, if ever, **only as hidden, rare, highly technical playtesting calibration**, not as a published rule, random dodge check, mandated outcome frequency, or substitute for the approved difficulty ladder and tactical pressure. Do not canonize the earlier suggested percentages.

## November 5, 2026 — Attribute identity, predictive Dexterity and high-stat limits (CANON)

**DEX and SPD roles:** Speed (SPD) governs physical velocity, immediate reflexes, reaction time, rapid target tracking and evasion. **Speed does not itself grant movement prediction, ballistic forecasting, perfect aim or additional attacks.** Dexterity (DEX) governs precise execution and anticipation of physical movements and patterns; it also covers practical fine-motor abilities such as stealth, manual craft, lockpicking and careful physical observation. Intelligence (INT) governs abstract reasoning, learning, memory and evidence-based forecasts. DEX prediction is not a substitute for INT's broader analytical reasoning.

**Combat anticipation, roleplay-first:** An attacker or defender with **effective DEX at least 75% of the target's effective SPD** can reliably exploit an **accurate, player-stated prediction of that target's movement or attack** to improve the attacker's tactical circumstances or their own escape conditions. This is not a guaranteed hit, dodge, or free extra attack. The DM adjudicates the consequence through the existing four pressure situations (Open, Pressured, Trapped, Extreme Pressure) and existing six difficulty categories. A well-supported prediction in combination with other threats may escalate pressure dramatically, including **Pressured to Extreme Pressure** when the physical circumstances warrant. **Wrong predictions significantly worsen the predictor's circumstances** by committing movement or aim to the wrong opening. A player can still attempt clever tactics below the 75% benchmark, but cannot assume similarly reliable timing. No new prediction-roll system, discrete "prediction skill", resource, or numerical pressure-step formula is established. Examples: aim for a target's expected dodge away from an ally's explosion; prepare for a Drakyn repeating a previously observed fireball pattern.

**Exceptional INT is not supernatural knowledge:** At around **INT 100**, photographic memory is the desired benchmark. At **INT 150**, machine-like memory and reliably extraordinary, seemingly prophetic calculations are appropriate **only with sufficient relevant data**, with less complicated predictions requiring less information. INT itself does not grant visions or unknown facts. A genuine psychic/precognitive Naga may acquire information supernaturally without leaving a cave; a mundane high-INT Hero predicting large-scale world events needs relevant observations or an intelligence network. Exact capacities and benchmark tiers beyond these commitments are to be illustrated in a separate unapproved attribute-chart draft.

**Exceptional END and environmental resilience:** At approximately **END 150**, brief lava exposure is survivable if the creature **escapes immediately**, not comfortably swimmable. At approximately **END 300**, ordinary lava exposure is closer to sunburn-level injury; extreme natural hazards are mostly survivable, with events such as **volcanic explosions** still potentially dangerous. Survival time without oxygen, food or water scales **approximately linearly**, rather than quadratically, with END; at **END 300**, unsupported oxygen deprivation endurance is **about 10×** the ordinary human reference, not 20× simply because the stat is 20× higher. Exact breath-holding formula, food/water multipliers, terminal-velocity fall thresholds and environmental damage ratings remain **PROPOSAL/UNRESOLVED**, and physical durability does not remove physiological needs.

**Exceptional precision:** The previous suggestion to grant unseen-target aiming, ricochets, and long-distance ballistic precision through SPD is superseded. These are **DEX-driven precision feats**, with SPD relevant for reacting to or physically tracking fast targets. The requested indicative benchmarks are DEX **100** for accurately targeting unseen positions with information, **150** for complex multiple ricochets, and **300** for suitably supported extreme long-range aiming. These remain illustrative **chart proposals**, not automatic skill unlocks, guaranteed hits, magical senses or free equipment range.

The author has not yet approved numerical sprint speeds, oxygen-duration formula, detailed DEX stat chart, or final benchmarks for ordinary stealth/perception; keep such examples **PROPOSED** until review.


## November 5, 2026 — Dexterity, contingencies, stealth, and speed-of-motion clarifications (LATEST CANON)

### Dexterity: precise physical anticipation and stealth

**DEX** combines the previously considered *Anticipating Hand* and *Perceptive Hand* concepts: observing physical cues, predicting immediate movement from evidence, choosing timing, and executing precise actions. It **improves stealth and the ability to detect or foil stealth** through traditional means: moving quietly, exploiting concealment, reading openings, noticing small motions and detecting tells. **High DEX is never literal invisibility**; an uncovered character standing plainly in the open is ordinarily visible. Speed remains rapid reaction and movement, not foresight; Intelligence remains general analysis and data-dependent prediction.

**Player-stated predictions and contingent plans:** When a character has **DEX ≥ 75% of an opponent's effective SPD**, a reasonably grounded, accurate prediction can reliably improve tactical circumstances as previously approved; an incorrect prediction normally significantly worsens the predictor's situation. **New exception: if DEX ≥ 200% of that target's effective SPD, an incorrect prediction incurs NO penalty attributable specifically to the wrong prediction**. Such a character is sufficiently precise and anticipatory to build effective contingencies into their actions. This is **not** a guarantee of success, an extra action, a free evasion, elimination of unrelated danger, or a new percentage-based hit calculation. The DM still considers the actual battlefield, attack/evasion ratio, four pressure situations, and other source-specific consequences.

**Mundane expertise is NOT automatically unlocked by DEX:** Specializations such as lockpicking, detailed artisan trades and other practiced mundane techniques may eventually form a separate **PROFICIENCIES** system, potentially purchasable with SP and gated by attribute prerequisites. **PROFICIENCIES IS A PROPOSAL, NOT CANON:** names, prerequisites, cost structure, ranks, applicability and interaction with ordinary Shimmer progression are not developed or approved. High DEX supports precision but does not automatically grant a profession's training. Revisit Proficiencies **when the author raises general skill systems from other tabletop RPGs**, **not** when they are discussing Mystaria's already established magical Skills or Crafts.

### Ordinary training versus Hero advancement

**Ordinary mortals can raise actual attributes through appropriate training.** This is an in-world avenue for improving strength, stamina and coordination; no numerical training schedule has been approved. **Heroes cannot gain attribute points merely by training** because their Shimmer governs their attribute progression through **experience/leveling**, preserving the existing +2 allocatable base points per level and +1 to all five base attributes every fifth level. Practice may still improve player tactics or allow mastery of appropriately purchased/learned techniques, without extra Hero attribute points.

**END 25, not 15, is the baseline for marathon-capable endurance** under appropriate conditions. At END 15 a creature has ordinary adult stamina and does not automatically possess marathon capacity. The longer-endurance superhuman/lava and approximately linear survival-needs guidelines remain unchanged. The exact marathon duration, athletic conditioning modifiers and all endurance-distance tables remain unresolved.

### High-speed impact and movement modes

**Approved design principle:** Movement at extraordinary speed can modestly increase the **Power of a suitable movement-assisted attack**, subject to **relative closing speed and sufficient run-up**, not automatically multiply every weapon or spell or grant extra attacks. A charging combatant must have **room and time to accelerate**; an exceptionally fast Hero cannot immediately apply full dash momentum from rest. If the opponent accelerates in the same direction to **cancel the relative closing-speed difference**, the speed-derived attack-Power bonus is **fully neutralized**. Opposing movement, collision geometry, attack mechanics and the user's actual dash trajectory matter.

**Three movement regimes:** **Dash** is the extreme maximum short-burst speed. **Sprint** is **one progression tier below** the character's dash speed; a **sustainable jog** is **two progression tiers below**. The currently discussed illustrative tier sequence (SPD 10/15/25/35/50/75/100/150/300 with draft dash speeds 10/15/22/30/45/75/110/200/500 mph) is **NOT YET APPROVED AS A NUMERIC SPEED TABLE**, and the exact duration/acceleration/terrain requirements are unresolved. This tier-language applies to the future speed chart, rather than treating dash as sustainable travel pace.

**Provisional, not yet finalized numerical calibration:** Approximately **+25% attack Power** at a roughly **100 mph effective charge** and **+50% attack Power** at a roughly **500 mph effective charge** were suggested as suitably *modest* boosts. Exact rounding, intermediate interpolation, minimum run-up, and whether a specific attack qualifies remain **UNRESOLVED/PROPOSED**, not binding multipliers. If approved later, bonuses should apply to final attack **Power**, not separately to squared STR, avoiding an unintended quadratic multiplier, and should not increase number of attacks. Special effects or existing skills could differ only under their own approved rules.

The existing six-category evasion ladder, the four pressure states and armor's pending SPD penalty continue unchanged. The bounded percentage-dodge model remains disfavored for ordinary gameplay.

## November 6, 2026 — Construct squad pressure and casualty direction (LATEST)

## November 6, 2026 — Windling sustained sonic attack (APPROVED)

The commanded swarm pursues this same target during that bounded order. ## November 6, 2026 — Reach-entry opportunity, coverage nomenclature and weapon attribute formula

**APPROVED:** Do not measure entry past a defended Long weapon by mandatory feet/meters or fixed movement expenditure. Entry into Short fighting distance is an **opportunity and execution** question based on relative effective SPD, DEX-based anticipatory decisions, terrain, weapon control, actual Pressure and maneuver. **Accepting a spear strike or charging blindly does NOT itself confer an automatic chance to reach grapple distance** when a sufficiently fast, ready defender can maintain separation. Defenders may use the existing roleplay-first DEX prediction mechanics: an accurately stated prediction with DEX >= 75% of the moving opponent's SPD meaningfully helps; an incorrect one generally penalizes except at >= 200% of target SPD, where the mistake-specific penalty is removed. This does not guarantee an unbreakable defense. An illustrative **1.5x defender-SPD-over-attacker-SPD** cutoff was proposed by the author but expressly left open to alternatives, so is **NOT YET NUMERICAL CANON**. They represent attack-space geometries, wholly separate from Short/Medium/Long Reach and Light/Medium/Heavy weapon class. **Line** is an attack path rather than a synonym for extra reach or automatic penetration; resolve eligible individual targets as described in the opening Line ruling.

**APPROVED WEAPON POWER FRAMEWORK:** `Weapon Power = (A * F)^2`, with `A = effective STR`, `effective DEX`, `max(STR,DEX)`, or **`0.65 * (effective STR + effective DEX)` for weapons requiring BOTH minimum STR and DEX**. The both-stat eligibility requirements must actually be satisfied. `F` is the single calibrated attack-rating modifier for the actual weapon/attack without double counting overlapping shape rules. The already-approved base racial +10 rebase controls: **no additional combat-only +10**. Existing injury-state thresholds and Shimmer Guard remain. Exact weapon-specific F, armor bypass, DEX/STR minima and formula for special ranged/magical attacks remain their respective unfinished systems. The approved Light weapon baseline remains 1.00; individual daggers do not have an immutable 1.00 form-specific multiplier.

**Illustrative reference to preserve in future discussions:** a Short dagger fighter approaches a Long spear wielder; in the open spear reach can deny a reckless close; after a valid executed entry into inside reach, the spear loses ordinary strike leverage; in a successful close grapple the dagger remains usable but the spear's ordinary long-reach strike cannot target the grappler. This is an illustration of state changes, not measured distances or free action economy.

## November 6, 2026 — Three-point tactical pressure and ranged preparation (LATEST AUTHOR RULING)

### Pressure Points (PP): new controlling framework

**APPROVED:** Replace the older qualitative open/pressured/extreme accumulation method with **three pressure points as the threshold for Extreme Pressure**, calculated from *meaningful, stat-qualified tactical circumstances*. **Net 0 PP = Open; net 1 or 2 PP = Pressured (exact same evasion consequence at either count); net 3 or more PP = Extreme Pressure (further points do not increase the evasion penalty)**. No automatic pressure merely because another combatant acts, stands nearby or nominally attacks; they must have an actual threat or credible control relative to the target's abilities. Sources normally contribute **one PP**, when applicable: credible flanking, suppression by archers or appropriate squad, a meaningful reach-control advantage, a valid offhand Attack tactic, and correctly predicted opponent movement. A **bad DEX prediction** can instead expose the predictor to a qualifying opposing PP under its existing prediction rules, rather than acting as a free source for the mistaken predictor. Sources can be **counteracted, removed or reduced** by genuinely effective cover, evasion positioning, control of space, protective actions, a defender's own credible offhand or other countermeasures. Net PP floors at 0; exact cancellation/exclusivity between simultaneous sources is **UNRESOLVED**. Repeated redundant descriptions of the same threat are not automatically separate sources; do not multiply one offhand weapon, one flank or one swarm into unbounded PP.

**Trapped is a SEPARATE POSITIONAL CONDITION, not a numerical tier between Pressured and Extreme Pressure and not obtained automatically by reaching 3 PP.** An **aware Trapped** character **cannot perform an ordinary dodge** against the relevant threat, but **can still respond with applicable other defenses** (shield/block, barrier, ward, interception, item ability, attack or other legal counteraction) under the general action rules. Awareness does not restore ordinary dodge; Trapped is not synonymous with unconscious, incapacitated or helpless. The exact interaction of Trapped with simultaneous incoming threats, pre-existing Shimmer Guard, applicable attacks, active defense timing and special escape powers remains to be resolved. Where old material called Trapped an intermediate evasion state or suggested Extreme Pressure always removes dodge, this later ruling **controls**. Extreme Pressure strongly constrains ordinary evasion but **does not by itself equal Trapped or absolute impossibility of dodging**. The six-category underlying SPD evasion difficulty ladder remains operative for non-Trapped circumstances, but exact numerical shift from PP states remains unfinished.

**Squad and horde scaling:** A four-Windling group, *if actually statistically credible*, may provide a single PP; stronger/more populous coordinated units may create more sustained or stronger suppression, but **reaching 3 PP through numerical swarm concentration alone must require exponentially increasing effective forces** (whether troop count, effective stats, coordination, or genuinely independent tactical threats). Do not grant +1 PP per four bots indefinitely or assert that 12 bots automatically yield 3 PP. The exact increasing thresholds and stat relative multiplier are **UNAPPROVED**, pending generic monster-horde/gang/army group mechanics; all categories of combatants use this system, not just Cogmarshal constructs.

**Dual wield:** the secondary weapon assigned to Attack may earn 1 PP if it provides a meaningful secondary threat, while a Defense assignment can credibly offset/remove an incoming threat's PP if physically capable and applicable; the latter does not count as an extra action or an unconditional permanent 1 PP shield. Resolve the opposing threat as actual position/DEX/SPD and physical control require. Do not count the same offhand tactic as a duplicate of a separate +1 evasion shift until the precise category integration is designed.

### Ranged reload and aim: author approved direction

**Reloading vulnerability:** Reload preparation is a tactical weakness whose severity depends on weapon and ammunition system. Bows have **negligible additional opening** from ordinary nock/draw during their usual shot. Light crossbows and similar mechanical arms have a **modest exploitable opening**. Heavy crossbows carry more serious opening. **Single-shot pistols** require a **meaningful full-turn action** of careful reloading and are vulnerable while doing so. **High-capacity magazine loading/replacement** likewise requires **a full-turn action** of careful handling, not one free attack action; this is the magazine-loading/replacement step, not a reload after every bullet in a multi-shot weapon. Siege weapons have significant vulnerability and can require at least one full-turn loading procedure; *additional* turns, crew roles and stabilization are unresolved by design. Exploiting vulnerability requires a *credible*, stat-qualified attacker and real opportunity, not free attacks for every onlooker. Existing action economy and physical geometry control any reaction attacks; none are granted automatically. Specific pressure-point treatment of an open reload, reload stage lengths and weapon-specific exceptions remain unfinished.

**Deliberate Aim:** A ranged user may commit one meaningful potential attack action to **Aim** rather than fire, with the goal of improving the following shot's hit/evasion difficulty. For a standard user able to fire once per action, this sacrifices **one of two potential attack opportunities** across aiming and shooting; it does **not** divide an actual attack's Power in half, create an extra attack or guarantee a hit. Aim improves the *chance of connection*, not the gun's inherent Damage Rating, arrow's material output or armor bypass. Meaningful Pressure, loss of target tracking, forced movement, shielding or other interruptions may defeat the held advantage. Final numerical accuracy improvement, whether aim itself counts as PP, stacking, persistence/time-out and target switching remain **UNAPPROVED**.

**Ranged mounts:** user accepted differences between fixed side mounts (restricted arc), articulated mounts (wider arc with engineering requirements), supported heavy mounts (load distribution and bracing), and assisted-loading mounts (mechanism-specific reduction of loading burden). These may combine if physically compatible. Mounting does not automatically boost shot Power, grant free attacks or entirely negate recoil. Moving-fire accuracy and exact mount requirements remain unresolved.

**Proficiencies proposal only:** Add prospective mundane proficiencies aimed at shortening reload time and reducing reload vulnerability (illustrative working names *Rapid Reload* and *Protected Reload*), without establishing a skill, rank, prerequisite or SP cost yet.

## November 6, 2026 — Recoil Strain and ranged Cover: latest author approval

### Recoil Strain: pressure only, NOT a DEX reduction

**APPROVED:** Consecutive firearm attacks can create cumulative **Recoil Strain**, representing loss of stable stance and the ease with which opponents can exploit an unbalanced shooter. The strain remains until the shooter completes **one entire turn without shooting**. During that recovery turn the shooter may **run, cast spells, change position, defend or perform ordinary nonshooting actions**; none of these automatically prevent recovery. A shot during the turn interrupts/prevents that full-turn reset; weapon-specific partial-turn cooldowns are NOT implied. Reloading without firing can serve as a qualifying nonshooting turn, provided the full turn is spent without a shot. This does **not** reduce the actual **DEX attribute**, automatically worsen DEX-based precision actions, or impose the earlier proposed precision-task penalty. Previous assistant proposal for a DEX penalty at two strain is **REJECTED**.

**Approved Recoil Strain pressure mapping:** 0 Strain = 0 recoil PP; 1 Strain = **1 PP against the shooter**; 2 Strain = **2 PP against shooter**; 3 or more Strain = **3 PP against shooter**, capped. The existing general PP system still combines qualifying recoil vulnerability with other *distinct* credible threat sources: net 0 PP Open, 1–2 Pressured (one category easier to hit), 3+ Extreme Pressure (two categories easier to hit). Recoil strain is a **defensive vulnerability of the firing character**, not an offensive PP the shooter grants to their target. Two strain has the same current evasion effect as one, but creates a shorter path to Extreme Pressure when additional distinct threats qualify. Other genuine protection can counteract or subtract relevant net PP by the preexisting tactical rules. Strain does not make someone automatically Trapped or grant every onlooker a free attack.

**Approved discharge progression principle:** firing repeatedly **increases** strain according to gun discharge/recoil and the ability to control it. A normal imperfectly managed single discharge is a candidate **+1 Strain**, and a heavier full-auto volley is a candidate **+2 Strain**; exact gain by firearm, high Strength control, body-mounted/brace stability, and whether a very easy weapon can generate 0 Strain remain **calibration proposals, not fixed universal shot increments**. No DEX-specific effect is imposed by accumulated recoil. Consecutive fire does not itself add per-bullet full attack actions.

**Cross-reference separate approved burden:** Too-high current equipment STR requirement may independently reduce **situational SPD by 2% per 1% STR requirement shortfall** (current approved rule recorded on PR #48), with the greatest applicable Support/Recoil STR shortfall counted once, not added twice. That **physical equipment burden** is separate from Recoil Strain PP: do not invent another recoil-specific SPD percentage or double-count a single vulnerability as a second generic PP. An individual discharge or full-auto volley can inflict **at most ONE recoil-caused injury-state advance** (approved PR #48), not a fresh recoil injury per projectile.

### Ranged Cover: a HIT-DIFFICULTY modifier, never PP

**APPROVED COVER TIERS:** **Normal Cover** (e.g. waist-high rock): **incoming ranged attack 1 category harder to land**. **Full Cover** (e.g. a fighter leaning around a solid brick wall so only a portion is exposed): **2 categories harder to land**. **Amazing Cover** (e.g. an arrow slit or an exceptionally tiny aperture that favors the defender): **3 categories harder to land**. These are **direct changes to the six-tier hit-difficulty ladder**, not Pressure Points, not armor/Resistance, and not a free increase to DEF, SPD or DEX. Where no target or line of fire is exposed through a fully blocking solid wall, a normal direct attack is **not eligible at all**, rather than merely being two categories harder. The modifier applies to a plausible actually exposed portion of the target, and may change with angle and shot geometry; specific blast, area, projectile destruction and bypass rules remain separately governed. Different cover objects don't add three bonuses for the same physical exposed line; use the applicable best actual cover.

**Interaction order for ordinary eligible attacks:** determine baseline hit difficulty from existing relative effective SPD; apply meaningful Pressure improvement (1 step easier at net 1–2 PP, 2 steps easier at net 3+ PP); apply eligible cover penalty (+1/+2/+3 steps harder); apply other **distinct**, already established aiming and tactical effects without double counting; clamp to the existing six difficulty categories for adjudication. Aimed shot's approved 1 category easier is not automatically an extra PP. Example: defender under Extreme Pressure but behind Full Cover: 2 steps easier due to PP and 2 steps harder due to cover, cancel numerically while the actual PP remains. Trapped is **independent** of PP and ordinary evasion; Trapped defenders may still have physical cover, barriers and other lawful defensive responses. Cover is not inherently a method of reducing the attacker's automatic Suppressive Fire PP against every target: it may block a meaningful threat, but it changes hit probability through the separate cover ladder while the shooter still controls an actually threatened region.

**Pending numerical balance:** Determine strain added by weapon output, firing mode, user Recoil STR above/below requirement, rated bracing/saddle, recoil reset timing edge cases, and how extreme recoil injury is triggered. Cover cannot be assumed to block all explosive/shock/wide-area attacks from every direction, and geometry remains critical.

## November 6, 2026 — Approved armor resistance, location, rear advantage and future acid effect (reconciled)

**COVERED-SURFACE ARMOR RATING, APPROVED:** Ordinary reference Light **AR 25**, Medium **AR 45**, Heavy **AR 75**. For a successfully landed attack, use the established model `Defense Power = effective END² + [effective covered AR]²`, with the already applicable armor penetration factor acting on covered AR *before* it is squared. Coverage classifications **Partial / Standard / Full** alter whether a region is protected, **not** the main covered AR. The earlier test-only status of AR 25/45/75 is superseded by explicit latest user approval. Armor is still very strong at low levels because of the square, an **approved DM warning**: balance typical heavy-armor acquisition through fair shop prices and availability, not invented character level gates.

**JOINTS / GAPS / EXPOSED AREAS:** Medium and Heavy suits include built-in **Light-equivalent AR 25 underarmor** at protected joints and structural gaps, resolved instead of (never added to) AR 45/75; protected Light armor regions use their Light AR 25. Completely unprotected places such as exposed face/eyes/skin have **AR 0** if an attack successfully and legitimately targets them. Do not separately track paid light armor layers beneath Medium/Heavy. Different extents of armor coverage create tactical opportunities with different future SPD/STR burdens. A single outfit-level burden is required, with details pending.

**REAR ARMOR BONUS, APPROVED:** The defender must be genuinely vulnerable to an exposed-rear hit via surprise or a tactically earned positional opening (including appropriate meaningful Pressure); a conventional shot does not get it merely because the attacker is behind an alert opponent. A qualifying rear attack adds **+0.10 absolute penetration fraction** (+10 percentage points) to existing applicable armor penetration, **capped at 1.00**, before squaring remaining covered AR. Example at 50% baseline penetration: `effective covered AR = AR × (1 − 0.60)`; this is **not** −15% AR. No automatic new PP, free hit or extra wound multiplier; it provides no additional benefit if target AR is already 0.

**FUTURE ACID ELEMENT/EFFECT:** Acid is to be available as an elemental *enchantment* offensive effect capable of **reducing Armor Rating no lower than 0**. It does not automatically reduce END², nor does it establish another mana color. Damage/reduction amount, duration, repair, material interactions, stacking and rule sequencing remain to be designed and approved later.

**UNRESOLVED NUMERICS:** Specific armor-mobility and STR tables; actual percentage penetration for ordinary Slashing/Blunt/Piercing and dedicated rigid pike-type points (previous 0%/50%/65% were proposed *tests* only); exact valid gap targeting/hit difficulty; shield effects; material and quality armor modifiers; costs and time of availability; Acid corrosion numeric rules. Preserve other preexisting Power, impact/injury and Shimmer Guard rules as written.


## November 6, 2026 — Shield durability and tactical defense APPROVED; numerical test open

**SHIELD DEFENSE IS INDEPENDENT:** On an actually intercepted blow, a shield has its own **Shield Defense** from shape, material, craftsmanship and appropriate future enchantments. Do not automatically add defender END² or body armor AR² to shield resistance, and do not treat the shield as universal full-body protection. **Durability = 3 losses**: **Intact 0/3, Dented 1/3, Cracked 2/3, Broken 3/3**. *Dented* supersedes *Strained*. At 1 and 2 losses shield functions remain **fully unimpaired**. At 3 losses shield is **Broken and useless until repaired**; a single major strike may count as multiple losses. Weak blows that fail to threaten shield Defense should not produce inevitable attrition. The exact Shield Defense Power formula, durability loss ratio thresholds and any breaking blow's overflow to the wielder are **unapproved**.

**SHIELD DEFENSIVE GEOMETRY:** On a plausible angle against a capable, conscious and properly equipped operator not under Extreme Pressure, a shield interferes with directly aiming at the *wearer rather than the shield*. Suggested testing hit-difficulty shifts: **Buckler +1, Standard +1, Tower +2**, with their own STR and DEX handling thresholds unresolved. This modifies the existing six-tier hit ladder like other justified geometry, not Power/END, and cannot be added multiple times for the same obstruction. An intentional active interception/block may instead redirect the strike onto the shield and potentially apply durability loss. Three canonical shield roles are physical passive location coverage, deliberate active block, and appropriate deflection/body hit-difficulty interference, not three simultaneously multiplied defensive effects.

**LATEST SHIELD-SPECIFIC PRESSURE RULE:** If **net meaningful PP is 3+ (Extreme Pressure)** the user **cannot actively block/parry or deliberately maintain the shield-obstruction/deflection hit-difficulty bonus**. Attacks that actually collide with the shield's already-present position still strike that object and use its own durability defense. **Do not globalize** this shield-specific exception: Extreme Pressure does not make all other defense impossible, and **Trapped alone** still removes ordinary dodge while permitting a physically possible shield response at 0–2 PP. Body armor and magical defenses still function as applicable. Shield enchantments need a later equipment stage, likely shared with or close to armor enchantments.


## November 6, 2026 — Shield breakthrough: approved torso direction, numerical tests pending

**USER-APPROVED HIGH-LEVEL INTERACTION:** A shield that **breaks** while physically intercepting a legal attack can allow residual attack Power to continue to the **defender's torso**, after a significant fraction has been absorbed by the shield. The surviving hit is then adjudicated against the **defender's own effective END, relevant torso Armor Rating, and existing armor-penetration classification**. It is not an automatic unarmored torso hit, a guaranteed injury, or a second full attack; never add shield defense to the defender's END²/AR². Attacks that do not actually contact shield are resolved normally. A shield already Broken cannot perform a block or obstruction, and Extreme Pressure removes deliberate shield use, although a shield in an actual existing physical path may still be hit. A shield's condition before the impact matters: an intact shield may break under a tremendous blow, while a previously Cracked shield can break under a smaller strike. **Exact residual Power formula is still PROPOSED ONLY.**

**SHIELD TEST NUMBERS, ENDORSED FOR CALIBRATION:** Shield Rating Buckler 30 / Standard 50 / Tower 75, proposed shield-defense square, and durability loss tiers at Shield Impact below 1:0; 1–<2:1; 2–<3:2; >=3:3. Before final approval verify breaking blow vs partial damage and Power absorbed. The recommended unapproved numerical breakthrough rule is `P_overflow = max(0, P_attack − Shield Rating²)` **only when accumulated damage reaches 3 (Broken)**. If no break, shield takes the durability losses and intercepted attack does not hit torso; if broken, torso receives only `P_overflow`, assessed once against the ordinary END² + remaining torso AR² Defense Power. A weapon's approved body armor penetration category applies against torso armor after overflow, but not by default as extra shield damage multiplier, pending separate shield-breaker mechanics. This is a tabletop balance abstraction, not a physical conservation-of-energy assertion. Material improvements to shields would change Shield Rating when approved. No overflow or separate secondary strike is automatically granted for the same hit until a shield breaks.

**SHIELD HANDLING, UNAPPROVED NUMERIC CANDIDATES:** STR/DEX requirements Buckler 10/14, Standard 22/15, Tower 35/16, consistent with proposed armor support and simple weapon manipulation. Consequences of unmet STR or DEX need testing, not automatic universal prohibition or unauthorized PP. Shield/weapon repairs later in mundane economy; shield and armor enchantments developed together after armor; Nymbrin shaping resistance of hard shield/armor materials is a future Wetcraft constraint.


## November 8, 2026 — Approved shield Remaining Durability Absorption formula (supersedes earlier one-square breakthrough)

**RESOLVE ONLY A REAL SHIELD INTERCEPTION:** `D = Shield Rating²`; with proposed/calibrating durability damage `loss = 0 if P < D; 1 if D ≤ P < 2D; 2 if 2D ≤ P < 3D; 3 if P ≥ 3D`, capped by the three-loss track. Let `R = 3 − prior accumulated shield durability losses` (Intact 3; Dented 2; Cracked 1). If `prior losses + loss < 3`, shield survives with full protection and **no** Power proceeds to body. If `prior losses + loss >= 3`, shield becomes Broken and approved overflow is `P_overflow = max(0, P − R×D)`; pass **only this residual Power** once to the **torso**, calculate torso `Defense Power = effective END² + [effective torso AR after applicable penetration]²`, and apply standard impact/injury and Shimmer Guard as relevant. This *supersedes* the proposed `max(0,P−D)` overflow regardless of prior state. R changes the final blow's remaining absorption capacity, **NOT** the rating, hit obstruction or blocking competence of a Dented or Cracked shield. Ordinary Piercing/Blunt/Slashing armor bypass is used against the torso as applicable, not multiplied into shield-defense attrition by default. Keep extreme-pressure restriction, physical positioning, and no automatic double attack.

**APPROVAL BOUNDARY:** The **overflow formula and only-on-break sequencing are approved**. Conventional shape ratings `S = 30/50/75`, the numerical impact-to-durability-loss tier table and shield minimum STR/DEX are still **endorsed test candidates, not finalized item canon**. A shield calculation aid may be acceptable; playtest whether repeated calculations slow down combat, especially relative to wound resolution. A useful lookup is at `P = D, 2D, 3D`; only broken shields need the overflow subtraction.


## November 8, 2026 — Approved unified equipment-shortfall SPD handling and shield eligibility

**LATEST APPROVED GENERAL HANDLING:** After valid loadout-synergy adjustments to equipment **requirements**, compare **current effective** relevant STR and DEX to each present item's applicable requirement. Let `q_i = max(0, 1 − effective_stat_i / effective_requirement_i)` for each separately relevant requirement; take `q = max_i(q_i)`. **Equipment-shortfall SPD loss = 2q**, representing 2% of situational SPD per 1% of greatest unmet requirement. Apply this **once**, not as separate shield/weapon/armor penalties and not doubled for distinct firearm Support/Recoil STR deficits; never conflate it with firing-based Recoil Strain PP or underlying armor's own mobility burden. A shield remains fully capable of **normal active blocking and obstruction** when **both STR and DEX are at least 75%** of effective requirements; below that floor in either stat, ordinary shield use is unavailable, though an actual physical shield surface can still intercept an attack already in its path. **No separate weakened shield-block rule** between 75% and 100%. Apply the existing absolute effective SPD minimum 1. Other item classes retain their already approved restrictions for dangerously low stat ratios, and exact armor inherent penalties remain for calibration.

**WOUNDS AND DISPLAY:** Evaluate effective stats **after existing wound penalties**, then compute gear shortfall and apply one situational SPD reduction; do not use SPD already reduced by that shortfall to recalculate a STR/DEX deficiency or add the injury penalty twice. Reassess when a character becomes Wounded or Gravely Wounded, changes gear, or obtains attribute/support enhancements. Future character sheet MUST prominently show **RED text** for active gear-caused shortfall penalties naming deficient equipment, STR/DEX requirements and adjusted stat, percentage and resulting SPD loss, and whether a wound triggered the deficit. Distinguish inherent armor mobility, Recoil Strain Pressure Points and other speed effects. Existing six-category SPD evasion/Pressure resolution continues normally; an actual Extreme Pressure condition still disables deliberate shield interception regardless of eligibility.

**RELATED LOADOUT SYNERGY, APPROVED WORKING VALUES:** Shield required STR ×0.75 with no armor; ×0.80 with Light; ×0.90 with Medium; ×1.00 with Heavy. Heavy armor required STR ×0.80 for genuinely unburdened, empty-handed casting configurations, not a universal buff to all heavy armor. Round requirements upward after reductions. Eligibility and further gear discounts to be playtested; see `09_Equipment_and_Economy.md`. Individual shield STR/DEX stat entries 10/14, 22/15, 35/16 and nine armor ensemble burdens remain testing values.


## November 8, 2026 — Author-directed SPD initiative and synchronized opposing attack geometry

**APPROVED CORE INITIATIVE:** Order combatants by **current effective SPD**, highest first. This is the existing combat-effective SPD after applicable wounds, inherent armor mobility and equipment-qualification shortfall reductions, not raw pre-equipment SPD and not the superseded extra combat-only +10. On an SPD tie, **Heroes and Legends act before ordinary non-Hero/non-Legend combatants**; when a **player character** ties with an NPC Hero or Legend, the **player character wins that tie**. These are prioritization rules, not new attacks. Further equal-tier ties (PC versus PC, Hero versus Legend, ordinary versus ordinary) require later procedure; no invented DEX or dice tiebreaker here. Exact initiative refresh timing after mid-round stat changes remains open.

**APPROVED TEAMWORK / SHIELD GEOMETRY:** A shieldbearer physically threatened by **genuinely simultaneous attacks from opposite front and rear directions** can actively shield-intercept **only one direction in that same exchange**; the shield cannot face both ways at once, even if the wielder is below Extreme Pressure. The other attack remains an ordinary legal attack against appropriate evasion, END, armor, guard, real cover, etc.; it does **not automatically hit, bypass armor, gain bonus penetration, or grant a second attack**. Only an actually shield-intercepted blow uses the shield durability/overflow sequence. Defenders can block in separate successive exchanges when credible repositioning permits; do not conflate simultaneity with an arbitrary global once-per-turn blocking cap. At net >=3 meaningful PP, deliberate active shield interception remains prohibited regardless. Unaware defender's rear, physical geometry and earned positional openings continue to matter; the existence of a second character alone does not establish a qualifying rear hit or automatic Pressure.

**DELAYED INITIATIVE TO COORDINATE (APPROVED DESIGN PRINCIPLE):** A combatant whose initiative comes earlier may **voluntarily delay its action to a later ally's initiative** to attempt one synchronized coordinated exchange, provided both can actually act, threaten their designated targets and maintain the necessary timing/position. Delaying does not grant extra actions, additional movement or retroactive opportunities; the slower ally does not act earlier. A faster character can forfeit their earlier opportunity and expose themselves to intervening opponents, so teamwork has a real timing tradeoff. **Generalize** to credible multi-directional melee, ranged, grappling and spell attacks without assuming identical actions or effects can always resolve simultaneously. Synchronization itself grants **no automatic PP or universal difficulty modifier**; apply the established stat-qualified independent Pressure sources, actual cover, legal exposure, ammunition and casting rules as relevant, avoiding double counts. The precise handling of delays, interruptions, shifting positions or unused delayed actions belongs to later full action-economy calibration, not an invented reaction-point economy.


## November 8, 2026 — Initiative exact-tie adjudication and approved conventional attack-form armor pass

**LATEST AUTHOR APPROVAL: INITIATIVE REMAINING TIES.** After comparing current effective SPD, then existing Hero/Legend and player-over-NPC-Hero/Legend tie priorities, **the DM determines any still-tied combatants' relative initiative order**. Encourage a fun **coin flip or rock-paper-scissors** when practical. **Once decided, the tied combatants' relative initiative order is SET for that combat's initiative sequence**, not rerolled/reselected each turn. Ordinary voluntary action delays for coordination still work and do not permanently reorder the established initiative. Exact recalculation timing for actual mid-combat SPD changes is separately unfinished; this rule closes the *identical-SPD tie* issue.

**Historical 65% entry:** The separate specialized profile has been retired. **AUTHOR-APPROVED CONVENTIONAL-MATERIAL WORKING ATTACK-FORM PENETRATION (subject to post-material/enchantment recalibration):** Slashing **0% AR bypass**, Blunt **25%**, ordinary Piercing **50%**, and **specialized purpose-built rigid armor-piercing point/projectile 65%** when its actual attack design warrants it. Specialized penetration is **not** universally granted to Piercing, Line coverage, pikes regardless of construction, or an ordinary greatsword's secondary stab. Apply the appropriate fraction to actual covered Armor Rating first, then square the retained Armor Rating contribution: `Defense Power = effective END² + [AR × (1 − applicable penetration)]²`. The approved legitimate rear +5 **percentage points** applies only on an earned attack angle and caps at 100%. No bypass applies to END², shields' independent Shield Rating, psychic/magical defenses or the bodily area if no armor is present. The figures are approved as **current reference-material working values**; material/quality/enchantment-specific rules may alter them later through explicit author decisions.

**AUTHOR-APPROVED TEST-BASELINE WEAK-POINT TARGETING:** An ordinary struck armor-covered main surface uses full coverage-class AR. A deliberately selected **clearly exposed body region** is **+1 hit-difficulty category harder** if precise targeting is genuinely necessary, and **AR 0** on genuine contact. A deliberately selected **protected joint/structural gap** is **+2 categories harder** and uses its already-approved built-in **AR 25** instead of main AR, not added to it. A deliberately selected **exceptionally tiny genuinely exposed opening** is **+3 categories harder**, **AR 0** if actually accessible. A target that does not physically exist or cannot be reached has **no eligible attack**, not a fake capped difficulty; context can remove the need for additional precision where a much larger exposed portion is the natural target. Never award an automatic weak-spot hit, a second unearned penetration bonus for the same structural gap, or duplicate positional modifiers describing one and the same angle. Apply earned Pressure, true Cover, aiming and existing rear rules distinctly under their normal stacking constraints.

**AUTHOR-APPROVED SHIELD RESPONSE SIMPLIFICATION:** Below Extreme Pressure, a competent/aware qualified shieldbearer may attempt a credible **active directional interception** regardless of baseline Speed-based hit-difficulty category, provided the physical shield can cover the threatened line in that exchange. This is **eligibility, not omnidirectional guarantee**. Passive shield-body obstruction and actively intercepted shield contact are different results: **do not award an additional automatic free shield-block roll on top of the same shield maneuver's hit-difficulty obstruction**, or combine overlapping passive/active/dodge defense for the same interference. If an attack actually strikes the shield, use shield durability and the approved only-on-break torso-overflow sequence; a strike that passes by the shield is resolved as an ordinary legal hit or miss. Even below Extreme Pressure the shield cannot actively intercept two genuinely simultaneous front and rear attacks; choose one possible facing. At net >=3 meaningful PP no deliberate active shield block/active obstruction; physical collisions with already-present shield remain possible. **Exact exceptional timing/interrupt procedure remains subject to general action-economy design**; no arbitrary once-per-turn shield blocking cap is introduced.

**IMPORTANT FORM INJURY BOUNDARY:** The approved relative form identities Slashing injury 5/5, Blunt injury 3/5, Piercing injury 1/5 are **NOT numeric injury multipliers**. The ordinary pre-form `Attack Power / Defense Power` injury-ratio tier may be computed to inspect penetration performance, but **the exact final form-specific injury-ratio adjustment remains UNAPPROVED**. Do not present bypass-only simulated wound counts as final attack-form injury counts, and do not implement a blunt one-wound hard cap. This remaining issue will be rechecked after materials, weapon shapes and enchantments.


## November 8, 2026 — Approved multi-target coverage and squad Power baseline

**AUTHOR APPROVAL:** Ordinary attacks, Point and Narrow target one enemy, **Sweep up to two**, **Wide Sweep up to three** within the attacker's selected **forward 180°** facing, provided each distinct target's occupied-space center approximately lies in that arc, is physically in weapon reach, and is actually vulnerable to this legal attack action. The attack action may depict multiple quick narrative strikes without granting additional actions or universally independent full Power attacks. Surrounding, flanking and attacks from behind counter ordinary sweeps by defeating their chosen attack arc. Facing choice is attack-local and no free reposition/extra Pressure. Existing approved attack Coverage names remain Point/Narrow/Sweep/Wide Sweep/Line; see the opening Line ruling for melee capacity. Ranged multiple-body penetration remains subject to its own rules.

**AUTHOR-APPROVED WORKING GROUP-CASUALTY AGGREGATE (RETEST WITH COGMARSHAL AND MATERIALS):** If N distinct squad members can actually be threatened by this one attack under the relevant attack's coverage, provisional aggregate squad Power factor **×[1 + 0.5 × (N−1)]**, counting **N ≤ 1** ordinary, **N ≤ 2** Sweep, **N ≤ 3** Wide Sweep. Consequently ×1.00 for one, ×1.50 for two, ×2.00 for three genuinely covered squad members. Aggregate group resolution ONLY: no added Power against each individual defender, no F multiplication before squaring, no guaranteed casualty count or phantom full bonus versus one adversary. Actual per-target hits/Defense, distributed injury, physical squad grouping, generalized squads versus clockwork, and large monster/horde edge cases remain future calibration. One ordinary attack action remains one action. Future spinning-sweep and line/ranged penetrating-multiple-target Proficiencies are design requirements only, without approved SP costs, ranks, specific bonuses or free player access.
