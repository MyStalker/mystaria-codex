# Mystaria Ranged Equipment Access Levels — REVISED NOVEMBER 6, 2026 (DESIGN COMPARISON)

**Status:** Computed teaching/review example, NOT a new equipment requirement canon. This replaces previous ChatGPT tables computed from erroneous Thornguard STR +20% and Moontouched DEX +30%. The **racial corrections are CANON**; the 34 ranged weapon stat gates remain DRAFT in `27_Ranged_Weapon_Catalogue_DRAFT.md`. Do not mistake level minimums for statutory equipment level gates, mandatory drop levels, Gem affordability or guaranteed ownership.

## Racial and progression assumptions (canon)

- **Thornguard level 1:** base STR18 / DEX14; permanent **STR +25%**. Effective STR **22.5**, DEX **14**. Speed is base 15 with no racial multiplier.
- **Moontouched level 1:** base STR11 / DEX17 / SPD20; permanent **SPD +25%**, **no DEX bonus**. Effective STR **11**, DEX **17**, SPD **25**.
- Every advancement from level L to L+1 grants **two freely assigned base attribute points**, and every fifth actual level grants **+1 to every base attribute**. Permanent racial multipliers are applied to base stats after all level allocations.
- **Specialist:** optimize every earned allocation between STR and DEX to meet THIS weapon's two stated handling thresholds as early as possible; does not invest in END/INT/SPD, and does not imply the same build qualifies for every weapon in this table. No class training or race skill bonus is assumed. Ordinary *generalist* averages the two allocated points over five stats = **0.4 points per attribute per level gained**, plus fifth-level +1 per stat; fractional allocations represent a long-run average, not real character level-up choices. Earliest generalist level is approximate, with rounding-dependent one-level variation.
- **Gear model:** The +6 test uses **ONE Quality I pure-stat bonus of +6 to STR OR DEX**, whichever permits an earlier fully qualified level. Equipment stat bonuses apply **after** racial multipliers. Do not simultaneously apply +6 to both; do not assume an affordable quality-I item exists in a level-1 backpack. The benchmark equipment effect is authorized in `09_Equipment_and_Economy.md`; precise modular item slot allocation and price of a finished weapon are separate.
- **Full qualification:** Bows: draft full draw/support STR threshold plus draft handling DEX; the bow's engineered **draw cap is a maximum output**, not another minimum and a weaker archer might be able to shoot with less Power. Crossbows: draft loading/support threshold and DEX; a working cocking aid changes practical effort and must be specified. Guns: at least **max(Support STR, normal single-shot Recoil STR)**, and Handling DEX; ineligible heavy automatic full-auto volley has a separate higher Recoil STR not included in this *normal-fire* table. For other weapons, read proposed STR/DEX requirements literally. Rated mounts, crew and machinery make meaningful personal required STR undefined without a configuration; do not invent a personal level for artillery or Centaur side mounts.
- No extra race armor, DEX for Moontouched, class stats, material-derived weight discounts, naturally pre-owned Gems, race anatomical bonuses or multiple stacked enhancement items are assumed.

## Progression examples at ordinary level 10

Under the generalist **average** from levels 1–10: 9 earned level-ups × 0.4 base points per attribute = **3.6 base points to each**, plus universal +2 at levels 5 and 10. Thus level-10 generalist adds **5.6 base points to every stat** (not including gear).

- Thornguard lvl 10: STR `(18+5.6)*1.25 = 29.5`; DEX `14+5.6=19.6`; SPD `15+5.6=20.6`.
- Moontouched lvl 10: STR `11+5.6=16.6`; DEX `17+5.6=22.6`; SPD `(20+5.6)*1.25=32`. Their **DEX is not multiplied**, while their **SPD is multiplied**.
- Adding one Quality I pure +6 STR enhancement yields lvl-10 Thornguard STR **35.5** and lvl-10 Moontouched STR **22.6**. A +6 DEX item would increase just DEX instead.

## Balance consequence

- Thornguard **Warbow** (draft STR38/DEX21): **level 10 specialist without gear**, **level 7 with +6**, **level 15 generalist with +6**.
- Moontouched **Warbow**: **level 15 specialist without gear**, **level 12 with +6**, **level 36 generalist with +6**. This reflects their true SPD gift, which assists evasion but does not satisfy draw STR or aim DEX.
- Thornguard **Longbow** (29/19): lvl6 specialist no gear, lvl4 +6, lvl10 generalist +6; Moontouched lvl10 specialist no gear, lvl7 +6, lvl21 generalist +6.
- **Heavy automatic rifle** draft Support STR65, Recoil single55, DEX25: Thornguard specialist lvl20 without gear or lvl18 +6, Moontouched lvl27 without gear or lvl25 +6; +6 generalists lvl50/81. Full-auto additionally needs draft Recoil STR90 and appropriate strain control, so do NOT interpret these levels as full-auto competency.
- **Armaments are not necessarily level-gated.** STR for safely handling/cocking/loading and real bracing matters; it is a physical eligibility requirement. Material and quality adjustments, mount load transfer, accurate intrinsic ranged Power, actual proficiency, and the staged arsenal's affordability remain separate, future approval issues.
