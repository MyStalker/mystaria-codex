# Mystaria — Weapon enchantments

**Approved November 18, 2026.** The Pass 2 shortlist contains 36 generic weapon enchantments and Guardian for shields. A generic entry can be installed on any weapon meeting its eligibility and the applicable equipment rules.

## Shared rules

- When an entry lists six values, read them in order from one through six occupied enchantment slots. Holdout Weapon, Close Quarters, Concussive, Boomerang and Second Face each require three slots. Rank Measure requires one.
- All Power percentages modify the applicable completed Power. The equipment’s physical Quality determines its normal physical effects. Occupied slots determine the enchantment’s strength.
- Mixed and Full values describe the magical effect. Blank is attribute-only. Mixed’s separate attribute benefit and the applicable Soul Strain use the [equipment rules](09_Equipment_and_Economy.md). Existing die, action, reach, cover, handling and defense rules govern eligibility. The standard powered window lasts five rounds and uses compatible Leywell MP. Per-enchantment mana colors, MP bands and final six-slot economy remain open.
- Unless an entry explicitly expands its application, apply the enchantment once per round to one eligible contact or result. Use the strongest applicable menu bonus on an overlapping result. Natural misses grant no reroll. Any granted reactivity or follow-up keeps the ordinary offensive action limit.
- This reference records the enchantments themselves. Sharpness was a balance benchmark. Simulation policies, encounter assumptions and historical drafts can be found in the author’s review materials.

See [Combat](22_Combat_Endurance_and_Shimmer_Guard.md) for action and injury resolution, [Magical Media](35_Magical_Media_and_Contests.md) for Elemental Magnitude and Channel Limit, and [Shield Profiles](29_Physical_Shield_Profiles.md) for shield benefits.

## Generic weapon enchantments

### Mantis Blade / Mantis Strike · 1–6 slots

- **Eligibility** Any compatible one-handed melee weapon in legal Double Strike
- **Trigger** Both hands connect with bodies in the same valid Double Strike. Only the enchanted hand benefits. Arm after resolving both attacks, including when both aim at one target.
- **Mixed** +20 / 32 / 48 / 65 / 80 / 95% final Power
- **Full** +40 / 64 / 96 / 130 / 160 / 190% final Power
- **Limits and duration** Through end of next turn; refresh only; one enchanted hand per character exchange.
- **Counterplay** Separate targets with cover or Parry; make the offhand illegal or attack during its self-Pressure.

### Split Rhythm · 1–6 slots

- **Eligibility** Legal dual melee weapons; two separate opponents
- **Trigger** Both Double Strike body hits land against two different, reachable opponents on opposite sides of the facing centerline; the enchanted hand gains the reward after resolution.
- **Mixed** +36 / 57 / 85 / 115 / 140 / 165% Power
- **Full** +72 / 114 / 170 / 230 / 280 / 330% Power
- **Limits and duration** The bonus lasts through the end of the wielder’s next turn and is exclusive with Mantis Blade.
- **Counterplay** Refuse opposing lanes, protect either line, or move out of the short hand's reach.

### Achilles’ Heel · 1–6 slots

- **Eligibility** Any weapon with legal body-wound delivery and an identifiable reachable wound line
- **Trigger** A meaningful weapon wound opens one marked line. Later attacks by this rune's wielder can declare and reach that wound line. Wounds from the entire offensive action are processed before new marks are placed.
- **Mixed** Ignore 5 / 7 / 9 / 11 / 13 / 15% of final Defense Power per stack
- **Full** Ignore 10 / 14 / 18 / 22 / 26 / 30% of final Defense Power per stack
- **Limits and duration** Cap 2 per target across users, max 1 gained per offensive action. Persists on the injury until qualifying local healing; heal one linked injury to remove one stack, fully heal line to clear. Requires powered rune to exploit. Uses the existing eligible body-line difficulty.
- **Counterplay** Cover the wound line, turn away, intercept, break contact or heal. Real armor and shield routing still apply.

### Holdout Weapon · 3 slots

- **Eligibility** Light weapon that is reachable and can be lawfully drawn
- **Trigger** First legal combat draw of this item while active. Draw this item action-free if a hand is available; no free retrieval through restraints or substitution of additional items.
- **Mixed** +21% Power
- **Full** +42% Power
- **Limits and duration** Draw turn and next two turns. One item benefit per wielder per engagement. Pre-drawn item has no trigger.
- **Actions** Draw the eligible weapon without spending an action once under its trigger. Attacks cost their ordinary actions.
- **Counterplay** Pressure the draw lane, force early use, stall out the window.

### Close Quarters · 3 slots

- **Eligibility** Melee weapon wound at ordinary legal Technique distance
- **Trigger** After the action wounds, choose one otherwise legal disarm, trip or grapple on that same target. Count the greatest stage result from one contact. No second wound is used to fuel it.
- **Mixed** Technique difficulty: one stage +1 harder, two unchanged, three −1 easier. +10% Technique for this contest. 1 maneuver total.
- **Full** The same category adjustments and one maneuver apply. Add +20% to the Technique score before evaluating the contest.
- **Limits and duration** The maneuver costs no additional action. One use per wielder per encounter, against the wounded target. The granted Technique cannot trigger another enchantment or wound. Trip and disarm require their own legal procedures.
- **Actions** The granted legal Technique uses the action waiver in this entry.
- **Counterplay** Prevent wound, deny Technique geometry, resist normally or exploit the once-per-encounter use.

### Whirlwind · 1–6 slots

- **Eligibility** Genuine Sweep or Wide Sweep on a normal Full Attack
- **Trigger** Resolve legal bodies in one real swing. At least two valid declared targets and full clearance required.
- **Mixed** One extra target per occupied slot; +3/5/7/9/11/13% final Power on eligible Sweep contacts.
- **Full** Same extra target capacity; +6/10/14/18/22/26% final Power on eligible Sweep contacts.
- **Limits and duration** One Full Attack. Use only distinct targets actually within the real clear Sweep. Additional targets gain no extra squad multiplier from the enchantment. No Line or Double Strike extension.
- **Counterplay** Spread out, shelter behind terrain, crowd the swing with impassable obstacles.

### Concussive · 3 slots

- **Eligibility** Melee Blunt, single connected Full Attack
- **Trigger** After one eligible connected Blunt Full Attack, choose one trade based on its resolved injury stages. Keep all stages if you decline. A two-stage hit can trade one for one lost action. A three-stage hit may trade one for one lost action or two for Stunned, never both.
- **Mixed** Spend 1 injury stage to remove 1 action on the target’s next turn, or spend 2 stages to Stun for the whole next turn. Leave at least 1 stage. After a successful trade, gain +5% final Power on the next qualifying attack against that target.
- **Full** The costs, minimum retained stage and action denial are the same. The follow-up Power bonus is +10%.
- **Limits and duration** Choose at most one trade per attack. Control ends after the target’s next turn, then that target has three turns of immunity to Concussive control. The follow-up bonus applies only on the wielder’s next turn against the same target. Concussive grants no free attack or separate Trapped condition. Use the strongest applicable action denial.
- **Counterplay** Keep the impact below two stages, interrupt or deflect the body hit, or use one-action tactics when you lose one action.

### Skirmish · 1–6 slots

- **Eligibility** Legal melee/light thrown attacks after real movement
- **Trigger** During own turn voluntarily travel at least half ordinary Move allowance, rounded up, and finish on a different tile before attacking.
- **Mixed** +22 / 38 / 54 / 70 / 86 / 102% Power; all nonqualifying attacks −8 / 12 / 16 / 20 / 24 / 28%
- **Full** +44 / 76 / 108 / 140 / 172 / 204% Power; same drawback
- **Limits and duration** One body contact this turn. Qualifying Move generally leaves Quick Attack; forced movement and square loops excluded.
- **Counterplay** Hold lanes, block paths, press in close, attack mobility or force stationary exchanges.

### Dash Edge · 1–6 slots

- **Eligibility** Melee or qualified thrown attack after Half Dash
- **Trigger** Spend a legal Half Dash, pay its action, and end on a different tile before the attack. Full Dash consumes both actions.
- **Mixed** +10 / 18 / 26 / 34 / 42 / 50% Power
- **Full** +20 / 36 / 52 / 68 / 84 / 100% Power
- **Limits and duration** One Quick contact that turn. Does not supply extra tiles, action, or Dash Speed outside its approved duration.
- **Counterplay** Block real movement lanes or make the attack line illegal.

### Retribution · 1–6 slots

- **Eligibility** Any body-hit weapon
- **Trigger** When a real hostile attack wounds an ally within 10 ft, or when a qualifying nearby ally is defeated, arm the applicable one-round bonus. Friendly or staged injury does not qualify.
- **Mixed** Wounded +8 / 14 / 20 / 26 / 32 / 38%; defeated +16 / 28 / 40 / 52 / 64 / 76% Power
- **Full** Wounded +16 / 28 / 40 / 52 / 64 / 76%; defeated +32 / 56 / 80 / 104 / 128 / 152%
- **Limits and duration** One next qualifying hit before the end of the wielder’s next turn. Keep the greater live bonus. One wound trigger and one defeat trigger per ally per encounter.
- **Counterplay** Separate allies, control follow-up reach, or withhold committed melee exchanges.

### Berserker · 1–6 slots

- **Eligibility** Any weapon with body contact
- **Trigger** Wielder is Wounded or holding through Shimmer Guard; double own rank bonus when Gravely Wounded.
- **Mixed** Wounded/Guard +3 / 5 / 7 / 9 / 11 / 13%; Grave +6 / 10 / 14 / 18 / 22 / 26% Power
- **Full** Wounded/Guard +6 / 10 / 14 / 18 / 22 / 26%; Grave +12 / 20 / 28 / 36 / 44 / 52%
- **Limits and duration** One hit per action, based on genuine injury at commitment. Temporary ailments or self-inflicted wounds excluded.
- **Counterplay** Deny contact through spacing, or end the fight before a wounded user gets a return swing.

### Spellseal · 1–6 slots

- **Eligibility** Weapon with actual contact on individually identified enchanted equipment
- **Trigger** Physical item contact with an enchanted weapon used to Parry, shield that physically intercepts, or actual armored surface. One mark per item per offensive action; blades do not mark items behind the contacted surface.
- **Mixed** Add Seal Magnitude equal to 8 / 12 / 16 / 20 / 24 / 28% of the reference on an eligible contact.
- **Full** Add 16 / 24 / 32 / 40 / 48 / 49% of the reference. At six slots, the doubled value of 56% is limited by the 49% per-contact cap.
- **Limits and duration** Use max(1, holder’s effective END²) as the reference, fixed at first contact. The MR interaction remains open. At 50% of the reference, suppress one named powered effect for three of the holder’s turns. Accumulation lasts three turns and refreshes on eligible contacts until the threshold. There is no refresh while suppressed. Clear the accumulation before a one-turn grace period. One active suppression per wielder. Permanent stat bonuses, equipment integrity and the Leywell remain active.
- **Counterplay** Avoid item contact, swap the targeted item through legal actions, or ride out the duration.

### Working Edge · 1–6 slots

- **Eligibility** One-handed weapon and genuine spell-and-blade build
- **Trigger** Eligible cast genuinely heals an ally, prevents a credible incoming attack, or lands unresisted against an enemy. A separate offensive spell and weapon attack cannot occur in the same turn.
- **Mixed** +12 / 22 / 32 / 42 / 52 / 62% Power
- **Full** +24 / 44 / 64 / 84 / 104 / 124% Power
- **Limits and duration** One earned contact through the end of the wielder’s next turn; refresh without stacking. Genuine healing or a paid credible defense may enable a same-turn legal attack. A separate offensive spell can only arm a later turn.
- **Actions** Pay the cast’s legal action, compatible MP and real medium. A one-action cast observes floor(full applicable Channel Limit / 2). The weapon attack costs its ordinary action.
- **Counterplay** Deny an eligible cast's outcome, interrupt or separate the follow-up.

### Elemental Wrath · 1–6 slots

- **Eligibility** Any weapon with genuine body contact against status-bearing creatures
- **Trigger** Use the strongest applicable elemental status band on the target immediately before the attack. The weapon itself contributes no Elemental Magnitude. Distinct elements do not stack for this bonus.
- **Mixed** Below 0.5× SR: −12/18/24/30/36/42% final Power. At 0.5×, 1× and 4×: +5%, +10% and +19% final Power per slot, subject to real status thresholds.
- **Full** Same no-status drawback. Eligible bonuses at 0.5×, 1× and 4×: +10%, +20% and +38% final Power per slot.
- **Limits and duration** One hit per action. Use each status’s actual eligibility and threshold, including strict exceedance where applicable. Dormant Lightning Static grants no benefit.
- **Counterplay** Cleanse, spread statuses over several targets, deny feeder contact.

### Mastery · 1–6 slots

- **Eligibility** Any weapon; wielder can perform a legal Technique
- **Trigger** Succeed at a contested meaningful Technique, including relevant movement-control escape, against a genuine opponent.
- **Mixed** +10 / 18 / 26 / 34 / 42 / 50% Power
- **Full** +20 / 36 / 52 / 68 / 84 / 100% Power
- **Limits and duration** One contact through end of next turn; refresh only. No free Technique granted by another rune, opposed friendly drill or duplicate benefit.
- **Counterplay** Contest on stronger terms or move beyond follow-up reach.

### Cleave · 1–6 slots

- **Eligibility** Legal Sweep or Wide Sweep with real clearance
- **Trigger** Resolve contact for all declared targets first, then compute one final Power adjustment using count of distinct body hits.
- **Mixed** 1 hit −4% per slot; 2 +5% per slot; ≥3 +12% per slot
- **Full** 1 hit −4% per slot; 2 +10% per slot; ≥3 +24% per slot
- **Limits and duration** Apply to those hits after independent body routes, before injury; no shield routing changes or repeated target. Ranks beyond normal arcs use only genuine permitted multi-target rules.
- **Counterplay** Spread out, deny full arc, interpose shields or cover.

### Gap Finder · 1–6 slots

- **Eligibility** Weapon capable of a legal real armor-gap called shot
- **Trigger** On a legal successful hit into an actual exposed armor gap, mark that exact gap if defender effective END is at or below 40/65/90/115/140/175 for slots 1–6. New marks help only subsequent attacks into the same gap.
- **Mixed** Each mark refunds one existing called-shot difficulty category against that same gap. Maximum two marks and two total refunded categories.
- **Full** Each mark refunds up to two existing called-shot categories, with the same maximum two total. Full reaches the accuracy cap after one mark when the gap has a two-category penalty.
- **Limits and duration** Gain at most one mark per offensive action. Marks on that gap expire after two of the wielder’s turns without another successful hit into it. The refund is capped at the ordinary legal line’s difficulty. The gap retains its actual AR and coverage. This enchantment supplies an accuracy benefit.
- **Counterplay** Conceal the exact gap, change facing, intercept the physical line or exceed the slot END ceiling.

### Artillerist · 1–6 slots

- **Eligibility** Gun/crossbow on a genuine rated brace with useful firing arc
- **Trigger** Remain stable on existing brace from the start of the turn and fire a lawful single projectile through the supported arc.
- **Mixed** +16 / 28 / 40 / 52 / 64 / 76% Power
- **Full** +32 / 56 / 80 / 104 / 128 / 152% Power
- **Limits and duration** One shot per attack action; bracing and reload use actual cost. No burst, siege or portable bow brace inferred.
- **Counterplay** Attack flank/cover, force relocation or advance during reload.

### Ambush · 1–6 slots

- **Eligibility** Weapon capable of legal strike at target that cannot ordinarily evade
- **Trigger** At commitment, normal dodge is legally unavailable for that threat (including actual Trapped); gear, cover and barriers still apply.
- **Mixed** +16 / 28 / 40 / 52 / 64 / 76% Power if eligible; otherwise −7 / 10 / 13 / 16 / 19 / 22%
- **Full** +32 / 56 / 80 / 104 / 128 / 152% if eligible; same penalty
- **Limits and duration** One body hit per attack; merely being Pressured or slow does not qualify. No automatic hit.
- **Counterplay** Retain escape paths, break traps, use shield or cover.

### Strafing · 1–6 slots

- **Eligibility** Reach weapon with genuine existing control boundary
- **Trigger** A real hostile move across your legal control zone fails its Technique contest. Your next eligible body hit against that same target gains the bonus.
- **Mixed** +16 / 28 / 40 / 52 / 64 / 76% Power against that creature
- **Full** +32 / 56 / 80 / 104 / 128 / 152% Power
- **Limits and duration** One hit through the end of next turn; cannot trigger a hit during the movement contest. No repeated same-turn failure farming.
- **Counterplay** Take a different path, win the contest, force wielder off its line, or wait out the mark.

### Double Edge · 1–6 slots

- **Eligibility** Legally equipped weapon making a genuine secondary strike
- **Trigger** Use the enchanted weapon for a genuine secondary attack. A legal offhand strike in Double Strike and a listed secondary weapon form are both eligible.
- **Mixed** +16 / 28 / 40 / 52 / 64 / 76% of the secondary attack’s otherwise final Power.
- **Full** +32 / 56 / 80 / 104 / 128 / 152% of the secondary attack’s otherwise final Power.
- **Limits and duration** One qualifying secondary hit per attack action. In Double Strike, apply the established ×0.60 to the empowered hit. A listed secondary form retains its own F, bypass, reach and legality. If both routes qualify, apply one bonus to that contact.
- **Counterplay** Keep outside offhand reach, force a one-action turn, shield the secondary line.

### Backstab · 1–6 slots

- **Eligibility** Any legally deliverable rear body strike
- **Trigger** Earn ordinary rear attack through actual relative facing and credible repositioning. Adjudicate geometry before rolls.
- **Mixed** +12 / 22 / 32 / 42 / 52 / 62% Power
- **Full** +24 / 44 / 64 / 84 / 104 / 124% Power
- **Limits and duration** One lawful rear body contact per attack action. No bonus for stat-only flank fiction.
- **Counterplay** Turn, reposition or protect your rear, cut off alternate routes.

### Sniper · 1–6 slots

- **Eligibility** Ranged single shot with legal Aim at Far or Extreme
- **Trigger** Spend legal Aim on same tracked target and fire within existing Aim validity.
- **Mixed** +12 / 20 / 28 / 36 / 44 / 52% Power
- **Full** +24 / 40 / 56 / 72 / 88 / 104% Power
- **Limits and duration** One valid shot. Aim may be interrupted; must obey real optic, user and weapon reach constraints. No stacking with brace bonus on a contact.
- **Counterplay** Break tracking, close the gap or use real cover.

### Silence · 1–6 slots

- **Eligibility** A weapon or launcher emitting real sound in its normal operation
- **Trigger** Activate for the ordinary five-round powered window and apply the effect for the occupied slots.
- **Mixed** 1 slot: half weapon sound. 2 slots: 10% of normal weapon sound. 3 slots: silent weapon. 4 slots: silent weapon and ambient sound at 90% of normal within 5 ft. 5 slots: a 10-ft silence zone. 6 slots: a 25-ft silence zone.
- **Full** At slots 1–3, use the Mixed effects. At slot 4, silence the weapon and reduce ambient sound to 80% of normal within 5 ft. At slots 5 and 6, use 20-ft and 50-ft silence zones respectively.
- **Limits and duration** The effects last five rounds. Sound zones affect allies and opponents.
- **Counterplay** Use line of sight, signals, sources outside radius or alarmed observers.

### Bolt Maven · 1–6 slots

- **Eligibility** Ranged load system with two distinct approved compatible ammunition forms
- **Trigger** Actually fire alternating valid ammunition types. For a clip, fire at least one shot from the previous clip, then complete a legal reload with a distinct type.
- **Mixed** +12 / 22 / 32 / 42 / 52 / 62% Power
- **Full** +24 / 44 / 64 / 84 / 104 / 124% Power
- **Limits and duration** One contact per eligible shot. Clip charges equal genuine shots spent from previous clip, up to real clip capacity. Dry fire, returned shots, empty cycling and same-type magazines grant no credit.
- **Actions** Pay real firing and reload actions and use actual ammunition.
- **Counterplay** Force poorly matched ammo choices, defend during reload, or interrupt ammunition changes.

### Boomerang · 3 slots

- **Eligibility** Reusable thrown weapon with open, lawful orbit and real safe clearance
- **Trigger** After a physically eligible thrown attack misses cleanly, the weapon orbits the target for two target turns. The target must meet the level-appropriate Speed limit. If the target becomes genuinely Trapped within that time and remains on the open orbit line, resolve the stored hit.
- **Mixed** 1 credible opposing PP, up to 2 target turns. Conditional orbit hit ≤35% of original final Power.
- **Full** Same +1 PP and two turns; conditional orbit hit ≤70% of original Power.
- **Limits and duration** The effect lasts at most two of the target’s turns and does not stack. The Trapped opening triggers the stored hit without a second independent accuracy roll. Resolve applicable physical interception normally. Count at most one return against the offensive action budget. The exact Speed cutoff and the scheduling of the delayed hit within that budget remain open.
- **Actions** Pay for the initial throw and account for the delayed hit under the ordinary offensive action budget.
- **Counterplay** Leave the fixed orbit or block the return line, catch/ground the throw if legally possible, or avoid becoming Trapped.

### Point Blank · 1–6 slots

- **Eligibility** Lawful ranged single shot; no concentrated burst
- **Trigger** Shot is in the weapon's own Close band. Effective neutral; Far and Extreme draw penalty.
- **Mixed** Close +12 / 22 / 32 / 42 / 52 / 62%; Far/Extreme −6 / 12 / 18 / 24 / 30 / 36% Power
- **Full** Close +24 / 44 / 64 / 84 / 104 / 124%; same penalty
- **Limits and duration** One shot, no change to range legality. Applies after independent projectile Power calculation, never stacks on the separate automatic-fire concentrated mode.
- **Counterplay** Open range, wield legitimate cover, engage the shooter at a reload opportunity.

### Rebound · 1–6 slots

- **Eligibility** A reusable projectile with an intact return route or firearm with special compatible feed
- **Trigger** A powered legal ranged single shot misses cleanly. Return that spent projectile to its wielder. A firearm round returns directly to its own clip as one loaded shot. A shield or Parry interception is contact and cannot trigger a return.
- **Mixed** +10/18/26/34/42/50% final Power on the rebounded projectile’s next legal attack.
- **Full** +20/36/52/68/84/100% final Power on that projectile’s next legal attack.
- **Limits and duration** One return and one empowered refire for each originally expended projectile. Refund only the registered empty clip position on that firearm. Arrows, bolts and reusable throws are returned to hand or ready storage and still need ordinary loading when applicable. Refire still spends the normal action and valid ammunition.
- **Actions** Pay the ordinary action and ammunition cost for the initial shot and its refire.
- **Counterplay** Interpose a shield or Parry where physically eligible, break the return line or remove the subsequent shot opportunity.

### Second Face · 3 slots

- **Eligibility** Single enchanted weapon with two pre-inscribed approved shapes and shared real construction
- **Trigger** Spend one action to switch between the enchanted item’s two pre-inscribed legal weapon forms. The new form has its own legitimate handling, reach and attack modes.
- **Mixed** +18% Power after a legitimate switch
- **Full** +36% Power
- **Limits and duration** The bonus lasts three rounds, including the change round, and never stacks with itself. At most one swap per encounter grants the bonus. Damage and Leywell state carry across forms.
- **Actions** Spend one action per legal form switch. Any follow-up costs its ordinary attack action.
- **Counterplay** Force a switch at a bad time, contest the new form's reach, or wait out its surge.

### Riposte Memory · 1–6 slots

- **Eligibility** Weapon eligible for the legal prepared Parry used
- **Trigger** Resolve a legal prepared Parry against the foe. Use the earned Parry result to enhance the next-turn weapon attack against that same foe.
- **Mixed** +12 / 22 / 32 / 42 / 52 / 62% Power
- **Full** +24 / 44 / 64 / 84 / 104 / 124% Power
- **Limits and duration** One eligible next-turn body hit. Use the ordinary Parry rule to determine which intercepts count, including its treatment of clean misses.
- **Counterplay** Feint, attack off-line, force use against a different attacker or exit reach.

### Boundary Bite · 1–6 slots

- **Eligibility** Weapon legally attacking at its outermost reachable tile
- **Trigger** Body contact at the outermost reach of the actual form. Short-only weapon's single ordinary tile is not a distinct outer band.
- **Mixed** +9 / 16 / 23 / 30 / 37 / 44% Power
- **Full** +18 / 32 / 46 / 60 / 74 / 88% Power
- **Limits and duration** One body hit. Medium lunge keeps its harder category; Long must actually preserve the outer tile.
- **Counterplay** Close or withdraw beyond reach, deny clean geometry.

### Step in Time · 1–6 slots

- **Eligibility** Weapon and one ally with independent legal lines on the same foe
- **Trigger** Legally delay into the ally's initiative, then declare a coordinated pair of attacks. Route both attacks before adding damage; apply this rune only if both reach bodies.
- **Mixed** Both contacts +7 / 12 / 17 / 22 / 27 / 32% Power
- **Full** Both +14 / 24 / 34 / 44 / 54 / 64% Power
- **Limits and duration** One paired benefit per encounter for each participant; can only use a table-supported timing method. No rotation around initiative to gain turns.
- **Counterplay** Break one line, block one attack, separate the pair, or use real cover.

### Exposed Stitch · 1–6 slots

- **Eligibility** Weapon reaching a genuine exposed body region
- **Trigger** Declare an already legal exposed-region called shot. Maintain actual obscurity and reach penalties. Connect with that real region.
- **Mixed** +12 / 21 / 30 / 39 / 48 / 57% Power
- **Full** +24 / 42 / 60 / 78 / 96 / 114% Power
- **Limits and duration** One body contact per action. Armor absence must be real; aimed head rule, when applicable, is adjudicated independently.
- **Counterplay** Cover the exposed region or change relative facing and reach.

### Practiced Return · 1–6 slots

- **Eligibility** One weapon with approved primary and genuinely available secondary attack
- **Trigger** On the previous turn use its real secondary; then connect with primary against the same target.
- **Mixed** +10 / 18 / 26 / 34 / 42 / 50% Power
- **Full** +20 / 36 / 52 / 68 / 84 / 100% Power
- **Limits and duration** One contact next turn. No grip flourish or use of an improvised uncalibrated mode satisfies the setup.
- **Counterplay** Deny the primary's clearance, interrupt cadence, or leave range.

### Rank Measure · 1 slot

- **Eligibility** Two adjacent allied reach users facing a common valid lane
- **Trigger** On a real attack declaration, sense whether up to two allied weapon paths would cross your weapon's chosen line.
- **Mixed** Read 2 planned lines up to 10 ft away
- **Full** Read 4 planned lines within the same 10 ft
- **Limits and duration** Five-round detection window, one declaration check each turn. Read physical paths only. No turning a blocked path into a legal one.
- **Counterplay** Separate allies, use independent lines, or attack from an unseen direction.

### Hand Over Hand · 1–6 slots

- **Eligibility** One-handed body-hit weapon, with or without shield offhand
- **Trigger** Gain the frontal cone benefit only on a credible legal front attack. If the wielder is genuinely flanked at commitment, replace this benefit with the listed penalty. A shield is optional.
- **Mixed** +8 / 14 / 20 / 26 / 32 / 38% Power in cone; flanked −8 / 12 / 16 / 20 / 24 / 28%
- **Full** +16 / 28 / 40 / 52 / 64 / 76%; same flank penalty
- **Limits and duration** One frontal contact. No free refresh of facing during attacker resolution. No extra reaction or shield modifier.
- **Counterplay** Open a second credible line or pull the wielder away from the cone.

## Shield enchantment

### Guardian · 1–6 slots

- **Eligibility** A usable wielded shield, an eligible ward path and one identifiable ongoing hostile spell or enchantment on the bearer.
- **Trigger** Spend one action to dismiss or disengage from the shield and suppress the chosen ongoing effect on yourself.
- **Mixed** Eligible opposing effect-rating ceiling of 0.50 / 0.75 / 1 / 1.25 / 1.50 / 1.75 × your applicable SR at slots 1–6.
- **Full** Double the eligible rating ceiling. The same action cost and shield surrender apply.
- **Limits and duration** One use per encounter. The shield provides no protection for the whole suppression. The original effect’s duration clock continues. Suppression lasts at most three turns and cannot outlast the remaining original duration.
- **Counterplay** Pressure the bearer while shield protection is withdrawn, use independent effects or use a threat outside the scope of suppression.
- **Open procedure** Final duration matching and the conversion of an opposing spell or enchantment into a comparable effect rating.

## Remaining implementation decisions

Per-enchantment colors and Leywell MP bands, the final six-slot economy, Spellseal’s MR interaction, Guardian’s cross-effect rating and duration matching, Boomerang’s Speed cutoff and delayed-action timing, and the still-open trip and disarm procedures remain to be specified.
