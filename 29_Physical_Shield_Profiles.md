## November 13, 2026 — Shield automatic interception, single-wound ceramic shatter, Junk shields and provisional repairs (LATEST AUTHOR APPROVED)

**Newest express approval controls over any older rules below that disagree.** This is an addendum to the previously approved Boughsteel 18/24/31 Quality-I through 105/140/180 Quality-V shield class Ratings and their conventional material factors. **Do not change** existing Boughsteel/Trufula/Adamantite Ratings, stat gates, baseline Gem prices, `STR F1.05` single bash, Light/Medium shield Double Strike, Heavy shield Double Strike prohibition, or 0% inherent shield SPD. Ordinary shields still have three usable condition states (Intact, Dented, Cracked) before Broken.

### Automatic shielding and the explicit synchronized-flank exception (APPROVED)

**Default qualified Guard:** While genuinely **wielding a usable shield in Guard**, and **not under Extreme Pressure (net three or more meaningful PP)**, the shield **AUTOMATICALLY INTERCEPTS any otherwise successful incoming blockable physical attack** that would hit its bearer. This applies to ordinary melee or physically interceptable ranged projectiles. There is **no declaration, reaction roll, separate shield-success check, per-projectile reflex/timing test or arc-selection bookkeeping** for the default one-attacker case. Shield coverage is treated as always protecting the wielder against this ordinary eligible attack; no further fixed facing cone, behind-the-back exception or exact projectile transit calculation applies just to obtain the intercept. **Ordinary attacker hit/evasion rules still determine whether the attack would connect**; automatically blocking does NOT turn a missed attack into a hit or make the shield permanently indestructible. If it would hit, direct the **one contact** to Shield Defense/its applicable condition track; continue torso overflow only on a breaking contact. Real shields cannot block attacks that are physically non-interceptable (e.g. pure mental/nonphysical effects), and a shield not held/usable/being used in Guard does not give a free automatic block.

**Existing stance choice still matters:** Voluntary **Deflect** explicitly trades away active Guard interception and deliberate body-line obstruction for one credible offhand Defense/Pressure counteraction; Light/Medium **weapon+shield Double Strike** likewise gives up deliberate Guard interception, deflection and obstruction and incurs its existing self-PP. Neither mode receives this automatic Guard block. **Extreme Pressure (net ≥3 PP)** prevents deliberate and automatic Guard blocking, but an actually prepositioned physical shield may still be struck as an incidental real object under the original collision procedure. Normal legal incapacitation, loss of possession or a shield already Broken do not create phantom protection.

**Adjacent-initiative coordinated flank (APPROVED):** Two actually positioned attackers **flanking the shieldbearer**, whose initiative entries are **adjacent in the current initiative order**, may **choose to make their otherwise legal attacks simultaneously**. Both consume their normal eligible attack actions; initiative adjacency is the simple timing criterion for this no-extra-delay case. The shieldbearer **chooses ONE of the two simultaneous threats to intercept automatically** while eligible for Guard. Resolve the other attack **against its intended body target using its own ordinary hit, evasion, END, relevant armor and other legal defense**; the shield does NOT block both. The unblocked attack is **not an automatic hit, bonus penetration, guaranteed rear hit or free extra PP**, and two attacks are not merged into one larger shield-Power packet. If one attempt independently misses, it remains a miss. The existing approved option for a faster attacker to delay to synchronize with an ally remains available under its own conditions; this explicit adjacent-initiative case does not erase those existing legal delays. Additional unusual mass synchronized-attack conflicts and specific nonphysical attack types remain for future action/Proficiency work, rather than inventing a new reaction budget now.

**Obstruction precedence:** The previously approved Guard body-target obstruction values **Light +0, Medium +1, Heavy +1** are retained for a genuinely applicable attempted body line **not simultaneously being resolved as one of these automatic shield-intercepted contacts**. Never grant **both** a shield's +1 body-line difficulty shift **and** its automatic shield interception as two layers for the **same physical interference**. Because default block now intercepts ordinary successful body hits, the number of circumstances in which +1 is separately operative is narrower; evaluate that practical utility later rather than silently deleting this approved category modifier.

### Ceramic load-bearing shields (APPROVED BREAKAGE RULE AND CALIBRATION)

**A ceramic shield must provide comparatively HIGH Shield Defense for its effective STR handling requirement while intact, but shatter as soon as it is actually WOUNDED.** In this shield context **"wounded" means an intercepted physical contact strong enough to cause at least ONE ordinary shield durability loss** under the current `P ≥ D` injury threshold, where `D = (actual shield Rating)²`. An ordinary contact below `D` does not wound or damage it. **At the first contact with `P ≥ D`, the entire ceramic load-bearing shield IMMEDIATELY SHATTERS/BREAKS**, losing all shield protective function until genuine repair. There is **NO Intact→Dented→Cracked progression**, no second chance from otherwise surviving one or two condition losses, and no partial defense degradation while intact. No healing restores the shield.

**Approved breaking-hit overflow unchanged in principle:** Because this is a genuine breaking shield interception, apply the previously approved residual absorption rather than sending the attacker an unmitigated full hit: let `D = Rating²`, the ceramic shield has its **full three ordinary-capacity units still available immediately before this first wound**, and `P_torso = max(0, P_attack − 3D)` on the shattering blow. If `P` is between `D` and `3D`, the shield shatters and the torso receives **zero residual Power**. A truly overpowering `P>3D` may carry Power through the same legal torso-defense sequence. This does not transfer ceramic armor's separate wound-severity-triggered breakage procedure to shields, or create an extra body attack.

**Approved physical calibration:** Balanced B applies to legal load-bearing ceramics. **Glassweave** uses **×1.25 physical Rating, ×0.95 support STR, ×1.75 magical Rating**. **Nullporcelain** uses **×1.15 physical Rating, ×0.75 support STR, ×3 magical Rating**. Apply the magical multiplier to Rating before squaring for eligible Guard contacts. First-physical-wound shatter and breaking-blow overflow still apply. Ceramic shield prices follow the existing standard Gem rules. Legal exotic recipes and sourcing remain unresolved.

**SUPERSEDES** the preceding report's ceramic shield-only 0 losses through 0.75D, 2 losses from above 0.75D through 1.75D, and 3 losses thereafter. Those former multiple-condition ceramic bands are **NOT OPERATIVE**. Also supersedes older rules claiming ceramic shields can be Dented or Cracked from ordinary shield contact without immediate shattering.

### Junk shields (APPROVED)

**Junk shield = Quality I, Boughsteel-based**, as for existing ordinary Junk equipment. In contrast to an ordinary shield's **three usable durability conditions** (Intact, Dented, Cracked), a Junk shield has only **TWO usable conditions, Intact (0 losses) and Dented (1 loss), followed by Broken at 2 accumulated losses**. Dented retains full remaining Junk shield functionality; Broken has none.

**Junk shield Defense Power is `D_Junk = 0.90 × D_normal`, where `D_normal=(matching ordinary QI Boughsteel Shield Rating)²`.** This is **−10% to final Shield Defense Power**, NOT −10% to Shield Rating before squaring (which would inadvertently reduce defense by 19%). For a Junk shield, compare intercepted `P` to **its own `D_Junk`** using ordinary 0/1/2/3 contact-loss thresholds at `D_Junk`, `2D_Junk`, `3D_Junk`, then **cap losses at its two remaining-condition limit**. A two-loss or three-loss contact from Intact breaks it immediately. On a Junk breaking blow, count **`R = 2 − prior losses`** and use `P_torso = max(0, P_attack − R×D_Junk)` once. Other Junk weapon/armor hit-category penalties **do not automatically apply** to a shield solely because its Junk shield is held.

By the existing **half-comparable-Quality-I** Junk valuation rule, ordinary QI Light/Medium/Heavy shield Gems **15/30/30** imply Junk QI shield valuations **7.5/15/15 Gems** respectively (7 Gems and 50 Glints for Junk Light). This is a derivation of the already-approved general Junk pricing, not a new price multiplier. No special Junk enchantment, heightened conditional obstruction or STR requirement discount is added by this decision.

## Approved ordinary physical shield contact, durability and overflow

The existing durability track is **Intact (0 losses) → Dented (1) → Cracked (2) → Broken (3)**. Dented and Cracked remain **FULLY functional**. Physical actual-contact `P` compared against `D = Rating²` causes:
- `P < D`: **0 losses**.
- `D ≤ P < 2D`: **1 loss**.
- `2D ≤ P < 3D`: **2 losses**.
- `P ≥ 3D`: **3 losses**.

Apply no more losses than the shield has remaining, and never add an independent HP pool. The shield grants its full interception benefit while not Broken. Its **already approved only-on-break Power overflow** is resolved **only when a real shield-intercepted blow newly breaks it**. Let `R = 3 − losses already accumulated before this hit`. When the new blow breaks the shield, apply `P_body = max(0, P_attack − R × D)` *once* to the wearer's **torso** against the wearer's own effective END, real torso Armor Rating and the actual attack's standard body-armor penetration. If the hit does not break the shield, **zero attack Power passes through to the body**, even if the attack caused one or two shield losses. Existing approval of this formula/condition is preserved.

**Example:** Quality V Boughsteel Medium `Rating 140, D 19,600`. `P=45,000` takes 2 losses from Intact, **no torso overflow**. From Dented, `R=2`, the same hit breaks the shield and passes `max(0,45,000−39,200)=5,800` remaining Power toward ordinary torso defense. Actual contact from an incoming Double Strike or more than one opponent is resolved **per separate credible hit**, not merged into one combined larger blow. One facing cannot actively catch simultaneous opposite-front/rear threats.

## HISTORICAL manual-interception geometry and stances (SUPERSEDED by automatic Guard interception above where incompatible; Guard/Deflect/Double Strike choices retained)

- **Light Guard** grants **no automatic hit-difficulty category shift**, but still allows a real active interception on a physically possible line. **Medium and Heavy Guard** increase hit difficulty **by one category** only against an otherwise open body-target attack line genuinely obstructed by the shield's physical edge; apply this **once**. Heavy's larger surface may physically obstruct more distinct lines, but where Medium also obstructs the same line, both give exactly **+1**, NOT Heavy +2. No universal shield-facing arc, automatic 360° dodge, free category bump to uncovered limbs, or separate percentage-of-body coverage has been approved.
- A shot that actually contacts the shield is resolved as a **shield interception/contact** and not additionally as a separate body-target obstruction penalty. A true wall/cover may physically block the path entirely; do not count the same shield surface as both ordinary independent cover and new shield obstruction. An unobstructed flank, gap or limb gains **no shield category bonus**.
- **Guard (all shields):** One normal main-hand attack OR one **single** approved `STR F1.05` Narrow Bludgeoning shield bash as the attack action; eligible deliberate active block and shield positioning remain available. The ordinary 25% Bludgeoning body-armor bypass applies to a real bash hit, not to an incoming shield contact. There is no extra free bash on every block.
- **Deflect (all shields):** Voluntarily forgo **deliberate active block and intentional body-line obstruction** to gain the already-approved normal credible offhand Defense response, which can counter **one qualifying incoming Pressure Point** when physically addressable. An incidental physical shield collision is still a real object interaction, not a deliberate active intercept.
- **Double Strike (Light/Medium only):** Two independently eligible attacks in one action, one weapon and one shield bash, each **60% of its own final physical Power**, one real target per hand, the existing single self-imposed PP through start of next turn, and **no deliberate active shield block, shield-body obstruction, Deflect or regular offhand Attack/Defense benefit**. **Heavy shields may never Double Strike**, but can perform one full-Power normal F1.05 bash under eligible Guard. Heavy-armored fighters remain eligible for ordinary Double Strike with two genuinely separate legal weapons if they are not wielding a Heavy shield.
- Active shield interceptions still require an **aware, qualified, physically credible defense under net 3 PP**. The existing trapped, Extreme Pressure, real attack geometry, actual reach, attention to specific direction, and synchronized two-opponent opposite-angle rules are not overwritten. The report did **not** establish a new reaction-action economy, interception interrupt timing or universal projectile auto-parry.

**Shield bash material and Quality rule boundary:** Keep the **already-approved plain F1.05** (STR-led, Bludgeoning Narrow) as the **ordinary shield bash baseline across conventional shield materials and Qualities**. Do NOT automatically import material-specific *weapon* F bonuses, workmanship bash-F bonuses, special bash-head profiles or an extra attack from the shield's defensive Rating unless separately authorized by its actual offensive construction. Existing independently approved offensive item effects, if actually legal and operative, still apply under their own rules.
