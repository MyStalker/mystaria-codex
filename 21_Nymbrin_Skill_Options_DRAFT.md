# Nymbrin technique selection — PROPOSAL, NOT CANON
Twelve selectable concepts for each of the six approved Wetcraft disciplines. These are candidates to assemble into rank progressions, not twelve automatically granted abilities. Select multiple favorites, reorder or supply a custom field. No choices have yet been made.

## Design constraints
- Active ranks should open new targeting, placement, timing, movement, rescue and cooperation opportunities rather than repeat an effect with larger numbers.
- Crafting follows the centaur modular draft's emphasis on distinct functions, tiered/costed power, real inputs and logistics. That centaur redesign remains noncanon.
- All six disciplines generate hex. Containment relocates the burden; it never erases it. No unlimited contamination-free cleansing loop.
- Consumable strength is tied to paid Gem-equivalent cost. Skill access is not free stock. Yield, delivery, reach, duration, targets and reliability all enter pricing.
- Calibrate a representative loadout against 0.30 × a Hero's typical Gem budget over the same explicit progression/adventure window. The 30% is an allocation target, not a free stipend, maximum allowed spend or discount. Exact budget income/window and prices are unresolved; permanent equipment prices are not per-dose prices.
- Resisted offensive effects need counters. Consent is stated for voluntary adaptations; hostile transformation is not an automatic instant defeat.
- Permanent body alterations do not mint free SP, attribute increases or unpriced extra actions. Mass and feedstocks, preparation, hex and ongoing bodily support remain relevant.
- SP, MP, Gem costs, numerical thresholds, action timings and final rank placements await selection and balance approval.
