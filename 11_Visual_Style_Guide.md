**Authority:** Visual design reference. **Status:** The five mana emblem designs, Mystaria’s signature purple identity, purple Shimmer, and purple/red sky tendency are **USER-APPROVED VISUAL/WORLD CANON**; other art direction below is either an established presentation preference or a proposed production guideline, as labeled.

**Scope:** Illustration and presentation of the Mystaria Living Codex, including the website, future rulebooks, academic in-world diagrams, and related art.

## 1. Approved five-color mana emblems — VISUAL CANON

The **reference image is the source of truth for the actual emblem designs**. Text below is an identification and usage guide, not a replacement for the image.

**Approved sequence:** Red → Blue → White → Green → Black, when the five colors are shown as a complete set. This is the order used in the approved artwork, not a claim of rank or hierarchy.

**Design use:** These are recurring identification marks whenever a mana color is represented in rulebooks, magical scholarship, academic diagrams, spell indexes, charts, and similar contexts. They should remain identifiable even in compact presentations. Use the reference artwork to maintain their shapes; do not substitute generic unrelated fire, drop, star, leaf, or skull icons as the official designs.

**Important lore boundary:** The descriptive taglines printed on the original emblem sheet (such as “Fire · Force · Passion”) are *artwork captions*, **not newly approved statements of mana philosophy, powers, or mechanical effects**. For actual mana lore, use `03_Sorcery.md` and other approved subject dossiers.

## 2. Established art direction for the Living Codex

### November 4, 2026 — Approved purple identity and atmospheric color

**USER-APPROVED VISUAL CANON — Mystaria's signature color:** **Purple** is the primary identifying color of *Mystaria* as a publication and setting brand. Give it particular prominence on title pages and in recurring backgrounds, framing, and decorative elements throughout the digital rulebook. Purple should recur frequently, without sacrificing the legibility of mechanical tables or overpowering other mana colors.

**USER-APPROVED WORLD/INTERFACE CANON — the Shimmer:** The Shimmer's signature color is **purple**. Its patron-reflecting appearance, established in `01_World_and_Shimmer.md`, may still vary in other respects; the purple identity should remain recognizable. This ruling does not alter Shimmer functions, patrons, or progression mechanics.

**USER-APPROVED WORLD CANON — skies:** Mystaria's skies **tend toward shades of purple** and are **occasionally red**. This describes a characteristic tendency, not a permanent color at every hour, in every region, or under every weather condition. The exact cause and frequency have not been established.

**Important scope boundary:** Purple is not a requirement for all in-world architecture, clothing, plants, species, or magic. Keep the physical world diverse in color and mood. In particular, purple as Mystaria's visual identity does **not** create a sixth mana color or replace the five approved mana emblems.

**Not yet specified:** An exact purple hex code, print color standard, gradient system, and palette proportions remain **artist-directed / undecided**. Do not present suggested color codes as established canon.

**User-selected presentation preferences:**

- **Living storybook** aesthetic for lore: painterly, atmospheric, whimsical and sometimes unsettling; make room for Mystaria's cruelty, moral complexity and divine contradictions.
- **Readable tabletop reference** treatment for rules and numbers: structured tables, clear typography, restrained decoration, legible contrast and useful navigation.
- A coherent shared visual language across the website introduction and the full Codex. The website is the preferred reading format; do **not** prioritize further development of a separate introduction PDF.
- Every one of the thirteen major Codex volumes and each race page should receive a distinctive illustration.
- Illustrate lore-oriented expandable sections generously. Avoid cluttering mechanics-only dropdowns with decorative pictures.
- **Page placement:** place the principal illustration at the **top of the page**.
- **Lore dropdown placement:** put the illustration **beneath the descriptive text** within the expanded section.
- **Deep lore spoilers:** preserve intentional click-to-reveal behavior; do not reveal hidden secrets through unguarded images or thumbnails.
- The user is the artistic decision-maker. Offer **at least three composition candidates** for each illustration before treating an illustration concept as selected.

### Approved Drider visual anatomy — November 4, 2026

Driders have dark skin reminiscent of a drow elf, pointed ears, **two arms**, and **six long spider-like legs** supporting an enormous arachnid lower body. They have **four solid-color eyes with no pupils**; eye color varies between individuals. Their long legs and imposing bodies make them **one of Mystaria's largest mortal races**. Depict exactly two arms, six spider-like legs and four pupil-less, solid-color eyes. Preserve the humanlike upper body and arachnid lower body. Earlier four-armed Drider illustrations and descriptions are superseded. Exact dimensions, eye arrangement and precise skin tones remain unspecified. This correction applies to Driders and does not change Arrest's own appearance. See `02_Races.md` for the owning racial description.

## 3. Proposed production guidelines — NOT YET APPROVED VISUAL CANON

The following are practical recommendations for future art production, not immutable rules:

- Prepare a full-color detailed emblem set, a compact emblem set, and a monochrome/high-contrast set **derived from the approved motifs**, subject to the artist's approval.
- Consider both light and dark background versions, while preserving the approved silhouette and meaning.
- For website performance, create optimized image sizes and accessible text labels; retain the high-resolution approved reference unchanged.
- Keep individual illustration credits and version notes next to each art asset.
- Store art decisions as explicit approvals in this guide rather than assuming a generated draft is canonical.

## 4. Art approval workflow

1. **Candidate:** Propose a concept, subject, composition and location in the Codex.
2. **Artist selection:** The user chooses a candidate or supplies an alternative and may request revisions.
3. **Approved visual:** Mark only explicitly approved designs as approved; preserve their source images.
4. **Integration:** Add approved assets to the website at the specified placement and link the relevant page or dropdown.
5. **Revision record:** Log replaced motifs or superseded assets, and update the Canon Index if the visual reference structure changes.
