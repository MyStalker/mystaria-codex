# Mystaria — Visual Style Guide and Approved Mana Emblems

### November 8, 2026 — Kharak provocative punk civilian dress (CANON)

Adult Kharak civilian clothing strongly favors **provocative punk / Coil Street Samurai** styling: asymmetric fitted dense-woven crop tops, high-waisted reinforced trousers, broad utility belts, exposed Tatao, and **leather jackets worn open or cape-style**. Approved variations include cropped/open vests, halter or open-back tops, fur-trimmed jackets, and Warclan tooth ornaments. Bare scales, shoulders, midriff, back and upper chest are socially ordinary in Mystaria; do not default to skirts or over-cover Kharak females. Tail-friendly tailoring and powerful tyrannosaur anatomy take priority. These styles are options, not uniforms, and men can wear similarly revealing looks. Precise collars and reference art remain pending.

### November 7, 2026 — Kharak urban fashion and war kit (APPROVED DIRECTION)

Kharak favor **dense woven cloth** in ordinary dress, with traditional fur accents and **leather jackets sometimes worn cape-style over the shoulders**. Combine Industrial Vanguard, Coil Street Samurai and Hearthborn Bruiser influences, **slightly prioritizing Coil Street Samurai**. Female casual fashion should recover Mystaria's mature sensuality through anatomical tailoring and confident silhouettes, but **no exact outfit cuts have been approved yet**. Imperial peacetime soldiers, including monster hunters and military police overseeing enslaved people, usually wear **light protection**. All-out war gear should evoke **Viking-inspired fur and teeth ornamentation**, industrial fantasy and subtle **power-armor-like aesthetics** without implying actual powered armor technology. **Thornguard** retain a contrasting traditional **knight-and-rogue** aesthetic. See `33_Kharak_Rexfolk_Culture_and_Command.md`.

### November 7, 2026 — Kharak industrial Imperial aesthetic (APPROVED DIRECTION)

Kharak possess **fully developed humanoid arms and hands**, a key distinction from Thornguard; they are upright, strong tyrannosaur people suited to common military equipment, not short-armed dinosaurs or exotic Draykin. Imperial fashion embraces **Kobold-influenced technological manufacture**, near-modern utility, dark practical clothing, industrial clean-lined melee weapons, and fantasy detailing. Broad visual inspirations: **street samurai from Cyberpunk** and **Cloud Strife's industrial military styling in Final Fantasy VII Remake**, as inspiration only, not literal copied outfits or weapons. Casual clothing leans toward **furs and leather jackets, particularly for men**. Precise uniforms, material recipes, armor, weapon specs, collars and master art remain unapproved. See `33_Kharak_Rexfolk_Culture_and_Command.md`.

### November 6, 2026 — Duskpaw salvage and Lastwear clothing (CANON)

Free/self-chosen Duskpaw attire exceptionally often incorporates torn, patched, mismatched, asymmetrical and reclaimed garments, including formal clothing. **Lastwear** visibly recalls the former wearer's death, through surviving real wear or damage, and may be scavenged, memorial or provocative trophy attire. Faux battle damage is commonly ridiculed for wastefulness. Reclaimed open locks can be worn as emancipation symbols, distinct from compulsory Nightlock collars. **Enslaved Duskpaws in the Scarlands or Imperium may be required to wear tidy, identifiable work uniforms rather than their personally preferred free patchwork aesthetic.** No specific Duskpaw uniform shape has yet been approved. See `32_Duskpaw_Fashion_and_Lastwear.md`; keep species anatomy and approved visual style.


### November 6, 2026 — Duskpaw Lastwear terminology (CANON)

**Lastwear** names Duskpaw clothing **visibly associated with its previous wearer's death**. Do not universalize specific wounds, bloodstains, distress patterns, ceremonial symbolism, or legally proven origin: these additional details await approval. The author's preferred direction also emphasizes torn, mixed and reused clothing, but no definitive outfit sheet has been approved. Preserve Duskpaw anatomy, previously approved Nightlock collars and open-lock emancipation symbol. See `32_Duskpaw_Fashion_and_Lastwear.md`.


### November 6, 2026 — Approved Bloomkin Bannerweave and Twinmantle

In Thornguard and Bloomkin paired fashion, **Bannerweave** uses **Bloomkin-led, complementary heraldry**: a grand caped knight and a smaller, perfectly tailored squire or Shieldmate whose insignia, trim and colors complete the design rather than duplicate it. **Twinmantle** means especially coordinated outfits. Tiny, grandiose heroic coats, capes and accessories should project **awe**, not generic servile cuteness; heroic legends inspire designs and **purple is revered** among Bloomkin. Preserve 3–4-foot mousefolk vs 5–7-foot ratfolk scale and actual functional outfits, along with unchanged collar conventions. The **First Fitting** is an important tailoring milestone. Owning reference: `31_Bloomkin_Bannerweave_Fashion.md`. Individual art still needs user approval.


### November 6, 2026 — Approved Moontouched fashion cues

In Moontouched casual fashion favor soft **sweaters and socks for warmth**, while **pantyhose are common in formal clothing**. Maid outfits belong to maid employment and maid cafés or similar popular themed hospitality, **not every scene featuring rabbitfolk**. ### November 6, 2026 — Approved Tidekin Imperial clothing

Depict Tidekin in **close-fitting, aquatic, swimmer-friendly clothing**; **bikini-style tops are very common in casual wear**. Brief skirts, short jackets, sleeveless or fitted tops, quick-drying fabrics and removable social layers are appropriate. Kin-knots are valued Raft-linked keepsakes and may decorate wrists, waist and garments. Free Tidekin favor colorful individual expression, while Saltbound outfits accommodate aquatic labor and the approved streamlined two-ring Harborband. Preserve small otterfolk anatomy and practical water movement; no required specific accessory colors or fabrics. Owning reference: `29_Tidekin_Imperial_Fashion.md`; individual artwork requires approval.


### November 6, 2026 — Approved Kobold Presentation and Demonstration Dress

**Presentation Dress** is clean, tailored, professor-like attire with optional petticoats, spectacles and commemorative tools for formal audiences. **Demonstration Dress** emphasizes authentic practical workwear: modified, patched, scorched or worn laboratory coats, useful tools and appropriate safety equipment. The cherished **Working Coat** may retain years of custom repairs; a **Proud Tool** can be symbolic in formal dress and functional in working dress. These are flexible cultural conventions, not uniform specifications. Do not depict false wear as an automatic skill or status badge. Preserve ordinary Kobold scale and established Orb Collar/bracer distinctions. Full text: `27_Kobold_Imperial_Fashion.md`.


### November 6, 2026 — Kobold scholar/engineer fashion and visual scale (CANON)

Kobold fashion remains practical even for prestigious household specialists: mechanic and professor silhouettes, petticoats, functional technical apparel and frequent spectacles (especially casual wear). Kobolds have poor natural distance vision adapted for caves, with possible exceptions among Corvyn-imprinted Kobolds, but no fixed visual acuity mechanic. Free Kobolds often have torn, scorched, stained or repeatedly patched lab coats when unconstrained by masters' uniform policies. Enslaved household specialists can be sharply maintained. Preserve **small Kobold stature**; earlier generated tall-looking Kobold fashion examples received only conditional feedback on clothing, not anatomy approval. See `27_Kobold_Imperial_Fashion.md`; new images require separate approval.


### November 6, 2026 — Approved Naga fashion (The Silks and Chains of the Eternal Coil)

**Naga design canon:** Short silk dancer-style tops and hip drapes with exposed shoulders/back/midriff; ornaments and fine metalwork, rather than excessive fabric, express aristocratic wealth. **Ornamental chains and manacles** (light, heavy, lavishly jeweled and deliberately impractical) memorialize ancestral Serpentari-era enslavement as modern status symbols, not contemporary working restraints. **Matriarchs are customarily circleted**, even poor Matriarchs with copper, but the tradition is not a legal requirement. Ritual dress much more explicitly evokes historic bondage; **literal self-binding is exceptional**, chiefly as zealot symbolism or abolitionist protest. Do not impose generic harness motifs as the Naga's defining fashion language. Full owning reference: `26_Imperial_Fashion_and_Dress.md`. Separate image approval remains required.

### November 6, 2026 — Approved collar, regional fashion, and Kobold reference directions

**Regional visual distinctions:** Naga Imperium: polished, manufactured, registered and hierarchical; Scarlands: dark, worn, repaired, repurposed and individualized; Canopy Kingdoms: fine craftsmanship, botanical/heraldic, woven and engraved. These overlay, not erase, species-specific design. Approved fashion uses mature, often provocative adult costume design, but practical fit and established anatomy remain primary.

**Kobold reference:** The author's uploaded red Kobold engineer image is the major clothing inspiration: fantasy-friendly white fitted laboratory coat, high-low skirt, goggles, belts, restrained leather accessories and useful harnesses. The Orb Collar is a **smooth metal band with one central colored functional orb** and **no visible ring, clamp, buckle or lock**. Free Kobold technical professionals instead favor Orb bracers. A local information aurora appears during direct paw gestures.

**Other approved directions:** Tidekin Harborband typically two separate neck rings (upper value, lower security/obedience); Duskpaw **open lock** represents emancipation; Corvyn robes are flamboyant witch/wizard garments with extravagant hats/hoods. Expensive Mirror, Trance and Coilsting collars are special-purpose forms overriding ordinary race shapes, not standard clothing. See `25_Imperial_Collar_Culture_and_Restraints.md`. **Coilsting Collar** is the approved name formerly called Shock Collar; former proposal **Censure Collar / Censureband** is not canon. Do not treat unapproved additional sketches, colors, or naming options as canonical.

## 2. Established art direction for the Living Codex

### November 5, 2026 — Defining official style: Direction 4, detailed ink-and-color fantasy

**LATEST USER CORRECTION:** Direction **4**, not Direction 5, is Mystaria's defining official art style. Use delicate ink-and-color fantasy illustration with Japanese manga-influenced character construction. This supersedes the mistaken woodblock/anime selection and the earlier uniform bold-outline cel rendering requirement.

| Element | Required treatment |
|---|---|
| Linework | Delicate ink contours with varied weight and occasional broken edges; avoid uniform heavy outlines |
| Texture | Sparse fine hatching for deep creases and textural accents; subtle warm-ivory paper grain |
| Color | Restrained translucent color washes beneath crisp drawing; muted moss greens, dusty teal, russet, indigo, plum and warm ivory, with small brass accents |
| Faces | Economical, carefully constructed features, nuanced character-specific expressions; vary eyelids, ears, beak angles and mouth tension |
| Shapes and anatomy | Strong readable silhouettes and deliberately stylized anatomy, preserving species identity and established body plans |
| Fur and feathers | Designed groups and masses; individual strands only at focal points |
| Clothing and equipment | Believable construction, functional fastenings, layered fabric, stitching, selective folds and modest signs of wear |
| Shading and light | Calm tonal range, soft restrained washes, selective ink accents and diffuse warmth; avoid glossy realistic rendering |
| Detail | Selective detail around faces, hands and focal objects; descriptive environmental drawing that recedes behind the cast |
| Pose | Clear readable gesture and character personality; neutral consistent poses when documenting anatomy |

The result should feel observational, tactile and drawn: neither dense all-over crosshatching, ornate gold embroidery everywhere, flat heavy-outline cel art, nor photorealistic painting. Preserve atmosphere and narrative range without overwhelming the drawing. Japanese influence concerns visual design rather than automatic changes to Mystaria clothing or architecture. Preserve racial anatomy, emblems, purple identity, collars and world-specific features.

### November 4, 2026 — Approved purple identity and atmospheric color

**USER-APPROVED VISUAL CANON — Mystaria's signature color:** **Purple** is the primary identifying color of *Mystaria* as a publication and setting brand. Give it particular prominence on title pages and in recurring backgrounds, framing, and decorative elements throughout the digital rulebook. Purple should recur frequently, without sacrificing the legibility of mechanical tables or overpowering other mana colors.

**USER-APPROVED WORLD/INTERFACE CANON — the Shimmer:** The Shimmer's signature color is **purple**. Its patron-reflecting appearance, established in `01_World_and_Shimmer.md`, may still vary in other respects; the purple identity should remain recognizable. This ruling does not alter Shimmer functions, patrons, or progression mechanics.

**USER-APPROVED WORLD CANON — skies:** Mystaria's skies **tend toward shades of purple** and are **occasionally red**. This describes a characteristic tendency, not a permanent color at every hour, in every region, or under every weather condition. The exact cause and frequency have not been established.

**Important scope boundary:** Purple is not a requirement for all in-world architecture, clothing, plants, species, or magic. Keep the physical world diverse in color and mood. In particular, purple as Mystaria's visual identity does **not** create a sixth mana color or replace the five approved mana emblems.

**Not yet specified:** An exact purple hex code, print color standard, gradient system, and palette proportions remain **artist-directed / undecided**. Do not present suggested color codes as established canon.

### Approved Drider visual anatomy — November 4, 2026

Driders have dark skin reminiscent of a drow elf, pointed ears, **two arms**, and **six long spider-like legs** supporting an enormous arachnid lower body. They have **four solid-color eyes with no pupils**; eye color varies between individuals. Their long legs and imposing bodies make them **one of Mystaria's largest mortal races**. Depict exactly two arms, six spider-like legs and four pupil-less, solid-color eyes. Preserve the humanlike upper body and arachnid lower body. Earlier four-armed Drider illustrations and descriptions are superseded. Exact dimensions, eye arrangement and precise skin tones remain unspecified. This correction applies to Driders and does not change Arrest's own appearance. See `02_Races.md` for the owning racial description.
