## Method

13 current racial arrays, levels 1 through 10 inclusive, 2L earned allocatable stat points and floor(L/5) automatic increases to each base stat. Enumerate every nonnegative integer allocation triple STR/DEX/END whose sum does not exceed 2L; other points may be unspent or allocated to INT/Skill and do not alter these checks. Apply racial multipliers to base stats, retain fractions and floor shared capacity 20+effective STR+effective END. No equipment bonuses. Each race/level includes the zero-allocation state and all possible projected investments. 75,205 race/level/allocation states; 88 configurations per state (62 from 31 shapes with Light/Medium armor and 26 named configurations), 6,618,040 comparisons. These are not 6.6 million unique characters or fights.

Use approved early Boughsteel reference Weight as QI/Junk baseline for audit; no new Quality ladder, material rules, price or damage. Conventional armor 6/12/24, shield 4/8/16, implement worst-case Weight 4; no extra generic DEX gate. Honed same Weight, QI DEX 6; Hulking ×1.5; Deflection Weight 0 and DEX equal to equivalent conventional armor Weight. No coverage subdivisions. Firearm test uses normal full recoil threshold, not claims of absolute impossibility with existing supported/shortfall rules. Ordinary shortbow/hand crossbow/sling use approved minima STR 1, DEX 1. Physical body fit, exact hands, ammo compatibility, actual craft access and availability remain item-level checks, not simulated. Junk specialties are not granted. No free shield/Gems or Heavy armor in the normal starter grant.
