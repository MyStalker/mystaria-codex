# Upgrade 3.4 — Specialty weapons and equipment identity

Status: **AUTHOR-APPROVED EXPERIMENTAL CAPABILITIES; NUMERICAL CANDIDATES NOT FINAL CANON**.
Implementation owner: [Mystaria Combat Lab](https://github.com/MyStalker/mystaria-combat-lab),
[full experimental interface and limitations](https://github.com/MyStalker/mystaria-combat-lab/blob/main/docs/SPECIALTY_WEAPONS.md).
This document preserves operative canon and all historical experimental controls.

## Approved research scope

Compare conventional, Hulking and Precision weapons under identical numerical foundations,
independent WR/progression, attribute damage, purchased damage and specialty policies.
Retain lawful STR/DEX/Either/Both scaling, independent handling/support minima, actual
Weight/capacity, 1/2 hands, real alternate forms, fixed construction AP and additive armor.
Keep natural 1 misses, natural 20 hits with exact-modifier confirmation, confirmed
post-armor critical doubling, zero-damage armor and existing Shimmer in the experimental
natural-roll policy. No chip floor or percentage mitigation is approved.

Attribute specialization and TP specialization remain independent. Research reinforced,
attribute-led complementary, training-led complementary, hybrid and sensible generalist
builds; arbitrary TP targets include fixtures at 5/20/35/50/65/80 percent. Attribute-led
80%-complementary means roughly **20% specialty TP and 80% tailored supporting TP**.
Keep natural Skill-derived income distinct from equal-available-budget controls. Report
actual purchases, omitted mechanics and all equipment eligibility decisions. Optional
final spending must be explicit, deterministic, supporting-stat-only and provenance-tagged.
No opponent-specific optimization is implied.

## Current evidence — supplied by the author

These are prior research findings supplied with the Upgrade 3.4 mission, not new balance
measurements from the implementation. Original experiment identifiers were not supplied.

- Generalists initially exceeded 90% average wins in many specialist comparisons.
- Attribute-led specialists spending approximately 80% TP on weaknesses materially
  improved matchup diversity and duration. On the original 3.3 foundation,
  representative level-100 generalist average wins reached approximately 60%.
- Health and armor recovered considerably from construction improvements alone;
  broad price changes are therefore no longer a priority.
- Improved-foundation tests reported no mechanically unwinnable matchups or timeouts
  across 106 matchup configurations.
- Reducing maximum HP rank benefit 40→30 improved damage's direct health matchup
  from 21.5%→42% in a 200-trial comparison.
- Lowering proportional HP caps sometimes favored high-END health specialists
  relative to lower-END opponents. Maximum benefits 25/30/35/40 remain candidates.

## Author's balance preferences

Generalists should be reliable, ideally approximately 60% against representative
specialists; 75–80% is acceptable if necessary. Damage should be attractive. Accuracy
may be weakest overall while viable. Evasion needs competitive high-level advantages;
health and armor need distinct durable identities. Intentional counters may approach
90%, while comparable-level unwinnable routes should have reasonable counterplay.
Powerful armor may still completely negate inferior attacks. Damage specialists may
one-hit ordinary targets; comparable damage Heroes should generally survive an ordinary
normal hit, preferably through ordinary HP. Hero advantage is intentional. Prioritize
L5–100; L1–4 need functional introductory encounters. These are preferences, not
hardcoded acceptance thresholds or invented parity requirements.

## Unresolved author decisions and unsupported systems

Combat's November 22 additive-HP equipment direction explicitly preserves Hulking Speed
caps and Precision restrictions. The current Equipment/melee/ranged/modular references,
index, changelog and archived Lab equipment snapshots do not provide a detailed specialty
benefit/restriction table or ratified numerical conversion. Preserve that requirement;
do not invent canonical doubling, accuracy bonuses, armor prohibitions or stat minima.
Conventional catalogue identities and approved Heavy gates remain available as historical
sources; no universal legacy-F-to-linear-WR conversion is authorized.

The stationary Lab can enforce explicitly supplied stat/support/hand/Weight and armor/
form/scaling restrictions. Legacy SPD caps, movement, clearance/reach/facing, action and
reaction costs, Pressure, shields and dual wield remain omitted. Full material/Quality,
monetary equipment, racial and spatial conversion are unfinished. Experimental policy
labels and explicit omission reports must accompany any partial specialty comparison.
Recover or approve the historical specialty details and decide their conversion before
calling these full Hulking/Precision implementations. No final balance winner is selected.
