# Mystaria — Jestercraft: Authoritative Canon Reference

**Authority: CANON.** Updated November 3, 2026, following explicit approval of the full Jestercraft versatility overhaul, Sponge storage/absorption formulae, wetsuit protection, 18-second energy puppetry, Pincushion recovery timing, and independent purchase of both Hemweaving ultimates. This subject dossier supersedes previous `05_Jestercraft.md` editions, the November 3 126-SP snapshot, and the older Jester reference files where mechanics conflict.

**Classification:** Artifact-bound Craft, attainable by any race bonded to a living Regalia.  **Rank gates:** I/level 1, II/10, III/25, IV/50, V/80. **Full mastery:** **130 SP** when both Rank V Hemweaving ultimates are purchased; **126 SP** if only one is purchased. **Six free Rank I foundations; 18 modifiers at 3 SP each; 19 applications at 4 SP each** with both ultimates.

**Important boundaries:** Jestercraft does **not** use inversion, Recompense, or Final Inversion. The Regalia are fully sentient and speaking; Hollow Jesters still exist. Existing rules not expressly revised below remain operative. This is the authoritative merged reference, not a proposed design menu.

## 1. Identity and acquisition

A **Jester** is the wearer of a **Regalia**: a rare, **fully sentient**, living magical outfit originally created by the Serpentari as an instrument of punishment and control. Regalia can **speak aloud**, engage in conversation, reason independently, form opinions, remember experiences, and express their own intentions. Each Regalia bonds to its wearer through the **Donning** and is the source and instrument of every Jester technique. A Regalia may be playful, cruel, possessive, protective, calculating, affectionate, or otherwise independently minded.

### 1.1 The choice of the living Regalia

The Serpentari fashioned Regalia to punish, control, and ultimately **hollow out** victims, leaving a wearer whose identity could be overpowered by the garment. Their creations, however, developed independent minds. **In most cases, Regalia chose to make their victims into powerful Jesters rather than render them hollow.** They transformed intended victims into living weapons, granting them access to the Regalia’s extraordinary fabric magic while preserving a mind capable of acting, learning, and conversing with its garment.

That decision does **not** make every Regalia benevolent. Some see an empowered wearer as a companion or partner; others as a favored champion, treasured possession, useful agent, or entertaining instrument. The relationship can be cooperative, manipulative, affectionate, contentious, or coercive. A Regalia’s voice is its own and is not necessarily aligned with its wearer’s wishes.

**Hollow Jesters still exist.** They are the exceptional result when a Regalia overwhelms or extinguishes a wearer’s identity instead of preserving an empowered individual. Their continued existence is a matter of lore; there is **no Recompense meter, Final Inversion progression, or mandatory numerical hollowing rule** in the revised Craft. The detailed capabilities and occurrence of Hollow Jesters remain unspecified.

### 1.2 Speech and magical limits

Regalia speak normally and can address their wearers or bystanders. Their sentience does not automatically grant separate turns, free spellcasting, limitless mana, independent duplicate bodies, or control of fabric beyond the established Stitch and skill rules. **All mechanical limitations elsewhere in this document remain unchanged.**

Jester Magic is formally a **Craft** even though it is not an inherited racial Craft. Anyone of any race may become a Jester if they can obtain and bond with a Regalia. No tutor is required. A wearer does not independently learn to cast the same fabric magic without the bonded garment.

The Regalia possesses its own magical conduit and access to a finite mana reservoir, permitting a wearer who lacks conventional spellcasting to operate the Craft. Where compatible, personal mana can supplement the Regalia's accessible capacity. The exact means of supplying or replenishing mana remains subject to Mystaria's general mana rules; Jesters do **not** gain unlimited mana merely by wearing a Regalia.

The outfit serves as **weapon, armor, tool, disguise, and resource reserve**. As it is spent, extended, or cut, its physical coverage decreases. The wearer's defenses consequently diminish throughout a fight.

**Armor restriction:** A Jester cannot wear conventional armor over or beneath the Regalia. The garment may imitate the appearance of armor, but the imitation offers no actual protection beyond that provided by Regalia fabric and its abilities. Ordinary weapons and non-armor equipment may still be used.

**Principal strengths:** Distributed sensing and precise disguises, puppetry and cloth duplication, acrobatic mobility and bodily flexibility, selectable kinetic/thermal/fluid/arcane defense, extraordinary liquid reservoirs, reality-altering ribbons, living-thread sabotage, and prepared battlefield control.

**Principal vulnerabilities:** Exposed flesh and unprotected body gaps; sharp edges and cutting, burning, or magical attacks when the relevant attunement is not selected; resource attrition, interrupted recovery, and irreversible deployment in Black Masquerade. Fluid protection requires a sealed covering, and only one passive Curtainwall attunement operates at a time.

## 3. Fabric/Stitch economy and protection

A **Stitch** is genuine physical living Regalia fabric, normally **0.05 m² at ordinary thickness**. Extending fabric does not duplicate it; longer ribbons become narrower or thinner. A 5-cm-wide ribbon uses approximately one Stitch per meter; a 1-cm ribbon approximately one Stitch per 5 meters; a 5-mm ribbon approximately one Stitch per 10 meters; an ordinary very thin Seamcraft strand up to about **150 meters per Stitch**. *Vanishing Thread* can stretch a sufficiently fine strand to approximately **300 meters per Stitch** at reduced strength.

### Stitch capacity, regeneration and material geometry

Let `Mmax` be the **maximum accessible mana capacity of wearer and Regalia together**, not mana already spent.

- **Stitch capacity:** `Smax = 20 + floor(Mmax / 4)`.
- **Maximum regenerated Stitches per six-second round:** `Rmax = 1 + floor(Mmax / 25)`.
- **Mana cost to grow replacement fabric:** **2 MP per new Stitch**; never exceed current capacity or the per-round regeneration limit.
- Increased maximum accessible mana raises capacity without instantly filling it.
- **Rag Doll V, Unspooled Self / Last Thread** can create up to **3 Stitches per round** by injuring the user's actual body instead of spending mana; still limited by `Smax`. The resulting injury is real and not healed by replacing fabric.

**Reference body coverage (ordinary thickness, average adult humanoid; overlap between parts):** hand 1–2 Stitches; forearm 2; one full arm 4–5; front torso 5; back torso 5; short-sleeved shirt 14; head and neck 4–5; thigh 4–5; lower leg 3–4; whole leg 7–9; both feet 3–4. Allocating material to remote effects removes it from body protection. A Jester cannot wear normal armor over or under the Regalia.

### Waterproof and wetsuit property — innate to every Regalia

At **Rank I**, a Jester may command their Regalia to become entirely waterproof. Liquid beads off; its weave can form a watertight hood and seal. For complete ordinary-water dry coverage of an average humanoid, allocate **at least 4 Stitches** as a thin watertight wetsuit. This is a **configuration of existing fabric**, not an additional paid skill. It prevents wetness but **does not by itself negate heat, acids, breathable-air needs, cutting, magic, or pressure**.

With Sponge learned and **Fluid attunement selected**, the wearer may allocate an upgraded all-over protective membrane:

| Immersion | Full-body protective Stitches | Result |
|---|---:|---|
| Ordinary water | 4 | Dry, sealed, watertight suit; innate waterproofing suffices |
| Toxic water / contaminated sludge | 8 | Resists hazardous contact and liquid contaminants |
| Strong acid / burning oil / corrosive fluid | 12 | Resists the specified chemical or liquid hazard |
| Molten lava | 16 | Insulates against molten rock contact and its heat |

These are full-body estimates for an **average adult humanoid** and must scale with body size. The selected membrane protects against the **substance itself**, not an unrelated physical sword attack, curse, or magical property. A tear exposes the body. It does **not** produce oxygen; a sealed head requires an actual air supply for long submersion. Reservoir Stitches and protective Stitches cannot simultaneously be assigned to both functions.

## 4. Five Stitch states, recovery and failure

| State | Meaning | Recovery |
|---|---|---|
| **Worn** | Intact fabric worn on the body; can cover flesh or be shaped | Already available |
| **Committed** | Intact fabric allocated to a bow, seam, ribbon, restraint, reservoir, clone, membrane, etc. | Usually retrievable through normal recovery unless made sacrificial |
| **Loose** | Intact detached or fallen fabric, possibly still sensed with Living Senses | Can be physically retrieved if not destroyed |
| **Frayed** | Damaged material holding stored kinetic / thermal / fluid-related or arcane charge, where applicable; ordinary fate is Cut | **Normally irrecoverable**, except **Pincushion** once per day |
| **Cut** | Destroyed/burned/severed/sacrificed material; capacity now missing | Cannot be collected; must be newly regenerated, or replaced through Rag Doll V bodily sacrifice |

**Accounting:** `Smax = Worn + Committed + Loose + Frayed + Cut` while the entire capacity is allocated. Cut is missing fabric capacity, not a detachable thread on the battlefield. Frayed is a distinct temporary charged state; it is **not** the same as Loose.

### Normal recovery

A Jester must **beckon** intact remote fabric with a dedicated action; retrieval is not instantaneous thought. Selected magical knots loosen, and the effects end **when the fastening actually unties**. Intact fabric slithers back about **10 meters per six-second round**, with distance, impediments and tying delay relevant. Several effects can be recovered together. The Jester must maintain a stable opportunity: attacking, sprinting, or elaborate acrobatics interrupts recovery. Already loosened effects remain ended, and interrupted returning material becomes **Loose** wherever it stops. Ordinary recall costs **0 MP**.

**Living Senses exception:** A Loose and fully detached fragment can still transmit senses even while it is distant. This does not grant remote control of the fragment outside the relevant control-range limits.

### Pincushion exception to Frayed irrecoverability

**Curtainwall V: Pincushion** works **once per day**. On a brief still recovery gesture, **all currently Frayed Stitches become Worn again**, their charge dissipating **harmlessly**. The user needs **no minimum number of intact Stitches**, and may perform Pincushion **at the very beginning of the same normal beckoning/recovery action** before recalling other material. It does not restore Cut Stitches, expired Frayed Stitches, or fabric already discharged and destroyed. It restores whatever is *currently Frayed* at activation; no free return of separate Loose/Committed fabric is implied.

### Cutting and Black Masquerade

A cut magical bow or essential thread destroys the affected material and ends dependent effects where appropriate. Black Masquerade makes **used/committed** fabric sacrificial: when the empowered effect ends, those Stitches become Cut. Merely worn and unspent fabric remains safe. Pincushion cannot reverse an already Cut Black Masquerade sacrifice.

## 5. Curtainwall: passive attunements and charges

**Impact Loom** is the free Rank I foundation: while **Kinetic attunement** is selected, a physical impact that hits actual covered Regalia fabric may be passively intercepted, fraying the necessary Stitches and accumulating kinetic feedback. There is no mandatory mana upkeep for ordinary physical interception. An unprotected gap is an exposed body part. Against incoming impact, approximate costs remain: light blow 1 Stitch; ordinary solid weapon blow ~2; heavy strike 3–5; powerful monster blow 6–10; an overwhelming strike can penetrate.

**One passive attunement at a time.** Deliberate Sponge reservoir storage uses intact **Committed** fabric; a hostile splash or fluid attack intercepted defensively can damage/fray its covering according to the hazard, but there is no separately approved universal Frayed-per-liter exchange. Stored liquids do not become free Frayed kinetic charges. Switching attunement requires a deliberate action (no reactive free switching is granted by the selected tree). At Rank I the only passive choice is **Kinetic**. Ember Catch unlocks **Thermal**, Sponge unlocks **Fluid**, and Spell Catcher unlocks **Arcane**. The selected type is the only energy/substance category currently passively intercepted. For example, Arcane does not stop a sword, and Fluid does not confer kinetic protection. Existing stored charge keeps its usual timer while another type is selected. The earlier proposed separate Atmospheric attunement is **not part of this approved tree**.

| Attunement | Unlock | What it passively addresses | What it does NOT automatically do |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Kinetic** | Impact Loom, Rank I | Blows/impacts striking covered fabric; store impact feedback | Defeat spell energy, curses, exposed flesh, or cutting with impunity |
| **Thermal** | Ember Catch, Rank II | Heat, flames, and dangerous ambient contact temperatures | Stop a sword or unrelated magical beam |
| **Fluid** | Sponge, Rank III | Safe contact, absorption, storage, and manipulation of hazardous liquids, including toxins, fuels, blood and lava | Stop a sword; create breathable air; resist every special magical effect in a liquid |
| **Arcane** | Spell Catcher, Rank IV | Direct incoming magical energy striking covered Regalia | Copy an incantation, stop spellcasting, or block simultaneous kinetic attacks |

**Kinetic Frayed feedback:** **200 joules per Frayed Stitch** is the established nominal baseline, *not scaled by rank, stats, or mana*. Charges normally last **45 seconds**; Split Current's old 60-second timer has been superseded and does not survive automatically. The wearer can discharge any selected subset, including in midair, from a connected portion of the Regalia. Charged Frayed Stitches become Cut upon ordinary discharge or expiry. They cannot be regained except by timely Pincushion. Thermal, Fluid and Arcane handling keep the **absorbed type**, rather than automatically converting it to multiplied kinetic energy. The exact nonkinetic Frayed conversion into Stitch-by-Stitch energy units is **not separately established**; use the storage and membrane rules below where explicitly defined.

**Approved kinetic feat benchmarks, not physics calculations:** 2–3 Frayed Stitches for a dramatic spring hop; 5–6 for a rooftop leap (~10–15 m); 20 for a cannonball escape (~50–80 m); 2–4 for a midair double-jump; 1–5 for a short (~3–5 m) air cannon. Severe landings can damage the body or demand extra impact interception, particularly before Rag Doll III. One-to-three Stitch bursts can unbalance a person; concentrated larger releases can cause serious injury but never guarantee one.

### Ember Catch energy puppetry

Ember Catch lets the Jester puppet **released energy they legitimately hold**, especially heat/flame, into controlled trajectories and shapes. A **simple** effect (e.g., homing firebolt or curved release) needs little ongoing intervention. A **complex** independent sequence (e.g., an enduring fire dragon performing a responsive dance) demands the Regalia's **direct attention**, may be maintained for **up to 18 seconds = three rounds**, then resolves or disperses. Complex energy puppetry is unavailable while that same Regalia's full attention is already assigned to **Severed Double**. Released energy is finite; shaped flame is not an independent summon or endlessly fueled actor.

### Sponge reservoir, flows and fluid filtering

Sponge uses actual committed Stitches to anchor **magically weightless-to-the-wearer storage** for real fluid volumes. Stored liquids retain their identity and quantities; different liquids may be isolated rather than mixed. The reservoir may absorb hazardous and molten liquids safely when Fluid attunement is selected, and may expel, redistribute, puppet, or physically filter accessible fluids. Pure water and separated pollutants occupy actual capacity until expelled. Liquid escaping from a damaged reservoir leaks at the **normal release rate**, rather than instantly emptying the whole reservoir. Capacity scales by level; there is **no free infinite lake storage**.

Let `L >= 25` (level with Sponge learned); `N` be Stitches committed **to reservoirs**; one combat round is about 6 seconds:

- **Maximum reservoir volume per Stitch:** `V(L) = 5 × 2^floor((L − 25) / 5)` **liters**.
- **Total storage:** `N × V(L)` liters, limited to actual available Stitches.
- **Intake/release rate per active intake Stitch per round:** `Q(L) = 2 × 2^floor((L − 25) / 10)` **liters/round**.
- **Maximum flow with N active intake Stitches:** `N × Q(L)` liters per round; cannot exceed available fluid, free capacity, and valid physical fluid access.

| Level | Storage / Stitch | 20-Stitch storage | Intake/release / Stitch / round |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 25 | 5 L | 100 L | 2 L |
| 30 | 10 L | 200 L | 2 L |
| 40 | 40 L | 800 L | 4 L |
| 50 | 160 L | 3,200 L | 8 L |
| 60 | 640 L | 12,800 L | 16 L |
| 70 | 2,560 L | 51,200 L | 32 L |
| 80 | 10,240 L | 204,800 L | 64 L |
| 100 | 163,840 L | 3,276,800 L | 256 L |

One Rank III reservoir Stitch at level 25 can store roughly the blood volume in a human-sized body (~5 L). **Access and time still matter:** at 2 L per round, five liters takes about **three rounds** from a suitable wound, if uninterrupted and not resisted; blood cannot be removed from an unwounded body's intact vessels by simply declaring it absorbed. Unwilling living targets may resist appropriately. A level-80 Jester with 20 reservoir Stitches can hold 204,800 L and absorb 1,280 L/round if enough fluid is accessible; absorbing a ~200,000 L pond takes roughly **157 rounds (~16 minutes)**, not one turn. Internal Black Masquerade thread may filter poisons from accessible body fluids; that is not blanket healing, automatic antidote against all supernatural curses, or resurrection.

Fluid storage has physical access, choice of passive attunement and finite Stitch investment. The fluid remains real but **does not encumber the wearer while stored**. The Regalia may collect and preserve breathable air where possible, but this rule does **not** conjure oxygen or give a separate quantified air-compression capacity. Any nonliquid atmospheric use outside basic fluid manipulation requires care, as only liquid flow figures are fixed above.

## 6. The six complete discipline trees

Every entry below is **approved canon**, except where an *explicit unresolved numerical limit* is noted; no additional unapproved technique is assumed. Each discipline retains its free foundation. Components may be purchased individually at the rank gate for their listed price.

### 6.1 Hemweaving — perception, masquerade, puppetry and the divided performer

**Hemweaving active CONTROL range by rank:** I **30 m**; II **45 m**; III **60 m**; IV **80 m**; V **100 m**. A range limit does not supply free cloth length or negate obstructions. Living Senses can perceive through disconnected fabric at unlimited separation, but does not grant unlimited-range manipulation. **Empty Performer** is the explicit independent-range exception.

| Rank | Feature | Class | SP | Capability |
|---|---|---|---:|---|
| I | **Living Hem** | Foundation | 0 | Reshape, extend, twist, retract and animate existing worn Regalia fabric. |
| II | **Living Senses** | Modifier | 3 | Sight, hearing, touch, taste and smell through any Regalia fabric, even fully disconnected; simultaneous sensory feeds. |
| II | **Perfect Masquerade** | Application | 4 | Ultra-precise thin-fabric mimics of people, textures, architecture and terrifying almost illusory shapes. |
| III | **Fine Filament** | Modifier | 3 | Divide material into many separately directed strands seeking gaps and weaknesses across a broad area. |
| III | **Puppet Strings** | Application | 4 | Attach to joints for physical puppetry; puppet the incapacitated wearer; hover or fly by committing many self-supporting threads. |
| IV | **Severed Double** | Modifier | 3 | A full cloth clone controlled by the Regalia; both share powers, resources, equipment functionality, stats and temporary buffs, linked by a fragile red thread. |
| V | **The Living Stage** | Application | 4 | Black-mana puppet theatre draws in nearby miniaturized targets; theatre fabric is manipulable as Regalia, with free temporary strings. |
| V | **The Empty Performer** | **Additional independent application** | **4** | Regalia separates and operates at **any distance**, no red-thread range/tether restriction; its survival remains linked to wearer. |

**Living Senses:** The wearer can look behind with a cloak, feel or see using a scarf around a corner, and taste a stone with a bow or attached fragment. No special cloth eye is necessary. Many concurrent sensory feeds are natively integrated, without individual sustained concentration. This conveys ordinary sensory data, not magical omniscience or mindreading; disconnected fabric still remains subject to destruction and cannot be remotely manipulated outside normal range by this feature alone.

**Perfect Masquerade:** A small number of Stitches can be stretched to thin imitation-film across a much wider decorative area; apparent skin, fur, scales, stone, wood, metal, facial features, and voiced disguise can be terrifyingly convincing. Such film is fragile, cannot grant additional armor or fabricate real separate gear, and may be exposed by physical contact, cuts, or magic. **Exact square meters per Stitch of decorative illusion-film are not numerically fixed.**

**Fine Filament:** Individually directed hair-thin strands probe seams and openings, attack from multiple directions, and exploit weak points, but cannot multiply actual total fabric, automatic damage or target access. Living Senses supplies contact and visual feedback through them.

**Puppet Strings:** Physically attached to body joints, not a mind-control spell. Material examples: ~2–4 Stitches to influence one limb; ~6–8 multiple joints; ~10–12 coordinated movement of a humanoid; ~16+ attempt comprehensive control. These are **material guides, not automatic success**. Hostile puppetry is typically no longer than **two rounds** per successful attachment attempt, with resistance each round; the Jester normally controls at most one unwilling target at a time. Cooperative puppetry may last ~one minute. Regalia can puppet the wearer during unconsciousness, paralysis or other incapacity without curing it. Approximate self-flight: **2–5** Stitches extra jump/brief hover; **6–11** slow hover; **12–17** limited sustained flight; **18–24** free gravity-defying flight for an ordinary adult. Large bodies need more support. It works without an external anchor and trades off protection.

**Severed Double:** Clone is **100% cloth**, so each hit against it counts as striking Curtainwall-covered fabric when its relevant attunement applies. No exposed-flesh gaps, but it can be damaged, burned, severed, overwhelmed, or struck by an unattuned threat. Original and clone **share one mana pool, one Stitch ledger, spell uses, equipment resources/charges, stats and buffs**; duplicated-looking equipment does not create a second real stock of consumables or enchantment charges. The Regalia devotes **full independent attention** to controlling the clone: other abilities that require the Regalia's direct autonomous intervention are suppressed for as long as the Double is controlled; wearer-directed/passive abilities are not globally disabled. A **thin red connecting thread** is fragile if attacked directly. Cutting it collapses the clone; the thread is Cut, other surviving intact cloth becomes Loose and must be recovered normally.

**The Living Stage (V, 4 SP):** Expends **black mana** and attempts to draw surrounding creatures into a miniature puppet theatre. Targets may **dodge initial capture** or **resist with magic**. Inside, all theatre scenery behaves as Regalia fabric under the Jester's command, and the theatre grants **20 temporary free stage strings each round**—only inside and never added to normal capacity or carried out. Captives can **attack the walls to escape**, weaken or destroy the stage. **Working encounter benchmarks:** ~15–20 m capture radius, **three rounds (~18 seconds)** duration. **Exact black MP activation fee and wall durability are unresolved**, not to be silently guessed. Normal Regalia resource use and target resistance still matter outside its special pocket environment.

**The Empty Performer (V, 4 SP, separate purchase):** Regalia can separate from wearer and exist independently at **any distance**, acting with acquired powers while sharing resources with its original. Unlike Severed Double there is no red-thread tether or ordinary Hemweaving distance limit. If detached Regalia is destroyed, the Jester coughs up a hairball-like mass that reforms into a new Regalia. If the Jester **dies**, the Regalia becomes an inert, powerless outfit until it finds a new master; its consciousness is preserved. Whether two distinct fully independent full-action bodies can operate simultaneously beyond the described shared resource limits is **not separately quantified**; the ability does not duplicate pool, equipment charges or permanent purchases.

**Hemweaving mastery:** 25 SP with **both** Rank V applications; 21 SP with only one. Both are available at level 80 and are **not** exclusive.

### 6.4 Tailor's Measure — seven reality-changing ribbon properties

Every ribbon/bow physically attaches and alters **one** reality property. This entire tree supersedes the former progression (Sure Knot, Pocketed World, Graduated Measure, Grand Refit, Heavy Pattern, Living Measure). Resizing no longer automatically alters mass.

| Rank | Feature | Class | SP | Property |
|---|---|---|---:|---|
| I | **Measuring Bow** | Foundation | 0 | Precisely change **size** of any physical target, including living targets, with **no mass change**. |
| II | **Burden Knot** | Modifier | 3 | Change **mass** independently of size, entailing real change to matter. |
| II | **Surface Law** | Application | 4 | Change **friction** of a marked surface, making it slippery or grippy. |
| III | **Compass Knot** | Modifier | 3 | Reorient the direction of **gravity** for a target. |
| III | **Porous Pattern** | Application | 4 | Change a material's **permeability** to selected substances. |
| IV | **Resonant Ribbon** | Modifier | 3 | Suppress or enhance **existing ongoing magical effects** including passive equipment enchantments; does not stop spellcasting. |
| V | **Clockmaker's Ribbon** | Application | 4 | Freeze a target in **time** briefly, or accelerate its time up to **2×**. |

**Bow rules:** One action may launch **multiple ribbons/bows simultaneously, with no extra elapsed action time per ribbon**. Each still costs material, needs its own successful physical attachment and individual resistance against unwilling creatures. Multiple bows may stack; removing a specific bow removes only *its* contribution. Bows usually need no routine upkeep; return/reversion occurs when fastening **actually unties**, not by instant thought. Non-Jesters struggle to untie them; cutting destroys the affected Stitches. Avoid inferring unlimited automatic hits or unlimited size changes from same-action volleys.

**Per-ribbon material cost by ORIGINAL target mass (before effects):** up to **1 kg = 1 Stitch**; over 1–20 kg = **2**; over 20–200 kg = **4**; over 200–2,000 kg = **8**; above 2,000 kg = **16**. This applies to each attached reality ribbon, not just size-bows; a mass-reduced target's later ribbons do **not** get cheaper. Powerful targets and enchanted structures may resist. Precise universal maximum size/mass multipliers were not newly specified; do not claim arbitrarily infinite effect.

**Reality effects:** Base changes linear scale while retaining mass/density tradeoffs; Burden Knot affects real mass without changing dimensions. Surface Law modifies traction/sliding. Compass Knot sets the target's down direction, but does not create free general telekinesis. Porous Pattern permits or blocks passage of suitable materials through marked substance; wards may resist. Resonant Ribbon can strengthen/weaken an **already-existing** effect without teaching new spells, changing its fundamental magical type, or permanently deleting a passive enchantment. The enchantment returns to baseline after the ribbon ends unless independently expired. Clockmaker's Ribbon may suspend a target or hasten its progression up to 2×; resistance applies. **Working human-sized suggested benchmarks accepted with the revision:** Freeze ~15–20 Stitches for up to **one round**; accelerate ~10–15 Stitches for up to **two rounds**; larger magical targets need extra investment. A frozen creature does not take ordinary instant attack damage while its time is stopped; effects can be prepared for when it resumes. These special Tier V Stitch costs supersede the usual small-bow cost when higher. The Ribbon does not duplicate mana/skill points or bypass Channel Limits.

**Tailor's Measure mastery:** 21 SP.

### 6.5 Black Masquerade — The Black Second Skin

The free Black Masquerade transforms the Regalia into its dark sacrificial mode. Activation and ordinary maintenance cost **0 MP**. Any fabric **committed or used in the transformation** is sacrificial and will become **Cut** when its effect ends; unspent Worn fabric survives. Ordinary skill-specific MP costs remain. The Rank I on-demand split/release remains: **200 joules nominal per severed Stitch**, **1 MP per started group of four severed Stitches** for a deliberate burst, without any rank/stat multiplier. The earlier approved **baseline empowerment benchmarks** remain where compatible: roughly **double effective fabric reach or strength** for invested sacrificial cloth, and potentially **half ordinary Tailor’s Measure Stitch cost (round up)** or unusually rapid resizing; these never increase actual physical Stitch capacity, negate resistance, or multiply per-Stitch kinetic feedback. The earlier Black Gauge/Severance Burst/Nightflight/Ruinous Ribbon/Wild Loom/Final Curtain upgrade lineup is superseded; foundational sacrificial mechanics remain.

| Rank | Feature | Class | SP | Capability |
|---|---|---|---:|---|
| I | **Black Masquerade** | Foundation | 0 | Sacrificial empowerment, immediate intentional severance feedback, black transformation. |
| II | **Understitch** | Modifier | 3 | Move living fibers beneath skin, through tissue; concealed bodily reinforcement and internal threads. |
| II | **Bandage** | Application | 4 | Send fibers through wearer's or ally's blood, expanding at open wound to temporarily close it and stop bleeding. |
| III | **Pulse Reader** | Modifier | 3 | Perceive bodily strain, heartbeat, breathing and injury in self **and living creatures in line of sight**. |
| III | **Thread Parasite** | Application | 4 | Introduce physical threads into another creature through existing wound/cooperation to interfere with movement. |
| IV | **One with the Thread** | Modifier | 3 | Strengthen STR/DEX/END/SPD by internal Stitch commitment. |
| V | **Encour** | Application | 4 | Temporarily substitute for failing vital functions; emergency biological support, not resurrection. |

**Bandage:** Internal fibers follow flowing blood and expand at a **real open wound**, sealing vessels and reducing/stopping bleeding. It is **not healing**: no tissue regeneration, restored blood, infection removal or permanent organ repair. When the sacrificial internal fibers vanish, bleeding can recur. Appropriate access/cooperation are required to thread another creature safely. Sponge can additionally filter accessible blood toxins with internal thread.

**Pulse Reader:** Can read physical physiological signs of any living creature the Jester can see, not thoughts, spell lists, hidden mana stores, or every disease. **Thread Parasite** depends on an actual wound or consent rather than magical penetration of intact flesh at this rank; target can resist.

**One with the Thread:** Every **2 internally committed Stitches grant +1 effective attribute point**, distributed among **STR, DEX, END and SPD** as the wearer chooses; INT not eligible. These are **temporary effective bonuses, not permanent base statistics**. Committed fabric cannot cover the body simultaneously; it is Cut when Black Masquerade's use ends. Example: 10 internal Stitches grant +5 total effective points, allocable +3 STR and +2 SPD.

**Encour:** Regalia internally supports circulation, breathing and some damaged organs to keep a critically injured wearer alive for emergency treatment. It does **not** heal, resurrect, permanently replace destroyed anatomy, or confer immunity to fatal destruction. As sacrificial fabric depletes, support can fail. Precise maximum duration/organ coverage is **not separately established**.

**Black Masquerade mastery:** 21 SP. **A critical limitation:** Pincushion unfrays existing Frayed fabric; it cannot restore already Cut sacrificial cloth.

### 6.6 Seamcraft — the predatory thread and Silk Road

**Normal Seamcraft placement/control range:** Rank I **120 m**, II **180 m**, III **240 m**, IV **330 m**, V **450 m**. A fine normal strand is up to ~150 m/Stitch; Vanishing Thread can reach ~300 m/Stitch at ~25% reduced tensile performance, and from Rank III doubles applicable range **240/330/450 → 480/660/900 m**. This concerns Seamcraft, not Hemweaving or unlimited connectionless action. Ordinary placed threads persist beyond active range as long as anchored and intact.

| Rank | Feature | Class | SP | Capability |
|---|---|---|---:|---|
| I | **Anchor Stitch** | Foundation | 0 | Anchor physical thread to objects/terrain, make ties, snares and basic bindings. |
| II | **Razorwire** | Modifier | 3 | Ultra-thin razor-sharp seam wire; barbed wire costs **2×** comparable Stitches. |
| II | **Weed Wacker** | Application | 4 | Placed threads lash out when commanded; continued focus sustains a sweeping attack. |
| III | **Burrowstitch** | Modifier | 3 | Hide threads within existing seams/gaps, secretly alter clothing/armor fit, and perform Black Masquerade Burst Hem sabotage. |
| III | **Vanishing Thread** | Application | 4 | Thin to practical invisibility, ~25% strength reduction, **2× range**. |
| IV | **Silk Road** | Modifier | 3 | Move intact committed Stitches **through an already connected thread network** without returning to wearer first. |
| V | **Stubborn String** | Application | 4 | Let seams and other Jester-discipline threads burrow through formerly solid material, including living tissue. |

**Razorwire:** Cutting lines are physical hazards when tensioned/moved properly. Barbed variants cost double material and are more dangerous to escape, but neither is infinitely sharp nor indestructible.

**Weed Wacker:** A placed set of seams can lash out on command; the initial offensive activation takes an action. Continued **concentration** sustains sweeping/altering attacks longer than a brief burst. While focusing, the Jester cannot perform unrelated elaborate magical interventions simultaneously, but may still move normally.

**Burrowstitch:** Enter fabric, clothing, armor joints, straps, and vulnerable mechanisms through existing access. Once placed correctly, an inactive embedded seam is **undetectable through ordinary senses and is detectable only by magic**; it may tighten, loosen, jam equipment or deform the fit. It cannot pass through seamless solid matter until Stubborn String is learned. During **Black Masquerade**, committed Burrowstitch gains **Burst Hem**: sacrificial embedded threads rupture from within, with the same deliberate-severance **1 MP per started four Stitches**, not a separate unlimited explosive engine.

**Vanishing Thread:** Material is near invisible; lines are more fragile/effective approximately **25% less strength**, range **doubles**, and a single Stitch may span ~300 m in suitably fine form. It does not turn all cloth into intangible matter.

**Silk Road:** Physically transfer **intact Committed** Stitches from one part of a connected network to another. No new Stitches appear; source thins or weakens as destination strengthens. Cannot teleport across gaps or transfer Cut/Frayed; normal user recovery remains the only way to return unused intact thread to Worn without a special rule.

**Stubborn String:** Threads can burrow through dirt, stone, wood, metal, armor, and living tissue despite no ordinary opening. It lets threads from **other Jester disciplines** use such routes where compatible, but does not produce free cloth. Targets resist where appropriate; wards, resilient materials and cutting counters still matter. Suggested injury scale, conditional on a successful attack: **2–4 Stitches** localized skin tearing, **5–10** serious injury to a vulnerable part, **11–20** severe internal damage, **21+** potentially catastrophic harm to an unprotected ordinary target. These are narrative benchmarks, **not guaranteed lethality, automatic hits, or deterministic damage tables**.

**Seamcraft mastery:** 21 SP.

### Tactical interactions

- **Living Senses + Fine Filament:** observe and feel through widely dispersed probes. Fully disconnected fabric can still act as a stationary sensor, not an unlimited-range remote arm.
- **Puppet Strings + Curtainwall:** Puppet Strings provides sustained controlled flight; kinetic feedback gives explosive, temporary midair propulsion. The defensive attunement still matters.
- **Severed Double + Impact Loom:** full cloth-body coverage makes every physical strike at the clone a fabric contact while Kinetic is active, but the fragile red tether remains the clear countermeasure.
- **Severed Double + Ember Catch:** the clone consumes the Regalia's direct autonomous attention and excludes simultaneously complex energy choreography.
- **Sponge + Understitch + Bandage:** filter certain accessible blood-borne poisons or stop bleeding temporarily; neither grants regeneration or true healing.
- **Sponge + wetsuit:** safely enter hazardous liquids while Fluid-attuned, at the full-body Stitch allocation; a ripped covering immediately creates exposure.
- **Resonant Ribbon + enchanted object:** suppress or enhance an active equipment enchantment, but not forbid new spellcasting or copy the enchantment.
- **Vanishing Thread + Silk Road:** hide a long network while sliding committed fabric between useful positions. Range and strength reductions remain.
- **Burrowstitch + Black Masquerade:** concealed threads become sacrificial internal sabotage and cannot be reclaimed as ordinary intact Stitches afterward.
- **Stubborn String + Rag Doll:** bypass walls with thread attack paths while still respecting material count and bodily vulnerabilities.
- **Rag Doll V + total fabric loss:** bodily self-sacrifice yields up to three emergency Stitches each round; real injuries persist.

### Counters and critical vulnerabilities

The Regalia cannot be protected everywhere when most Stitches are elsewhere. Attack uncovered body parts or the direct red connection of a Severed Double. Cut/burn/tangle strands; interrupt normal recovery; press an attack with a **different type** than the active Curtainwall attunement. Hazardous-liquids protection needs maintained sealed coverage and cannot substitute for breathing or resist every curse. Strong magical resistance matters against hostile ribbons, puppetry, magic drain and stage capture. Destroy Committed structures and their anchor points; oppose Stubborn String with durable structures or appropriate magic. Black Masquerade sacrifices deployed fabric permanently. Neither a large pond reservoir nor stored thermal charges creates instant unlimited offensive damage; flow and resource limits persist.

## 8. Current canon and resolved/superseded terms

1. **Jestercraft total:** **130 SP** for every purchasable ability including **both** independent Hemweaving V applications; **126 SP** if only one of the two has been purchased. **Six** free foundations, **18** modifiers, and **19** applications at full mastery.
2. **Two thinking beings:** Regalia are independent, fully sentient, speaking; Serpentari originally intended hollowing, while most Regalia chose to preserve their wearers as empowered living weapons. Hollow Jesters remain possible without any Recompense/Final Inversion rule.
3. **Thread states:** Worn, Committed, Loose, Frayed, Cut, with a once-daily **Pincushion exception** reversing all currently Frayed to Worn. Frayed timer is 45 seconds in the current tree.
4. **Resources:** Real cloth/Stitches, Regalia+wearer accessible mana capacity, distinct mana costs; regenerating a Stitch costs 2 MP. Black Masquerade sacrifices committed/used cloth, but only on its actual expenditure; Worn cloth survives.
5. **Selectable Curtainwall:** Kinetic → Thermal → Fluid → Arcane unlock sequence; **only one passive attunement** may protect at once, and changing it takes an action. Thermal energy puppetry is finite; complex interventions last at most **18 seconds**.
6. **Sponge formula and immersion protection:** `V(L)=5·2^floor((L−25)/5)` L per Stitch; `Q(L)=2·2^floor((L−25)/10)` L per intake Stitch per round; full-body water / toxic / corrosive / lava costs 4/8/12/16 Stitches. Waterproof mode itself is innate at Rank I.
7. **Hemweaving advancement:** Living Senses, Perfect Masquerade, Fine Filament, Puppet Strings, Severed Double; purchase either or both Living Stage and Empty Performer for **4 SP each** at V. Old Long Hem/Puppet Sleeves/Broadcloth/Grand Unfurling are superseded; ordinary rank-based range remains.
8. **Tailor's Measure:** precise **size alone** is the foundation, on living or nonliving targets; **mass alone** requires Burden Knot. Several bows may launch in one action, each paying physical Stitch costs. Other ribbons control friction, gravity, permeability, ongoing magic and time; old automatic mass change on resizing is superseded.
9. **Black Second Skin:** Understitch, Bandage, Pulse Reader, Thread Parasite, One with the Thread, Encour replace older modifiers/applications. The free baseline includes sacrificial on-demand kinetic severance; 1 MP per started 4 Stitches.
10. **Seamcraft:** Razorwire, Weed Wacker, Burrowstitch, Vanishing Thread, Silk Road and Stubborn String replace the older lineup. Base Anchor Stitch continues to allow ordinary traps and anchors. Range doubles only with Vanishing Thread's thin fragile form.
11. **Rag Doll remains unchanged** and normally costs no mana or fabric to use. Rank V emergency replacement of up to 3 Stitches/round injures the actual body.
12. **Unresolved rather than silently invented:** Black MP activation cost and exact wall durability for Living Stage; exact square-meter coverage of Perfect Masquerade imitation-film; some bounds for reality ribbon size/mass change and enchantment amplification; cross-target limits for Sponge filtering; exact per-type Frayed conversion outside kinetic, and numerical durability of hardened cloth. Approved existing feat, Stitch, and SP benchmarks still apply.
