# Canopy Kingdoms and Industrial Mana Economy

**Status: author-approved worldbuilding directions recovered from November 23–24, 2026 discussion and November 24 handoff.** Numeric economic benchmarks explicitly identified below are experimental, not fixed market prices or Craft mechanics. See [World and Shimmer](01_World_and_Shimmer.md) for approved crop and security details.

## Canopy polity and society
The Canopy Kingdoms are a feudal confederation of sovereign kingdoms with multiple regional High Kings and no supreme monarch. The Crown Council conducts collective diplomacy and has no permanent army of its own. The ancient Canopy Concord governs collective defense and has evolved over time. Common commerce and silver standards, cross-border infrastructure, arbitration, and mutual recognition of titles and citizenship coexist with substantial local autonomy over House Scrip, Avowed grants, treatment of enslaved residents, religion, taxes/tariffs, criminal law, recruitment and regional foreign diplomacy. Internal warfare was historically common and is presently uncommon, but feuds, trade disputes, border conflicts, house wars, crown wars and collective wars remain possible. Five to nine major crowns is a provisional geographic model only; exact count and boundaries remain open. Contrast the negotiated confederation with the centralized Imperium and decentralized rival powers of the Scarlands. Use Scarlander and Canopy lord as established terminology.

Avowed are hereditary free citizens of any race, commonly granted citizenship through emancipation, patronage, distinguished service or asylum. Hereditary freedom is recognized across kingdoms in principle; residence is guaranteed by the patron house; travel is generally permitted. Permanent relocation is subject to receiving-house and local property laws. Avowed status does not confer automatic nobility. Prejudice and house feuds can endanger travelers. 

Approved demographic direction: about 75% enslaved inhabitants. Working split of total population: 12% Avowed, 9% native free commoners, 2% nobility, 2% temporary foreign residents. Enslaved households generally have comparatively high material living standards, private family apartments, leisure and local economic participation while lacking fundamental freedom. House Scrip is locally minted nonprecious currency, unauthorized external exchange generally illegal; lords set goods restrictions. Preferred provision combines guaranteed necessities and discretionary scrip. Enslaved households generally participate in Plenty Day meat subsidies in upstanding towns (see World and Shimmer).

Approved house identity archetypes for later development, not specific houses: Traditionalist (ancient lineage/estates); Industrialist (clockwork, foreign specialists, recent wealth); Agricultural (plantations, Leshy and Beanstocks); Cosmopolitan (Avowed, trade, diversity); Militarist (frontier, guards, fortification); Scholarly (universities, magical research, Centaur science); Declining (historic prestige and dwindling wealth, resistance to industry). Individual lords may differ from archetype.

## Industrial agriculture and energy
Clockwork invented no more than 300 years ago; Canopy industrialization began approximately 70 years ago, shifting power gradually from land and lineage toward industry and trade. Clockwork requires operators and suitable mana fuel, not autonomous work. Leshy cultivation, Tidekin irrigation/sourcing, Cogmarshal/Anvilcant physical machinery and transport, Centaur engineering, Drakyn protection and parrot-folk logistics complement ordinary labor. Ground fields dominate ordinary staple grain; elevated farms favor valuable/perishable produce. This later direction supersedes the earlier generalized treetop-grain assumption in Leshy Foundations. Whole-farm productivity and acreage remain distinct; see latest November 24 entry in World and Shimmer.

Leshy synthesize Nectar using light and mana particles and may rapidly convert their own MP into Nectar. Crop-specific color composition is required; wrong fertilizer may encourage unwanted growth or damage crops. Nectar cannot be converted back to usable MP as an energy loop; bottled Nectar experiences greater entropy than Nectar retained in the Leshy. Ordinary cultivation specialists can perform several conventional farmers' cultivation work; harvesting is not automatically accelerated. Experimental fertilization benchmarks: 1–3 treatments per growing season, about twice requirement without rotation, about four times without rotation or soil rest. No finalized dose, conversion rate, Craft rank or crop yield.

## Mana economy
Distinguish dietary biologically available managmins from depleted environmental mana particles and usable MP. MP is a consistent energy unit. Ordinary civilian capacity is 10 + racial starting bonus; 15 MP is the default reference when unspecified. A demanding workday uses approximately one ordinary reserve; two short rests can restore a combined 50% maximum MP, overnight recovery restores the reserve. Elementals have exceptional reserves/output (working reference 5×; top 2% approximately 100×, top 2% of that group approximately 1,000× baseline); ordinary physical work consumes the same absolute mana as for others. Vital extraction only doubles elemental harvesting efficiency; no general multiplication of ordinary labor. Elementals should generally have other occupations and contribute spare mana. Ordinary work plus surplus collection should normally outperform dedicated conventional harvesting economically, barring special fuel demand/collection constraints.

Refined stored industrial fuel half-life 30 days (working benchmark); frozen Vital Essence spoils in 1–3 years. Leywell collection about 90%; cheap elemental crucibles 75%, expensive 100%; elemental mana is pure/usable but requires crucibles for collection. Codexes prioritize portability and compact storage of large mana quantities but are unstable; Leywells are smaller and extremely stable reservoirs. These industrial reference efficiencies do not alter separately established combat mechanics. Small machinery experimental work-per-MP efficiency ~3× ordinary creature work, commercial ~5–10×; this is not labor productivity.

Environmental associations: white plains, green forests, blue oceans, red mountains/volcanoes, black swamps. Industrial expenditure leaves depleted particles; persistent mismatched discharge can transform ecology.

### Three refining traditions (approved framework)
- Leshy Beanstocks: legendary, powerful-ley-line-associated, year-round plantation-scale plants consuming mana waste and producing bulky mana-rich juice; beneficial waste uptake but cultivation, transport and supporting labor remain necessary. No universal output or conversion rate.
- Nymbrin alchemical circles: 1 compatible usable catalyst + 2 depleted target mana produces 2 usable target + 1 depleted catalyst; Hex and waste generated 1:1 as approved, with precise engineering accounting unresolved. Catalyst cycle: red to blue to green to black to white to red (target red from white, blue from red, green from blue, black from green, white from black). Can circulate/transport enormous quantities, but failures can release catastrophic mana/Hex. Preserve all existing Nymbrin Hex rules.
- Kobold Worldspindles: ancient, irreplaceable, ley-line-bound infrastructure combining prepared matter into one-color mana without magical waste during refining. Green requires cultivated algae. Shutdown risks ley-line collapse. This does not mean downstream mana expenditure is waste-free. Preserve Imperial gazetteer lore; exact material ratios and capacity unresolved.

Experimental bulk prices (silver accounting units/MP): white and green 0.08–0.15, blue 0.12–0.20, black 0.15–0.25, red 0.20–0.35; red 0.25 initial industrial test. Validate against supply labor, losses and capital before fixing prices. Further details not recovered are unresolved, not invented.

## November 24, 2026 — Natural mana ecology and physical geography (AUTHOR-ENDORSED DIRECTION)

Physical geography and material requirements constrain which ecosystems can develop; mana availability and circulation reinforce or gradually transform those ecosystems. Natural mana cycling follows the direction of the established red → blue → green → black → white → red cycle. Healthy ecosystems can regulate mana with finite capacity; industrial discharge is distinct from healthy natural circulation. No mandatory sequence of adjacent biomes. Mana richness or poverty can occur independently of ley-line proximity; abundant aligned mana does not replace missing physical inputs (e.g. green-rich, water-poor land cannot develop a healthy forest solely from green mana).

Author direction: white feeding red may increase geological/tectonic activity even away from existing fault lines, reshaping landscapes. Physical ecological engineering may influence mana conditions, e.g. damming a valley to improve water availability and blue mana supply, which could support downstream green ecology. These are qualitative possibilities, not guaranteed outcomes or new mana sources; timescales, mass/energy balance, conversion mechanics, geological thresholds, ecological impacts and terraforming costs remain open. Apply the latest approved directional 1/2/4/6/8 discharge-impact benchmark above; this does not establish that naturally coexisting colors cannot mix or that ordinary neighboring ecosystems poison one another.
