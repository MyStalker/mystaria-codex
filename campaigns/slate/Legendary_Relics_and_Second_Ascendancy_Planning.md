# Legendary Relics and the Second Ascendancy — Slate campaign long-range planning

**Established DM-approved direction (November 9, 2026), NOT played events.** This is campaign arc canon/planning, not a global rewrite of Mystaria's fundamental magical rules. Proposed item specifics and antagonist names are clearly separated below.

## Central premise — APPROVED

- A few concealed surviving **true ancient Serpentari** are pursuing legendary treasures connected to **named former Heroes or Legends**, who each possess their own meaningful history and **may still be alive**. Under the established world canon **fewer than ten true ancient Serpentari survive, all concealed**.
- Each treasure has an independently valuable and usable normal power. **Anyone may use the normal artifact effect** where its practical interface allows; it should never merely be an inert key to a later device. Do not thereby grant an ordinary user the ability to perform its associated Craft.
- The artifacts are associated with important Mystarian **Crafts**. A user with the appropriate Craft can under exceptionally extreme **genuine shared belief** temporarily realize the power of the object's legend in an astonishing, near-godlike expression of magical control, comparable in narrative impact to an Avatar-State-like surge. The effect is **brief, unsustainable, inconvenient, potentially catastrophic and dangerous**, not a standard skill, free enhancement, or repeatable combat mode.
- The final inventory is intended to contain **six** relics, with **two intentionally open** while more racial Crafts are developed.
- The treasures are independently desirable, and their collection by a concealed Serpentari network is part of a **much larger conspiracy**, eventually surpassing the personal/political scale of the Blackweir arc. Specific machine design, final capability and strict belief thresholds are not yet fixed.
- **Long-term antagonist:** the DM approves the previously proposed character direction for **Ishkarith**, a surviving ancient Serpentari mastermind whose attempt to amass legendary relics and turn mortal legendary power into something approaching a divine or self-sustaining authority underlies the conspiracy. His full four-part name, personal history, the exact workings of a 'Second Ascendancy' / 'Throne of Remembrance', and whether any claimed breakthrough actually works remain **proposals**, not confirmed global history.
- **Foreshadowing:** In the middle part of the story, one character should plausibly have ties to a branch of Ishkarith's intelligence/collector network. A **mid-arc disappearance or theft of the Drowned Meridian**, followed by a possible recovery adventure, is a desired twist, subject to player choice and what actually occurs. No forced choice to pursue it, guaranteed theft, or prewritten outcome.

## Six relic slots — DM-approved design assignments

1. **The Drowned Meridian**: **Tidekin Wakecraft** artifact; a highly prized treasure with independent practical navigational utility relating to hidden waterways, currents and/or buried infrastructure. Its exact normal function and its connection to Neri, Sella and Blackweir's buried Serpentari/kobold infrastructure are still to be finalized. **Proposed** legendary surge: astonishing, dangerous command of connected water and lost channels.
2. **The First Regalia**: replaces the earlier proposed *Last Masque*. This is the **original sentient Regalia** from which the Jester tradition arose, explicitly tied to **Jestercraft**, which is accessible to any compatible wearer rather than racially inherited. Preserve the established history: Regalia were created by Serpentari as tools of punishment and identity control, are living, thinking, speaking entities and can have independent choices. **Proposed** legendary surge: extraordinary stage-scale fabric manipulation; exact former legendary wearer and interaction with Regalia's own sentience TBD.
3. **Witness's Quill** (*working title, slated for reflavoring*): tied jointly to **Vren Bloodbond magic** and **Corvyn Witchcraft / Living Ink tattoos**, potentially also Kharak Tattao later only if the lore can support this. Its exact nature, ordinary effect, legend and legendary surge are **unapproved**. It must not simply erase or rewrite Crimson Ledger bonds, violate the new immutable contract rules, or grant mind reading. Preliminary idea: perceiving or inscribing interactions among marks, oaths and testimony.
4. **Reserved:** name, former wielder, Craft and abilities deliberately undecided.
5. **Reserved:** name, former wielder, Craft and abilities deliberately undecided.
6. **Celestite weapon**: must be one of the six and associated with the Centaur forging Craft **Anvilcant**. **Proposed historical significance:** this weapon may have killed the ancient Serpentari supreme ruler/high king and forced the surviving Serpentari into hiding at the end of the God War. The death and identity of the supreme ruler and the weapon's name/wielder are not yet canon; world naming uses the **Nine Thrones and First Sovereign**, so do not casually create a replacement 'High King' office. Any prospective killing needs reconciliation with established chronology. The weapon is not routine enchanted gear: preserve canon Celestite's **triune-goddess and elite Centaur forging origin**, anti-ley-line effects, **every-stroke permanent soul erosion**, danger of False Apotheosis, and triggering every Drakyn Treasure Compass.

## Essential rules and storytelling safeguards

- Every relic needs a **particular Hero or Legend** and their independent story. An ancient object or original maker is not by itself proof that its associated legendary wielder existed; develop the bearer.
- The surge must require genuine high-order belief/renown. It cannot arise solely from manufactured enthusiasm, a copied title or a simple applause meter. Whether any conspiracy can exploit belief remains unproven.
- A practitioner should not have to imitate the original legendary bearer's values to wield the relic; the artifact's history nevertheless matters. Exact gameplay interface, gating and upper limits remain TBD.
- Preserve Hero divine Shimmer Guard versus Legend reputation-and-belief Guard distinction. Do not assert that either Guard supplies universal omnipotence or that stored popular belief is already a proven extractable power source.
- **Peri's Artificial Legend Matrix** remains an independent incomplete experimental device, not automatically an existing Serpentari project or a functioning relic.
- The personal Blackweir/Sella/Neri/Dusk story remains meaningful. Ishkarith's organization should emerge organically and not retroactively force Sella's own motives or make her a secret collaborator.
- Future NPC affiliations, theft/recovery events, supernatural outcomes and relic locations remain possible plot hooks only until played.
