### Narrator

Lilia was in a most compelling cage, dressed in a top with not but two strips of course fabric around her chest and a flowing, if thin skirt. Velvet panels lined the eloquent stone carved prison cell that was to be hers. She had a cylander of spring water by the door with a large metal rod extending down for her use. And a hampster wheel far to her flank. There were several well worn poetry books on her nightstand and a statue of a most stunning vampire, crudely constructed near the rear of the room. A stone barred window let her peak outside with a sliding door before her.  That's when she felt it down to her very bones. An electrifying feeling, neigh a challenge.
