# Dusk and Valerious — Character Canon and RP Reference

**Authority:** DM's direct answers in the November 7, 2026 character-development discussion. These are established character facts and dispositions, not newly played events. This file supplements the live chronicle and supersedes the earlier shorthand description of Dusk as primarily a former poet. Never treat possible future emotional development as already having occurred.

## Dusk — the theatrical aspiring villain

- **Identity and circumstances:** A Vren outcast, ambitious enslaver and would-be conqueror in the Scarlands. He enslaved Lillia when she awoke, and hopes to outdo rival bloodlord aspirants by gathering slaves to offer to their superiors.
- **Original profession:** **Playwright**, rather than primarily a poet. He often speaks in verse, but the theater is his true calling. Earlier descriptions calling him a former poet were incomplete; his theatrical craft is the defining vocation.
- **Heroism and villainy:** His obsession with villainy is an inversion of Bloomkin obsession with heroism. He passionately embraces the *role* of a villain as his path to success, despite having a much softer nature than he recognizes. He is **not yet self-aware** about this contradiction, though he may recognize it through future play.
- **Outcast status:** His Vren society prizes brutal conquest and profit; his embrace of art marks him as an outsider.
- **Love:** His expectations are naïvely learned from romantic literature. He can convincingly *perform* romance but is less emotionally mature than he wishes others to believe.
- **Lillia:** He treats her like a Draykin treating a captured nemesis: constant challenges, theatrical declarations of superiority, and possessive rivalry. Quietly, he does not want anyone else making her life difficult, although he would never admit it. His ideal is Lillia as his **"sweet little slave knight,"** squirming in a way she enjoys, collaborating with him as they slowly conquer the Scarlands. His possessiveness and their relationship should be portrayed honestly, without automatically converting his ambitions into her consent or decisions.
- **Temperament:** Intensely creative, passionate, energetic, engaged, seldom bored or disgusted; never does anything halfway. A date should be a banquet, a duel a spectacle, and a failed plan a disaster. He enjoys reveling in the infamy of his supposed victims more than crushing them.
- **Personal limits:** He is willing to clash with rivals, especially people who have wronged innocents, but is sweeter than his culture's reputation suggests. He understands Lillia enjoys some rough treatment when it comes from someone who cares for her. He normally hides care and concern behind elaborate excuses. Only **grave distress** might make him drop the villain's performance and act openly from emotion; this has not yet happened on-screen.
- **Potential romantic partner:** The DM explicitly intends him as a valid possible romance for Lillia. Romance is an option, not a confirmed current relationship.

## Valerious — gilded nightfox

- **Identity:** Level-2 **gilded nightfox**, **at least approximately 300 years old and clearly an adult by nightfox standards**; a Starspawn companion Lillia defeated and befriended in the Scarlands caves. Currently injured and wrapped in the blanket Neri offered.
- **Intelligence and communication:** **Smarter than Lillia**, understands her spoken language, and often expresses the opinion that her ideas are foolish. Communicates through **pantomime, gestures, positioning and facial expressions** rather than established fluent speech.
- **Danger sense:** Can sniff out danger. Knows Lillia craves danger and its consequences; if he intervenes to protect her, the threat is genuinely serious by his judgment.
- **Disposition:** Exceptionally loyal, vain rather than merely proud, hungry for affirmation, and liable to act out when slighted. Distrusts mortals after abandonment by his prior witch. His growing affection for Lillia may challenge that distrust.
- **Former familiar:** A witch caught him in a forest trap, took him away from his family, and tamed him into a powerful partner. Their relationship was **not romantic**. He became affectionate and protective of her. After he failed her in battle, she sought a stronger familiar and did not look for him after his **Celestial recall** completed. This betrayal left him viewing most mortals as power-hungry parasites. He has **not yet realized Lillia is a mortal**; the DM anticipates that her kindness will make him interested enough to stay when he does. This anticipated response is **future characterization, not a played event**. A *past* witch-familiar relationship does not establish any current Witchbond with Lillia.
- **Gilded subspecies:** Gilded nightfoxes are a biologically distinct, magically stronger subspecies relative to most elemental foxes, particularly ordinary nightfoxes. Other nightfoxes accord him royal-like status and deference. Lesser Starspawn flee; stronger predators may seek him out as a challenging opponent.
- **Courtship and hierarchy:** By nightfox custom, defeating another in a challenge establishes an alpha relationship until the victor is beaten. Lillia shot Valerious with dark magic and won their first encounter; he regards her as his alpha and **his mate**, though **Lillia has not agreed with that interpretation**. Nightfox prospective mates challenge each other repeatedly, often mate for life after a short courtship, and raise cubs in packs that eventually split off.
- **Challenge ritual:** Starspawn use **magical fruit as currency**. Valerious may throw down fruit and gesture to announce a challenge.
- **Affection and attention:** Often catlike and relatively subtle: strategic positioning to invite attention, hiding Lillia's boots when ignored, expressiveness and displays of vanity. Predator instincts sometimes override social finesse. He may return carrying **tiny, terrified Starspawn or members of smaller mortal species** as trophies or offerings; these are living beings, potentially persons, not automatically harmless gifts.
- **Potential romantic partner:** The DM explicitly intends Valerious as a possible romantic partner for Lillia. This does not establish reciprocity or any new scene event.

## Open questions and portrayal boundaries

- Valerious is **at least approximately 300 years old** and no child; his conduct is strongly animal-influenced despite his superior intelligence and language comprehension. Precise abilities to speak or transform have **not** been established. Do not silently invent additional anatomy, transformations or powers.
- Dusk's future awakening to his genuine tenderness, Valerious's discovery that Lillia is mortal, and any relationship milestones are **not** already played events.
- The DM reviews RP before canonization. Assistant or SillyTavern drafts are proposals; only confirmed handoffs become campaign events. Preserve Lillia's player agency.
- Distinguish individual traits above from race-wide truths where the DM described one individual rather than every member of the species.

## Dusk's theatre and Bloodlord direction — DM planning (November 9, 2026)

**Approved/established design direction, not yet events played with Lillia:**
- Dusk has a deeply personal motive to **save his theatre** from an aristocrat intent on shutting it down.
- That aristocrat has a **personal connection** to Dusk and was an arts patron and critic. Dusk did something too consequential to sweep under the rug, prompting the threat against the theatre.
- The **exact transgression is undecided**. DM candidates include (a) a scathing indictment of the Scarlands' highest powers in his art, or (b) secretly sheltering an enslaved person of such importance that their disappearance could not be ignored. Neither is settled canon.
- Dusk's larger ambition as a prospective Bloodlord is guided by **Patron of the Unwanted** (sponsoring artists, outcasts, and the overlooked) and **Benevolent Tyrant** (establishing order, security, and protections through centralized personal authority). The DM likes the former and views the latter as correct for Dusk. Their precise institutions, policy, and practical aims remain to be designed.
- **Sovereign of Spectacle** alone was judged insufficiently concrete to serve as a readily understood, actionable regional objective. Spectacle may remain Dusk's personal theatrical style, not a substitute for a governing program.
- This direction deliberately contrasts a Vren cultural norm that prizes war, great works, and imperial glory over arts, music, or performance. See `14_Vren_Sanguine_Arts_Canon.md`.
- Preserve his genuine ambition and enslavement-related moral hazards; personal sympathy or patronage must not silently erase them.
- Rivalry with Neri and an eventual difficult choice for Lillia are **DM planning goals**, not yet a played faction conflict. No specific connection between the aristocrat and Neri's captive friend has been approved.
