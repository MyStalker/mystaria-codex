## Scene 1 — Relic choice and return to Neri

**Intent:** Make opening the vault rewarding, establish Neri's need for Meridian, offer two *additional* curiosities, allow practical clues and character reactions.

**Suggested beats, not scripted outcomes:**
1. Opening: surviving kobold worktables, pressure-proof drawers, a Serpentari confiscation catalog and distinct research displays. Lillia may inspect, interact, barter, or avoid. No forced acquisition.
2. Meridian: ornate water-sensitive multiple-needle instrument. Dry needles nearly inert; a little water stirs them. Neri, once reunited, recognizes **the largest needle's sought route** but cannot explain the others or the hidden water scaling.
3. Present **five curiosities**, let Slate choose **any two** in addition to the Meridian; avoid handing out all five automatically.
4. Investigator finds: **damaged kobold research notebook** (partial translation/restoration needed) and **deliberately sealed ancient vial of water** containing potential Mirrorwake traces. These are *clues*, not part of the two-item choice; other clues remain available for later.
5. Aftermath: account for cold Lillia, Emberwrap now on Peri, injured Valerious and unfinished Matrix. Neri has concerns about Peri and Sella; players decide where to go next. Do not award Surrender quest completion or perks without DM deciding.

### Five proposed choices (choose two; ALL item mechanics and exact costs DM approval pending)

1. **Pocket Surveyor**: palm-size kobold clockwork beetle; maps and retraces a short route, explores tiny gaps; limited memory; cannot be a free fully aware spy/drone or grant full Orbcraft. *Exploration.*
2. **Echo Pearl**: tiny experimental orb records and replays about one minute of sound, replacing old recording. *Deception, testimony, messages.* Does not copy minds or create real voices from nothing.
3. **Coilkeeper's Grip**: Serpentari test bracelet, once per short rest supplies one brief stabilizing grip to hold an unattended object, anchor its user or secure a rope; no automatic hostile grapple. *Traversal and rescues.*
4. **Comfortweave**: small kobold thermal experimental fabric maintains ordinary comfortable temperature over a limited local body area for hours, then recharges during rest; not protection from serious exposure extremes, immersion or elemental attacks. *Restores an avenue to comfort for underdressed Lillia, distinct from her gifted Emberwrap.*
5. **Second-Sight Mirror**: ancient inspection mirror highlighting faint magical traces on nearby viewed objects, not universal True Sight, spell identification or through-wall vision. *Investigations.*

**Suggested default recommendations:** Comfortweave and Coilkeeper's Grip, but the player gets a real choice of two. Optional alternative: make all five objects viable through rough repairs and tradeoffs instead of arbitrary loot restrictions.

## Scene 2 — Return to Dusk and his hideout

**Intent:** Reintroduce Dusk as both Lillia's former captor and a compelling theatrical patron with a concrete recruit quest. Her earlier escape remains relevant.

**Minimal hideout proposal, not yet approved map/canon:** A small repurposed Vren **service annex** hidden near Blackweir's underground transit/workshop connections; iron cages and a dramatic ceremonial dais share cramped space with discarded scripts, costume trunks, stage lamps, a battered writing desk and a locked archive. A side maintenance exit plausibly leads into the underground cave from which Lillia escaped within minutes. Does not replace Dusk's **separate endangered theatre** in the Grand Arch; don't accidentally make the theatre the prison/cage room. Avoid implying he secretly already owns a vast underground fortress.

**Suggested beats:** He reacts to the escaped 'little slave knight' with genuine surprise concealed behind self-important theatrical speeches; may demand explanations or attempt to posture. His urgent needs: save the theatre from a patron/owner offended by sheltering Sella, recruit three independently minded dangerous outlaws, and ultimately establish an autonomous Blackweir. He offers Lillia a commission, resources, information or a mutually appealing goal rather than presuming obedience. She can refuse, argue, bargain, return later or go elsewhere. No fabricated unplayed magical Bloodbond binds Lillia without a valid roleplay event; she was caged, not proven to be legally bound.

**Sella information Dusk can share:** she once escaped enslavement with his help, became the beloved theatre diva, disagreed with him intelligently, and is now missing in relation to a self-directed political operation. He **does not know her full secret legal conspiracy**. What he last heard from her, how much he suspects, and what leads he has should be determined at the table. Neri separately wants Sella found and a slave-free Blackweir. Dusk must not speak for Sella or settle her fate.

## Scene 3 — Mara Vetch, first outlaw recruit

**Intent:** Launch a dangerous mission with an intelligible target, not teleport Lillia into a completed recruitment.

**Mara:** Duskpaw monumental architect, active outlaw commanding construction/resistance efforts at the **Broken Colossus**. She refuses uncompensated forced labor; Dusk seeks enough crisis powers to rapidly build and defend a nascent city-state.

**Three-access proposal:** Lillia could follow a hazardous labor-smuggler supply line; acquire a foreman's mark or stolen survey map; or gain help from builders sympathetic to Mara. Each route can lead to a distinct encounter or discovery, no single required check. The first peril might be a patrol intercepting runaway laborers, unsafe collapsed scaffolding, or a camp of suspicious workers. Avoid immediately forcing level-3 Lillia into a boss-scale army fight.

**Player agency:** Earning a hearing is the initial victory. A policy negotiation is not required on first meeting, and neither Dusk nor Mara is predetermined to win. Possible settlements: paid emergency service, strictly voluntary labor mobilization with meaningful incentives, time-limited exceptional authority with oversight, or other solution proposed by Slate. Exact design unapproved.

## Vault loot revision — DM-directed upgrade, November 9 2026

**SUPERCEDES the five weak curiosity choices above.** The DM wants an upgraded, player-meaningful selection of **SIX additional reward options** (the Meridian is separate); Slate gets to choose **two bonus items**, not every object. The six proposals below are a working slate. User-mandated categories and properties are accepted design constraints; precise names, exact stats, daily recharge and individual item details are assistant proposals requiring further approval. Research notebook and sealed Mirrorwake-readable water vial remain **story clues outside the two bonus rewards**. Check [Meridian loot clue register](Meridian_Loot_Clue_Register.md).

1. **Trufula weapon, 'Cinderleaf' prototype:** a lightweight **Trufula short blade** with a strange utility effect: on a successful strike or tap against an inanimate object, the blade momentarily marks the spot with a faint, visible colored trail of magical sparks, letting the bearer trace a retreat or mark a target for a short while. Better proposed variant: **'Backstep Thorn' Trufula knife**, its on-hit special tags a **single inanimate surface or target** with a faint shimmer; the wielder can recall the knife once, sending it along the last clear straight return path, no teleportation or extra attack. **Avoid free high-level magical damage.** Canon Trufula is light, almost physically indestructible, **×4 fire-weak** and exceptionally combustible; ordinary water doesn't reliably extinguish an established blaze. Handle this honestly.
2. **Adamantite 'Pocket Surveyor' upgraded:** intelligent clockwork reconnaissance beetle with **small Adamantite defensive shell/joints** (not a mass of heavy solid Adamantite). Can independently scout simple assigned routes, hide, observe, return, and relay a short audible account or stored visual snapshots. It is a non-sapient limited machine, not independent unlimited spy AI, no free stealth guarantee, bounded range and recharge. This showcases second iconic Centaur forged material without creating an impossibly heavy early-level sword.
3. **Refilling restoration flask, 'Dawnwell Flask' (working name):** contains **one modest healing draught** that magically refills once each day. Never more than one dose stored, no infinite decanting/selling, not a resurrection/heal-all. Unlike alchemy kit, this is the sole healing reward option.
4. **Earth smartphone, 'The Far-Screen' (working identifier):** authentic Earth cell phone preserved among confiscated curiosities, formerly gifted by a goddess to **another Hero**, not Lillia's own previously unmentioned starting gift; her **Warlock path is her approved original divine gift**. Phone's battery remains at 100% and it has Earth internet connectivity despite the interuniversal divide, but not unrestricted contact with Mystaria's networks. Websites can provide general Earth knowledge/media but not real-time Mystaria information, guaranteed salvation, free spellcasting or credible directions. Define practical UX limitations with DM (data, apps, contact messages, location services).
5. **Portable Leywell, 'Prayerwell' or 'Twin-Draw Well' (working names):** genuine small charged Leywell suitable for a Warlock to absorb mana from **approximately twice her current per-absorption cap**, i.e. a total reservoir for **two full draws** when full, not double per-action intake. **Exact MP amount** must reference Lillia's actual current Warlock absorb cap (not fetched/confirmed here), so write it as `2 × Lillia's normal one-action mana absorption amount` until numbers are verified. Leywells charge through sincere prayers per existing canon, not automatically once per day unless DM adds that exception. Avoid creating mana from nothing.
6. **Self-cultivating Wetcraft field kit, 'Sprigglass Atelier' (working name):** portable Nymbrin-style training alchemist kit: tools, a beginner notebook, tiny renewable ingredient culture that yields limited small batches after cultivation, and **nonhealing general-utility potions** such as short-duration light/visibility aid, anti-fog breathing preparation (not immunity to Haze), adhesive solvent, and brief odor masking. **No healing potions in this kit.** Supports exploration of learning foreign-racial Crafts through ordinary progression; does not automatically grant Wetcraft ranks, SP, free materials in unlimited quantity, or skip the ethics/side-effects of Wetcraft. All Nymbrin alchemy produces hex; manage its containment and scaled low-level risk rather than ignoring established hazards.

**Immediate DM prep:** Show physical prototypes as separate research pieces, each visibly different. The choice belongs to Slate. The cellular device is especially strange to local residents, but familiar to the Earth-born Hero. Ensure staff can describe each item before choosing, ideally with limited demonstrations or lab tags. Don't script player choices or grant her two items until played.
