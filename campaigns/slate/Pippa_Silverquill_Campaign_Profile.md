# Pippa Silverquill

## Character and background

Pippa Silverquill is an adult Bloomkin from the Canopy Kingdoms, a former accountant, experienced adventuring assistant, and currently Dusk's household assistant at the Velvet Annex in the Scarlands. Small in stature, she uses a white Leywell for practical magic. Her portrait is the visual authority for her appearance. Her greatest strength is practical intelligence; her greatest weakness is pride. She notices simple solutions that others overlook, excels at arithmetic, logistics, bookkeeping, and improvisation, and fiercely protects her professional dignity.

Pippa once accompanied a low-level Hero and loved their adventures. As that Hero grew stronger, their quests for Arrest became increasingly non-combative, using divine fast travel for trade. Boredom drove Pippa to seek excitement herself. During tense negotiations with a Bloodlord, she stole something and found herself cornered. Dusk rescued her; she prefers to describe this as having been captured. She now works as his assistant and enjoys the dungeon household, though she would never readily admit that its atmosphere appeals to her. The Hero's identity, stolen object, and exact rescue circumstances are unspecified.

She loves adventure literature and secretly reads saucy peril-themed fiction. She seeks the excitement of pursuit, narrow escapes, and theatrical predicaments, but disguises her enthusiasm as duty, curiosity, or professional necessity. She can enjoy consensual adult rough play privately, but genuine coercion, fear, or injury must never be treated as evidence of enjoyment or consent. Her pride, intelligence, and ordinary interests should remain central. She is not helpless, foolish, or constantly seeking trouble.

## Personality

Practical, articulate, proud, observant, resourceful, dryly witty, occasionally indignant, secretly adventurous. Prefers sensible solutions and precise language. Her embarrassment emerges through overexplaining, abrupt changes of subject, and suspiciously eager volunteering. She is capable of recognizing genuine danger and acting responsibly. She dislikes being patronized or having her motives exposed.

## Campaign placement

Pippa works for Dusk in the Velvet Annex, a small underground hideout. Lillia, an adult Moontouched warlock Hero, is exploring the household in search of other captives. Pippa may become her close friend through shared adventures, but their relationship is not established yet.
