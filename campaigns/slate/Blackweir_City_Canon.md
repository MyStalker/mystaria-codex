# Blackweir — Campaign City Canon and Working Districts

**Status:** DM-approved setting for Slate's first arc (November 9, 2026). These are established **campaign-setting facts**, not events that have occurred on-screen. Some district-specific features are **provisionally accepted canon** and explicitly subject to DM review. Do not promote to universal Mystaria canon without a separate decision.

## Identity and location

- **Blackweir** is a substantial, politically and economically important **secondary city in the Scarlands**, **not the capital** and not the seat of the Crimson Sovereign.
- It grew atop **major Serpentari ruins**. Ancient **kobold workshops beneath the city** are an important part of its roots and ongoing success, including the buried infrastructure associated with the existing cave-workshop adventure. Exact geography of individual caves, entrances and underground connections remains to be mapped.
- Blackweir is a **waterway and trade city** whose locks, reservoirs, canals, and alchemical pumps incorporate, reuse or depend upon ancient subterranean engineering. The present-day city does not fully understand all its older systems.
- Nymbrin guild expertise and alchemical maintenance are economically important, so guild cooperation, maintenance disruption, water control, embargoes, trade networks and military access could substantially affect any attempt at independence.
- Blackweir's population, map, ruling families, exact district boundaries and distance from other cities remain undecided.

## The Grand Arch / the Archouse

- **The Grand Arch** is the primary and prestigious name of Blackweir's enormous surviving Serpentari monumental structure; **the Archouse** is its common vernacular name.
- **Supersedes** the earlier proposed square-plan opera complex named the Grand Coil or Coilhouse. The approved monumental form is now an **immense arch**, rather than a square building.
- The arch was built at a **colossal, Serpentari-compatible scale**, for immense serpentine inhabitants and public ceremony. Much of the **modern city lies beneath and inside it**, not merely around a self-contained opera house. Whole neighborhoods and districts occupy its interior spaces, foundations, arches and sheltered expanses.
- It is understood as **Blackweir's last surviving major aboveground monumental Serpentari construction**; this does not negate the **surviving buried Serpentari and kobold ruins and workshops** beneath the city.
- The ancient monument originally supported ceremonial display, public declarations, spectacles and imperial functions; later inhabitants adapted its spaces for civic, commercial and theatrical life. Exact original religious/political uses remain open for refinement.
- A modern opera and theatre district operates within the Grand Arch. **Dusk's endangered theatre is part of this theatrical ecosystem**, making preservation of the site central to his personal ambitions.
- The Arch also makes an excellent practical and symbolic strategic prize in the competing plans for the city, but no faction's final ownership or victory is predetermined.

## Working internal districts — provisionally accepted as canon, open to revision

The DM accepts these zones **for now** but has **not carefully scrutinized every detail**. Preserve their names and broad functions as a current working map; do not treat every illustrative shop, passage or institution as permanently fixed.

1. **The Velvet Ring:** The modern theatrical and artistic quarter: Dusk's theatre, stages, rehearsal halls, costume makers, performer lodgings, drinking houses and patron establishments.
2. **The Old Galleries:** Vast surviving ceremonial halls, serpent-scale balconies, monumental galleries and elite rooms adapted to present-day storage, residences, gatherings and intrigue.
3. **The Market Coil:** Commercial districts nested into the arch: food stalls, markets, booksellers, jewelers, traders, guild representatives, couriers and potentially illicit trade.
4. **The Understage:** Ancient service spaces, lifts, machine chambers, performance mechanisms and concealed corridors with potential links to the deeper kobold workshops and city infrastructure.
5. **The Hollow Wards:** Partially ruined, sealed, improvised or repurposed quarters housing poorer residents, hidden workshops, fugitives and forgotten chambers. Their precise social composition and physical limits remain provisional.

## Campaign roles and future design

- **Dusk:** Wants to save his endangered theatre and eventually establish an **independent city-state** capable of defying larger Scarlands political institutions. He values artistic patronage and strong governing authority.
- **Neri:** Wants Blackweir to become a **bastion without slavery**, possibly helping enslaved people beyond its walls. The practical pressures of independence, guild dependence, corruption, sovereignty, military power and compromised emancipation will challenge her, but total abolition should remain a viable hard-won outcome.
- **Sella Brightwake:** The Tidekin star of Dusk's theatre and Neri's profoundly loved companion; her disappearance may involve a self-directed, dangerous political scheme. Details are still open.
- **Mara, Varrin Hollowpage and Tikka:** Their active outlaw networks may separately contribute infrastructure, intelligence and communications/press to different prospective Blackweir futures. Their eventual loyalties and political compromises depend on Lillia's choices.
- **Potential treasure connection, NOT established:** The Drowned Meridian or an alternative vault MacGuffin might reveal/access buried waterways or Serpentari routes. Neither specific treasure nor its effect has been approved.
- The city and district work is **worldbuilding**, not a record of Lillia having visited these places or witnessed particular events.

## Unresolved

Blackweir's exact ruler, aristocratic patron of Dusk's theatre, the powerful owner who formerly enslaved Sella, Sella's current whereabouts, the Grand Arch's precise silhouette and engineering, the civic map, specific guild relationships, holiday details, underground access points, the military revolutionary and the fate of any proposed independence movement.
