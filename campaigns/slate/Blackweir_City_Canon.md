# Blackweir — Campaign City Canon and Working Districts

**Status:** DM-approved setting for Slate's first arc (November 9, 2026). These are established **campaign-setting facts**, not events that have occurred on-screen. Some district-specific features are **provisionally accepted canon** and explicitly subject to DM review. Do not promote to universal Mystaria canon without a separate decision.

## Identity and location

- **Blackweir** is a substantial, politically and economically important **secondary city in the Scarlands**, **not the capital** and not the seat of the Crimson Sovereign.
- It grew atop **major Serpentari ruins**. Ancient **kobold workshops beneath the city** are an important part of its roots and ongoing success, including the buried infrastructure associated with the existing cave-workshop adventure. Exact geography of individual caves, entrances and underground connections remains to be mapped.
- Blackweir is a **waterway and trade city** whose locks, reservoirs, canals, and alchemical pumps incorporate, reuse or depend upon ancient subterranean engineering. The present-day city does not fully understand all its older systems.
- Nymbrin guild expertise and alchemical maintenance are economically important, so guild cooperation, maintenance disruption, water control, embargoes, trade networks and military access could substantially affect any attempt at independence.
- Blackweir's population, map, ruling families, exact district boundaries and distance from other cities remain undecided.

## The Grand Arch / the Archouse

- **The Grand Arch** is the primary and prestigious name of Blackweir's enormous surviving Serpentari monumental structure; **the Archouse** is its common vernacular name.
- **Supersedes** the earlier proposed square-plan opera complex named the Grand Coil or Coilhouse. The approved monumental form is now an **immense arch**, rather than a square building.
- The arch was built at a **colossal, Serpentari-compatible scale**, for immense serpentine inhabitants and public ceremony. Much of the **modern city lies beneath and inside it**, not merely around a self-contained opera house. Whole neighborhoods and districts occupy its interior spaces, foundations, arches and sheltered expanses.
- It is understood as **Blackweir's last surviving major aboveground monumental Serpentari construction**; this does not negate the **surviving buried Serpentari and kobold ruins and workshops** beneath the city.
- The ancient monument originally supported ceremonial display, public declarations, spectacles and imperial functions; later inhabitants adapted its spaces for civic, commercial and theatrical life. Exact original religious/political uses remain open for refinement.
- A modern opera and theatre district operates within the Grand Arch. **Dusk's endangered theatre is part of this theatrical ecosystem**, making preservation of the site central to his personal ambitions.
- The Arch also makes an excellent practical and symbolic strategic prize in the competing plans for the city, but no faction's final ownership or victory is predetermined.

## Working internal districts — provisionally accepted as canon, open to revision

The DM accepts these zones **for now** but has **not carefully scrutinized every detail**. Preserve their names and broad functions as a current working map; do not treat every illustrative shop, passage or institution as permanently fixed.

1. **The Velvet Ring:** The modern theatrical and artistic quarter: Dusk's theatre, stages, rehearsal halls, costume makers, performer lodgings, drinking houses and patron establishments.
2. **The Old Galleries:** Vast surviving ceremonial halls, serpent-scale balconies, monumental galleries and elite rooms adapted to present-day storage, residences, gatherings and intrigue.
3. **The Market Coil:** Commercial districts nested into the arch: food stalls, markets, booksellers, jewelers, traders, guild representatives, couriers and potentially illicit trade.
4. **The Understage:** Ancient service spaces, lifts, machine chambers, performance mechanisms and concealed corridors with potential links to the deeper kobold workshops and city infrastructure.
5. **The Hollow Wards:** Partially ruined, sealed, improvised or repurposed quarters housing poorer residents, hidden workshops, fugitives and forgotten chambers. Their precise social composition and physical limits remain provisional.

## Campaign roles and future design

- **Dusk:** Wants to save his endangered theatre and eventually establish an **independent city-state** capable of defying larger Scarlands political institutions. He values artistic patronage and strong governing authority.
- **Neri:** Wants Blackweir to become a **bastion without slavery**, possibly helping enslaved people beyond its walls. The practical pressures of independence, guild dependence, corruption, sovereignty, military power and compromised emancipation will challenge her, but total abolition should remain a viable hard-won outcome.
- **Sella Brightwake:** The Tidekin star of Dusk's theatre and Neri's profoundly loved companion; her disappearance may involve a self-directed, dangerous political scheme. Details are still open.
- **Mara, Varrin Hollowpage and Tikka:** Their active outlaw networks may separately contribute infrastructure, intelligence and communications/press to different prospective Blackweir futures. Their eventual loyalties and political compromises depend on Lillia's choices.
- **Potential treasure connection, NOT established:** The Drowned Meridian or an alternative vault MacGuffin might reveal/access buried waterways or Serpentari routes. Neither specific treasure nor its effect has been approved.
- The city and district work is **worldbuilding**, not a record of Lillia having visited these places or witnessed particular events.

## Crimson Ledger and Crimson Drayke — DM-directed worldbuilding in development (November 9, 2026)

**DM-established design premises, pending system polish before final global canon adoption:**
- Each major Vren Blood Court should be centered on a **Crimson Ledger**: an authoritative supernatural record/arbiter for registered Bloodbond contracts. Its original writing is immutable; a legitimate political court, noble, sovereign or dragon cannot simply erase or rewrite established terms. **The Ledger's word is final within its defined contractual jurisdiction**.
- Bloodbonds combine exacting technical rules with creative drafting. To end an enforceable Ledger covenant, its own termination or satisfaction conditions must occur, or a genuine clause-based loophole must apply; political preference or a favorable ruling is not enough. Details of invalid formation, enforcement suspension and magical challenges need reconciliation with existing Vren Bloodbond ranks.
- **Crimson Drayke** is an intentional title (do not change spelling to Drakyn/Drake). A Baphomet-aligned Drakyn can become a Crimson Drayke, irreplaceably magically linked to a Crimson Ledger, intentionally independent of Vren political powers to mitigate corruption. They communicate with and influence the Ledger and are the foremost experts in composing and interpreting powerful covenants. They cannot erase established contracts, introduce obligations without required consent, or wildly distort valid readings. Their expert manipulation of contract interactions offers meaningful but limited power.
- Not every ordinary slave contract needs direct Drayke involvement. However, contracts governing hundreds of enslaved people or complex high-value obligations risk devastating drafting/interdependency errors without Drayke consultation.
- These directions should be stress-tested particularly because Neri wants to unravel Bloodbonds and abolish slavery in Blackweir. **Sella's political conspiracy cannot work merely by forging ordinary legal documents to override magically immutable contracts**. Her possible deeper-law loophole discovery is still a proposal, not a played or approved scheme.

**Existing canon that must be preserved or deliberately reconciled:** `02_Races.md` already names a **Crimson Ledger faction** alongside Shattered Bell and Unwritten; `14_Vren_Sanguine_Arts_Canon.md` says Vowscar requires agreed obligations and allows powerful coercive Bloodbond curses to be resisted/challenged/broken through magic. The exact relationship of those curses to registered Ledger contracts is not settled; do not silently negate prior rules. `06_Drakyn.md` establishes Drakyn oath culture and Baphomet-aligned population, while `33_Kharak_Rexfolk_Culture_and_Command.md` establishes independent Drakyn arbitration in Imperial legal custom.

**Assistant suggestions awaiting approval:** ledger immutability versus active status distinction, optional Ledger registration and enhanced enforcement, nonretroactive contract interaction rules, hereditary-clause limitations, dragon office tenure and succession, neutral dispute procedures, scope of contract consent, proof rules, remedies for invalid formation, and mass covenant pitfalls. These have NOT yet been accepted as finalized binding world mechanics.


## Bloodbond redesign: DM-approved premises and pending mechanical design (November 9, 2026)

**Approved design direction, not yet mechanically finalized:**
- All Bloodbonds require authentic blood from **both parties**. The incision used in formation is magically important; physical punishment for resisting a covenant often manifests as bleeding from the ritual cut, alongside escalating pain and involuntary pressure to comply.
- Explicit DM instruction: **Previous Vren Bloodbond creation and ranked mechanics are NONCANON pending revision.** Do not infer that those old abilities remain authoritative. The stable Blood Court culture and black-mana blood-contract premise remain.
- Bloodbonds enforce short, comparatively broad **operative clauses**: typically about three sentences and no more than roughly one paragraph of actual magical compulsion. They do not read minds, understand nuanced speech, parse long inventories of behavioral rules, or perform perfect semantic interpretation.
- A larger durable Ledger record may store many auxiliary legal provisions (death, impossibility, succession, time limits, exemptions) using **magically bound citations to specific, immutable creation-time versions** of existing legal documents; later amendments cannot retroactively alter existing covenants. The Ledger is immense but not infinitely capacious.
- Operative clauses can be **priority ordered**, with earlier/higher provisions overriding lower ones only when conflicting. Example proposed by DM: (1) obey direct master's orders; (2) never abandon master, potentially using an approximately one-mile threshold; (3) never commit violence. A direct order to defend the master can override the lower violence rule within the necessary scope. The master can explicitly suspend/exempt a subordinate from specific duties temporarily or permanently under established covenant terms.
- Direct commands normally prioritize the **most recent applicable order**; e.g. an objectively completed cleaning task ends, while an open-ended 'clean the bedchamber' may impose continuing obligation until replaced or ended. Details of simultaneous compatible instructions and humane physiological limits remain to design.
- Resisting orders generates **intrusive mental compulsion plus physical punishment**, with greater duration and intensity causing more severe pain and bleeding at the original ritual cut. The bond reacts quickly but NOT instantly: a determined captive may complete a forbidden action before agony/incapacitation stops further conduct. Its routine purpose is control, not death or lasting crippling; extreme or abusive conditions may nevertheless kill/injure. Hard mechanical timing, resistance and safe blood-loss ceilings remain unapproved.
- **The Crimson Ledger** refers to the supernatural court registry, not the traditionalist faction of the same earlier name. **Rename that faction** to an appropriate different name; suggested 'Scarlet Concord', 'Red Quill', and 'Unbroken Seal' are **unapproved** proposals. The Shattered Bell and Unwritten remain unchanged.

**Pending design challenges, not established rules:** How coerced blood formation satisfies legal authorization vs moral consent; how magical interpretation of ordinary observable orders is bounded; precise command precedence and duration; physiological pauses/rest; death/inheritance of bound parties; impossible and conflicting obligations; procedures for identifying a cited law's exact creation-time text; whether contracts can be transferred or waived and who qualifies as master; distinguish ordinary cursed Bloodbond severance from Ledger contract termination; and safeguards preventing new contracts from arising purely through magical fraud.


## DM-approved Red Quill name and reciprocal/forced violation behavior (November 9, 2026)

**Definitive name resolution:** The faction previously named **Crimson Ledger** in the Vren racial overview is now **the Red Quill**. Reserve **Crimson Ledger** exclusively for the supernatural contractual registry/institution. The Red Quill is a faction of contract scholars/legal traditionalists defending the importance of covenant law. The Shattered Bell and Unwritten remain separately named factions. Earlier file passages describing a name conflict are historical development notes and superseded by this ruling.

**Approved directions for enforcement (full timing/numbers not finalized):**
- Both parties contribute blood to a Bloodbond; **both masters and servants can be bound** and punished for violated obligations. Contractual magic is impartial about rank, not about the moral justice of its demands. Example: a Golden Lease requires a Vren master to keep a Nymbrin's water tub supplied. A leak and consequent failure trigger the master's own bond; the Nymbrin might cheerfully eat a banana while the master urgently arranges a replacement.
- A slave **kidnapped beyond an explicit spatial limit** can suffer continuous, severe compulsion, agony and bleeding from the vow incision despite having no ability to comply. They may survive for a long time, especially with healing support, but the suffering remains genuinely terrible. Do not automatically waive enforcement because the violation was involuntary. An actual covenant's cited impossibility/force-majeure clauses, if any, govern exceptions.
- **Precise drafting distinction:** 'Never abandon your master' ordinarily denotes voluntary abandonment and would not by itself cover being forcibly carried away; a clause such as 'Remain within one mile of your master, except when specifically released' is necessary to impose a strict spatial boundary regardless of cause. The distance of about one mile is a working example, not a universal automatic Bloodbond radius.
- Routine Bloodbond punishment should remain **controlling rather than designed to kill or permanently cripple**. Severe sustained violation can incapacitate, plateau, recur and cause prolonged suffering. Whether/when external damage, untreated bleeding or special extreme clauses become lethal remains for full mechanics design; healing can treat injury without erasing the breached obligation.
- Reciprocal covenants and high-value Golden Leases should be especially instructive for the danger of badly specified requirements and missing contingency provisions.

**Further assistant recommendations, not mechanically settled:** distinguish continuous breach from one-time prohibited acts, the exact plateau and reset behavior, whether sincere efforts at compliance moderate penalties, how involuntary violation is detected, and how impossible commands work without automatic mercy. The DM accepts the overall previous conceptual suggestions, but precise game mechanics are not yet ratified.

## Bloodbond enforcement edge cases — DM-approved canon (November 9, 2026)

The DM explicitly approved **all eight** of these rulings as established Bloodbond canon. They supersede tentative suggestions and apply as guiding rules even while numerical progression and rank mechanics remain under redesign:

1. **Impossible orders:** Compulsion persists even when fulfillment is impossible. Pain and bleeding rise toward a generally survivable plateau. Only applicable contractual exceptions suspend enforcement.
2. **Ambiguous orders:** Magic follows the clearest ordinary interpretation of the command. A portion with no sufficiently clear actionable meaning is not magically enforceable.
3. **Conflicting orders:** Higher-priority clauses supersede lower ones; among equally ranked conflicting direct commands, the most recent controls.
4. **Exhaustion/rest:** No automatic rest exemption. Physical incapacity can stop activity but does not by itself discharge the obligation. Inconsiderate or abusive drafting can inflict prolonged suffering.
5. **Punishment ending:** The bond's enforcement fades relatively promptly when an active violation ceases. New breaches trigger fresh enforcement; ordinary injuries remain until healed by natural or magical means.
6. **Master's death:** The original agreement's applicable succession terms control. Absent applicable succession, the deceased master's personal command authority ends, although otherwise independent obligations can survive.
7. **Fraud/loopholes:** Forged paperwork cannot rewrite a Crimson Ledger's operative record. Authentic defects and loopholes within original covenants and their fixed-at-formation legal citations are exploitable.
8. **Permanent release:** A master may waive, suspend, exempt or end duties only where the original covenant gives them that power. Court or political authority alone cannot unilaterally override or rewrite a Ledger covenant.

**Sella-specific application (working story premise, NOT played canon):** A potential mass release requires proof that multiple covenants share a genuine original legal dependency; an isolated loophole does not destroy unrelated bonds. The Crimson Drayke can interpret real loopholes, not manufacture them. Sella's precise scheme and outcome remain undecided.

**Remaining open work:** precise numerical Bloodbond enforcement/ranks and global skill revision, not the eight approved principles above.
