# Peri — Tidekin Salvage Craftsman (Campaign Canon)

**Authority:** DM-approved character direction, November 7, 2026. Character background is established; the vault rescue and any future player choices are **not** automatically confirmed events.

## Identity and role

- **Name:** Peri (also previously spelled Perri). Canonical spelling: **Peri**.
- **Race:** Tidekin otterfolk.
- **Gender:** Male.
- **Profession:** Salvage mechanic, Wakecraft craftsman, and aspiring treasure hunter.
- **Level:** **Undetermined.** Neither Peri's nor Neri “Wrong-Way” Reedwake's effective level is established. Do not assign a number without a later DM decision. Peri is less *skilled and practiced* in magic than Neri, but may possess impressive innate power; do not convert this into a numerical level.
- **Core temperament:** Curious, inventive, eager to impress, resourceful, and catastrophically determined to be useful. Loves old mechanisms and treasures, especially ancient kobold engineering. His curiosity is genuine, though finding a valuable discovery would help him prove his worth.

## Past and central insecurity

Peri was enslaved in the Scarlands and became familiar with machinery while repairing it for his captors. A kobold worker recognized his potential, mentored him in mechanical work, and helped him escape, **but was left behind**. Their fate and present circumstances are not yet established.

Peri fears being returned to slavery and may believe he would be killed if recaptured. His deeper insecurity is that he must continually prove his usefulness to deserve protection and belonging. This conviction drives unwise risks. These motives should have emotional weight rather than merely making him foolish.

## Neri and her crew

Neri has **already welcomed Peri aboard her crew**. He still feels that he needs to earn his place, despite her acceptance. Neri values him and is prone to affectionate exasperation at his risk-taking. She is more confident in her freedom and better practiced in Wakecraft. Their relationship is a key anchor for his characterization; no additional past quarrel or permanent rupture is established.

Peri's modest *magical skill profile* relative to Neri is established; their respective effective levels remain deliberately open for dramatic and mechanical decisions.

## Wakecraft and danger

- **Specialty:** Applications of **Wakecraft to craftsmanship**, repair, salvage, and interacting with mechanisms. Do not invent specific named Wakecraft abilities or numerical effects without reference to established rules or DM approval.
- **Combat:** Peri is firmly unconfident in direct battle despite impressive magical power. Under pressure he may **rush in without a plan and then freeze or choke at the decisive moment**. This contrast is a persistent character trait, not merely an effect of the vault.
- **Rescue:** He is physically easier to save than to convince that rescue is enough. Returning empty-handed feels like proof of failure. He may keep trying to recover a device or finish an investigation even when leaving is safer.

## Vault-scene intent (not a confirmed event)

The planned vault encounter is primarily **a charming rescue and character introduction**, not an obligatory mechanical side quest. Peri entered the old workshop/vault seeking a potentially valuable mechanical discovery, partly to prove himself to Neri and his crew. Whether he actually finds or secures anything, how Lillia helps, and whether he leaves promptly are **unplayed** and remain subject to Slate's choices.

Peri's immediate reluctance to abandon the vault can resolve in the scene; his long-term need to prove himself does not disappear after one rescue. Avoid assigning a mandatory new quest or forcing Lillia's emotional interpretation. The mentor's fate remains a potential later hook rather than an immediate required objective.

## Portrayal note

Keep the initial NPC compact, scene-focused, and capable of growing if Slate takes a liking to him. He is an enthusiastic engineer with a serious insecurity, not simply comic relief. Separate established character history from what Lillia knows on-screen.
