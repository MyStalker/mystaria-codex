## Lillia (Lilly)
- **Player:** Slate (sole player).
- **Race/class/level:** Level-3 Moontouched Warlock Hero, patron Surrender.
- **Character motivation:** Enjoys experiencing pain and physical abuse; seeks a master who genuinely cares for her.
- **Current condition:** Cold and inadequately clothed; she used Neri's blanket to warm injured Valerious. Exact current HP and MP are not recorded here.

## Valerious
- **Identity:** Level-2 gilded nightfox Starspawn, at least approximately 300 years old, intelligent and strongly animal-influenced.
- **Relationship:** Lillia defeated and befriended him. He privately regards her as his alpha and prospective mate under nightfox courtship custom; **Lillia has not agreed to a relationship**.
- **History and personality:** Former familiar abandoned by a witch; vain, loyal, expressive and wary of mortals. He communicates through pantomime and gestures, not confirmed speech. Some earlier cave encounters involved seducing other foxes.
- **Current condition:** Injured and wrapped in the blanket Neri offered.
- **Extended canon:** [Dusk_and_Valerious_Character_Canon.md](Dusk_and_Valerious_Character_Canon.md).

## Dusk
- **Identity:** Vren outcast and **playwright** who frequently speaks in verse. Earlier shorthand calling him merely a former poet is incomplete.
- **Role:** Enslaved Lillia when she awoke; an aspiring conqueror and spectacularly theatrical would-be villain, outcast because he embraced art in a society that prizes conquest and profit.
- **Lillia dynamic:** Treats her as a prized nemesis and imagines her as his 'sweet little slave knight'; privately caring under layers of theatrical excuses. His wishes do not decide hers.
- **Extended canon:** [Dusk_and_Valerious_Character_Canon.md](Dusk_and_Valerious_Character_Canon.md).

## Surrender
- Lillia's goddess and patron; sold Lillia the treasure map. Other scene-specific details remain unresolved.

## Neri “Wrong-Way” Reedwake
- **Identity:** Female Tidekin otterfolk smuggler captain, about four feet tall; formerly called Nerissia/Narissa in superseded notes.
- **Traits:** Quick-witted, flirtatious, frequently calls Lillia “Babe,” and occasionally uses surfer slang. Her Wakecraft skill and effective level are not numerically fixed.
- **Current confirmed interaction:** Encountered Lillia and offered a blanket, which Lillia gave to Valerious; Lillia then asked if Neri needed help. No clothing handoff is confirmed.
- **Extended canon:** [Live_Chronicle.md](Live_Chronicle.md).

## Peri
- **Identity:** Male Tidekin otterfolk salvage mechanic and Wakecraft craftsman, warmly accepted onto Neri's crew but anxious to prove his usefulness. Effective level not assigned.
- **Motivation:** Escaped slavery with help from a kobold mentor left behind; feels he must earn safety and belonging.
- **DM-only future scene:** In a planned vault rescue he has a portable **unfinished Artificial Legend Matrix**, a failed prototype intended to imitate Shimmer Guard. He may find crucial inspiration by witnessing a real Guard manifest. His survival, rescue and all player responses remain **unplayed**.
- **Extended canon:** [Peri_Character_Canon.md](Peri_Character_Canon.md).
