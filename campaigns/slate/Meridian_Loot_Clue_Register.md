## Water vial design safeguards

1. A vial can preserve water's **exposure history** and is an excellent Mirrorwake archaeology clue. The DM should specify what water actually experienced, how the vial was sealed, and why it survived.
2. The water does **not** know what occurred behind walls, does not spontaneously generate historical truth, and is not omniscient. High-level Mirrorwake may be needed to recover old impressions; lower-level users might discover only a vague trace.
3. Strong Cipherlight exposure can obscure water memories and sophisticated manipulation may produce convincing false impressions. Treat this as a discoverable complication, not a guaranteed fakeout.
4. The mere presence of a sealed vial is a mystery; its contents should ideally have value independent of dumping out every secret at once.
5. **Do not consume or destroy the clue automatically** when reading it, unless later specifically approved.

## Suggested find order, never mandatory

- **Immediate vault loot:** damaged research notebook and/or vial, with enough legible information to make experimentation inviting but not enough to explain all needles.
- **Blackweir investigation:** a translation key, second set of restoration materials, Serpentari network marker, and confiscation register linking the Meridian to Tala.
- **Later maritime research:** song, ship log, suppressed testimony or eyewitness historical records exposing the half-truths and fabrications.
- **After Sella is found, if the player has not already deduced it:** the love-needle revelation can pay off retrospectively; do not retcon its prior behavior or suppress a successful earlier deduction.

## Must preserve

- All six Wakecraft disciplines: Wakeforce, Longwake, Deepdraw, Mirrorwake, Reefcalling, Saltwake.
- Tala Sixwake is the **DM-approved** Tidekin former wielder, living late in the God War, a master of all six, remembered to the present. Her specific loved person's identity and her final fate remain unresolved.
- Large and connected water sources extend Meridian navigation detail, but Neri and Lillia only initially know that wetting the compass makes it work. Neri recognizes what the **largest needle** points to.
- Plans, rumors and find suggestions must not be reported as **played discoveries** until Slate actually encounters them.
