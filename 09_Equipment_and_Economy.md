**Authority:** Existing equipment baseline is CANON; the later modular-equipment expansion is **PROPOSAL, NOT CANON**. **Assembled:** 2026-11-03.

| IV | **70 Gems** | +14 | +28 | 6 | Major, 1/day |
| V | **90 Gems** | +18 | +36 | 8 | Exceptional, 1/day |

These are the established benchmark values used in the Centaur and Sorcery design conversations. A conventional stat enhancement applies after racial multipliers. Gem price is a divine/equipment economic unit, not mana. Soul Strain normally reduces a magic user's usable reserves; different sources of strain may stack. Ordinary material weight/durability and added magic must be accounted for separately.

### Centaur equipment affinity — CANON

Centaurs take **half equipment-derived Soul Strain** and gain **half equipment-derived numerical stat bonuses**, each rounded upward **once at the item total**. Other physical properties, armor functionality, weapon properties, and activated powers remain unhalved. This reduction does not apply to other Soul Strain such as dragonmarks or Celestite soul destruction. See `07_Centaurs_and_Smithcraft.md`.

## November 5, 2026 — Modular smithcraft, shop access, and prayer-charged reservoirs (LATEST)

### Approved design rules and supersession

**Fully Modular Architecture B approved.** Equipment is modular in form, material, quality, magical installation, and combat application. The quality I–V craftsmanship ladder is retained. **Moonlit Forge +1 through +5 equipment reinforcement and its independent Gem-price ladder are RETIRED / SUPERSEDED for current design**; do not offer a second divine enhancement track. **Historical Moonlit Forge prices and Moonlit Garden equipment prices are not controlling for current new designs**, though the older catalogue remains historical lore and the agreed five-quality benchmarks may inform economics. Shimmer shop goods are fairly priced and may be higher-level items purchasable in advance, but usable physical equipment is constrained by actual STR/DEX or handling requirements, and the best combinations are expected to need direct Centaur craftsmanship, multiple disciplines/master smiths, and substantial materials. The five-quality stat-bonus/Soul-Strain benchmark table remains in effect **pending detailed reallocation**; avoid treating suggested rune bonuses as additional unrestricted budgets.

**Physical item strength** is balanced by form/coverage, appropriate weapon stat requirements, weight, SPD penalties and strength/handling (not mainly Gem cost). **Magical enhancements** are balanced chiefly by **cumulative equipment Soul Strain** and item limits rather than cost. Multiple items cannot freely multiply the same named magic technique: **multiple implements cannot enhance the same magic simultaneously**. Heavy armor cannot ordinarily be layered as multiple independent suits; **light layers beneath outer armor can cover exposed gaps** (e.g. chain links at plate joints) without double-counting identical coverage. Exact item-slot counts, local protection stacking and strain caps remain under design.

Weapons may scale with **STR, DEX, either STR or DEX, or a deliberately rewarding combination of STR and DEX**, with corresponding physical requirements. Simple/less powerful weapons such as a dagger or longsword may use either; unarmed attacks may use either. Balanced-stat weapon designs should earn significantly more generous scaling in return for qualifying in BOTH attributes and sacrificing single-stat specialization. Existing base multipliers (unarmed 0.85, dagger 1.0, gauntlets 1.0) remain until explicitly replaced; long/greatsword proposed baselines and STR 400 progression have no finalized universal model. This direction does not grant additional attacks or free Skill Points.

### Prayer-charged divine reservoirs (CANON principle; numerical completion unresolved)

Former `Manacistern` is a **working name**, subject to replacement. These reservoirs are **divine technology**, charged by a creature with **compatible mana affinity** through actual **prayer/devotion**, not by freely transferring ordinary personal MP and not by passively regenerating with rest. Prayer may be sincerely directed toward **any person or object of devotion**, not necessarily one of Mystaria's three goddesses. This draws upon a **basic form of the devotional/divine-energy principle** associated with the Shimmer and Legends, but does not grant Hero powers or independent mana generation without the special apparatus. Religious convention often **incorrectly insists the prayer must address recognized deities**; smiths often know otherwise but are constrained from explaining this to their masters by entrenched social and religious norms.

**Recharge design target:** approximately **1 MP per prayer turn for each 10 effective END** of the praying compatible-affinity contributor at full sincerity; an **unenthusiastic prayer loses 90% efficiency** (produces only 10% of the devotional rate), while wholehearted devotion to another is fully efficient. Exact rounding, partial progress, turn time, maximum concurrent contributors, capacity by item quality, prayer quality adjudication, strain, and exploitation constraints are **UNRESOLVED**. The *recharge rate* should not be mistaken for the reservoir's *storage capacity*. Praying consumes real participant effort and action time; it is not an unpriced, instant combat refill. Historical one-color capacity and discharge tables in the earlier proposal have not been approved.

### Still-open modular and rune specifics (PROPOSAL)

Candidate names for reservoirs, proposed passive and active rune effects, Longsong doubling behavior and Thrift's pricing model, model-specific item slot policy, armor rating/strength movement penalties, equipment-quality requirement reduction, and dual-attribute scaling are **not canon** until approved. In particular, a Quality V sword's historical **pure +36** benchmark is **not automatically cumulative** with five separate +18 stat runes; use one shared enhancement budget pending final formula. ## November 5, 2026 — Approved modular runes, Leywell, slots and requirement rules (LATEST)

### Rune and reservoir approvals

- **Longsong** is approved as **+100% of base duration per rune rank**, making rank I–V duration **2× / 3× / 4× / 5× / 6×**. This is *additive relative to base duration*, NOT exponential 2ⁿ duration. Interaction with pay-per-round upkeep, permanent effects, duration maxima and multiple Longsong effects is **UNRESOLVED**; do not grant free upkeep.
- **Thrift** contains BOTH flat `−rank MP` and percentage `−(rank × 5)% MP` options at rank I–V. For an eligible casting, apply **only the larger discount**; they **do not stack** with each other. Thrift should be intentionally especially useful for **repeated low-cost spells**, a fundamental balancing assumption for spellcasting economics. The minimum positive MP fee is at least 1 MP. Exact rounding of percentage savings, source interaction, spell upkeep eligibility and stacking multiple enchanted items remain to be finalized. Earlier exclusive-percentage preference is superseded.
- **LEYWELL** is the approved name for the previously termed Manacistern / Heartwell: a prayer-charged divine mana reservoir, informally **well** where clear from context. Its established devotional principle, compatible-affinity contributions, prayer directed to anyone, approximately 1 MP per 10 effective END per prayer turn at full sincerity, and one-tenth efficiency for reluctant prayer remain. Actual capacity, discharge rates, Gem price, contributions, action timing and rounding are unresolved. Old standalone Manacistern capacities/prices are unapproved examples, not the new official Leywell values.
- **Active crystal names remain**, but their activation identity is now **preprogrammed existing Sorcery Incantations**, optionally with known/valid alterations at higher quality, rather than inventing many new effects. A crafter may imbue a valid elemental manifestation at fabrication: **fire, ice, water, lightning, air, toxic (hex)**. This follows the concept of Drakyn elemental Sorcery integration without automatically granting it unrestrictedly or altering the underlying Incantation's prerequisites. Detailed crafting knowledge/qualification, gem quality-versus-alteration slots, per-use MP, mana source, and output Power still need numerical review.
- **Passive elemental attunement rune effects** are distinct from active spell gems, use **no deliberate activation**, and are intended to be weaker than equivalent Sorcery activation. They may contribute a **small separate elemental effect** even when physical armor makes the primary hit superficial. At rank V the weapon may become **entirely its chosen element**, replacing (not stacking with) the original material's special benefit. Fire burns, ice can freeze limbs/make applicable armor brittle, shock benefits from conductive metal and is weak against nonconductive glass-based armor, and toxic/hex contributes contamination whose detailed combat side effects remain **UNRESOLVED**. Specific magic/elemental resistances must be particularly effective against passive elemental damage. No rank-based elemental Power or exact auxiliary effect is established.

### Physical scaling and item slots

- **Weapon source attribute:** strength-only, dexterity-only, either/higher of STR or DEX for suitable simpler weapons (including daggers, longswords, unarmed), or both for advanced balanced weapons. The **balanced-stat pitch** `Power = ((effective STR + effective DEX) × balanced weapon form/material multiplier)^2` is approved as the proposed standard **subject to minimum prerequisites for BOTH stats**; exact multipliers, individual weapon profiles, and comparison against 400-STR single-stat specialists remain to be calibrated. Single-stat and flexible forms use `Power = (chosen effective attribute × applicable multiplier)^2`. Existing approved unarmed 0.85×, dagger 1.0×, gauntlets 1.0× remain baseline until individual forms are reviewed. Momentum is applied separately to Power where previously permitted.
- **Quality I–V decreases each applicable physical STR and/or DEX weapon requirement**, first via **percentage reduction** of **0% / 5% / 10% / 15% / 20%** by quality, **then a further flat −5 to each required attribute**. Do not subtract 5 once from combined STR+DEX, and do not apply the −5 before the percentage. Required stat has a hard minimum of **1**. Rounding for fractional requirements is pending, and quality never directly grants unlimited attack multipliers. Physical armor follows the same design philosophy where relevant.
- The **proposed** armor-speed test values (light 5%, chain 15%, plate 30% base SPD burden) and **STR relief** `burden × max(0, 2 − effective STR / adjusted armor STR requirement)` are retained **for playtesting**, not finalized armor tables. Armor SPD penalties from physically worn **applicable layers stack when not independently eliminated by sufficient STR**; separate layers may protect gaps, not double-count the same protected surface or bypass the single-outer-suit restriction. Effective SPD cannot fall below 1.
- **Item slots approved:** one primary head item, one neck, one outer suit, one compatible light protective underlayer, one bracer pair/arms, one pair of gloves, one belt/waist, one boot pair/feet and **up to three actively enchanted rings**. Mundane adornments are not arbitrarily forbidden. Held implements are limited by actual ability to use/carry them; **multiple implements cannot multiply effects on the same named magic simultaneously**. Cumulative magical **Soul Strain** is the primary limitation, with individual rune and item quality budgets still being reviewed.

### Production and economics

**Mastery Intersection adopted:** Shimmer shops may fairly sell usable or future-attribute-gated, fairly priced high-quality standardized equipment, but the **best truly bespoke equipment** requires specialized Centaur smithwork crossing disciplines, through either one unusually broadly mastered crafter or **several master smiths collaborating**, together with suitable materials and extensive Gem expenditure. Gem cost controls economy and materials, not the mechanical strength of worn magical effects; Soul Strain and circuitry/capacity constrain those. Specific prerequisites per fabrication step, division of labor, pricing and maximum item enchantment budget remain open. Moonlit Forge independent +1–+5 progression stays superseded.
