# Mystaria — Equipment, Gems, Soul Strain, and the Enchantment Economy

**Authority:** Existing equipment baseline is CANON; the later modular-equipment expansion is **PROPOSAL, NOT CANON**. **Assembled:** 2026-11-03.

## Current five-quality equipment baseline

| Quality / tier | Standard value | Mixed stat bonus | Pure stat bonus | Ordinary Soul Strain | Legacy active use allotment |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| I | **14 Gems** | +3 | +6 | 1 | Minor, 5/day |
| II | **30 Gems** | +6 | +12 | 2 | Moderate, 3/day |
| III | **50 Gems** | +10 | +20 | 4 | Powerful, 2/day |
| IV | **70 Gems** | +14 | +28 | 6 | Major, 1/day |
| V | **90 Gems** | +18 | +36 | 8 | Exceptional, 1/day |

These are the established benchmark values used in the Centaur and Sorcery design conversations. A conventional stat enhancement applies after racial multipliers. Gem price is a divine/equipment economic unit, not mana. Soul Strain normally reduces a magic user's usable reserves; different sources of strain may stack. Ordinary material weight/durability and added magic must be accounted for separately.

### Centaur equipment affinity — CANON

Centaurs take **half equipment-derived Soul Strain** and gain **half equipment-derived numerical stat bonuses**, each rounded upward **once at the item total**. Other physical properties, armor functionality, weapon properties, and activated powers remain unhalved. This reduction does not apply to other Soul Strain such as dragonmarks or Celestite soul destruction. See `07_Centaurs_and_Smithcraft.md`.

## Modular enchantment overhaul — PROPOSED ONLY

> **Do not treat the following numbers as finalized.** User preferences are decided but the assistant's concrete slot tables, gem activation rules and eight-skill restructuring were a proposed revision awaiting approval. The earlier six Anvilcant skill names remain the last formally approved Smithcraft tree, with Formwright and Monumentcraft specifically opened for replacement.

**User-approved design preferences (not approval of the full resulting formulas):** keep five original quality and Gem benchmarks; quality increases rune and gem slots; additive/hybrid upgrade strain; material effects distinct from passive runes and activated socketed gems; one mana color per normal item's magical circuitry; allow advanced Smithcraft to splice multiple colors; specialty casting implements can both enhance known skills and emulate limited access; conservative external mana storage with real recharging requirements; mundane high-quality equipment can carry **zero Soul Strain**.

**Proposed slots (not approved):**

| Quality | Rune slots | Gem sockets | Standard Gem benchmark | Standard enchant strain |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| I | 1 | 1 | 14 | 1 |
| II | 2 | 1 | 30 | 2 |
| III | 3 | 2 | 50 | 4 |
| IV | 4 | 2 | 70 | 6 |
| V | 5 | 3 | 90 | 8 |

**Proposed passive rune families:** Might/Grace/Vigor/Insight/Swiftness = stat +3/+6/+10/+14/+18 over I–V; Farreach = +10/20/30/40/50% range; Thrift = 5/10/15/20/25% mana discount; Longsong = 10/20/30/40/50% duration; Conduit = +1/+1/+2/+2/+3 channel capacity. Specialist Mastery, True Aim and Steadfastness runes exist only as proposals. Explicit restrictions against stacking duplicate rune families, minting free SP, and unrestricted spells apply in the proposed form.

**Proposed active socketed gem families:** Emberheart (red fire), Dawnshield (white protection), Lifebloom (green healing), Tideglass (blue water), Dreadstone (black fear), Stormpearl (red electrical), Rootheart (green binding), Gloamglass (black darkness). Rank I–V activation cost **1, 2, 4, 7, 11 MP**; pay from compatible source or reservoir. Names/rank effect details are NOT approved yet.

**Proposed Manacistern storage:**

| Quality | Capacity (MP) | Extra Soul Strain | Gem surcharge | Max discharge/action |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| I | 4 | 1 | 6 | 2 |
| II | 8 | 2 | 10 | 4 |
| III | 16 | 4 | 18 | 8 |
| IV | 28 | 7 | 30 | 12 |
| V | 40 | 10 | 44 | 18 |

The proposed reservoir consumes a rune slot, stores one color and costs about **1 strain per 4 MP capacity**, not per charge held. It needs a real donor/source, does not self-recharge on rest, cannot fuel unrestricted general-purpose spellcasting and is discharge-limited. Proposed extra rune and gem costs are not canon yet.

**Casting implements (proposed):** staffs, wands, rings and other gear attuned to one named Incantation, technique or Craft. Both rank enhancement and constrained temporary emulation are proposed; no permanent SP award. Cost-discount floor remains a proposal and requires review (percentage cap and INT-related floor cannot make ordinary 18-MP spells effectively free). Do not treat the earlier draft formula as approved.

**Clockwork expansion (proposed):** Socketwright replaces Formwright; Clockwright, Cogmarshal and Hostforge jointly replace Monumentcraft. The proposal increases Smithcraft to **eight disciplines / 40 ranks / 200 SP**, **not canon**. Its constructed machines, Command Points and historical Cogminds are also not yet explicitly approved.

## 31. Surrender's Gem Shop — The Moonlit Garden

**Patron:** Surrender. **Currency:** Gems. **Early-level focus:** Levels 1–10, with extensive items costing three Gems or fewer and selected upgrades at a maximum of twenty-five Gems. **Theme:** Protection, healing, companionship, everyday usefulness, and conventional equipment trusted to responsible Heroes.

Surrender originally preferred to sell only healing charms, friendship accessories, robes, and defensive blessings. After Heroes complained about facing enormous hostile Starspawn unarmed, and Arrest pointed out that protective authority includes equipping others to defend themselves, Surrender added ordinary weapons, armor, and even lethal equipment. She prefers diplomacy and compassion but does not prohibit her Heroes from defending themselves. The list below preserves applicable stock while applying the later Shimmer rules: free map, clock, compass, and shrine fast travel are not sold as baseline unlocks.

### The Moonlit Forge — Reinforcement Tiers

The forge can bless ordinary acquired equipment as well as Gem Shop purchases. Every tier is purchased consecutively; costs are **per step**, not the total cost from an unupgraded object. Reinforcement affects equipment quality rather than the Hero's own Strength, Dexterity, or other Shimmer attributes.

| Reinforcement | Gems | Effect | Requirement |
|---|---:|---|---|
| +1 | 1 | Minor weapon damage or armor protection | Level 1 |
| +2 | 2 | Further reinforcement and durability | Level 1 |
| +3 | 3 | Additional performance, degradation resistance | Level 3 |
| +4 | 6 | Stronger reinforcement, possible secondary property | Level 7 |
| +5 | 10 | Greatest early-level reinforcement | Level 10 |

# PART VIII — THE VELVET HERESIES

## Integration cautions

- A plain quality-V Adamantite sword can be high-grade and very expensive **without Soul Strain** if no enchantments are installed; this principle is a confirmed design preference but modular prices remain proposed.
- Attribute points, skill points, Gems, Soul Strain and MP are five distinct resources; never substitute one as a unit for another.
- Magical gifts through Drakyn dragonmarks have a separate Soul Strain-to-SP buyoff mechanic; see `06_Drakyn.md`.
- Celestite has **no ordinary Gem tier**; strokes damage the soul/XP and may cost levels; see `07_Centaurs_and_Smithcraft.md`.

## Approved Nymbrin materials and consumable design
The [Nymbrin dossier](20_Nymbrin_Wetcraft_and_Hexen_Canon.md) controls **Pazara** living construction, hardened Pazara **chitin**, **clearbreaths**, five intoxicants, and **Rapture Salt** as Vren standard alchemical ordnance. All alchemy generates hex.
Nymbrin consumable power is tied to Gem costs and calibrated against **30% of a Hero's typical Gem budget** allocated to consumables. This is a design benchmark, not a compulsory purchase or free grant. Exact prices/yields and the budget's progression window remain unresolved.
