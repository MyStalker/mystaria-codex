## I. What the Mnemonic Arts Are

The **Mnemonic Arts** are an advanced and costly specialization of Kobold **Orbcraft and Mirrorcraft**. They use **Mindprism**, carefully focused encoded **Cipherlight**, to deliver information **through a person's retinas and into the brain**. Unlike ordinary Coilnet beams, which are dangerous to stare at and can cause extreme confusion, deliberately calibrated Mindprism equipment turns an otherwise overwhelming signal into an organized memory or experience. The equipment is tailored to its user; ordinary exposure to a Coilnet beam is not a substitute for a safe Mindprism session.

The technique can record and transmit memories, feelings, perception, learned knowledge and, at its most advanced, authentic skills. The brain remains a biological bottleneck. High sophistication, abundant mana, and specialist labor do not eliminate neurological strain. The exact optical coding, calibration time, safe viewing geometry, equipment requirements, and exact mana costs are **not quantified**.

## II. Recording, Cutting, and Receiving

### Echoing: copy without removing the original

- **Requires user-specific Mindprism calibration** and the use of carefully encoded Cipherlight.
- **Consumes extravagant quantities of actual mana.** Copying is therefore costly, typically impractical for routine recordkeeping, and generally reserved for important or wealthy clients. It is not a cheap duplication or mass-copy operation.
- **Causes immense neurological strain, not curable by magical healing.** Recovery requires time. Even a modest memory may be an ordeal; extensive copying can take **days, months, or years**, and a full **Deepprint** may take years. The time depends on how much of a mind is copied, its complexity, and the subject's tolerance.
- Copies retain the connection to their **original source person**. Copying does not transfer the source person's identity or physical body.

### Mindcut: destructive cut-and-transfer

A Mindcut can extract a large quantity of information **much faster than Echoing** but **erases the original memories from the target's own mind**. It can be used intentionally to produce amnesia. It risks high or extreme brain injury and loss of personal continuity; the most severe damage may be permanent. **Magic cannot repair Mnemonic neurological strain.** Normal recovery takes time; some injuries do not fully recover. An Echo returning later can return its informational content but **does not repair underlying brain injury**.

A faster Mindcut is not intrinsically safer or free of power demands. Exact MP use and procedural time are unestablished.

### Receiving a stored Echo

A calibrated Mindprism session can upload information into another person's mind. A finished recording can be delivered faster than the original lived experience, but the recipient suffers neurological strain and may require time to integrate it. Memory receipt does **not** guarantee reliable understanding, physical compatibility, mental stability, or automatically unlimited mastery. Receiving, like recording, is specialized equipment work rather than ordinary Coilnet access.

## III. The Law of Returning Echoes

1. **A recorded memory cannot be destroyed by mortal technology.** An Echo may be contained, transferred, hidden, removed from a mind, or lose its physical storage medium, but its memory-content is not simply annihilated by breaking an Orb. This law does not give Echo Orbs physical indestructibility.
2. **All copies remain bound to their original source person.** As long as the original owner is alive, an unsealed stored Echo tends to **return to that owner**. An active **Everseal** prevents this return by consuming a small but real ongoing supply of mana and requiring regular maintenance. No exact rate or inspection interval is approved.
3. **Distributed copies are not an escape:** if every remaining copy of an Echo is inside living creatures, and those holders die or lose the copied memory, the Echo returns to its **living original owner**. Its route of return and timing have no quantified mechanics.
4. **Death of the original owner ends the automatic return mechanism.** Echoes still preserved elsewhere can remain available, but no automatic return to the deceased original owner or to their soul is established. No universal afterlife-recovery or rebirth rule is implied.
5. **Retrieval is not healing.** Return of memory content does not restore neurons or undo damage from Mindcuts. No mortal Cipherlight procedure overrides the established separate mechanics of divine memory interference; divine acts are outside this law's scope.

**Caution on unspecified cases:** Whether a particular foreign recipient can resist a returning Echo, the exact return time, what occurs with a memory after all hosts and storage hardware disappear when the original owner is dead, and whether preservation technology can recover every damaged representation are **UNRESOLVED**. Do not infer a new universal soul-storage or resurrection mechanic.

## IV. Skillprints and the Shimmer

**Skillprints can convey genuine skills.** The latest user approval specifically makes transferred **Shimmer skills count as purchased even though the recipient spends no SP**. This can satisfy a requirement that a prior skill rank has been purchased, once the transplanted skill is successfully integrated. The underlying ability still must be possible for the recipient: **racial compatibility, prerequisite chains and Hero level gates** remain applicable unless a separate canon effect overrides them. Skillprints do not automatically confer a Hero Shimmer on an ordinary resident, give a person another race's anatomy, or provide an unrelated power source or mana affinity. They do not supply free attribute points, XP, or a personal mana pool.

**Approximately 5 SP of assimilated skills per month** is the approved **modest safety benchmark**. Attempting to integrate more risks neurological injury; this is not a universal hard maximum or a guarantee that every 5-SP upload will be harmless. Neurological adaptation, the individual's limits, compatibility and rest remain important. The transferred skills have **effective purchase credit** for the receiving Hero's progression without charging their actual earned SP balance. The source person's purchased skills are not inherently erased merely because an Echoing process created a Skillprint; using **Mindcut**, however, erases the memories removed from that source.

**Rarity and rewards:** Skillprints, particularly those conveying high-level or magical mastery, are **exceptionally expensive and rare treasures**, primarily appropriate as **medium- to high-level Hero adventure rewards**. Adventurers ordinarily build their craft over a lifetime; a rare Skillprint is a meaningful exception, not a commonplace retail shortcut. A skill's normal **rank cost** governs how much of the month's approximate 5-SP assimilation budget it occupies even though no SP are spent. A skill incompatible with a recipient's level or prior ranks cannot be used simply by owning the Orb. Rules for a partially installed or deferred skill and detailed upload time are **UNRESOLVED**.

## V. Expenses and Medical Risks

- **Basic Mnemonic recording** is expensive at approximately the **expense of purchasing a firearm**; this is a relative benchmark, **not** an approved universal Gem price. Individual circumstances and the type of firearm matter.
- **Copying an Echo requires extravagant mana** on top of scarce equipment, skilled Mindwright labor and calibration; a full Deepprint is prohibitively expensive for most people.
- **Skillprints** and sophisticated transfers stand among the most expensive civilian applications; **Mindcut** is faster but imposes greater neurological danger.
- **Everseals** require a comparatively small ongoing mana supply and constant maintenance, making long-term archives expensive to operate at scale even when each seal's individual consumption is modest.
- **Ordinary recording strain** recovers only with time, not magic. Severe invasive procedures can leave lasting or permanent neurological damage. The user's latest ruling is not a universal promise of full recovery.
- **Dreamcasting access** may be sold at a shared venue for less than individual memory recordings, but Mindprism hardware and professional operation are still costly. Exact admission prices and network bandwidth are unapproved.

No specific MP price, timescale per memory, damage score or resistance roll has been established; do not extrapolate from ordinary spell costs.

## VI. Dreamcasting and the Entertainment Economy

**Dreamcasting** uses calibrated Mindprism apparatus to share another person's current experience **in real time** rather than instantly depositing a finished Echo. The viewing audience may share perception, emotion, or a participant's perspective; the rate of experience is the rate of the actual event. This contrasts with the faster transfer of a previously completed recording.

**Dreamhouses** offer paid sessions. Professional **Dreamcasters** may broadcast sports, performances, journeys, difficult explorations, personal experiences and private entertainments through Coilnet-connected equipment. A championship Coilrun might be watched through a participant's senses, but such a broadcast does not grant their purchased abilities. Audiences require safe interfaces, and extended exposure can impose strain. The industry includes licensed productions, patronage, theatrical and sporting distribution, elite collections, exploitative recordings, illegal surveillance and disputes about consent.

Existing **Cipherlight Rainbows** remain physically observable indications of Coilnet activity, with heavy network traffic potentially intensifying them. This does not imply instant globe-wide availability, unlimited viewers, or that direct exposure to ordinary Cipherlight is safe.

## VII. Echo Vaults, Memory Bonds, and Control

Secure **Echo Vaults** store sealed Echo Orbs containing treasured personal experiences, family histories, contracts, technical knowledge, espionage intelligence, and memories taken from unwilling captives. A vault must maintain Everseals; failed maintenance can cause stored memories to return to surviving original owners.

**Memory Bonds** enable a debtor's memory to be pledged as collateral. Noble Coils or lenders may hold an Echo or extracted memory until repayment; cruel lenders can exploit forfeiture, resale or continued containment. Exact legal enforceability, licensing, ownership of derivative copies and contractual norms are not numerically or universally defined. The scale and ultimate irreversibility of the harm depend on whether memories were merely copied or **Mindcut** from the debtor.

The Mnemonic Arts intensify the Empire's class divisions and political leverage. They are also useful to archival historians, independent custodians, criminal investigators, activists protecting endangered testimony, and persons seeking to preserve memories voluntarily. Such uses are possible, not proof that all uses are sanctioned by Imperial law.

## VIII. Living Reliquaries and Hollow Reliquaries

A **Living Reliquary** is an **enslaved person deliberately used as a living repository of Echoes** from other people. They are individually calibrated, often trained and conditioned for years, and may possess historical accounts, court secrets, institutional knowledge, technical information, or Skillprints. Unlike a passive Echo Orb, they can **answer questions, compare memories, and contextualize records** through their own intelligence. They remain persons, not equipment. Their possession of another person's memory does not make them its original owner.

Reliquaries are expensive, strategically prized, frequently guarded, and neurologically vulnerable. Their archive is limited by genuine biological capacity and integration strain. They may need long recovery intervals, and their knowledge is not proof of consent or legal freedom. Owners may keep **Eversealed Echo Orb backups** to avoid losing a rare collection on a Reliquary's death.

A **Hollow Reliquary** is the particularly abused subtype whose **own autobiographical memories have been substantially removed by repeated Mindcuts**, allowing imposed organization of foreign memories at great personal cost. This does **not** create infinite space or free neurological capacity. They may retain language and practical skills while losing much of their personal history. The neurological harms may be permanent; nothing in this classification renders them nonsapient or erases their legal and moral personhood.

A Reliquary's death does not destroy an Echo. Return to the living original owner remains subject to the Law of Returning Echoes. When the original owner has died, automatic return ends even if the Reliquary later dies; the ultimate disposition of an Echo with no remaining medium is **unresolved**, not permission to contradict indestructibility.

The terms **Living Reliquary** and **Hollow Reliquary** are specifically approved. They describe a slaveholding practice, not a new playable race, a formal Shimmer class, or new purchased ranks.

## IX. Interactions and Limits

- **Orbcraft / Mirrorcraft:** Mindprism is specialized encoded optical Manatech. It must be designed, powered and maintained; ordinary viewing Mirrors are not automatically memory-transfer tools.
- **Tidekin Mirrorwake:** Cipherlight through water leaves exceptionally powerful water impressions, and sophisticated traffic can overwhelm or counterfeit other impressions. This may offer forensic evidence of Mnemonic transmissions **without automatically decoding memories, reading an uploaded mind, or extracting an Echo**. See `13_Wakecraft_The_Six_Arts_of_the_Living_Tide.md`.
- **Warlock Soulwork:** A Mana Imbuement soul vessel preserves a disembodied **soul**; an Echo Orb stores **recorded memories**. Neither device silently gains the other's function. Separate already approved Warlock activation/upkeep fees remain unchanged.
- **Shimmer:** True purchased-skill credit via sufficiently advanced Skillprints is an explicit **new exception** to normal SP expenditure, not a change to the cost of buying skills conventionally.
- **Divine acts:** Divine memory seals, intervention, and control are separate from the mortal law governing Echo persistence.
- **Mindprism medical limits:** Healing magic cannot erase the particular neurological strain inflicted by Mnemonic recording and uploading. Time is required; severe injuries may remain permanent.
