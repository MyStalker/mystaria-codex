# Mystaria — Magical Philosophy and the Mana Cycle

**Status:** Author-approved foundational lore, November 13, 2026. The subject references own individual techniques, prerequisites and costs. The open design work below has no new mechanical values.

## Finite mana and the one-way cycle

Mana was created at the beginning of the universe. Mystaria loses some mana to space, while the slowly dying sun releases enough of its own supply to keep the planet's balance positive for now. Mana can change color and form through transformation. In matter, each atom has one mana color. Pure substances have one color, while compounds and alloys can combine colors. Oxygen corresponds to red, and iron and adamantite embody dense green.

When magical or natural activity spends mana, it can disperse over a vast area as **Depleted Mana**, too diffuse and disorganized for casting. Environmental recycling concentrates it as **Formless Mana**, which flows in ley lines. The cycle runs forward. Even Nymbrin Wetcraft cannot restore Depleted Mana directly to Pure Mana. Black mana decomposes other colors in most cycles.

- **Biological route.** Plants incorporate mana into **managmins**. All nutritional needs ultimately involve managmins, whose different colors underlie the food groups. Digestion supports the anima's usual production of **Pure Mana**. Eating supplies nutrition over time and gives no immediate MP recovery. Established racial exceptions to innate mana production and recovery still apply.
- **Environmental route.** Ordinary environmental processes can consume Formless Mana directly when suitable conditions exist. A volcano can express red mana as heat and ultimately release it as Depleted Mana. Matter can hold mana for ages. Geological activity can break down rock and release the mana bound in it.
- **Artificial refinement.** Suitable equipment and resources can refine Formless Mana. Refinement uses existing mana and still follows the cycle. Equipment, inputs, outputs and throughput remain unspecified.

A soul keeps usable mana in motion and stabilizes it temporarily. The soul itself undergoes slow, controlled decay, which underlies aging. Internal reserves persist inside an antimagic field, subject to separately established effects that explicitly drain them. The soul is normally bound to the body and is lost as the body decays. Established soul-preservation exceptions apply. Reincarnation occurs, with its conditions unresolved.

## Manifestation and situational denial

Mana takes its expression and shape through a suitable medium. Every spellcaster is vulnerable to conditions that can deny use of their power even while mana is available. A pyromancer channels red mana through an existing flame such as a lantern. Wakecraft requires water. A Cogmarshal needs their cogloom for cogsong.

Spellcasting can shape nearby matter or express a phenomenon through it, such as red mana taking the form of plasma through flame. Ordinary spells create no matter from nothing. Wetcraft transmutation rearranges existing resources.

## Ley lines and ecology

Ley lines distribute Formless Mana. Redirecting or severing a line changes which places receive that flow. Deprived regions can suffer drought, famine, reduced fertility and wider ecological decline. Excessive concentration can also destabilize ecosystems. The network naturally tends to correct diversions when intervention stops. Sustained redirection can be maintained through active work, and sufficiently resourced societies can harness ley lines. Routing a current grants no direct reservoir of Pure Mana.

[Warlock Ley Carving](04_Warlock_Manacraft.md) opens, redirects or severs pathways for existing Formless Mana. The [Drakyn Silent Thunder mark](06_Drakyn.md) accelerates existing Formless Mana through the cycle into its one chosen spell. [Worldspindles](18_The_Gilded_Coil_Imperial_Gazetteer.md) harness and guide ley currents. After a spindle's destruction, diverted currents can return toward their former courses. The ancient [Worldcoil](34_Ancient_Serpentari_Aquatic_Waterways_Canon.md) may also guide ley lines through water. The extent and operation of that secondary function remain open.

## True philosopher's stones

True ancient philosopher's stones enabled **perfect equivalent exchange** and transmutations of complexity beyond what a living practitioner could ordinarily achieve. The finite-mana principle applied. The Nymbrin gathered stone and mud as feedstock, transmuted it into gold, and alloyed it with titanium to build El Dorado's towers. Modern stones and [Wetcraft](20_Nymbrin_Wetcraft_and_Hexen_Canon.md) retain their existing constraints.

## Cipherlight and Psycraft

**Cipherlight** is information-bearing light. Contact with a suitable sensory organ is sufficient for perception without direct visual observation. Suitable magical technology can store or record it. Existing ordinary exposure hazards and the specialized retinal calibration of [Mindprisms](19_The_Mnemonic_Arts_and_Echo_Economy.md) still apply.
