# Nymbrin — Wetcraft, the Golden Lease, and the Hexen

**CANON — all current Nymbrin lore explicitly accepted by the author November 4, 2026.** Latest terminology: **Hexen** collectively names mutants. Earlier provisional mutant names are superseded. Nymkin/Nimbrin in the source conversation are spelling variants; use **Nymbrin**.

## Identity and biology
Nymbrin are predominantly humanoid poison-dart-frog people, close to Tidekin in size, aligned with **blue mana**. Their smooth skin has exotic, vivid colorations, contrasting bands, patches and spots. Strong legs permit jumps much farther than any other mortal race. Their muscular, grasping tongues extend about **ten meters on average**, supporting combat, manipulation, anchoring and mobility. They are amphibious and breathe underwater. Webbed feet aid swimming and wet-ground movement; footwear leaves the webbing free. A second set of transparent eyelids protects their eyes underwater and closes reflexively against splashes.

Their default skin secretion causes **dangerous numbness**, potentially hiding injury and impairing coordination. Venomcraft can modify it for sleep, heightened sensitivity, hallucinations and other poisons, using appropriate preparations rather than unrestricted instant selection. Each individual is immune to their **own exact toxin** and heavily resistant, not universally immune, to others' skin toxins and modified mixtures.

They become uncomfortable after about **one hour** of dryness and dangerously dehydrated after about **eight hours**; heat and exertion accelerate this. Hydration elixirs temporarily extend independence from water. Their cities feature canals, soaking chambers, submerged rooms and humid workshops.

A layer of water beneath the skin, especially around the gills, protects them from Haze. Drying in Haze is excruciating even for acclimatized Nymbrin and can cause scarring and wheezing. Their water must remain free of Haze contamination. Clean-water, temperature, humidity and maintenance provisions are central contractual protections.

Among furred peoples, **Tidekin alone have fur thick and oily enough for harmless ordinary contact with Nymbrin skin**. Duskpaw partners and relatives use managed secretions, clothing and precautions.

## Origins: El Dorado and the Great Taking
Nymbrin once possessed a civilization rivaling the Serpentari, centered on **El Dorado, the City of Gold**: canals, terraced pools, great alchemical works and wealth sufficient to make gold an ordinary architectural material.

They created **true philosopher's stones which violated equivalent exchange**. These supported extraordinary abundance. The Serpentari famously robbed El Dorado in **the Great Taking**, seizing treasures and stockpiles and destroying or dismantling institutions sustaining its prosperity. Collapse followed as workshops and waterways failed and guilds dispersed with incomplete knowledge. True stones and their manufacture have since been lost to time; surviving examples remain an unresolved possibility.

Modern stones are lesser successors: they concentrate, store and mediate alchemical potential while obeying equivalent exchange. Scholars sometimes bitterly call them **accountant's stones**.

**Duskpaws lived in El Dorado alongside Nymbrin before the Night of a Thousand Locks.** Their shared dispossession motivates their alliance and contributes to Duskpaw fascination with theft, crime and reclaiming stolen prosperity. This adds El Dorado to Duskpaw history; their established Serpentari enslavement, Fraycraft blessing and liberation remain canon. Exact sequencing of El Dorado's foundation, the Great Taking and particular God War events is not numerically dated.

El Dorado relied on **homunculi**, alchemically modified Starspawn, for roles most modern societies assign to slaves. This was an extremely reciprocal arrangement: Starspawn gained permanent power, prestige and privileged positions in return for service. Very powerful mutations were once common. Some ancient homunculi survive and have become legendary.

Modern homunculi are still made, but their modifications end on **celestial recall**. Recreating expensive modifications and the rarity of extreme transformations have reduced their popularity. These additions do not change the established divine God War origin of Starspawn; exact El Dorado/Starspawn chronology remains unquantified.

After the God War, Vren brought Nymbrin to their lands and accepted steep contracts and extensive amphibious infrastructure requirements to develop the Scarlands into a full kingdom. Nymbrin are poorly suited to its dry wasteland.

## Personality, faith and money
Nymbrin combine hedonism, fatalism, persistent optimism and unusually strong pragmatism. They seek pleasure, look on the bright side, identify surviving assets after disaster and treat dependence as a business opportunity. Kobolds are intelligent but overeager; centaurs emphasize service, form and function; Nymbrin assess worth, demand and the enjoyment a sale will purchase.

They worship **Baphomet**. Everyday observances include raising a first sip in her name, celebrating successful leases with meals, maintaining workshop shrines adorned with enjoyed luxuries and earmarking part of a payment for deliberate indulgence. An apprentice's first post-lease celebration is religious and social recognition. They can be warm companions while remaining cruelly indifferent to exploitation. Their famously dangerous parties include intoxicants, modified secretions and reckless competitions; conscientious hosts provide warnings and antidotes.

Their attachment to wealth rivals dragons. They keep exact accounts and resist robbery or extortion beyond almost all reason. Spending recklessly on equipment and parties is compatible with this: choosing an expenditure expresses ownership. Theft receives cruel, swift and often disproportionate revenge; recovering a sum may cost more than the sum itself.

They accept extortionate contracts and blood bonds entered without clear coercion, including harsh bargains accepted out of desperation. Direct threats forcing acceptance invalidate a bargain under their cultural standard. The boundary is disputed.

## The Golden Lease and guild power
**The Golden Lease** is the canonical custom of voluntarily selling oneself into slavery for steep compensation and a specified period of expertise and ownership. **Selling the season** is a common casual expression. Short bursts are common, and Nymbrin eagerly sell to anyone meeting their fee. Terms can reserve protections, exclusions and living conditions. They attach little inherent shame to a paid voluntary sale.

Their guilds deeply influence the **Blood Courts** through construction, processing, medicines, weapons and maintenance. Members enjoy unusual social protections: enforceable terms, compensation, living conditions and arbitration. Exact rights vary by court and contract.

Guilds defend their position through strikes, sabotage, rebellion and a notorious history and threat of alchemical bioterrorism. It is poorly concealed that they could seize control from Vren. They retain Vren rulers because defense, government and other burdens are handled on their behalf, and keeping the arrangement is more profitable.

Bankruptcy is another matter: Nymbrin commonly sell bankrupt members **permanently** to settle debts, outside the temporary Golden Lease. Deep debts justify hunting their own guild members beyond the Scarlands. Guild protection against abuse does not cancel enforceable obligations.

| Rank | Role |
|---|---|
| Apprentice | Maintenance, cleaning, deliveries, nursery work and other grunt work while seeking recommendations. |
| Proven | Completed a first Golden Lease and lost apprentice status; their expertise has secured a buyer. |
| Master | Supervises apprentices, endorses candidates, leads major gigs and runs an independent workshop. |
| Factor | Negotiates major agreements, manages assets and represents members before courts and buyers. |
| Guildholder | Holds a governing share or chartered seat; decides policy, strikes and retaliation. |

Some guilds accept young apprentices, but most expect applicants to prove useful without assistance first. Apprentices are eager to earn recommendations: a good first lease is almost impossible without one or several. Their labor keeps society functioning during seniors' celebrations.

## Pools, tadpoles, love and age
Households are loose, shifting **pools** containing lovers, siblings, unrelated partners, tenants and Duskpaw relatives. Membership rests on affection, convenience, property and business. Children may inherit a household name without much protection; family relationship and a willingness to vouch for someone are distinct.

Nymbrin **lay eggs in water**. Eggs must be fertilized **before laying** to hatch. Nursery pools range from guarded household basins to communal waterways. Unfertilized eggs are called **caviar**, are key ingredients in several medicinal elixirs and are sold without inherent Nymbrin stigma.

Young are **tadpoles**. Within days of hatching they move independently, develop simple vocabularies, recognize opportunities and perform tasks other races take years to learn. Their long tails gradually shrink. Early competence is not adult judgment: they remain impressionable and vulnerable to exploitation.

Parents are frequently absentee. Prolific gangs of orphaned or effectively abandoned tadpoles inhabit canals and hidden Pazara chambers, sharing food, territory, errands and favors. Childhood gangs can become lifelong partnerships or rivalries. Attentive parents also exist.

Physical adulthood is around **eight years**. Healthy natural lifespan is **sixty to seventy-five years**; heavy Haze exposure shortens it by at least a third. Elders retain influence through wealth, knowledge and recommendations. Retirement depends on assets and arrangements rather than guaranteed family care.

Love is exuberant, affectionate and indulgent. Gifts, hospitality, shared experiences and foregoing profitable work express devotion. A gift without an obligation can be especially intimate. Partnerships may be exclusive or shared, temporary or lifelong. Property and dependents can be contractual without every affectionate relationship being a contract. Potion-induced feelings can complicate existing attachments. Family poison precautions remain relevant.

## Duskpaw alliance and Wakebound
Duskpaw black markets benefit from Nymbrin patronage, introductions and customers; Nymbrin benefit from discreet procurement, information and distribution. **Stealing from Nymbrin is a deep Duskpaw taboo**. Breaches threaten entire networks and can bring compelled restitution or withdrawal of protection.

Nymbrin paranoia about thieves rarely applies to trusted Duskpaws. Both enjoy competitive contractual swindles, hidden advantages, audacious prices and clever clauses, distinguishing an opportunity to refuse a bargain from taking a purse. A fine defeat may earn admiration and a rematch.

Duskpaws appear in Nymbrin guilds and pools as spouses, adopted kin, partners and apprentices; Nymbrin likewise join Duskpaw Caches and networks.

Outside the Scarlands, Nymbrin most often join **Tidekin rafts**; these expatriates are **Wakebound**. Raft interdependence shifts their values toward reliable cooperation, shared necessities, generosity and hospitality. Small debts become less important and useless accumulated wealth less impressive. They retain trade-minded humor; inland guilds may see them as sentimental, while Wakebound find inland acquisitiveness exhausting.

## Linguistic tendencies
Nymbrin use casual, playful trade metaphors rather than constant legalistic speech: a kingdom's staggering debt is its **tab**, a job a **gig**, an investment a **bet**. They become precise when actual terms matter. Given, lent, purchased, entrusted and owed remain meaningful distinctions.

Examples: “The duke's tab could buy us a coastline.” “Nice little gig. Terrible master, excellent bathing facilities.” “I like her. Bad bet, wonderful company.” “We can afford a catastrophe. Two, if the drinks are included.”

Affectionate expressions include “my treasure,” “sweet draught,” “dearest expense,” “my favorite extravagance,” and “my favorite bad bet.” “A gift, darling. No balance remains” marks generosity; “You have made yourself expensive” is a threat. “An uncovered vessel” insults carelessness. Speech patterns are tendencies, not mandatory accents. Ordinary speech uses magical colors; technical rules may use mana.

## Wetcraft: six skills and equivalent exchange
**Wetcraft** is fluid-based alchemy using blood, tears, venom, secretions and other vital fluids taken from other races. **Greater suffering during extraction yields purer and more potent fluid.** Harvesting is among the setting's most brutal industries, often concealed from consumers of its civic comforts.

| Discipline | Domain |
|---|---|
| Elixircraft | Consumable remedies, restorative draughts, intoxicants, temporary adaptations and permanent mutation preparations. |
| Venomcraft | Poisons, antidotes, debilitating substances and skin secretion modification. |
| Ordnance | Alchemical weapons, payloads, corrosives, ammunition and military charges. |
| Reshaping | Active alteration of physical form and structure; construction, passages, reinforcement and machine repair. |
| Recomposition | Active chemical changes, separation, purification and industrial material processing. |
| Vital Transmutation | Active tissue alteration, wound closure, reconstruction, mutation and, at the highest mastery, near-lossless conversion of one mana color into another. |

Three skills craft consumables and three practice active transmutation. Written **alchemical circles** define and constrain exchanges, from portable inscribed tools to permanent industrial arrays. Modern philosopher's stones mediate demanding work and require replenishment.

**Weight is a primary factor in equivalent exchange**, alongside constituents and complexity. Blue mana powers changes without providing unlimited matter. Repair requires surviving pieces or replacement resources; wound closure does not freely replace missing blood or tissue. Blood purification yields water and residues. Concrete can become limestone powder with its other constituents separated or redirected. Recomposition can make oxygen-rich pockets that intensify existing fire; actual explosive effects need further magical or alchemical properties.

Alchemy is the technological root of the Scarlands, especially construction. Kobold engineering uses interacting mechanisms; Nymbrin alter substances and organisms. Repair can rejoin matter; pumps can be living organs; lamps luminous compounds; storage uses preservatives. Both traditions collaborate. Kobold machines need components and maintenance; living alchemy can require food, rebalancing and medical care. Exact new skill/rank/SP/MP and reagent costs remain unapproved.

## November 21, 2026 — Physical stability governs Wetcraft persistence (AUTHOR APPROVED)

**Approved rule:** The physical stability of the **finished result alone** determines whether an otherwise lawful Nymbrin transmutation continues to exist after its casting finishes. An alteration that reaches a physically viable, self-supporting final configuration is **stabilized** and persists as an ordinary physical result, without ongoing magic, a surviving circle, or a separate stabilization action. The original casting circle may then be removed or destroyed without reversing that finished result. This is not a blanket grant of superior workmanship, an item's Quality, enchantment, ongoing animation, or a new source of matter.

An alteration that **cannot physically maintain its changed configuration by ordinary means** is **unstable** and requires a functioning magical **anchoring circle** for its continuing altered state. Disruption of the required anchor ends its support and causes the remaining affected matter to attempt physical reversion or settle under ordinary forces. This does **not** reverse time, restore absent or destroyed matter, undo injuries or other external consequences, or create irresistible/infinite reversion force. Actual reversion outcomes depend on existing physical obstacles and material constraints. The exact anchor durability, valid placement/linkage, active duration, maintenance MP, potential Soul Strain, and supported force remain **UNRESOLVED**.

**Separation from Warlock Blue Morph:** Nymbrin Wetcraft can produce genuinely stabilized physical changes directly; Warlock Blue Morph retains its own approved temporary/reversion rules, including lawful mold-assisted mundane manufacture. Neither craft borrows the other's special permissions automatically. Approved Morph quantity/area, material-category, Power and Magnitude rules remain the reference for future Wetcraft calibration, **not** a new finalized Wetcraft numerical cost table.

**Preserved restrictions:** Equivalent exchange still demands sufficient eligible mass and constituents, replacement resources where material is missing, suitable knowledge for functioning mechanisms, and all established material resistance. Especially hard shield/armor materials resist alchemy; alchemical reshaping does not waive equipment Quality, legal crafting qualifications, forge requirements, or exceptional manufacture rules. **Every alchemical working produces Hex.** The proposed glove circles, enlarging/linked circle arrays, trigger mechanisms, natural Hex-release rate, circle sizes and precise casting/action/MP/SP costs remain **proposals**, not approved mechanics. Pazara remains living, nourished construction matter; no new free growth or matter creation follows from this decision.

## November 21, 2026 — Wetcraft Circuit, anchoring and Sigil Scar design direction (AUTHOR-SELECTED; numerical calibration OPEN)

**Circle network, called a Circuit:** Alchemical circles are the physical mediums of Wetcraft. A circle operates on eligible material **inside its own boundary**; universal Magnitude improves the allowable **size** of circles, not their inherent Reshaping reach. **Extended Range** is the separate legal reach in which a circle can create or connect to additional circles, and a caster must be inside the Extended Range of an accessible circle to operate through it. Circles can link into a **Circuit** to relay access and blue mana through multiple legal links. Circuits and very large circles can supply distant, lawfully connected sigils, but do not inherently increase spell Power, erase costs or waive Channel Limit. Large-scale circles may be restricted to correspondingly large-scale workings, while still enabling smaller-circle placement within their territories. A lower-Magnitude circle can create a circle **one Magnitude higher** in one action provided the next circle lies within legal placement reach, the caster has that rank and pays the relevant lawful cost; each circle-making operation generates Hex. Precise dimensions, allowed connection geometries, one-action cost pricing, very-large-circle work-size thresholds, and potential Circuit relay losses are OPEN.

**Physical extensions:** Eligible connected material may be shaped beyond a working circle's perimeter, but active manipulation cannot extend beyond that circle's **own Extended Range**, nor beyond valid physically continuous material. Severing the material connection ends active magical control of the disconnected section; independently stable resulting matter is not erased. The ordinary attack/delivery and defense framework still applies to hostile physical contact.

**Anchoring:** Physically unstable transformations, including supernatural hardening or softening, require an active anchor on the affected material. An anchor's required Magnitude is **at least two categories below the effect** (minimum category 0): `max(0, effect magnitude - 2)` is the proposed direct implementation of the author's size relationship. An anchoring sigil inherits the **ordinary, unmodified physical durability of its actual substrate** but **does not benefit from magical reinforcement**. The perimeter is the relevant vulnerable line, not decorative internal strokes; exact hit/break thresholds remain OPEN. Anchors obey ordinary spell Duration; an intact, lawfully accessible anchor may be **refreshed with actual blue mana equal to the original spell MP cost**, through an accessible Extended Range/Circuit connection. Refresh is not automatic free duration or increased Power. Its timing and interaction with Channel Limit and other ongoing buffs still require calibration.

**Reshaping hardening and softening:** Both are anchored and are intended to use **only a portion of legally generated casting Power**, provisionally **one-half Power** for testing with **one-quarter Power** as an alternative. These are not finalized numerical coefficients. Apply as an appropriate rider to the affected structure's lawful physical Defense, without silently equating structural Defense, armor AR, shields and creatures. Hostile attempts remain subject to legal delivery/resistance; weakening a beam does not automatically destroy the bridge without a physical load or subsequent failure. Multiple effects must not silently stack without limit.

**Vital Transmutation Sigil Scar:** Hostile, unnatural changes to living tissue may create a visible **circle scar**, mechanically treated as a new status condition with **1× applicable resistance for Severity I** and **4× for Severity II**. A lawful scar-applying attack contributes **2× the caster's applicable casting Power in status buildup**, rather than receiving automatic incidental scar buildup from other attacks. At Severity I the victim's applicable physical Defense is reduced; at Severity II the debuff increases. Exact Status Resistance identity, delivery, duration, reduction coefficients and repeat-application rules must be tested; illustrative 0.5× Power at Severity I and 1× at Severity II are **UNAPPROVED TEST VALUES**. Removing the affected scar-bearing part or lawfully healing with sufficient **healing Power at least equal to the scar-applying spell Power** ends the anchored scar, with **Toxicity generated** by magical scar removal. Existing canonical magical wound healing remains +20 percentage points Toxicity **per actual wound healed**; the exact incremental Toxicity when a scar alone is erased without a separately healed wound is not yet assigned and must not be invented. Scar disruption is not a generic one-heal deletion of already stabilized biological mutations or Hexen changes.

**Hex:** Every alchemical working, including circle creation, refresh and beneficial actions, produces Hex under approved Wetcraft rules. The author favors a future **natural gradual release/dissipation mechanism**; actual output, accumulation, safe release, environmental destination and numeric thresholds are OPEN. Do not treat natural release as annihilation of pollution or allow zero-cost infinite industrial throughput.

## November 21, 2026 — Nymbrin Hex-capacity casting and Circuit civic infrastructure (AUTHOR-SELECTED TEST FRAMEWORK)

**Selected casting economy, requires balance verification:** Nymbrin retain the ordinary applicable **Channel Limit** for legal actions/casts, but **do not directly spend their personal blue-MP reserve to fuel ordinary Wetcraft**. A working assigned an ordinary blue-mana-equivalent **spell cost M** instead creates **3M personal Hex** (provisional ratio selected for testing). Current accumulated personal Hex **cannot exceed maximum personal mana capacity**; if a working would exceed the cap, it is not legal in the ordinary model. The ordinary spell cost M still determines spell Power, Magnitude/other modular eligibility, and Channel Limit usage, not the inflated 3M Hex figure. **Ten uninterrupted minutes of rest** allow the Nymbrin to release their stored personal Hex **without paying personal mana or a separate recovery fee**. This is a short-burst/fast-recovery design: low simultaneous casting capacity but recovery between encounters. **Every operation still generates Hex**; releasing it removes the user's personal burden, **not** the environmental pollution. Exact Hex absorption and release conditions, environmental harm, non-rest recovery, whether external blue mana can pay an anchor's upkeep, and interactions with ordinary mana potions, rituals and other magic remain OPEN. Neither allow 3× Hex to replace the action's ordinary spell MP-equivalent for Channel Limit checks nor assume personal casting produces free mana.

**Mathematical implication for testing:** With personal Hex capacity `H_max = maximum personal MP`, remaining personal Hex capacity `H_max - H_current`, and 3 Hex per 1 spell-MP-equivalent, a single ordinary working must satisfy `3M <= H_max - H_current`, independently of the applicable Channel Limit and all other legal requirements. Maximum ordinary casting budget between releases is `floor((H_max-H_current)/3)` spell-MP-equivalent. This is a proposed test implementation of the chosen direction, **not** an established exception to independently applicable ritual or resource rules. Particularly small personal MP pools may prevent even a 1 MP working; resolve minimum viability and alternative supplies during calibration.

**Sigil Scar prevention and targeted Defense (author decisions):** A willing target may undergo qualifying magical scar removal **before the status becomes active**, clearing the accumulated Sigil Scar buildup if the healing working's applicable Power is **at least the original scar-applying casting Power**, and the target accepts the appropriate **Toxicity**. This same parity applies to active scars. Scar removal costs Toxicity even when performed on buildup/preactivation. **Author-accepted scar treatment:** successful magical removal of one scar, including preactivation buildup, adds **+20 percentage points of cumulative Toxicity**; any separate actual wounds healed by the same working still incur their normal **+20 percentage points per wound**. Healing Power must equal or exceed the original scar spell Power. Sigil Scar's defense penalty applies **specifically to the creature's END-derived Defense component**, **never reducing that component below 1**, and **does not directly reduce the Armor Rating/armor-derived component**, allowing armor to remain protective. At 1× and 4× resistance the reduction increases; use the same *provisional half-of-eligible-Power rider* being considered for ordinary Reshaping softening, with Severity II magnitude and interactions still to be calibrated. Status application remains a dedicated 2× casting-Power buildup action subject to lawful contact/contests. This is not a blanket penalty to finished armor or END attribute scores.

**Long-range infrastructure:** Cities may build dedicated relay or **antenna circles** to form wide-area blue-mana supply Circuits. Long chains can transmit mana access for miles and maintain distant anchored transformations if legally connected, appropriately paid, and inside applicable working/relay rules. Proposed civic applications include villages supported by remote supply networks and mine-town fault reinforcement. These are **possible infrastructure/worldbuilding extrapolations**, not an approved free spell supply or automatic Hex removal. Blue mana transmitted *to* a sigil and Hex pollution produced *by* alchemy must be tracked separately. Actual transmission loss, channel access permissions, damage, reserve requirements, power sources and environmental Hex handling are OPEN.

## November 21, 2026 — Purging Croak and fresh environmental Hex tiles (AUTHOR APPROVAL; buildup values pending)

**Purging Croak accepted as Nymbrin recovery mechanic:** A Nymbrin may commit **both actions in one turn** to sustained audible croaking. At the **beginning of their next turn**, if they were **neither wounded nor stunned during the channeling interval** and remained able to croak, the action completes. An existing wound alone does not prohibit beginning the action; an intervening wound or stun interrupts even if subsequently healed. On successful completion, choose the personal blue MP actually spent and remove **3 units of personal stored Hex per MP spent**, not exceeding the stored Hex; personal MP is spent only on success. Croaking neither creates mana nor clears environmental pollution. A normal **10-minute rest** remains the separate no-MP personal Hex-release method. Exact interaction with Silence, disabling effects, actions and external sources follows applicable future calibration rather than free exceptions.

**Fresh battlefield Hex:** When accumulated personal Hex is released, the Nymbrin can place resulting **Hex tiles** at legal positions anywhere within the **Extended Range of their accessible Circuit**. User direction: **one environmental Hex tile per MP-equivalent of released Hex**; with the provisional 3 personal Hex per equivalent MP casting model, the direct interpretation is **3 personal Hex released -> one Hex tile** (equivalently one tile per MP spent on successful Purging Croak). Outdoor tiles last **3 turns**; in enclosed areas such as rooms, **1 minute**. A creature entering a Hex tile at any point during its turn gains one application of buildup toward the **already-established Hexed status** on the **first entry that turn**, not another merely for passing through more tiles. This applies to the releasing Nymbrin, allies and enemies; released Hex is no longer safely sequestered in its originator. The exact per-tile buildup/Power, overlapping tiles, turn-start occupancy, sight/dispersion and environmental density rules are **OPEN**, not automatic full casting Power. Hexed keeps its existing mana/Leywell-drain and spell disruption identity; do not invent specific damage or MP loss values.

**Environmental Haze direction:** Airborne Hex/Haze **decays over time** and **living organisms contribute to its processing/absorption**, potentially harming their long-term health; the environmental consequences, numerical uptake and differing species resistances await calibration. A regional scale should have **zero ambient Haze in ordinary unaffected areas**, lighter exposure toward Scarlands outskirts and bordering foreign territories, a **critical threshold where vulnerable inhabitants collapse and exposed alchemical circles fail**, and a still higher saturation tier where unprotected air is unbreathable and alchemy needs special shielding. Such ambient concentration is distinct from short-lived fresh combat tiles. The author is considering **demons and Hexen as non-absorbing** and coercive civilian/Starspawn exposure as a grim civic response to Hex spills; these ecological immunity and absorption distinctions are **LORE PROPOSALS PENDING EXPLICIT APPROVAL**, not a completed species resistance table. Preserve prior canon: Starspawn are unusually susceptible, some resident groups tolerate dirty air at real lifespan cost, Hexen emit ongoing pollution, and severe Haze can suffocate outsiders.

## November 21, 2026 — Scarlands Haze tiers, Clearbreaths, recovery and Purity Circles (AUTHOR-SELECTED; exact calibration pending)

**Latest Haze precedence:** Use an environmental six-tier **Haze 0–V** scale instead of treating earlier descriptions of polluted districts, borders, and catastrophic smog as an unstructured single hazard. **0** represents ordinary clean regions; **I** trace contamination around Scarlands margins and nearby borders; **II** polluted ordinary Scarlands sites; **III** hazardous industrial/lower-district exposure; **IV** the **critical tipping point** where especially vulnerable creatures collapse and exposed alchemical circles become unreliable or cease functioning; **V** saturated, unbreathable air requiring shielding for alchemy and specialized respiration. Earlier atmospheric lore still governs qualitative sensitivity, shorter polluted-district lifespans, need for clearbreaths and Starspawn vulnerability except where inconsistent with this newer tier framework. Exact Hex-units-per-air-volume thresholds and accumulation/time steps are **OPEN**.

**Environmentally applied Hexed status:** Breathing contaminated ambient Haze accumulates the **existing Hexed status**, which drains actual personal MP and applicable enchanted-item Leywells, then inflicts injury/danger after available protection is exhausted; the existing greater severity can disrupt ongoing effects. Benchmark exposure Power is approximately that of a **level-10 Nymbrin alchemist with an implement and half earned allocatable attribute points assigned to INT**, with **zero additional MP invested in base offensive Power**. Under the approved neutral-implement benchmark this is `P10=(effective INT_10^2+IR^2)*0.55`; actual Nymbrin level-1 stat profile/implement Quality and hence exact `P10` are not finalized. Haze intensity must multiply exposure buildup as levels rise; first-pass **UNAPPROVED CALIBRATION** is tier I/II/III/IV/V = `0.5/1/2/4/8 × P10` per applicable environmental exposure. Whether ambient exposure resolves once per turn, breath, or elapsed interval and how it interacts with fresh Hex tiles still requires balance testing, not free extra simultaneous triggers. **Clean air removes 10% of the victim's applicable Hex Status Resistance in stored Hexed buildup per full turn**, a flat `0.10*SR_Hex` reduction per turn, not 10% of current buildup. Existing active-status downgrade/end thresholds at 4×/1× resistance still apply.

**Leywell-powered clearbreaths and personal purifying sigils:** The Scarlands' previously canonical specialized **clearbreath** mask is an **alchemical purity-sigil respirator** powered by a lawfully charged **matching blue Leywell**. While functional it reduces the wearer's **effective Haze level by two tiers**, minimum 0; thus Haze 0/I/II -> 0, III -> I, IV -> II, V -> III. A depleted/unpowered mask gives no purifying protection. Exact item quality, MP drain interval, mask endurance, capacity, shop price and duration are OPEN. Mask protection does not automatically stack with multiple masks or other purification without an explicit stacking rule, and it does not convert a saturated disaster into unrestricted clean air. Ordinary cloth remains inadequate.

**Purity Circles:** All Nymbrin alchemists may access an **extra-cheap** anchored purification-working option, with **two additional duration tiers free** relative to the ordinary legal paid duration selection, **still requiring an actual functioning anchoring circle** and the appropriate actual source/resources. A Purity Circle can lower the **effective Haze level within its eligible area by up to two levels**, floor 0; it neither grants an infinite amount of purified air nor automatically erases all exported Hex from environmental accounting. Exact casting MP/Hex costs, duration-tier interpretation relative to the provisional duration ladder, throughput/venting of removed pollution, scope/size and anti-stacking rules remain OPEN. A purity-sigil mask also ignores two Haze levels while powered. At Haze IV–V, unshielded circles may fail; purification arrays may need protected source areas, staged relays or purpose-built shielding to begin cleaning critical/saturated air.

**Living-alchemy mitigation:** An eligible learned **Vital Transmutation** working may remove a creature's accumulated Hexed buildup **into the surrounding environment** (pollution transfer, not annihilation), subject to real spell cost, lawful target and material requirements. A separate eligible living-alchemy effect may increase the target's **physiological resistance to inhaled Hex**. Rank, scope, Power, resistance increase, duration, and whether the change creates a circle scar remain OPEN. Such mitigation cannot silently grant immunity or reverse prior chronic harm.

## November 21, 2026 — Haze channel entropy, constructed circle capacity and circle-user compatibility (AUTHOR APPROVED)

**Haze entropy (superseding unexplained automatic circle failure):** In **ambient Haze IV**, a working loses **25% of mana being channeled to environmental entropy**, delivering **75%**. In **Haze V**, it loses **50%**, delivering **50%**. Haze 0–III do not incur this new special channel-loss surcharge. Apply the loss to the mana delivered toward a spell's actual requirements **before checking whether it meets its minimum effective mana cost**, not merely as an after-the-fact damage or Power reduction. Extra raw mana must remain within the caster's lawful action/ritual Channel Limit and genuine accessible supply; insufficient delivered mana causes the working to fail. Example: a 10-effective-MP transformation needs 20 channeled MP at Haze V, so full CL 14 cannot cast it in one turn even with two actions. At Haze IV, 14 raw delivers 10.5 effective, sufficient for a 10-MP requirement. For raw-to-effective rounding, use the physical fractional comparison before final rounding; a formal integer rounding convention still needs calibration. Existing cooperative/weekly ritual entropy remains separately approved and is not replaced; exact ordering with ambient Haze loss and other reductions remains OPEN.

**Why circles fail:** A working circle can be physically intact but become **operationally unable to supply sufficient effective mana** when ambient Haze destroys 25%/50% of throughput. A previously comfortable excess design capacity may cease to cover the supported spell. This is a **capacity failure**, not necessarily physical destruction of the perimeter or anchor. Restoration when channel conditions improve, which particular parts of a network suffer local ambient losses, and whether active anchor effects merely lapse or revert at failure follow their own eligible duration/stability rules and require location-specific implementation. Purity protection or shielding must be lawful and actually cover the affected part of the channel.

**Installed circle throughput:** A newly constructed circle's **rated maximum usable channeling capacity** is capped by the maker's **applicable Channel Limit at the time of creation**, using the legal casting action/ritual timeframe. More powerful later users do not retroactively raise the circle's installed capacity. Legitimate **ritual construction** may raise the capacity according to approved ritual Channel Limit multipliers and mana/time constraints; e.g. the approved ten-minute ritual capacity of up to four times applicable full CL, without free mana, skills or materials. Capacity is independently constrained by the user's current CL, the installed circle, environment, and remaining resources. This physical-capacity rule gives major industrial circles a reason to be prepared through substantial rituals.

**Interoperability and circle-style penalty:** Two alchemists can normally use **each other's unprotected circles**, including hostile use. A circle unfamiliar to the user suffers a **50% reduction of its usable installed channeling capacity** from style incompatibility; do **not** halve the visiting alchemist's attribute, their actual personal CL or the spell's Power automatically. Alchemists who spend **10 minutes communicating and aligning circle styles** can adjust their working circles for mutual full-capacity compatibility (still bound by individual current CL and installed circle limits). Scope of alignment (one circle, a linked Circuit or an ongoing compatibility agreement), whether each circle must be retuned in place, timing under hostile interruption, and fractional capacity rounding are OPEN.

**Secured reactive circles:** An alchemist who lawfully learns the universal **Autonomy III Reactivity** capability may imbue circles with **user-acceptance restrictions**, allowing them to recognize authorized operators and reject unauthorized ones rather than defaulting to the familiar/unfamiliar capacity rule. This is a meaningful security tool for major alchemical works and Circuit infrastructure. Authorization criteria, revocation, contested seizure/disabling, anti-spoofing, implementation cost/duration, whether restrictions apply to passive external inputs and preservation under Haze remain OPEN. The feature does not grant free immunity to damage, sabotage, or all forms of lawful magical contest.

## November 21, 2026 — Circle hacking, capacity rounding and layered entropy (LATEST AUTHOR APPROVAL)

**Circle capacities round DOWN** when a fractional throughput limit is produced, including a foreign circle's **50% unfamiliar-style reduction**. An installed circle's rated construction capacity, current user's lawful Channel Limit/action/ritual ceiling, and relevant Haze entropy all independently constrain actual workings. **Haze IV 25% and Haze V 50%** are additional multiplicative throughput losses in any valid working, **not additive percentage-point reductions** that zero out cooperative allies. Preserve approved separately applied cooperative 50% and one-week ritual 50% losses (100 contributed mana -> 50 -> 25); if also operating wholly within Haze V, the further half loss gives 12.5 effective mana before final relevant integer rounding.
