# Nymbrin — Wetcraft, the Golden Lease, and the Hexen

**CANON — all current Nymbrin lore explicitly accepted by the author November 4, 2026.** Latest terminology: **Hexen** collectively names mutants. Earlier provisional mutant names are superseded. Nymkin/Nimbrin in the source conversation are spelling variants; use **Nymbrin**.
**Authority:** This dossier owns Nymbrin lore, Wetcraft foundations, Pazara, Hexen and hex. The new selectable technique catalogue is a separate **PROPOSAL**, not included in this approval.

## Identity and biology
Nymbrin are predominantly humanoid poison-dart-frog people, close to Tidekin in size, aligned with **blue mana**. Their smooth skin has exotic, vivid colorations, contrasting bands, patches and spots. Strong legs permit jumps much farther than any other mortal race. Their muscular, grasping tongues extend about **ten meters on average**, supporting combat, manipulation, anchoring and mobility. They are amphibious and breathe underwater. Webbed feet aid swimming and wet-ground movement; footwear leaves the webbing free. A second set of transparent eyelids protects their eyes underwater and closes reflexively against splashes.

Their default skin secretion causes **dangerous numbness**, potentially hiding injury and impairing coordination. Venomcraft can modify it for sleep, heightened sensitivity, hallucinations and other poisons, using appropriate preparations rather than unrestricted instant selection. Each individual is immune to their **own exact toxin** and heavily resistant, not universally immune, to others' skin toxins and modified mixtures.

They become uncomfortable after about **one hour** of dryness and dangerously dehydrated after about **eight hours**; heat and exertion accelerate this. Hydration elixirs temporarily extend independence from water. Their cities feature canals, soaking chambers, submerged rooms and humid workshops.

A layer of water beneath the skin, especially around the gills, protects them from Haze. Drying in Haze is excruciating even for acclimatized Nymbrin and can cause scarring and wheezing. Their water must remain free of Haze contamination. Clean-water, temperature, humidity and maintenance provisions are central contractual protections.

Among furred peoples, **Tidekin alone have fur thick and oily enough for harmless ordinary contact with Nymbrin skin**. Duskpaw partners and relatives use managed secretions, clothing and precautions.

## Origins: El Dorado and the Great Taking
Nymbrin once possessed a civilization rivaling the Serpentari, centered on **El Dorado, the City of Gold**: canals, terraced pools, great alchemical works and wealth sufficient to make gold an ordinary architectural material.

They created **true philosopher's stones which violated equivalent exchange**. These supported extraordinary abundance. The Serpentari famously robbed El Dorado in **the Great Taking**, seizing treasures and stockpiles and destroying or dismantling institutions sustaining its prosperity. Collapse followed as workshops and waterways failed and guilds dispersed with incomplete knowledge. True stones and their manufacture have since been lost to time; surviving examples remain an unresolved possibility.

Modern stones are lesser successors: they concentrate, store and mediate alchemical potential while obeying equivalent exchange. Scholars sometimes bitterly call them **accountant's stones**.

**Duskpaws lived in El Dorado alongside Nymbrin before the Night of a Thousand Locks.** Their shared dispossession motivates their alliance and contributes to Duskpaw fascination with theft, crime and reclaiming stolen prosperity. This adds El Dorado to Duskpaw history; their established Serpentari enslavement, Fraycraft blessing and liberation remain canon. Exact sequencing of El Dorado's foundation, the Great Taking and particular God War events is not numerically dated.

El Dorado relied on **homunculi**, alchemically modified Starspawn, for roles most modern societies assign to slaves. This was an extremely reciprocal arrangement: Starspawn gained permanent power, prestige and privileged positions in return for service. Very powerful mutations were once common. Some ancient homunculi survive and have become legendary.

Modern homunculi are still made, but their modifications end on **celestial recall**. Recreating expensive modifications and the rarity of extreme transformations have reduced their popularity. These additions do not change the established divine God War origin of Starspawn; exact El Dorado/Starspawn chronology remains unquantified.

After the God War, Vren brought Nymbrin to their lands and accepted steep contracts and extensive amphibious infrastructure requirements to develop the Scarlands into a full kingdom. Nymbrin are poorly suited to its dry wasteland.

## Personality, faith and money
Nymbrin combine hedonism, fatalism, persistent optimism and unusually strong pragmatism. They seek pleasure, look on the bright side, identify surviving assets after disaster and treat dependence as a business opportunity. Kobolds are intelligent but overeager; centaurs emphasize service, form and function; Nymbrin assess worth, demand and the enjoyment a sale will purchase.

They worship **Baphomet**. Everyday observances include raising a first sip in her name, celebrating successful leases with meals, maintaining workshop shrines adorned with enjoyed luxuries and earmarking part of a payment for deliberate indulgence. An apprentice's first post-lease celebration is religious and social recognition. They can be warm companions while remaining cruelly indifferent to exploitation. Their famously dangerous parties include intoxicants, modified secretions and reckless competitions; conscientious hosts provide warnings and antidotes.

Their attachment to wealth rivals dragons. They keep exact accounts and resist robbery or extortion beyond almost all reason. Spending recklessly on equipment and parties is compatible with this: choosing an expenditure expresses ownership. Theft receives cruel, swift and often disproportionate revenge; recovering a sum may cost more than the sum itself.

They accept extortionate contracts and blood bonds entered without clear coercion, including harsh bargains accepted out of desperation. Direct threats forcing acceptance invalidate a bargain under their cultural standard. The boundary is disputed.

## The Golden Lease and guild power
**The Golden Lease** is the canonical custom of voluntarily selling oneself into slavery for steep compensation and a specified period of expertise and ownership. **Selling the season** is a common casual expression. Short bursts are common, and Nymbrin eagerly sell to anyone meeting their fee. Terms can reserve protections, exclusions and living conditions. They attach little inherent shame to a paid voluntary sale.

Their guilds deeply influence the **Blood Courts** through construction, processing, medicines, weapons and maintenance. Members enjoy unusual social protections: enforceable terms, compensation, living conditions and arbitration. Exact rights vary by court and contract.

Guilds defend their position through strikes, sabotage, rebellion and a notorious history and threat of alchemical bioterrorism. It is poorly concealed that they could seize control from Vren. They retain Vren rulers because defense, government and other burdens are handled on their behalf, and keeping the arrangement is more profitable.

Bankruptcy is another matter: Nymbrin commonly sell bankrupt members **permanently** to settle debts, outside the temporary Golden Lease. Deep debts justify hunting their own guild members beyond the Scarlands. Guild protection against abuse does not cancel enforceable obligations.

| Rank | Role |
|---|---|
| Apprentice | Maintenance, cleaning, deliveries, nursery work and other grunt work while seeking recommendations. |
| Proven | Completed a first Golden Lease and lost apprentice status; their expertise has secured a buyer. |
| Master | Supervises apprentices, endorses candidates, leads major gigs and runs an independent workshop. |
| Factor | Negotiates major agreements, manages assets and represents members before courts and buyers. |
| Guildholder | Holds a governing share or chartered seat; decides policy, strikes and retaliation. |

Some guilds accept young apprentices, but most expect applicants to prove useful without assistance first. Apprentices are eager to earn recommendations: a good first lease is almost impossible without one or several. Their labor keeps society functioning during seniors' celebrations.

## Pools, tadpoles, love and age
Households are loose, shifting **pools** containing lovers, siblings, unrelated partners, tenants and Duskpaw relatives. Membership rests on affection, convenience, property and business. Children may inherit a household name without much protection; family relationship and a willingness to vouch for someone are distinct.

Nymbrin **lay eggs in water**. Eggs must be fertilized **before laying** to hatch. Nursery pools range from guarded household basins to communal waterways. Unfertilized eggs are called **caviar**, are key ingredients in several medicinal elixirs and are sold without inherent Nymbrin stigma.

Young are **tadpoles**. Within days of hatching they move independently, develop simple vocabularies, recognize opportunities and perform tasks other races take years to learn. Their long tails gradually shrink. Early competence is not adult judgment: they remain impressionable and vulnerable to exploitation.

Parents are frequently absentee. Prolific gangs of orphaned or effectively abandoned tadpoles inhabit canals and hidden Pazara chambers, sharing food, territory, errands and favors. Childhood gangs can become lifelong partnerships or rivalries. Attentive parents also exist.

Physical adulthood is around **eight years**. Healthy natural lifespan is **sixty to seventy-five years**; heavy Haze exposure shortens it by at least a third. Elders retain influence through wealth, knowledge and recommendations. Retirement depends on assets and arrangements rather than guaranteed family care.

Love is exuberant, affectionate and indulgent. Gifts, hospitality, shared experiences and foregoing profitable work express devotion. A gift without an obligation can be especially intimate. Partnerships may be exclusive or shared, temporary or lifelong. Property and dependents can be contractual without every affectionate relationship being a contract. Potion-induced feelings can complicate existing attachments. Family poison precautions remain relevant.

## Duskpaw alliance and Wakebound
Duskpaw black markets benefit from Nymbrin patronage, introductions and customers; Nymbrin benefit from discreet procurement, information and distribution. **Stealing from Nymbrin is a deep Duskpaw taboo**. Breaches threaten entire networks and can bring compelled restitution or withdrawal of protection.

Nymbrin paranoia about thieves rarely applies to trusted Duskpaws. Both enjoy competitive contractual swindles, hidden advantages, audacious prices and clever clauses, distinguishing an opportunity to refuse a bargain from taking a purse. A fine defeat may earn admiration and a rematch.

Duskpaws appear in Nymbrin guilds and pools as spouses, adopted kin, partners and apprentices; Nymbrin likewise join Duskpaw Caches and networks.

Outside the Scarlands, Nymbrin most often join **Tidekin rafts**; these expatriates are **Wakebound**. Raft interdependence shifts their values toward reliable cooperation, shared necessities, generosity and hospitality. Small debts become less important and useless accumulated wealth less impressive. They retain trade-minded humor; inland guilds may see them as sentimental, while Wakebound find inland acquisitiveness exhausting.

## Linguistic tendencies
Nymbrin use casual, playful trade metaphors rather than constant legalistic speech: a kingdom's staggering debt is its **tab**, a job a **gig**, an investment a **bet**. They become precise when actual terms matter. Given, lent, purchased, entrusted and owed remain meaningful distinctions.

Examples: “The duke's tab could buy us a coastline.” “Nice little gig. Terrible master, excellent bathing facilities.” “I like her. Bad bet, wonderful company.” “We can afford a catastrophe. Two, if the drinks are included.”

Affectionate expressions include “my treasure,” “sweet draught,” “dearest expense,” “my favorite extravagance,” and “my favorite bad bet.” “A gift, darling. No balance remains” marks generosity; “You have made yourself expensive” is a threat. “An uncovered vessel” insults carelessness. Speech patterns are tendencies, not mandatory accents. Ordinary speech uses magical colors; technical rules may use mana.

## Wetcraft: six skills and equivalent exchange
**Wetcraft** is fluid-based alchemy using blood, tears, venom, secretions and other vital fluids taken from other races. **Greater suffering during extraction yields purer and more potent fluid.** Harvesting is among the setting's most brutal industries, often concealed from consumers of its civic comforts.

| Discipline | Domain |
|---|---|
| Elixircraft | Consumable remedies, restorative draughts, intoxicants, temporary adaptations and permanent mutation preparations. |
| Venomcraft | Poisons, antidotes, debilitating substances and skin secretion modification. |
| Ordnance | Alchemical weapons, payloads, corrosives, ammunition and military charges. |
| Reshaping | Active alteration of physical form and structure; construction, passages, reinforcement and machine repair. |
| Recomposition | Active chemical changes, separation, purification and industrial material processing. |
| Vital Transmutation | Active tissue alteration, wound closure, reconstruction, mutation and, at the highest mastery, near-lossless conversion of one mana color into another. |

Three skills craft consumables and three practice active transmutation. Written **alchemical circles** define and constrain exchanges, from portable inscribed tools to permanent industrial arrays. Modern philosopher's stones mediate demanding work and require replenishment.

**Weight is a primary factor in equivalent exchange**, alongside constituents and complexity. Blue mana powers changes without providing unlimited matter. Repair requires surviving pieces or replacement resources; wound closure does not freely replace missing blood or tissue. Blood purification yields water and residues. Concrete can become limestone powder with its other constituents separated or redirected. Recomposition can make oxygen-rich pockets that intensify existing fire; actual explosive effects need further magical or alchemical properties.

Alchemy is the technological root of the Scarlands, especially construction. Kobold engineering uses interacting mechanisms; Nymbrin alter substances and organisms. Repair can rejoin matter; pumps can be living organs; lamps luminous compounds; storage uses preservatives. Both traditions collaborate. Kobold machines need components and maintenance; living alchemy can require food, rebalancing and medical care. Exact new skill/rank/SP/MP and reagent costs remain unapproved.

## Permanent mutation and Hexen
Elixircraft can guide lasting transformations over time; Vital Transmutation directly reshapes living tissues. Combined work can change glands, replace structures, produce adaptations and reorganize living beings into **living machinery**: pumps, filters, secretion producers, heat regulators and motive systems.

Permanence does not guarantee health. Special diets, stabilizers or continued mana may be necessary. Intelligence may remain, be damaged or have communication deliberately suppressed. **Every mutant is a Hexen and generates hex.** Elementals and modified Starspawn are not exempt from mutation's pollution.

Hexen occupy the lowest Scarlands social position. For most, the realistic options are **slavery or banditry**; independent work and guild access are severely restricted. Intelligent people may be legally classified as equipment. Escaped Hexen pool specialized survival resources.

A previously respected Nymbrin who becomes Hexen **retains existing legal protections**. Their standing is a significant exception to typical exclusion. They may hide in secluded Pazara dens or use costly temporary disguising preparations. Extortionate contracts for treatment, shelter and discretion can consume their wealth even while their rights endure.

## Ardor draughts: passion potions
Love potions are more accurately **passion potions**, commonly **ardor draughts**. They induce attachment toward the individual **whose vital fluids were used in construction**, not an arbitrarily named person. Popular alongside blood bonds, they may supply emotional attachment while the bond enforces duties.

Adults may willingly accept them for a fee; some Nymbrin do. Contracts can specify donor, strength, duration, treatment and lasting-effect compensation. Attachment does not guarantee affection, obedience or sound judgment.

Unpredictable outcomes include possessiveness, separation distress, jealousy, confinement or violence framed as protection, lasting fixation, resentment, derangement and conflict with existing loyalties or bonds. Someone may love and hate the donor simultaneously. Repeated exposure or Vital Transmutation may make effects permanent; simply removing the original potion need not reverse them. These are fictional magical effects, not a real-world preparation recipe.

## Five intoxicants and Vren distribution
Nymbrin manufacture much of the supply; Vren merchants, court monopolies and military suppliers distribute it.

| Drug | Effect | Risk |
|---|---|---|
| Glimmerdew | Mild euphoria, brighter colors and sociability; common at meals and festivals. | Relatively harmless in ordinary use, low dependence; most popular. |
| Hushmilk | Skin-toxin derivative: numbness, pain relief and floating calm; medicinal and recreational. | Highly addictive; hidden injuries and dangerous incapacitation. |
| Crownwake | Confidence, wakefulness and apparent effortless competence. | Highly addictive; exhaustion, agitation and impaired judgment. |
| Velvet Mourning | Dreamlike emotional release and memory-colored visions; celebrations and funerals. | Highly addictive; withdrawal from ordinary life. |
| Rapture Salt | Prepared recreational forms produce exhilaration, warmth and apparent fearlessness; related military formulations are the Vren's standard alchemical explosive and preferred ordnance. | Highly addictive; contaminated or degraded recreational supplies can become unstable. |

No real-world explosive or drug recipes are established.

## Pazara and chitin
**Pazara** is dark, waxlike, living and semisentient. Resource scarcity means **most Scarlands homes are grown** from it. Young tissue is pliable; mature structures develop chambers, supporting tissues and **arteries** distributing nourishment. Skilled alchemists guide growth, shrinkage and changed layouts over time.

Nymbrin use this for secret passages, vaults, workshops and escape routes. Buildings can differ from recorded plans. Pazara needs a little vital fluid, but prolonged starvation weakens it and poisoned nourishment can sicken or kill it. Its presence indicates nearby alchemists.

Pazara forms vague attachments, resentments and habits without human-level understanding. It may conceal silverware, slam doors, disturb a clock to warn of intruders or make a favored room more comfortable. It can misidentify threats or keep preparing for a dead resident. Temperament affects property transactions.

A special alchemical process can create **monster houses**, violent structures using passages, doors and living tissue against occupants. **The process is fatal to the structure in the long run**, consuming and destroying sustaining tissue.

Processed, hardened Pazara is called **chitin** in the Scarlands and is common in Scarlant weapons, armor, shields, components and casings.

## Elementals, hex and the Haze
**Elementals** are the most influential Hexen: creatures producing apparently endless mana relative to other races. Fire and plant variants are planned for future racial development. Fire elementals supply enormous industrial power. Their full racial mechanics remain undeveloped; no free infinite gameplay mana is established.

**Hex** is magical pollution: acutely toxic, damaging to mana-powered systems and responsible for a special madness after prolonged exposure. The approved descriptive name for that madness is **hex delirium**: disturbed perception, compulsions, paranoia and unreliable magical control.

Airborne red smog is **the Haze**. Enchantments misfire, processes drift and living machinery develops abnormalities under contamination. Outsiders in severe city pollution can suffocate within minutes and need specialized masks called **clearbreaths**. Ordinary cloth is inadequate.

People born and raised in polluted lower districts adapt enough to avoid its acute sting, at a cost of lifespans at least **one-third shorter**; tolerance is not freedom from accumulated harm. Most Vren and many palace/upper-city slaves lack this tolerance. Masters use access to clean air and masks to prevent escape. Haze also makes invasion difficult.

Starspawn are especially sensitive. Organized groups attack hex-producing factories in self-preservation. **Hex storms** can cause environmental collapse and infrastructure failure, often gathering around escaped elementals or powerful Starspawn driven mad by smog.

Hex has long been theorized to explain the Scarlands' wasteland; this remains a theory, not a confirmed exclusive cause. **Haze exposure is the primary cause of dragon eggs developing into toxic dragons.**

## Universal balance foundation
**All Nymbrin alchemy produces hex.** This includes beneficial healing, construction, purification, manufacturing and near-lossless mana-color conversion. Greater scale, complexity, permanence and power increase the burden. Expertise improves efficiency; containment moves or delays exposure without erasing it. Hexen add continuing emissions.

Consumable design is tied to **Gem costs and power**, calibrated around **30% of a Hero's typical Gem budget allocated to consumables**. That is a design allocation, not compulsory spending or a free allowance. The budget time window, exact prices, yields, combat actions, numerical hex thresholds and rank effects remain **UNRESOLVED** and must be calibrated before final mechanics are approved.

## Preserved boundaries
No Nymbrin starting attribute array, stat multiplier, full SP/MP progression, clutch size, exact jump distance, exact toxin dose or complete elemental race is specified. Adult appearance follows the global guide while retaining Nymbrin anatomy. Modern stones obey equivalent exchange; lost true stones are the exception. Historical manufacture and exact permanent ancient homunculus mechanism remain mysteries.
