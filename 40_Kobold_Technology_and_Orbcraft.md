# Kobold Technology and Orbcraft

**AUTHOR APPROVED — November 25, 2026.** This is the owning reference for the technological framework, not a completed skill purchase catalogue. Existing combat, equipment, mana, Hex, Shimmer and racial prerequisites remain operative.

## November 26, 2026 — AUTHOR APPROVED: Codex stability, mana affinity and Hex containment

Use listed module MP costs (including Overdrive and other modifiers); no extra universal exponential output-cost curve. Preserve applicable Weight/Integration rules. Ordinary modern Codexes store multiple colors within one shared Capacity; unaligned modules accept any color, aligned modules require their constructed affinity and gain its benefits. Advanced colored cores hold one color with proposed-and-accepted working +50% Capacity; no color conversion. Multiple Codexes permitted, with actual weight, supply and stability constraints. Capacity/Discharge/Recharge denote stored MP, output and input per turn. Personal mana or another compatible supply can recharge in one action, bounded by available mana, source discharge, receiver recharge and free capacity. Rapid recharge releases accumulated Hex in connected Cell; gradual servicing avoids personal exposure under suitable conditions. No fictitious waste from empty cells. Absolute Codex ratings, model/submodule calibration and rounding remain open.

Stability: an assembly or independent gadget with Codexes checks on confirmed critical hit or crossing below half maximum HP; simultaneous triggers once, no recursive check from stability damage. d20 + Stability versus DC10, no automatic natural-20 success. Combined stored MP / combined capacity >=75% rolls twice takes worse, <=25% automatically passes, otherwise normal. First Codex +0, each additional -4. Failure makes machine inoperable one turn and loses half remaining mana in each Codex (preserve color proportions); fail by >=10 additionally deals 25% maximum HP damage. Stored Hex unchanged. Stabilizing Chamber per Codex grants +2 assembly Stability, +25% Codex Weight, -10% Discharge; replaces proposed rupture isolation chamber. Rounding and precise released-mana environmental routing open.

Hex generation working baseline 1 particle per actual MP, modified by applicable Hex efficiency; ordinary depleted mana remains distinct for industrial accounting. Cell capacity/weight; working reference capacity twice equivalent standard Codex MP. Filter equal/higher Quality than Codex captures all; each tier below loses one third, capped at total escape; no filter captures none. Shared filter benchmarks highest connected Codex Quality; direct feed uses powered module Quality. Capacity overflow escapes, no automatic shutdown. Personal exposure none/half/full of released particles depending on actual proximity and delivery; remote operator not inherently exposed, but local environmental hazards remain. Rapid absorbed particle gives 10 Hexed buildup per existing 20 canon. Working rounding upward for escaping and absorbed whole particles; precompute routine values. No additional remote-operation MP penalty yet.

Cell removal and installation each one action; Quick Cell combines both into one action. Normal replacement safe without special seal. Replace before rapid recharge to avoid dumping accumulated waste; recharge one action. Gradual servicing and facility collection remain available. Optional Cell submodules: Filter, Expansion Chamber (+50% capacity), Containment Seal (60-day versus standard 30-day leakage half-life), Exhaust Vent, Quick Cell, Purge Valve; advanced Compression Chamber (increased permanent capacity, mutually exclusive with Expansion), Transfer Pump, Weaponized Cell, Flux Cell, Emergency Containment. No Active Filter. Specialized functions can be submodules of host Cell; standalone independently operated device is Gadget.

Weaponized Cell uses host delivery (movement trails, weapon contact, emitter/sprayer); working 1 MP releases up to 5 stored Hex, generates ordinary operating waste in existing Cell, not independent attack or extra Mount. Actual absorbed Hex, host delivery and Quality output caps apply; exact attack/tile caps open, retain one environmental packet per turn. Flux Cell consumes balanced pure mana of all five colors and stored Hex with suitable physical organic carrier (e.g. water/carbon stock); working recipe 1 MP each color +10 Hex + carrier yields one provisional Flux unit, no additional Hex from conversion. No matter creation, free energy or repeal of existing industrial economics. Emergency Containment passive, no extra Hex, temporarily doubles capacity for five turns after crossing normal capacity; remaining excess released afterward, removing Cell does not reset expiration. Actual exposure and safe handling distinguish ordinary replacement from later overflow. Exact material quantities, prices, Flux unit, weaponized output and transfer/compression ratings open.

No free extra turns or automatic stacking of Speed. Arcane Field exact profile remains unresolved.

Arcane Field compatibility to be detailed. Seals are frame submodules, not propulsion submodules, for water-resistant construction. ## November 26, 2026 — AUTHOR APPROVED: frame construction and structural loads

Frames have HP, AR, ER, Weight, Load and Mounts; handling, Speed, MP consumption and Integration arise from finished equipment and application. Material, size, Quality and optional modifications determine frame statistics. Working Quality structural multipliers Junk/I/II/III/IV/V = 0.5/1/1.5/2/2.5/3 without automatic Weight increase. QI Boughsteel reference size Load/relative Weight: miniature 1/0.1, handheld 4/1, personal 16/4, vehicle 64/16, industrial 256/64; custom scale within categories is permitted. These are relative units, not kilograms.

Approved working material multipliers relative to Boughsteel (HP / sustained Load / Burst Load / operational Weight): Boughsteel 1/1/1/1; Springsteel 0.8/1/2/0.8; Adamantite 2/2/2/2.5; Reprise Bronze 0.75/0.75/0.75/1; Skyvein 0.6/0.75/0.75/negative (exact uplift unresolved; inertia and nourishment retained). Derive AR/ER from applicable existing material and protection rules, not a new independent armor table; Reprise Bronze working frame AR multiplier 0.75. Serpent Steel is removed from the technological frame catalogue: reforging kills it and loses its special properties, leaving Adamantite-equivalent stats; enduring living material requires underground access to its specific managmins. Preserve existing ancient salvage lore.

Reprise Bronze machinery specifically engineered to collapse has compressed Weight and volume **10%** of operational values, restores only original operational dimensions, and cannot function as a miniature version while compressed. Requires purpose-built compatible internals, not ordinary rigid internals indiscriminately; fabrication takes **twice** normal time. No free material creation or enlargement beyond design. 

Load demand = module Output × Stress Factor (working reference factors: sensors/computation 0, gentle movement 0.25, ordinary locomotion 0.5, sustained manipulation 0.75, heavy mechanical work 1, rapid propulsion 1.5, extreme acceleration 2, violent impulse/recoil 3). Output is family-specific, not necessarily damage. Compare simultaneously active demands with frame capacity; mutually exclusive functions can share capacity. Separate Burst Load only when supported; normally limit output rather than spontaneously break, deliberate overloading is risky and its procedure unresolved. Load is primarily a construction calculation; exact output units, burst duration and overload resolution open.

Optional frame modifications include reinforcement (Boughsteel/Adamantite, HP/Load), physical plating (Boughsteel/Adamantite, AR), magical plating (Nullporcelain, eligible ER), transparent plating (Glassweave), shock absorption and suspension (Springsteel/mechanical parts), stabilization (gyroscopes/fields), Weight compensation (Skyvein), concealment (ordinary stock/flux/coatings), thermal management (conductive/cooling parts), internal magical insulation. External magical plating protects against applicable incoming attacks; internal insulation protects vulnerable machinery from interference; neither automatically substitutes for the other. Nullporcelain is a modification rather than primary frame material; preserve ceramic fragility. Different real material recipes and costs, not uniform upgrade prices. No separate numerical Bulk stat: use Size, Weight, Mounts and physical geometry/compatibility.

Weight consequences depend on support: handheld/worn equipment uses applicable handling and burden, powered armor actuator demand, walking/wheeled machines speed/acceleration and efficiency, aircraft lift/payload, stationary devices transport and foundations. Pilots do not supply vehicle STR. Propulsion has Rated Weight, Speed and mana consumption; approved illustrative working model for positive Weight ratio r=actual/rated: relative speed min(1,1/r), MP multiplier max(0.5,r), subject to real physical limits, separate flight lift requirements and retained inertia. No infinite speed from reduced Weight. Exact propulsion profiles, lift limits, costs and advanced matching-color options are next design work, not yet approved.

## November 25, 2026 — AUTHOR APPROVED: mounts, primary modules and submodules

Terminology: a Module is a primary component providing a capability; a Submodule is an optional enhancement/support component; a Gadget is a complete functional device; a Frame supports an Assembly and its physical loads; a Mount is capacity for one integrated Gadget; an Assembly is multiple Gadgets working together. Assemblies require frames; mounted assemblies may use the host frame if it supports their combined loads. Standalone gadgets may have ordinary casing/handles without a separately engineered frame, but load-bearing or weapon handling requirements still apply. Supporting components do not consume Mounts on their own. Fabrication I–V retains 1/2/4/8/16 integrated gadget Mounts and corresponding module manufacturing Quality limits, not an ownership limit. Count genuinely distinct operational functions rather than every internal component; do not evade assembly limits through nested packaging.

Each functional family has a basic primary component and a minimal viable design; most auxiliary parts are optional. Crude designs can be extremely weak, inefficient, short-ranged or unable to meet actual task loads. Examples: raw mana through a focuser can produce a very poor beam; a vehicle without sufficient supporting output may be unable to move. Recipes identify compatible optional submodule types, normally one of each type per primary module; no unlimited same-type stacking or universal one-submodule limit. No additional Fabrication-rank submodule-slot progression. Submodules should be meaningful choices through Weight, handling, structural capacity, mana, Hex, material and other functional tradeoffs; avoid a separate universal internal space-point system unless later justified.

Working family catalogue (not finalized recipes): emitter with focuser/amplifier/stabilizer; Codex with regulator/reinforcement; Hex-Cell with filter/seal; Orb with accelerator/memory; receiver/transmitter with signal amplification/filtering/directional relay; Magic Mirror with optical enhancements; actuator with gearing/stabilization; neural interface with processing/rejection support; reaction chamber with mixing/dispensing/thermal regulation. Shared frames, Codexes, controllers, regulators, Hex-Cells and filters are permitted where physically compatible and adequately sized; connected functions compete for real capacity, share waste and may share failure vulnerabilities. Count actual physical functional modules once in assembly Quality, with equal weighting; exact optional/missing-part appraisal and rounding open. Common performance vocabulary Power, Range, Accuracy, Efficiency, Hex and Capacity; only applicable stats needed. Exact modifiers, recipes and prices open.

Powered gadgets may be direct-fed from compatible operator mana, self-contained with dedicated Codex, or integrated with shared supply. Direct feed does not add personal spell Power to gadget stats. Without adequate waste containment, direct-fed operation exposes operator immediately under approved Hex/recharge principles; exact absorbed fraction, output waste and servicing rules open. Hex-Cells are optional but valuable, filters can extend effective containment at physical cost. Preserve conservation and actual resource accounting.

Magic Mirrors, not cameras, provide visual sensing by encoding imagery into Cipherlight, with Orbs processing and compatible hardware storing data. Foundational field manipulation permits ordinary beams without color-specific bonuses. Advanced research may unlock color-specific effects requiring actual matching mana; explore applying this distinction across the second level of upgrades. Preserve red heat, blue precision, green kinetic power, white efficiency, black raw power identities. Exact upgrade tree, costs, prerequisites and modifiers deferred.

## November 25, 2026 — AUTHOR APPROVED: Integration lore and material preparation

Mana is a consistent energy unit. A powerful living Anima can extract substantially greater useful magical effect from a given MP than a novice Anima or an automated mechanism. Machines extract useful effect through their engineered capabilities, with finite hardware output and design-dependent efficiency; this does not imply all machinery is less efficient than ordinary biological labor or change existing industrial work-per-MP benchmarks. Integration is the efficiency middle ground: an operator's capability realizes the machine's engineered potential, while insufficient Integration wastes mana, reduces performance and generates more Hex. Full Integration does not exceed the hardware maximum. No additional harmonization mechanic, daily ritual, fuel ownership modifier, personalized MP grade or new named subsystem is adopted. Compatible commercially purchased fuel remains usable under existing color/storage rules. Preserve approved Integration curve and ordinary spell/implement rules.

Orbcraft Fabrication can use prepared exotic materials beyond Boughsteel, including appropriately graded Adamantite and alloys, obtained in the required quantities through purchase, salvage or lawful production. Orbcraft alone does not confer Anvilcant metallurgy: qualified Anvilcant practitioners can refine Adamantite ore and manufacture alloys from their actual constituent materials (including iron where applicable), subject to established recipes, qualifications and exceptional material restrictions. A dual Orbcraft/Anvilcant specialist can turn suitable metal-rich rocks into sophisticated machinery through lawful refining and fabrication; adding qualified Wetcraft permits exceptionally portable extraction/preparation using circles and eligible transmutation, without waiving equivalent exchange, material availability, mana/Hex costs, applicable tool/forge/skill requirements or exceptional divine/living-material restrictions. The chalk-only image illustrates interdisciplinary field capability, not literal creation from nothing or a blanket waiver of requirements. No new material recipes, Quality conversion, stock tariffs or cross-Craft prerequisites established.

## November 25, 2026 — AUTHOR APPROVED: equipment families, Integration and composite Quality

Directed technological weapons (laser pistols, Arcblades, arc welders) use applicable existing weapon scaling, wielder attributes, training, Weight and handling, rather than universally adding INT spell Power. Modular equipment contributions permit output, efficiency, precision and other tradeoffs. Magical handling or computationally assisted modes may support INT specialists where legitimately designed and balanced; exact mode requirements and weapon profiles remain open. INT remains the governing Orbcraft engineering attribute, not compulsory for every weapon user. The earlier universal Control Limit proposal is superseded by this direction. Integrated armor/implants and autonomous functions are distinct mechanical families; assemblies can combine them.

Implanted armor consumes Weight/handling efficiently but incurs chronic Hex Rejection. Powered armor grants module-derived protection and powered assistance (e.g. STR, mobility, reflexes, environmental protection); unpowered plating retains physical protection, powered assistance ceases, and physical burden remains. No recursive self-funded Weight capacity or stacked nested exoskeleton multiplication. Exact stats, costs and coverage remain open.

Autonomous or sophisticated body-integrated functions have fixed hardware performance and primarily INT-based Integration requirements (including relevant magical/neural handling where appropriate). Meeting requirements realizes full hardware performance; excess operator capability does not increase hardware maximum. Underqualified operators retain useful partial performance at steep mana cost, generating correspondingly more Hex from actual expenditure. Approved working curve: I=min(1, applicable operator capability / machine requirement); at I>=0.25, operational performance=0.5+0.5I, mana consumption multiplier=2^(4*(1-I)); below 0.25 meaningful Integration unavailable under this working model. At I=0.25/0.5/0.75/1, performance=62.5/75/87.5/100%, consumption=8/4/2/1 times. At I=0.9, exact formula gives approximately 1.32× (earlier illustrative 1.25× was approximate; rounding/calibration remain open). Do not scale passive physical HP/AR by Integration indiscriminately. Exact operator capability formula, machine requirements, output applicability and integer MP rounding remain open. Preserve bandwidth reallocation, actual energy, waste and action economy. No additional weapon-function Integration thresholds are approved: an underqualified operator who can integrate may fire the major weapon, with reduced performance and costly fuel/Hex consequences.

Gadget composite Quality is the equally weighted arithmetic mean of its actual functional component Qualities, not a weighted importance score. Individual module statistics remain independent; overall Quality is useful for classification, appraisal and reference requirements, but cannot conceal especially demanding subsystems. Exact missing/optional module accounting and rounding remain open. All component families matter, including Hex-Cells, whose effective waste containment is a major operating-cost/safety advantage; no automatic 50% MP discount or precise universal cost split inferred. Frame determines physical strength, durability, applicable weapon stat requirements/handling and supported output; a weak frame can bottleneck a strong emitter. Same-Quality material choices matter: Springsteel can support short bursts, Adamantite can support stronger loads at increased Weight, or frame Quality can be upgraded. Exact burst duty cycle, load limits, materials and Weight stats open; retain existing material canon.

Component specialization is distinct from Quality: balanced, high-output, efficient and precision focusers are examples. Higher-output components may trade energy/Hex, range or other demands for output; lower-Quality high-output components may exceed higher-Quality balanced ones in one metric. For working high-output line use illustrative performance multipliers 1/1.5/2/2.5/3 and relevant demand 1/1.5/2.5/4/6 across QI–V, not a universal multiplier for all components. Demands grow faster than performance, but not every penalty applies to every module. Efficient components can improve efficiency. Preserve resource, material, cost and action constraints; no new universal output formula.

## November 25, 2026 — AUTHOR APPROVED: modular Orbcraft progression and gadget servicing

Orbcraft is INT-governed, not DEX-governed. Technological research primarily unlocks distinct capabilities horizontally; five root technologies grant foundational use, with advanced research extending capabilities and unlocking specialized or exotic uses. Most gadgets require only a handful of technologies; final root names, research catalogue, SP costs and prerequisites remain open. The earlier six subject divisions remain descriptive, not a fixed purchase tree.

Gadgets are permanent, economically valuable physical inventions, not temporary conjurations or a limited daily inventory. Actual materials, tools and labor are required; no generic off-screen salvage or spontaneous components. Inferior materials, excessive fuel consumption and neglected filtration make improvised devices less desirable without automatic expiration. Standardized interchangeable modules have individual Quality and functional performance. Orb implements combine general ability with specialization choices such as bandwidth, mana storage and fabrication; bandwidth comes from both equipment and expertise, exact capacities open. Integrated machines combine multiple gadgets, with energy and bandwidth determined by their actual subsystems; even remote robots require perception, propulsion, power, control and useful tools. Preserve action economy and meaningful command requirements.

Fabrication ranks I–V permit manufacturing modules of Quality I–V respectively and integrating at most 1/2/4/8/16 gadgets respectively in a single assembly. This is not a limit on owned gadgets. Higher-Quality purchased or salvaged modules may be installed with suitable technology, tools and compatibility. Basic conventional Anvilcant weapon manufacture is permitted at two Quality tiers below Fabrication (III→I, IV→II, V→III; no ordinary positive Quality at I–II); no enchantment permission or replacement of Centaur expertise. Ordinary improvised tools remain distinct. Module sizes are miniature, handheld, personal, vehicle and industrial. Compatible same-size transfers preserve both items and cost time alone. Increasing a module one size category consumes additional resources and fabrication time but is cheaper than manufacturing the larger module anew; destructive downsizing/reworking is possible with significant time and fabrication. Exact recipes, salvage yields, size limits and times open.

Gadget use generates Hex waste. Adequately contained ordinary use (e.g. a filtered laser pistol) does not inherently add personal Hex each shot; poorly filtered emergency gadgets (e.g. jetpack leaps) may expose the operator. Codex refueling and Hex-Cell servicing are linked: slow out-of-combat recharging permits gradual release or appropriate collection without personal exposure under suitable conditions; rapid combat refueling causes a burst of Hex under existing status rules. Environmental release remains pollution, not annihilation. Preserve stored waste leakage and existing Hexed status and augmentation Hex Rejection rules. Exact waste per MP, cell capacity, overflow handling, recharge time/action, personal buildup conversion and tracking implementation remain unresolved.

## Industrial identity
Centaurs specialize in physically efficient, intricate clockwork, superior materials and permanent enchantments; Nymbrin specialize in direct magical transformation and consumables; Kobolds specialize in information, control, flexible fabrication and applied fields. These are comparative advantages, not racial monopolies. Imperial machinery is generally less physically/fuel efficient than comparable clockwork but compensates with intelligent control, precision, flexibility and reduced supervision. Its greater expenditure creates greater waste-handling demands. Advanced Imperial sectors have futuristic magitech; disadvantaged districts and regions can be grungy, poor and technologically uneven, with cyberpunk influences. Recent augmentation industrialization helps modernize a historically stagnant empire against growing competitors.

## Arcane Fields
Mana becomes controllable arcane plasma; controlled gravity manipulation (telekinesis fields) couples fields to ordinary matter and provides containment/manipulation rather than requiring ordinary magnetic attraction. Fields direct heat and kinetic force; thermal concentration is exceptional, whereas kinetic force is more effective over broad surfaces than at tiny points. Kinetic projection produces equal opposing recoil (1:1); anchors, reaction mechanisms or sufficient operator strength must accommodate it. Conversion/containment entail entropy, and fields do not create matter or free energy. Complex configurations require computational control. Vehicles still need lawful lift, thrust and momentum exchange. All five colors can generate basic fields, with specialties:
- Red: heat.
- Blue: precision.
- Green: kinetic power.
- White: efficiency.
- Black: uncontrolled raw power.

Kobolds may access colors through existing imprinting and lawful external supplies, not a new unrestricted personal mana grant. Field-generated beams suffer substantial divergence/range attenuation; inverse-square energy density is a useful physical model beyond the divergence-dominated reference distance, not an approved HP damage formula. Thermal cutters, welding, contained plasma Arcblades, field manipulators, energy shielding and distributed impact mitigation are applications. No automatic material penetration or bullet immunity. Centaurs originated ballistic gunpowder firearms; Imperial firearms favor field-projected energy or field-accelerated ammunition and micro-codex bullets, with warfare emphasizing heat and energy. Existing defenses, status, weapon budgets and action rules apply. Extraordinary esoteric effects (e.g. time manipulation) need appropriate additional magical expertise; no new ability granted.

## Adventuring and disciplines
Kobold scrappers can improvise inventions appropriate to their expertise using found materials, salvaged parts, tools, time, flux, mana and Orb bandwidth. High expertise remains useful without a full workshop. Ordinary toolmaking is accessible to other races; no monopoly on basic crafting. Resource categories include timber, cordage, metal stock/scrap, glass, conductors, mechanical, mana and optical components. Recipes distinguish consumed inputs from reusable tools, expertise, time and condition. Progression from improvised tools (e.g. pickaxes) through manufactured tools, powered tools and industrial systems uses a common design vocabulary. Ordinary nonmagical fabrication need not spend MP; specialized preparation and magical operation may.
Approved discipline framework: Fabrication (salvage, tools, repair, components), Computation (programming, storage, conditional control), Transmission (Cipherlight, sensors, communications), Manatech (fields, regulators, powered instruments), Augmentation (prosthetics and wearables), Automation (vehicles, drones and industrial systems). These are subject divisions, not approved ranks or prices.
Materials determine what can be built, mana what can be powered, bandwidth what can be intelligently controlled. Bandwidth is allocated ongoing capacity, not another consumable MP pool. Reallocation is quick but costs an action in combat, analogous to drawing a weapon. Simple local controllers need not consume the same bandwidth as complex intelligent devices. Bandwidth never grants additional attacks or turns; constructs are optional, and industrial autonomy does not authorize combat autonomy. Preserve meaningful command/action and combined attack safeguards; no blanket import of Cogmarshal CP rules. Existing equipment and resistance budgets remain.
Codex/Condex energy storage and transport is an Imperial advantage. Field fabrication is possible by salvaging sufficient Leywells with specialized tools; inexpensive mass production requires abundant glassweave and high-end Imperial machinery. High-grade Orbs similarly favor specialized production; field salvage and crude assembly are possible. Exact recipes and canonical spelling reconciliation with older references remain open.

## Fluxfluid
Fluxfluid (flux) is a versatile organic chemical derived from a balanced mix of the five managmins. A field crafter can combine suitable plants, water and heat (including an Arcane Field) to produce it. Base flux can be formulated for particular uses by adding a selected managmin. Uses include bonding/glue, acid, cleaning, corrosion treatment, purification, concentration, material preparation, ordinary chemical synthesis and rapid access to heat. It does not create missing matter, directly reshape bulk material, or replace magical potions or Wetcraft; suitable feedstock, equipment, labor and expertise remain necessary. Nymbrin industrial alchemy is an ideal production method; Canopy agricultural supply is strategically important. Ordinary organic synthesis is independently viable.
Flux is a chemical expression of mana equilibrium, not a universal metaphysical antidote to Hex. It is shelf-stable chemical product, not stored active Hex.

## Industrial waste and reclamation
Imperial operations produce Hex-based, comparatively portable waste collected in Hex-Cells. Cells contain and transport waste, not preserve it indefinitely; their loss follows the normal stored-mana half-life benchmark (30 days), with escaped material entering the environment rather than disappearing. Preserve distinct fresh Hex coherence (60 seconds) and ambient ecological dissipation and 8x environmental burden rules in 20/39; no new universal ambient half-life. Dedicated reclamation facilities combine collected waste with balanced inputs of all five mana colors, organic feedstock and extremely large energy expenditure to produce stable flux. This is less efficient than dedicated organic production, justified by avoided disposal costs; Hex is not essential to flux. Account for new operating pollution and ordinary depleted mana under existing rules; no self-powering loop. Exact capture efficiency, total input ratios, output yield, energy and disposal costs remain unresolved.

## Augmentation and Hex Rejection
Clockwork prosthetics primarily restore lost limb function mechanically; Kobold neural interfaces enable responsive replacements and enhancements beyond natural function. Examples include sensory aids, artificial eyes, reinforced limbs and field-assisted musculature. Industrial and medical productivity matter beyond combat. Kharak military cybernetics are a natural application, not a universal racial feature. Body-interfacing technology is inherently rejected by the soul, accumulating bodily Hex; ordinary non-interfacing mechanical replacements need not cause this effect. Tougher creatures tolerate more; Endurance is the intended tolerance basis, exact formula open. This is a distinct balancing mechanism from Soul Strain, not an exemption from existing equipment rules. Medication suppresses/removes accumulated Hex with strong side effects but does not remove the cause; continued augmentation can create dependence on Scarlands Hex expertise. Possible progression: tolerated, rejection (discomfort, fatigue, sensory disturbances), delirium (hallucinations, impaired judgment, emotional instability), critical corruption, Hexen transformation and potential demonic corruption subject to existing Hexen/possession lore. Not inevitable stages or approved thresholds. Exact rates, recovery, medication, implant slots/budgets and transformation mechanics open.

## Prisminds and network dangers
Orbs are computational hardware, Magic Mirrors encode/decode pictures, Cipherlight is the information medium, Coilnet the network. Prisminds are born individual artificial intelligences, capable of sophisticated reasoning but working best with guidance and human creativity. They cannot be copied; they can be transferred between compatible hosts and are not necessarily anchored to their original Orb. Commercial hatcheries raise and educate them; training particular skills/talents is approximately 20–200 times faster than conventional education depending on sophistication and available industry, yet requires intensive work. Hardware capacity and training still limit performance. Ciphergeist is a dangerous rogue intelligence; **Geist** is the corrected colloquial name. Phantoms are malicious software/viruses or demonic presences in machines; a potential demonic form can spread Hex through Coilnet, distinct from ordinary rogue intelligence. Exact transmission, safeguards and possession mechanics remain open. Powerful rogue minds can compromise city infrastructure and cause blackouts, an existential Imperial risk. The Cogmind incident involved close Kobold/Centaur collaboration, combining intelligent Orb control with clockwork; its fallout widened the political and technological division between Imperium and Canopy. No further incident particulars established.

## Industrial applications and infrastructure
Kobolds favor operator-extending vehicles (including small-scale gyrocopters), Centaurs favor labor-performing golems; neither preference is exclusive. Imperial applications include geological scanners, excavators, precision manufacturing, automated farms, diagnostics, surgery, construction, navigation, transport, inventory, energy management, sorting/recycling and scientific instruments. These are examples, not guaranteed universal availability. Worldspindles remain ancient irreplaceable ley-line-bound installations under existing limits, though Kobolds are approaching reproducibility. Other lost-knowledge miracles exist, generally of regional rather than empire-wide significance. No newly reproducible Worldspindle, free energy or named miracle established.
