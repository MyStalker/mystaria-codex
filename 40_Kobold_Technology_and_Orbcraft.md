Directed technological weapons (laser pistols, Arcblades, arc welders) use applicable existing weapon scaling, wielder attributes, training, Weight and handling, rather than universally adding INT spell Power. Modular equipment contributions permit output, efficiency, precision and other tradeoffs. Magical handling or computationally assisted modes may support INT specialists where legitimately designed and balanced; exact mode requirements and weapon profiles remain open. INT remains the governing Orbcraft engineering attribute, not compulsory for every weapon user. The earlier universal Control Limit proposal is superseded by this direction. Integrated armor/implants and autonomous functions are distinct mechanical families; assemblies can combine them.

Implanted armor consumes Weight/handling efficiently but incurs chronic Hex Rejection. Powered armor grants module-derived protection and powered mechanical substitution/assistance (e.g. machine-supplied STR, mobility, reflexes, environmental protection), not direct wearer attribute enhancements; unpowered plating retains physical protection, powered assistance ceases, and physical burden remains. No recursive self-funded Weight capacity or stacked nested exoskeleton multiplication. Exact stats, costs and coverage remain open.

Sophisticated actively integrated functions have fixed hardware performance and primarily INT-based Integration requirements (including relevant magical/neural handling where appropriate). Wholly automated functions instead use designed hardware output and normal autonomous costs without applying user Integration; ordinary manual gadget use follows the latest accessible-gadget rule above. Meeting requirements realizes full hardware performance; excess operator capability does not increase hardware maximum; see the November 26 above-requirement efficiency rule for active Integration. Underqualified operators retain useful partial performance at steep mana cost, generating correspondingly more Hex from actual expenditure. Earlier working underqualification curve (I capped here for output only; see newer MP rule): I=min(1, applicable operator capability / machine requirement); at I>=0.25, operational performance=0.5+0.5I, mana consumption multiplier=2^(4*(1-I)); below 0.25 meaningful Integration unavailable under this working model. At I=0.25/0.5/0.75/1, performance=62.5/75/87.5/100%, consumption=8/4/2/1 times. At I=0.9, exact formula gives approximately 1.32× (earlier illustrative 1.25× was approximate; rounding/calibration remain open). Do not scale passive physical HP/AR by Integration indiscriminately. Exact operator capability formula, machine requirements, output applicability and integer MP rounding remain open. Preserve bandwidth reallocation, actual energy, waste and action economy. No additional weapon-function Integration thresholds are approved: an underqualified operator who can integrate may fire the major weapon, with reduced performance and costly fuel/Hex consequences.

Gadget composite Quality is the equally weighted arithmetic mean of its actual functional component Qualities, not a weighted importance score. Individual module statistics remain independent; overall Quality is useful for classification, appraisal and reference requirements, but cannot conceal especially demanding subsystems. Exact missing/optional module accounting and rounding remain open. All component families matter, including Hex-Cells, whose effective waste containment is a major operating-cost/safety advantage; no automatic 50% MP discount or precise universal cost split inferred. Frame determines physical strength, durability, applicable weapon stat requirements/handling and supported output; a weak frame can bottleneck a strong emitter. Same-Quality material choices matter: Springsteel can support short bursts, Adamantite can support stronger loads at increased Weight, or frame Quality can be upgraded. Exact burst duty cycle, load limits, materials and Weight stats open; retain existing material canon.

Component specialization is distinct from Quality: balanced, high-output, efficient and precision focusers are examples. Higher-output components may trade energy/Hex, range or other demands for output; lower-Quality high-output components may exceed higher-Quality balanced ones in one metric. For working high-output line use illustrative performance multipliers 1/1.5/2/2.5/3 and relevant demand 1/1.5/2.5/4/6 across QI–V, not a universal multiplier for all components. Demands grow faster than performance, but not every penalty applies to every module. Efficient components can improve efficiency. Preserve resource, material, cost and action constraints; no new universal output formula.

## November 25, 2026 — AUTHOR APPROVED: modular Orbcraft progression and gadget servicing

Orbcraft is INT-governed, not DEX-governed. Technological research primarily unlocks distinct capabilities horizontally; five root technologies grant foundational use, with advanced research extending capabilities and unlocking specialized or exotic uses. Most gadgets require only a handful of technologies; final root names, research catalogue, SP costs and prerequisites remain open. The earlier six subject divisions remain descriptive, not a fixed purchase tree.

Gadgets are permanent, economically valuable physical inventions, not temporary conjurations or a limited daily inventory. Actual materials, tools and labor are required; no generic off-screen salvage or spontaneous components. Inferior materials, excessive fuel consumption and neglected filtration make improvised devices less desirable without automatic expiration. Standardized interchangeable modules have individual Quality and functional performance. Orb implements combine general ability with specialization choices such as bandwidth, mana storage and fabrication; bandwidth comes from both equipment and expertise, exact capacities open. Integrated machines combine multiple gadgets, with energy and bandwidth determined by their actual subsystems; even remote robots require perception, propulsion, power, control and useful tools. Preserve action economy and meaningful command requirements.

Fabrication ranks I–V permit manufacturing modules of Quality I–V respectively and integrating at most 1/2/4/8/16 gadgets respectively in a single assembly. This is not a limit on owned gadgets. Higher-Quality purchased or salvaged modules may be installed with suitable technology, tools and compatibility. Baseline conventional and technological weapon manufacture is permitted at full qualified Fabrication Quality using appropriate prepared stock; no enchantment or special-metal refining permission. Ordinary improvised tools remain distinct. Module sizes are miniature, handheld, personal, vehicle and industrial. Compatible same-size transfers preserve both items and cost time alone. Increasing a module one size category consumes additional resources and fabrication time but is cheaper than manufacturing the larger module anew; destructive downsizing/reworking is possible with significant time and fabrication. Exact recipes, salvage yields, size limits and times open.

Gadget use generates Hex waste. Adequately contained ordinary use (e.g. a filtered laser pistol) does not inherently add personal Hex each shot; poorly filtered emergency gadgets (e.g. jetpack leaps) may expose the operator. Codex refueling and Hex-Cell servicing are linked: slow out-of-combat recharging permits gradual release or appropriate collection without personal exposure under suitable conditions; rapid combat refueling causes a burst of Hex under existing status rules. Environmental release remains pollution, not annihilation. Preserve stored waste leakage and existing Hexed status and augmentation Hex Rejection rules. Exact waste per MP, cell capacity, overflow handling, recharge time/action, personal buildup conversion and tracking implementation remain unresolved.

## Industrial identity
Centaurs specialize in physically efficient, intricate clockwork, superior materials and permanent enchantments; Nymbrin specialize in direct magical transformation and consumables; Kobolds specialize in information, control, flexible fabrication and applied fields. These are comparative advantages, not racial monopolies. Imperial machinery is generally less physically/fuel efficient than comparable clockwork but compensates with intelligent control, precision, flexibility and reduced supervision. Its greater expenditure creates greater waste-handling demands. Advanced Imperial sectors have futuristic magitech; disadvantaged districts and regions can be grungy, poor and technologically uneven, with cyberpunk influences. Recent augmentation industrialization helps modernize a historically stagnant empire against growing competitors.

## Arcane Fields
Mana becomes controllable arcane plasma; controlled gravity manipulation (telekinesis fields) couples fields to ordinary matter and provides containment/manipulation rather than requiring ordinary magnetic attraction. Fields direct heat and kinetic force; thermal concentration is exceptional, whereas kinetic force is more effective over broad surfaces than at tiny points. Kinetic projection produces equal opposing recoil (1:1); anchors, reaction mechanisms or sufficient operator strength must accommodate it. Conversion/containment entail entropy, and fields do not create matter or free energy. Complex configurations require computational control. Vehicles still need lawful lift, thrust and momentum exchange. All five colors can generate basic fields, with specialties:
- Red: heat.
- Blue: precision.
- Green: kinetic power.
- White: efficiency.
- Black: uncontrolled raw power.

Kobolds may access colors through existing imprinting and lawful external supplies, not a new unrestricted personal mana grant. Field-generated beams suffer substantial divergence/range attenuation; inverse-square energy density is a useful physical model beyond the divergence-dominated reference distance, not an approved HP damage formula. Thermal cutters, welding, contained plasma Arcblades, field manipulators, energy shielding and distributed impact mitigation are applications. No automatic material penetration or bullet immunity. Centaurs originated ballistic gunpowder firearms; Imperial firearms favor field-projected energy or field-accelerated ammunition and micro-codex bullets, with warfare emphasizing heat and energy. Existing defenses, status, weapon budgets and action rules apply. Extraordinary esoteric effects (e.g. time manipulation) need appropriate additional magical expertise; no new ability granted.

## Adventuring and disciplines
Kobold scrappers can improvise inventions appropriate to their expertise using found materials, salvaged parts, tools, time, flux, mana and Orb bandwidth. High expertise remains useful without a full workshop. Ordinary toolmaking is accessible to other races; no monopoly on basic crafting. Resource categories include timber, cordage, metal stock/scrap, glass, conductors, mechanical, mana and optical components. Recipes distinguish consumed inputs from reusable tools, expertise, time and condition. Progression from improvised tools (e.g. pickaxes) through manufactured tools, powered tools and industrial systems uses a common design vocabulary. Ordinary nonmagical fabrication need not spend MP; specialized preparation and magical operation may.
Approved discipline framework: Fabrication (salvage, tools, repair, components), Computation (programming, storage, conditional control), Transmission (Cipherlight, sensors, communications), Manatech (fields, regulators, powered instruments), Augmentation (prosthetics and wearables), Automation (vehicles, drones and industrial systems). These are subject divisions, not approved ranks or prices.
Materials determine what can be built, mana what can be powered, bandwidth what can be intelligently controlled. Bandwidth is allocated ongoing capacity, not another consumable MP pool. Reallocation is quick but costs an action in combat, analogous to drawing a weapon. Simple local controllers need not consume the same bandwidth as complex intelligent devices. Bandwidth never grants additional attacks or turns; constructs are optional, and industrial autonomy does not authorize combat autonomy. Preserve meaningful command/action and combined attack safeguards; no blanket import of Cogmarshal CP rules. Existing equipment and resistance budgets remain.
Codex/Condex energy storage and transport is an Imperial advantage. Field fabrication is possible by salvaging sufficient Leywells with specialized tools; inexpensive mass production requires abundant glassweave and high-end Imperial machinery. High-grade Orbs similarly favor specialized production; field salvage and crude assembly are possible. Exact recipes and canonical spelling reconciliation with older references remain open.

## Fluxfluid
Fluxfluid (flux) is a versatile organic chemical derived from a balanced mix of the five managmins. A field crafter can combine suitable plants, water and heat (including an Arcane Field) to produce it. Base flux can be formulated for particular uses by adding a selected managmin. Uses include bonding/glue, acid, cleaning, corrosion treatment, purification, concentration, material preparation, ordinary chemical synthesis and rapid access to heat. It does not create missing matter, directly reshape bulk material, or replace magical potions or Wetcraft; suitable feedstock, equipment, labor and expertise remain necessary. Nymbrin industrial alchemy is an ideal production method; Canopy agricultural supply is strategically important. Ordinary organic synthesis is independently viable.
Flux is a chemical expression of mana equilibrium, not a universal metaphysical antidote to Hex. It is shelf-stable chemical product, not stored active Hex.

## Industrial waste and reclamation
Imperial operations produce Hex-based, comparatively portable waste collected in Hex-Cells. Cells contain and transport waste, not preserve it indefinitely; their loss follows the normal stored-mana half-life benchmark (30 days), with escaped material entering the environment rather than disappearing. Preserve distinct fresh Hex coherence (60 seconds) and ambient ecological dissipation and 8x environmental burden rules in 20/39; no new universal ambient half-life. Dedicated reclamation facilities combine collected waste with balanced inputs of all five mana colors, organic feedstock and extremely large energy expenditure to produce stable flux. This is less efficient than dedicated organic production, justified by avoided disposal costs; Hex is not essential to flux. Account for new operating pollution and ordinary depleted mana under existing rules; no self-powering loop. Exact capture efficiency, total input ratios, output yield, energy and disposal costs remain unresolved.

## Augmentation and Hex Rejection
Clockwork prosthetics primarily restore lost limb function mechanically; Kobold neural interfaces enable responsive replacements and enhancements beyond natural function. Examples include sensory aids, artificial eyes, reinforced limbs and field-assisted musculature. Industrial and medical productivity matter beyond combat. Kharak military cybernetics are a natural application, not a universal racial feature. Body-interfacing technology is inherently rejected by the soul, accumulating bodily Hex; ordinary non-interfacing mechanical replacements need not cause this effect. Tougher creatures tolerate more; Endurance is the intended tolerance basis, exact formula open. This is a distinct balancing mechanism from Soul Strain, not an exemption from existing equipment rules. Medication suppresses/removes accumulated Hex with strong side effects but does not remove the cause; continued augmentation can create dependence on Scarlands Hex expertise. Possible progression: tolerated, rejection (discomfort, fatigue, sensory disturbances), delirium (hallucinations, impaired judgment, emotional instability), critical corruption, Hexen transformation and potential demonic corruption subject to existing Hexen/possession lore. Not inevitable stages or approved thresholds. Exact rates, recovery, medication, implant slots/budgets and transformation mechanics open.

## Prisminds and network dangers
Orbs are computational hardware, Magic Mirrors encode/decode pictures, Cipherlight is the information medium, Coilnet the network. Prisminds are born individual artificial intelligences, capable of sophisticated reasoning but working best with guidance and human creativity. They cannot be copied; they can be transferred between compatible hosts and are not necessarily anchored to their original Orb. Commercial hatcheries raise and educate them; training particular skills/talents is approximately 20–200 times faster than conventional education depending on sophistication and available industry, yet requires intensive work. Hardware capacity and training still limit performance. Ciphergeist is a dangerous rogue intelligence; **Geist** is the corrected colloquial name. Phantoms are malicious software/viruses or demonic presences in machines; a potential demonic form can spread Hex through Coilnet, distinct from ordinary rogue intelligence. Exact transmission, safeguards and possession mechanics remain open. Powerful rogue minds can compromise city infrastructure and cause blackouts, an existential Imperial risk. The Cogmind incident involved close Kobold/Centaur collaboration, combining intelligent Orb control with clockwork; its fallout widened the political and technological division between Imperium and Canopy. No further incident particulars established.
