# Wakecraft — The Six Arts of the Living Tide

**Status: APPROVED CANON — definitive Tidekin racial magic.**  
**Source:** Original six-art Wakecraft expansion, subsequently approved explicitly by the user.  
**Region:** The Scarlands, Gilded Coil, and beyond.

“The Naga can move a sword without touching it. Very impressive, darling. Now watch me turn the water inside their expensive lock to ice.”

Wakecraft is one of Mystaria's most versatile magical traditions, comparable in depth to Naga Psycraft and Moontouched Manabending.

Its practitioners are called Wakewrights.

Where Psychics command the mind and Warlocks command raw mana, Wakewrights command water and everything water makes possible.

A master Wakewright can create ice bridges, cut through stone with pressurized water, extract drinkable water from the earth, reconstruct crimes from a spilled drink, command aquatic Starspawn, build living coral fortifications, and reduce enemies to desiccated dust.

They cannot heal wounds or manipulate the weather. Their power comes from controlling water as a physical substance, not from commanding nature generally.

> **Authority note (November 4, 2026):** The user's explicit approval of *Wakecraft — The Six Arts of the Living Tide* supersedes the older three-discipline summary of Tidekin magic. All six named skills and their five ranks are canon. The source's descriptive examples illustrate possible applications, not guaranteed effects independent of resources, resistance, or MP expenditure.
>
> **Naming:** The approved racial name in this source is **Tidekin**. **Wakewright** is the practitioner title. The label *Tideborn* used conversationally is not established here as an official renaming.

## I. The Structure of Wakecraft

Wakecraft consists of one innate foundational ability and six purchasable skills.

Hydrokinesis

Innate · Free

The universal Tidekin ability to manipulate existing water, including changing it between liquid, ice, and vapor.

All six Wakecraft skills build upon Hydrokinesis.

Three fundamental specializations

Wakeforce

Power, pressure, precision

Longwake

Range, paths, distance

Deepdraw

Sources and extraction

Three exotic specializations

Mirrorwake

Memory and reflections

Reefcalling

Starspawn and coral

Saltwake

Salt, sand, desiccation

The three fundamental skills improve how Hydrokinesis works. The three exotic skills introduce entirely new things that can be accomplished with it.

All six remain independent specializations. A Rank V Saltwake specialist can be a formidable Hero without purchasing Wakeforce, Longwake, or Deepdraw.

However, their techniques become considerably more versatile when combined.

## II. Hydrokinesis — The Innate Tide

All naturally blue-affined Tidekin possess basic Hydrokinesis.

It is available without Skill Points and represents an instinctive connection to the movement and physical behavior of water.

### Basic Capabilities

| Mechanic           | Hydrokinesis                                                                |
| ------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Mana affinity      | Blue                                                                        |
| Base range         | 2 + effective INT meters                                                    |
| Available water    | Existing accessible water                                                   |
| Physical control   | Pulling, pushing, carrying, circulating, and shaping                        |
| State manipulation | Liquid water, solid ice, and water vapor                                    |
| Currentweaving     | Basic water-flow manipulation                                               |
| Tidebinding        | Basic density and buoyancy manipulation                                     |
| Cantrips           | Harmless manipulation using trace mana                                      |
| MP                 | Natural effective END, reduced by equipment Soul Strain for usable capacity |
| Channel Limit      | Ceiling of effective INT ÷ 5 MP per immediate effect                        |

The classic Tidekin disciplines of Currentweaving and Tidebinding are preserved as established techniques, now understood as fundamental applications of Hydrokinesis that can be developed through the six skills.

### What Basic Hydrokinesis Can Do

A Wakewright can pull water from a nearby basin, make a small stream flow uphill, shape a floating sphere of water, freeze a puddle, or turn a little water into mist.

They can also adjust a small volume of water's density and buoyancy, although dramatic alterations require greater skill and MP.

The change from liquid to solid or gas is magical. It does not require a separate red-mana heat spell, but larger or faster phase changes require correspondingly greater blue-mana expenditure.

Importantly, Hydrokinesis does not create water from nothing.

If a Wakewright wants to make an ice bridge, they need enough water to construct it. If they want to create steam, they need water to transform.

At the beginning of their career, even a reasonably small amount of water can be remarkably useful.

### Hydrokinesis Cantrips

Using trace mana, Wakewrights can perform tiny, harmless effects without spending MP: levitating a few droplets, stirring a drink, forming decorative ice crystals, changing the shape of a small splash, or producing a brief ribbon of mist.

These cannot inflict meaningful combat damage, overcome active resistance, or be accumulated into a source of free MP.

A Tidekin might casually float their drink toward themselves while lounging beside a canal.

Or spray a few drops at a friend who just said something particularly foolish.

Both are culturally acceptable uses of magic. The second is considerably more popular.

### Why Wakewrights Can Still Fight in Dry Regions

Wakewrights are not completely powerless outside water-rich environments.

Most carry small reservoirs of water, which can be recovered and reused when an effect does not disperse or contaminate it.

Ice weapons, pressurized jets, and moisture-bound tools can all operate from carried supplies.

Deepdraw specialists can also recover water from humidity, soil, stone, vegetation, and underground sources.

A Rank V Wakewright who specializes in desert survival may be more comfortable crossing the Gilded Coil's Outer Reaches than a warrior carrying conventional water supplies.

Their greatest enemy is not dry land itself, but complete isolation from all accessible water and usable moisture.

# III. The Three Fundamental Skills

These three disciplines improve Hydrokinesis itself. Each specializes in a different question: How forcefully can I manipulate water? How far can I reach? Where can I obtain it?

## 1. Wakeforce — The Weight of Water

Power, pressure, precision, and physical engineering

Wakeforce transforms basic Hydrokinesis into a serious physical discipline. It allows water to become a weapon, construction tool, defensive barrier, or industrial machine.

| Rank | Technique        | New capability                                                                                                                                                                          |
| ---- | ---------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| I    | Pressurehand     | Concentrate moving water into powerful jets, compact masses, and directional bursts. Strike opponents, propel objects, or operate hydraulic mechanisms.                                 |
| II   | Razorwake        | Create exceptionally precise cutting streams and water-driven abrasives. Cut stone, carve wood, shape metal, and manufacture intricate components.                                      |
| III  | Tidebinding      | Exert sophisticated control over water's effective density, pressure, and buoyancy. Support heavy objects, manipulate underwater movement, and create highly resistant liquid barriers. |
| IV   | Threefold Weave  | Manipulate liquid, ice, and vapor simultaneously within a single effect. Produce intricate mechanisms, rapidly alternating barriers, or complex moving constructions.                   |
| V    | Abyssal Pressure | Create immense, precisely directed hydraulic forces capable of crushing heavy fortifications, resisting enormous physical loads, or reshaping substantial terrain.                      |

### An Industrial Revolution in a Water Bottle

A Wakeforce specialist becomes much more than a combatant.

At Rank II, they can operate precision cutting streams useful for masonry, sculpture, mining, and manufacturing.

At Rank III, they can help move enormous cargo loads using manipulated buoyancy.

At Rank IV, they might form temporary ice supports, pressure-driven mechanisms, and steam-powered tools without needing a traditional workshop.

At Rank V, a powerful Wakewright could perform feats normally requiring extensive industrial machinery, provided sufficient water and mana are available.

In combat, the same abilities become extremely dangerous.

A Wakewright does not need to carry a steel sword if they can turn a narrow stream of water into a blade capable of cutting through armor.

Defining strength: Direct combat, craftsmanship, construction, and heavy engineering.

## 2. Longwake — The Water Between Here and There

Range, indirect manipulation, remote control, and distant waterways

Longwake is less concerned with increasing raw distance numerically and more concerned with overcoming the practical limitations of remote manipulation.

| Rank | Technique       | New capability                                                                                                                                                                               |
| ---- | --------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| I    | Crooked Current | Manipulate water around corners, behind obstacles, and through indirect paths rather than requiring a straight line of effect.                                                               |
| II   | Wandering Drop  | Sustain precise control of a detached quantity of water as it travels through open air, across gaps, or away from its original source.                                                       |
| III  | Wake Anchor     | Establish a temporary magical connection to a designated quantity of water, allowing manipulation through that anchor beyond ordinary personal range.                                        |
| IV   | River Road      | Extend Hydrokinesis through continuous waterways, pipelines, underground streams, and interconnected bodies of water over extraordinary distances.                                           |
| V    | Farshore        | Maintain a deep connection to a previously established water anchor across vast distances, allowing sophisticated manipulation of water at that location without physically traveling there. |

### Practical Applications

A Longwake specialist could retrieve an object from the bottom of a locked well, manipulate a canal gate from a safe position, or send a controlled stream of water through a narrow passage to operate a distant mechanism.

At higher ranks, they become indispensable to cities built around waterways.

A Rank IV Wakewright might repair a submerged obstruction miles away by manipulating the water surrounding it.

A Rank V specialist could maintain a connection to a reservoir in another city and operate its hydraulic mechanisms remotely.

Farshore requires an established anchor, sufficient mana, and access to water at the destination. It is not unrestricted teleportation or omniscience.

Defining strength: Remote operations, exploration, transportation, infiltration, and infrastructure maintenance.

## 3. Deepdraw — The Hidden Waters

Extracting water from unexpected sources

Deepdraw is the discipline that makes Wakecraft valuable far beyond rivers and oceans.

It specializes in discovering and extracting water that ordinary Hydrokinesis cannot easily reach.

| Rank | Technique         | New capability                                                                                                                                                                   |
| ---- | ----------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| I    | Dewcatcher        | Gather dispersed moisture from the air, damp soil, and porous materials, concentrating it into usable liquid water.                                                              |
| II   | Pure Current      | Separate water from dissolved salts, suspended impurities, and many contaminants, making previously unsuitable water useful.                                                     |
| III  | Buried Spring     | Sense and extract water from underground aquifers, concealed reservoirs, and saturated rock formations.                                                                          |
| IV   | Borrowed Moisture | Draw water from absorbent organic materials, vegetation, wood, and other water-bearing substances. Extraction from living tissues faces biological and magical resistance.       |
| V    | The Last Drop     | Extract even tightly bound water from hydrated minerals and exceptionally resistant materials, potentially rendering substances completely dry or destabilizing their structure. |

### The Desert Wakewright

A Rank I Deepdraw specialist can survive in environments where an ordinary Tidekin would struggle, gathering moisture from cool nighttime air or damp ground.

However, water must actually be present. A hot, exceptionally dry desert may yield very little moisture, regardless of magical talent.

At Rank III, finding buried groundwater becomes a defining talent.

At Rank V, a Wakewright could extract water from unusual mineral deposits or geological formations that ordinary well-diggers cannot exploit.

This makes Deepdraw specialists valuable to caravans, frontier settlements, archaeological expeditions, and disaster relief operations.

A sufficiently powerful Deepdraw specialist can also weaponize dehydration, although Saltwake develops this application into a much more sophisticated offensive art.

Defining strength: Wilderness survival, water purification, exploration, resource extraction, and frontier engineering.

# IV. The Three Exotic Skills

These disciplines transform Wakecraft into something more unusual than physical water manipulation.

They retain the benefits of fundamental specializations when applicable, but each provides enough distinct capabilities to support an independent career or heroic fighting style.

## 4. Mirrorwake — The Water Remembers

History, investigation, reflections, and magical observation

Mirrorwake retains its established identity as the Tidekin ability to perceive memories preserved in water.

Its expanded version makes Wakewrights exceptional investigators, scouts, historians, and information specialists.

Unlike Naga Divination, Mirrorwake does not predict the future.

It reveals what water has already witnessed.

| Rank | Technique           | New capability                                                                                                                                                                  |
| ---- | ------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| I    | Lingering Ripples   | Read recent impressions from water, recognizing movements, disturbances, nearby presences, and significant events it experienced.                                               |
| II   | Second Reflection   | Examine an individual water memory in greater detail, recovering visual impressions, vibrations, and partial sounds that would otherwise be lost.                               |
| III  | Poured Witness      | Reconstruct a remembered scene across a suitable water surface, allowing others to observe the recovered imagery rather than relying solely on the Wakewright's interpretation. |
| IV   | Living Reflection   | Observe current events through a suitably established water connection, turning an accessible body of water into a remote viewing surface.                                      |
| V    | The River Remembers | Recover deeper, older, and more fragmented water memories, potentially reconstructing significant historical events from surviving magical impressions.                         |

### Cipherlight traces, interference, and counterfeit water memories — approved November 4, 2026

**Mnemonic Arts interaction (November 4):** Mindprism recordings, Skillprint uploads and Dreamcasts are specialized uses of Cipherlight; water exposed to their optical transmissions is subject to the same approved Mirrorwake impression, overload, and sophisticated-forgery principles. A Wakewright may detect that powerful Cipherlight passed through the water, **not automatically recover a person's private Echo, decode a Skillprint, or gain that person's abilities**. The existing five Mirrorwake ranks and their MP/SP costs remain unchanged. Full memory-technology rules: `19_The_Mnemonic_Arts_and_Echo_Economy.md`.


**Cipherlight** is the light used to carry encoded information through Kobold Orbcraft, Mirrorcraft, lighthouses and reflective underwater pipelines. Since **Mirrorwake reads impressions retained by water**, the passage of Cipherlight through exposed water is **exceptionally detectable by Wakewrights** and can leave **strong, persistent impressions**. An underground pipeline might conceal a transmission route from ordinary observers while leaving telltale traces to a Tidekin investigator with access to the affected water. Detecting evidence of transmission does not grant automatic decryption of its coded message.

An especially intense Cipherlight transmission can **overload and obscure other memories in the water**; purposeful use may hide traces of an event. With **particularly sophisticated manipulation**, an actor may also **manufacture false or misleading Mirrorwake impressions**. Such forgery is possible, not automatic or foolproof: a skilled Wakewright may recognize discrepancies, and the water's actual exposure, contamination, age and context still matter. This interaction does **not** give every Cipherlight user an unconditional power to rewrite water memories or make Mirrorwake unreliable in all cases.

The approved five Mirrorwake techniques (Lingering Ripples, Second Reflection, Poured Witness, Living Reflection, The River Remembers) and their SP/MP schedules remain unchanged. No new spell rank, fixed strength or duration, specific forgery procedure, decoding ability, or automatic defense is established by this addition. The larger infrastructure and security implications are documented in `18_The_Gilded_Coil_Imperial_Gazetteer.md`.


### The Detective Who Questions Puddles

Imagine a murder investigation.

The victim was discovered in an otherwise empty room.

A Rank I Mirrorwake specialist examines a spilled drink and recognizes that several people were nearby shortly before the death.

A Rank II specialist extracts a partial reflection of the murderer's clothing.

At Rank III, the Wakewright recreates the scene across a basin of water, allowing investigators to compare details.

A Rank V specialist might discover that the water in the room carries echoes of a much older event associated with the same location.

However, Mirrorwake is not infallible.

Water remembers what it was exposed to, not everything that happened nearby. Some impressions are incomplete, contaminated, obscured, or degraded by time.

A pool does not automatically know what occurred behind a closed door.

### Other Applications

Mirrorwake can also be used for archaeology, navigation, reconnaissance, verifying shipping accidents, reconstructing engineering failures, and investigating sabotage.

A traveler could carry a small vessel of prepared water as a limited magical witness to events along a journey.

Mirrorwake's most powerful techniques make the skill particularly valuable to adventurers.

Ancient Serpentari ruins often contain water that has circulated through their infrastructure for centuries.

Recovering even a fragment of its memories could reveal secrets that written records have long since forgotten.

Defining strength: Investigation, historical discovery, reconnaissance, and information warfare.

## 5. Reefcalling — The Court of the Deep

Aquatic Starspawn, reef formation, and living fortifications

Reefcalling combines two related aspects of aquatic life: communicating with water-dwelling Starspawn and manipulating the mineral structures of reefs.

It introduces an important new ecological possibility: some aquatic Starspawn produce coral-like mineral formations as part of their natural life cycles.

These formations can survive as physical reefstone after the Starspawn move elsewhere.

Reefstone is not itself a separate ordinary animal species; it is a mineral structure created by Starspawn.

Wakewrights can encourage, reshape, and accelerate the formation of these structures by manipulating water and dissolved minerals.

| Rank | Technique          | New capability                                                                                                                                                                         |
| ---- | ------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| I    | Current Courtesy   | Convey simple intentions and requests to aquatic Starspawn through deliberately patterned water movements, while interpreting their responses.                                         |
| II   | Reefseed           | Encourage dissolved minerals and suitable sediment to form coral-like reefstone around a prepared seed, producing useful hard structures.                                              |
| III  | Tide Captain       | Establish a magically reinforced command connection with a water-dwelling Starspawn, coordinating its movements and actions through the surrounding water.                             |
| IV   | Coral Bulwark      | Rapidly grow and shape substantial reefstone constructions, including walls, protective shells, barricades, and architectural supports.                                                |
| V    | Crown of the Shoal | Exercise extraordinary authority through aquatic magical connections, coordinating powerful Starspawn and shaping large, enduring reef fortifications sustained by suitable resources. |

### Commanding Starspawn

Reefcalling is not a Summoner Accord.

It cannot summon Starspawn from the Celestial Realm, automatically grant access to Named Spells, or override Celestial Recall.

Instead, its practitioners develop ways to communicate, coordinate, and sometimes exert authority over Starspawn already present in water.

At lower ranks, the relationship is primarily cooperative.

At higher ranks, a Wakewright can issue increasingly precise magical commands through the surrounding water.

A willing Starspawn may respond as a trusted partner. An unwilling one can resist, particularly if it possesses significant intelligence, magical strength, or an established relationship with someone else.

Reefcalling does not grant Tidekin the ability to speak Starsong. It communicates through a separate medium of water movement and magical resonance.

### The Coral Fortress

A Rank IV Reefcaller can rapidly build defensive walls by gathering and reorganizing minerals suspended in available water.

A Rank V specialist might establish an entire reef fortress, provided enough water, mineral matter, mana, and time are available.

In a desert, a Reefcaller could still construct modest reefstone barriers using carried water, local minerals, and prepared reefseeds.

The resulting structures are real mineral formations rather than illusions. Their size is constrained by the available material.

This makes Reefcalling valuable to military engineers, architects, caravan guards, coastal communities, and settlement builders.

### The Tidekin Attitude

Reefcallers are famous for treating aquatic Starspawn as difficult professional colleagues.

A skilled practitioner might spend an hour arguing with a territorial marine Starspawn over the precise position of a defensive reef.

The Starspawn could be perfectly capable of understanding the request and simply refuse to cooperate.

For a Tidekin, this is not necessarily a failure of diplomacy.

It is merely Tuesday.

Defining strength: Aquatic companionship, defensive construction, marine exploration, and battlefield fortification.

## 6. Saltwake — The Art of Thirst

Salt, sand, desiccation, and temporary material transformation

Saltwake is the most dangerous and unconventional form of Wakecraft.

It takes advantage of the relationship between water, dissolved minerals, and dry matter.

At lower ranks, it is a useful craft discipline.

At higher ranks, it becomes one of Mystaria's most frightening forms of magic.

| Rank | Technique    | New capability                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| ---- | ------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| I    | Salt Sifting | Control dissolved salts and suspended mineral particles through water, allowing precise separation, crystallization, and manipulation of grit.                                                    |
| II   | Dunebinding  | Bind loose sand and mineral grains into coherent temporary structures using manipulated moisture, pressure, and blue-mana adaptation.                                                             |
| III  | Saltbite     | Force water out of vulnerable materials and living tissue, causing cracking, brittleness, severe dehydration, and potentially lethal damage.                                                      |
| IV   | False Matter | Hydrate and magically transform prepared sand into temporary materials that imitate stone, glass, ceramics, and certain other inorganic substances.                                               |
| V    | Dustfall     | Unravel the relationship between moisture and physical structure, enabling catastrophic desiccation that can reduce a sufficiently overwhelmed living body or vulnerable object to granular dust. |

### Saltwake as a Craft

Saltwake's first two ranks are useful for food preservation, salt production, ceramics, construction, and sand-based engineering.

At Rank IV, the discipline becomes extraordinary.

A Wakewright could hydrate sand and shape it into a temporary bridge, a cutting implement, a protective wall, or the appearance of a more sophisticated inorganic object.

The material retains its original mass. It is not created from nothing.

These temporary transformations require mana and sufficient moisture to establish, and they eventually revert toward their underlying mineral composition.

A bridge made of False Matter is therefore useful for crossing a ravine, but a terrible foundation for a permanent palace unless supplemented with conventional construction.

### Dustfall — The Cruelest Tide

Saltwake's highest rank can inflict catastrophic dehydration.

A weak opponent might be reduced to brittle, sand-like residue when overwhelmed by an extremely powerful application.

A stronger opponent can resist the process through magical defense, Endurance, and other protective abilities.

Against an equal or superior enemy, Dustfall is generally a dangerous sustained attack rather than an automatic death sentence.

The resulting dust is desiccated and magically altered residue, not necessarily pure natural silica sand.

A Dustfall victim's soul is not automatically destroyed, and Starspawn retain their established Astral Heart and Celestial Recall protections.

The reverse application is equally remarkable: a Saltwake Grandmaster can rehydrate and reshape prepared mineral residue into elaborate temporary matter.

This does not restore a dead creature to life, even if the resulting object resembles its former body.

### A Rather Tidekin Sense of Humor

Saltwake practitioners have a reputation for especially cruel verbal humor.

One might approach a heavily armored warrior, glance at their equipment, and remark:

“Darling, I don't think you've considered how much of your body is water.”

Unlike Naga psychic domination or Vren blood curses, Saltwake is entirely physical.

That makes its threat particularly unsettling.

Defining strength: Desert survival, material transformation, manufacturing, battlefield control, and lethal desiccation.

# V. How the Skills Combine

The fundamental skills enhance the exotic disciplines wherever their effects apply.

They do not unlock the exotic techniques themselves: those still require their own ranks.

| Combination             | New possibilities                                                                            |
| ----------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Wakeforce + Mirrorwake  | Stabilize moving water to recover clearer impressions and construct durable viewing surfaces |
| Longwake + Mirrorwake   | Investigate distant water sources or observe through remote established water connections    |
| Deepdraw + Mirrorwake   | Recover evidentiary water from damp objects, soil, and otherwise inaccessible places         |
| Wakeforce + Reefcalling | Build stronger, more precisely engineered coral structures and hydraulic fortifications      |
| Longwake + Reefcalling  | Coordinate aquatic Starspawn and construct defenses from safer positions                     |
| Deepdraw + Reefcalling  | Obtain water and mineral-bearing moisture for reef construction outside aquatic environments |
| Wakeforce + Saltwake    | Combine abrasive particles with powerful water jets and precisely shaped mineral blades      |
| Longwake + Saltwake     | Shape sand structures or apply desiccation from greater distances                            |
| Deepdraw + Saltwake     | Extract moisture from difficult sources before using it to shape or dehydrate matter         |

A Tidekin with Rank V Saltwake and Rank I Deepdraw is fundamentally different from one with Rank V Mirrorwake and Rank I Longwake.

Both are viable Heroes, but they solve problems in completely different ways.

## VI. Wakecraft's Relationship to MP

Wakecraft follows Mystaria's established universal magic system.

| Mechanic                        | Rule                                             |
| ------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ |
| Natural MP                      | Floor of natural effective END                   |
| Available MP                    | Natural MP minus equipment Soul Strain           |
| Maximum MP per immediate effect | Ceiling of effective INT ÷ 5                     |
| Cantrips                        | 0 MP, trace mana only                            |
| Stronger effects                | Greater MP expenditure                           |
| Sustained effects               | Activation cost plus upkeep                      |
| Short Rest                      | 20 minutes, restores 25% of available maximum MP |
| Long Rest                       | 8 hours, restores full available MP              |
| Skill Rank                      | Determines what techniques are possible          |

A Wakewright's greatest accomplishments may require large amounts of water, external mana vessels, existing infrastructure, or an extended period of sustained casting.

For example, a Rank V Wakeforce specialist might be able to crush a fortified gate using Abyssal Pressure.

But doing so still requires enough water and MP to accomplish the feat.

The same principle applies to Reefcalling's fortifications and Saltwake's temporary matter.

# VII. The Professions of the Wakewrights

This expanded system makes Tidekin valuable almost anywhere in Mystaria, not just aboard boats.

Razorwrights — Magical Craftspeople

Primarily study Wakeforce.

They cut stone, produce intricate carvings, polish materials, and manufacture precision equipment. Wealthy Naga households often prize their services alongside those of Kobold engineers.

Dewfinders — Desert Guides

Primarily study Deepdraw.

They locate groundwater, purify emergency supplies, and sustain caravans through difficult terrain. Their services are particularly valuable in the Gilded Coil's Outer Reaches and the Scarlands.

Ripplekeepers — Investigators

Primarily study Mirrorwake.

They investigate murders, lost expeditions, stolen goods, historical mysteries, and damaged infrastructure.

Their testimony can be extremely valuable, although sophisticated courts recognize that water memories can be incomplete.

Reefmarshals — Wardens and Builders

Primarily study Reefcalling.

They establish aquatic defenses, negotiate with Starspawn, protect waterways, and construct coral fortifications.

Some work aboard ships; others build frontier defenses or maintain permanent reef settlements.

Duneshapers — Desert Artificers

Primarily study Saltwake.

They produce temporary structures, transform mineral materials, preserve provisions, and help construct settlements where traditional building materials are scarce.

Military Duneshapers are feared for their desiccating techniques.

Wakewrights also serve as mercenaries, scouts, engineers, caravan guards, miners, excavators, shipwrights, archaeologists, and security specialists.

The strongest military Tidekin might wield a pressurized water blade in one hand while trapping an opponent's feet in rapidly formed ice.

A less aggressive specialist could travel with a few bottles of water, an excellent memory, and a talent for discovering secrets.

Both can be successful Heroes.

# VIII. Wakecraft and Tidekin Culture

Wakecraft is an essential part of Tidekin identity rather than merely a magical tradition.

Tidekin admire clever improvisation, flowing movement, and the ability to make an opponent look foolish.

A Wakewright who defeats a physically stronger enemy through an ingenious trick is frequently admired more than one who wins through brute force.

This preference influences how Tidekin teach magic.

## The Three Principles of the Wake

Tidekin instructors traditionally teach three principles:

“Water always finds a way.” There is almost always another method of approaching a problem. A failed direct attack does not mean the situation is hopeless.

“Never fight the current when you can redirect it.” Cleverness and timing are more valuable than wasting energy on unnecessary force.

“If you're going to make a splash, make sure everyone sees it.” Style matters. A successful technique is admirable; an elegant one is unforgettable.

These are cultural sayings, not supernatural restrictions.

### The Importance of Sass

Wakewrights frequently practice competitive verbal sparring alongside magical training.

An instructor might deliberately distract a student with insults while they attempt to maintain a complicated water formation.

The intended lesson is concentration under pressure.

A student who loses control of their magic because an opponent mocked their appearance is considered insufficiently prepared.

Some schools award informal prestige for defeating another Wakewright while delivering an especially memorable insult.

This has produced an entire tradition of spectacular magical duels accompanied by increasingly ridiculous commentary.

Tidekin insist that mockery is an important part of combat education.

Their opponents are less enthusiastic.

### Wakecraft and Slavery

Wakecraft's expanded versatility adds substantial significance to the Tidekin enslaved as Saltbound within the Gilded Coil.

The Naga already employ Tidekin to maintain waterways and operate canals.

Under the revised system, enslaved Wakewrights might also serve as precision cutters, desalination specialists, magical investigators, military engineers, or designers of coral-based defenses.

This makes advanced Wakewrights valuable professional laborers, while creating new reasons for their owners to restrict their autonomy.

A Tidekin with Deepdraw could acquire drinking water even during confinement.

A Longwake specialist might communicate indirectly through connected waterways or manipulate remote hydraulic mechanisms.

A Mirrorwake investigator could potentially discover crimes their owner would prefer to remain hidden.

A Saltwake specialist might possess the power to undermine a stone enclosure or compromise a restraint through moisture and mineral manipulation.

The Naga's reliance on Wakecraft therefore creates a familiar Imperial contradiction: the more valuable Tidekin expertise becomes, the harder it is to keep its practitioners entirely under control.

Crucially, none makes the others obsolete.

A Naga psychokinetic may lift a sword directly. A Tidekin Wakewright may carry that same sword on a controlled stream of water, or use water pressure to cut it apart.

A Naga diviner can attempt to predict what will happen tomorrow. A Mirrorwake specialist can investigate what actually happened yesterday.

A Warlock can drain raw mana from an enchantment. A Wakewright can physically exploit the water powering or surrounding the machine.

They approach problems through different magical principles.

## X. Suggested Final Identity

Wakecraft is a versatile material-manipulation tradition, but not an unrestricted form of transmutation.

It excels wherever water interacts with the physical world: movement, pressure, temperature, memory, minerals, moisture, and aquatic life.

Its limitations are equally important:

- It cannot conjure water from nothing.
- It cannot heal injuries through magical regeneration.
- It cannot control storms, clouds on a regional scale, or weather systems.
- It cannot permanently create arbitrary materials without the necessary physical ingredients.
- It cannot automatically compel every Starspawn.
- It cannot transform Tidekin bodies into other creatures or grant conventional shapeshifting.
- Its strongest effects require substantial MP, preparation, and appropriate resources.

With these restrictions, a Wakewright can be an exceptional adventurer, craftsman, investigator, soldier, or explorer without overshadowing the specialties of other races.

And the Tidekin gain a wonderful new cultural boast:

“The Naga can read your mind. The Vren can taste your blood. The Warlocks can steal your magic. Me? Darling, I can tell you what happened here yesterday, cut through your front door, and make a lovely little bridge out of your driveway. You're welcome.”

**Canon status: APPROVED.** This expansion replaces the earlier limited Wakecraft framework while retaining Currentweaving, Tidebinding, and Mirrorwake in their revised roles. It was explicitly approved as the definitive Tidekin racial Craft.

## November 8, 2026 — Trufula fire suppression interaction (AUTHOR-APPROVED CROSS-CRAFT APPLICATION)

**AUTHOR'S EXPLICIT EXCEPTIONAL-FIRE RULING:** An established Trufula material blaze, including fire in composite armor, **cannot be put out by simply dousing it with ordinary water**. An appropriately **powerful and well-controlled Wakecraft** application **can extinguish** that blaze, using genuine controlled existing water and subject to the existing Hydrokinesis/skill-rank/MP/channel-limit/range/resource boundaries. Ordinary low-power currentweaving, splashing, or a free basic water cantrip is **not** declared sufficient. No universal Wakeforce rank threshold, water volume, exact spell cost, effect duration or fire Elemental Magnitude target has been set; settle those values during elemental and alchemical balance. High-end ice magic and suitable special alchemical extinguishing potions are also valid separate extinguishing methods. This expands *what powerful existing Wakecraft can accomplish* without adding a seventh Wakecraft discipline, revising the six-rank trees, allowing water creation from nothing, or granting an always-available additional combat action.
