## November 8, 2026 — Energy versus magic-generated physical attacks (LATEST AUTHOR GUIDANCE)

**AUTHOR-APPROVED DESIGN PHILOSOPHY:** Direct energy-based spells should generally **deal more damage and offer more tactical versatility/status options** than otherwise comparable magic-generated or magic-propelled physical attack spells. Physical spells are valid deliberate counters against high-MR opponents (for example, Nullporcelain mage hunters), but the ordinary damage catalogue should **not** be about half physical merely for variety or flavor. The ratio is qualitative, not a numeric spell-count quota or a universal attack multiplier. Elemental Magnitude applies to relevant energy spells; MR is a separate item magic-defense property, not ordinary physical AR. Material rules control whether a magically powered attack actually delivers energy or indirect physical impact.

**USER-AUTHORIZED OPTIONAL TEST EXAMPLES, NOT NEW SPELL CANON:** For later simplified benchmarking only, compare existing **Ignis** used as a basic fire-energy attack and a working test called **Slingshot** that produces/propels a physical projectile. An actual rock strike is defended by physical AR and ordinary relevant physical defenses, not by high MR just because magic produced the impetus. ## I. What Sorcery is — established

Sorcery is structured magic voiced through **Elder Resonance**, the draconic spoken interpretation of the Starspawn's Starsong. The **Drakyn originated mortal Sorcery**, although Serpentari magical history and Starspawn traditions are much older. Casting draws on a compatible **mana color**, the caster's **Intelligence**, and the **MP actually channeled**. Stronger applications may demand more mana and risk the caster's Channel Limit. Sorcery Incantations are **learned individually**, and each known Incantation can be modified by separately purchased **alterations**.

A color is not a moral alignment: **red** concerns heat, destruction and energetic empowerment; **blue** concerns water, perception, affection and transformation; **green** life, healing, growth and earth; **white** barriers, light, physical reinforcement and mental wards; **black** fear, darkness, draining, fortune and entropy. The named Incantation establishes a functional principle; an alteration changes how that principle manifests without freely turning it into an unrelated magical system.

### III. Sorcery and Drakyn-specific rules

- Drakyn have **no naturally generated MP**, recover **zero MP through rest**, and depend on feeding on mana-bearing Starspawn (or exceptionally other mortals) for usable mana. Refer to `06_Drakyn.md` for physiology and color replacement.
- Their **Wyrmcraft** metamagic is a separate five-discipline racial Craft, costed separately from Sorcery purchases.
- Any Drakyn can integrate its **innate egg-acquired element into any known Sorcery Incantation** for free, preserving the underlying effect's logic. This supplies no additional mana affinity and cannot bypass basic restrictions (e.g., Kalreth still needs a preexisting injury; Selvok cannot drain absent MP).
- A Drakyn can grant learned Incantations/alterations through a **dragonmark**. The bearer pays an equivalent **Soul Strain** rather than SP unless choosing to buy it off at the normal applicable SP price. See `06_Drakyn.md`.

## V. Sorcery cross-system constraints

- Drakyn may apply elemental integration to a known Incantation **without learning a matching alteration**; this does not supply knowledge of unrelated Incantations.
- Wyrmcraft **Matched Verse** may borrow delivery/range/targeting from one learned Incantation and use another's actual effect. For one-color Drakyn, only the **effect-bearing** Incantation must match held color; both must still be known and paid for separately in SP.
- Dragonmarks may grant a known Incantation or alteration directly, but create Soul Strain borne by the receiver. They do not grant free compatible mana or nullify the receiver's personal casting limits.
- Warlocks manipulate *raw externally available mana*, not structured Sorcery Incantations, except when redirecting or sustaining somebody else's existing spell through their separate skills.
- Wizardry uses fixed learned formulae (standalone formula **2 SP**, subsequent prerequisite formula **1 SP**) and is not the same as Sorcery's alteration system.
- A finalized baseline of **one active mana color at a time** applies specifically to Drakyn's harvested MP pool. It does not mean all Sorcerers of every race are limited to one permanent color.

## Combat vocabulary cross-reference — November 5, 2026

The approved universal vocabulary calls the relevant scaling result **Power**, which does not automatically mean damage; **Resistance** is baseline effective END, potentially subject to narrowly applicable resistance adjustments. **Defense** means withstanding an effect and never includes evasion. A casting implement can modify spell performance only as its actual approved effect specifies; do not assign unapproved Ignis damage, Power multipliers, oil-vulnerability adjustments or fire-resistance bonuses. See `22_Combat_Endurance_and_Shimmer_Guard.md` for the existing quadratic injury-comparison framework and unresolved modifier layering. ## November 5, 2026 — Elemental Magnitude as standard energy-magic notation (CANON direction)

**Elemental Magnitude** is the new universal name for the previously proposed *Contact Pressure* concept. It describes how intensely an attack's energy/element affects a target, accounting for appropriate elemental Resistance and the actual delivery pathway. Energy-based Sorcery Incantations, their alterations and compatible elemental manifestations should list **Elemental Magnitude and eligible Elemental Status** as important mechanical properties when their actual values are developed. This extends the *principle* beyond passive runes, but **does not copy the STR/DEX weapon-contact formula into Sorcery**: Sorcery still uses its own approved INT, mana color, channeled MP, Incantation and alteration mechanics. Do not invent spell damage, MP costs, alterations or scaling factors to fill missing catalogues. Non-energy effects need not produce an elemental status. Drakyn element integration remains governed by existing compatibility and prerequisite rules.

**Elemental Statuses** will use up to **three severity ranks**, conceptually separate from rank I–V runes, and may weaken Resistance to their own element, allowing successive valid effects to become more effective. **Exact** magnitude breakpoints, severity increases, duration, fire periodicity, ice freeze rules, lightning stun, water/air displacement and toxic/hex attribute weakening remain **UNAPPROVED PROPOSALS**. Merely choosing elemental magnitude terminology does not authorize universal guaranteed status or permanent END reductions. Consult `22_Combat_Endurance_and_Shimmer_Guard.md` for the selected contact method and `24_Modular_Equipment_Design_Options_DRAFT.md` for drafts.
