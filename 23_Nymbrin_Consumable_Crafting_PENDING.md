## Core model
Elixircraft, Venomcraft and Ordnance are manufacturing skills. Each rank unlocks the corresponding item tier and retains earlier tiers; ranks need no individually named active techniques. Recipes are separate preparable concoctions, not skill-rank powers. A rank does not grant all recipes, ingredients or free products.

| Rank | Elixircraft | Venomcraft | Ordnance |
|---|---|---|---|
| I | Tier I elixirs | Tier I venoms/antidotes | Tier I ordnance |
| II | Tier II elixirs | Tier II venoms/antidotes | Tier II ordnance |
| III | Tier III elixirs | Tier III venoms/antidotes | Tier III ordnance |
| IV | Tier IV elixirs | Tier IV venoms/antidotes | Tier IV ordnance |
| V | Tier V elixirs | Tier V venoms/antidotes | Tier V ordnance |

Elixircraft prepares medicine, recovery, intoxicants, adaptations, passion potions and mutagenic elixirs. Venomcraft prepares poisons, debilitants, antidotes, repellents and skin-secretion treatments. Ordnance prepares ammunition, charges, payloads and battlefield supplies.

## Cost and progression philosophy
- Gem-equivalent paid cost purchases capability; strength, duration, coverage, delivery, reliability and additional functions enter pricing.
- Calibrate representative supplies against **30% of a Hero's typical Gem budget** over the same explicit adventure/progression window. Not a free allowance, cap or mandatory purchase.
- Higher-rank crafters can still prepare cheaper earlier-tier items.
- Ingredients are consumed and inventory replenished. Recipe knowledge and manufacture are distinct.
- All manufacture produces hex. Containment relocates waste. Permanent mutations generate ongoing hex.
- Higher tiers can add tactical complexity and different applications, not just stronger numbers.
- Assign tiers after complete recipe effects and costs exist. Some recipes may have several versions, others a minimum tier only.
- **Delayed Receipt** and **Split Delivery** are compatible formulation modifiers rather than obligatory standalone recipes.
- Exact Gem costs, yields, rank/SP/MP particulars, action timings and recipe tiers remain unapproved.
- Reshaping, Recomposition and Vital Transmutation remain active skills; their selections are still open. A seventh hex-weapon discipline is now requested.
